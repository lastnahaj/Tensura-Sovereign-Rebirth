# Tensura: Sovereign Rebirth

**Reincarnate. Evolve. Build. Awaken. Rule.**

Tensura: Sovereign Rebirth is a curated fantasy RPG and nation-building
modpack built around **Tensura: Reincarnated**. Begin as a reincarnated being,
develop your race and skills, forge equipment that grows with you, recruit
subordinates, build a living colony, and challenge a world filled with
dungeons, bosses, dimensions, magic, and long-term awakening paths.

This is not a loose collection of content mods. Its systems are configured to
work as one progression model: Tensura owns character growth, Gear Evolution
owns equipment progression, MineColonies owns settlement development, FTB
Teams and Chunks own multiplayer claims, and FTB Quests guides players through
the pack.

## Forge your own sovereign path

- **Reincarnation and evolution** — choose a starting race, gather EP and
  Magicules, acquire skills, evolve, and pursue high-end awakening paths.
- **A growing quest campaign** — explore eight TSR-authored act chapters plus
  the MineColonies integration chapter. The beta loads 140 FTB Quests entries,
  including 64 TSR progression tasks, custom visual headers, and direct links
  to the project wiki.
- **Nation building** — develop a MineColonies settlement, specialize its
  citizens, and connect colony growth with Tensura progression.
- **Evolving equipment** — use Tensura Gear Evolution, Silent Gear, Productive
  Metalworks, artifacts, and carefully integrated storage and forging systems.
- **Magic, bosses, and dimensions** — master Iron's Spells, explore the Aether
  and Twilight Forest, hunt powerful creatures, and take on the wider Tensura
  adventure stack.
- **Exploration worth sharing** — expanded terrain, caves, structures, loot,
  waystones, Xaero maps, personal loot, and multiplayer voice chat support a
  long-running server world.
- **Curated client experience** — custom FancyMenu and Drippy presentation,
  readable HUD defaults, circular top-right minimap, tuned video settings, and
  persistent keybind customization.
- **Server-minded defaults** — separate client and server packages,
  conservative performance settings, backups, permissions, claims, profiling,
  and administration tools.

## Beta content

Version 1 Beta is playable and has passed clean dedicated-server world
creation, save and shutdown, warm restart, extracted server-package startup,
graphical client startup, quest loading, and changed-key persistence tests.
The beta is still being tuned for progression balance, high-concurrency
multiplayer, long-duration world generation, claim permissions, and late-game
boss scaling.

Worlds created during the beta should be backed up before every update.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**
- NeoForge **21.1.248**
- 64-bit Java **21**
- **8 GB** of client memory recommended; close heavy background applications
  if the system has limited RAM

Use the matching server package for dedicated hosting. Do not copy the entire
client instance to a server.

## Default controls and presentation

The first-launch profile uses a 150 FPS cap, GUI scale 2, 8-chunk render and
simulation distances, Bright brightness, VSync off, and view bobbing off.
Tensura skill activation slots default to **Z**, **X**, and **C**. Optional
resource packs are not bundled or enabled in this beta.

## Deliberately not included

- **Tensura Skill Trainer** is not distributed. Players who choose to use it
  must install and maintain it themselves.
- **TR Addon 2.0.1** and **Tensura: Unique Monsters 1.0.2** are deferred after
  reproducible ManasCore registry-order failures on dedicated servers.
- The tested C2ME alpha is deferred after a clean-shutdown hang.
- No diagnostic patched upstream JARs or unofficial replacements are shipped.
