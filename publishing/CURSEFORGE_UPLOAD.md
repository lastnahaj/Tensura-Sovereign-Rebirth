## Publication prerequisites

The present ZIP contains three override JARs: Ponder and two TSR-owned modules.
CurseForge requires non-CurseForge JARs to qualify under its approved
third-party rules. Before submitting:

1. Publish **TSR Client Stability** and **TSR Silent Gear Metalworks Compat**
   as separate CurseForge mod projects.
2. Replace their override JARs with their CurseForge project/file references.
3. Reconfirm that the exact Ponder artifact and source remain approved for
   third-party modpack inclusion.
4. Regenerate the ZIP and confirm `manifest.json` plus `overrides/` are at the
   archive root.
5. Import the regenerated ZIP into a clean CurseForge profile.
