# Quest Status for Version 1 Beta

## Playable beta scope: complete

The distributed pack contains eight TSR-authored act chapters. Each chapter
has eight progression tasks plus a visual header and a clickable wiki entry,
for **64 progression tasks and 80 loaded TSR quest entries**:

1. Act 0 — Welcome, Otherworlder
2. Act 1 — Reincarnated
3. Act 2 — Paths of Power
4. Act 3 — Monsters Among Monsters
5. Act 4 — Birth of a Nation
6. Act 5 — Beyond This World
7. Act 6 — The Harvest Festival
8. Act 7 — Sovereign Rebirth

The active MineColonies quest integration contributes the remaining 60 loaded
entries. The tested runtime therefore loads **nine chapters and 140 FTB Quests
entries**.

Every TSR chapter includes a custom visual header, a clickable wiki crest, and
a direct project-wiki link. The chapters cover first launch, teams and claims,
survival, Tensura progression, Great Sage, storage, guild progression,
adventure, MineColonies, dimensions, awakening, prestige, evolving gear, and
the endgame realm audit.

The current files are valid FTB Quests data and loaded successfully during the
expanded dedicated-server cold and warm tests.

## Not the final campaign

Version 1 Beta does not claim the long-term campaign is complete. The design
target remains 32 chapters and roughly 512 handcrafted quests. Beta work still
includes:

- replacing review/checkmark steps with registry-backed detectors where the
  installed mods expose reliable task types;
- balancing rewards against actual server progression;
- validating team-shared credit and reward behavior with multiple players;
- testing every boss, dimension, awakening, colony, and equipment milestone;
- expanding each act into its planned multi-chapter arc;
- revising descriptions and pacing from beta feedback.

Storefront copy should describe this as a **growing beta campaign with 140
loaded FTB Quests entries**, not a finished 512-quest campaign.
