## Your training route

<div class="skill-reading-guide">
<div><span>01</span><h3>Find a manual</h3><p>The upstream item guide lists loot chests and dwarf trainer trades. See the <a href="../tensura-reference/battlewill/items-misc-battlewill-manual/">Battlewill Manual reference</a>; actual availability depends on server loot and trading settings.</p></div>
<div><span>02</span><h3>Learn its result</h3><p>The tracked random pool contains 16 core techniques. A random manual cannot target a specific result and can be consumed without granting a new ability. Bound manuals use their stored technique instead.</p></div>
<div><span>03</span><h3>Train toward mastery</h3><p>Equip the learned technique through the character menu, use its documented controls, and track its learning and mastery separately. A successor enters learning through its predecessor’s mastery hook.</p></div>
</div>

## Before using another manual

- Check whether you already know the result. Consumption does not guarantee a new ability.
- A configured random pool is not a starting-race roll or a list of every skill-book reward.
- Read the technique’s active controls and resource cost before training it.
- Keep learning progress distinct from mastery progress.
