## Missions and contracts

**Beyond Adventures 1.1.9** is active in the tested client and server profile. It adds progressive missions, contract mechanics, currency, and mission rewards that can interact with Tensura progression. TSR disables companion-skill terrain griefing and keeps the complete reward economy under beta review.

The authored FTB campaign remains a separate guided path. Beyond Adventures supplies repeatable or system-driven adventure content; it does not replace TSR's campaign structure.

[Official Beyond Adventures page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/beyond-adventures){ .md-button }

## Shared structure loot

**Lootr** gives each player a separate inventory for supported generated loot containers. In multiplayer, opening a dungeon chest does not consume another player's copy of its loot.

- Open the container normally to receive your instance.
- A visually changed container is expected.
- Do not break shared exploration containers just to move their contents.
- Report modded containers that fail to instance before assuming they are intentionally shared.

[Official Lootr page](https://modrinth.com/mod/lootr){ .md-button }

## Finding biomes

**Nature's Compass** searches registered biomes, including supported modded biomes. Right-click the compass to choose a biome; shift-right-click resets its target. Searches can be expensive, so repeated long-range requests should be avoided while the terrain layer is still being profiled.

[Official Nature's Compass page](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-compass){ .md-button }

## Unlocking travel

**Waystones** provides activated destinations, travel between waystones, and supported warp items. TSR's progression target does not allow free early cross-dimensional travel; exact costs and gates remain under validation and should not be documented as final yet.

[Official Waystones page](https://modrinth.com/mod/waystones){ .md-button }

## Exploration loop

1. Use missions, the campaign, or normal exploration to choose a destination.
2. Use Nature's Compass when a specific biome is required.
3. Activate Waystones as the route expands.
4. Explore curated structures and dimensions.
5. Open Lootr containers normally so every teammate retains a personal reward instance.
6. Return to [Bosses & Dimensions](../bosses-and-dimensions.md) or the [Structure Index](../structures.md) for encounter status.

??? warning "Verified scope"
    The assembled profile starts with these systems installed, and Beyond Adventures has an explicit clean start/save/restart record. Final Waystones costs, every Lootr container integration, large-radius biome-search performance, and full mission reward balance remain under validation.
