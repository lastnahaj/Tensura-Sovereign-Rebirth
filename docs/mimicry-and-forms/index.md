# Retired mimicry integration

TensuraMorph, ReMorphed, and Woodwalkers have been removed. The former creature-form interface, absorption bridge, and 100,000-kill configuration are not current TSR progression instructions.

CraftedCore remains in the recorded inventory; its presence alone does not restore the removed form system. Base Tensura skill descriptions remain available, but they do not imply that the former integration is installed.
