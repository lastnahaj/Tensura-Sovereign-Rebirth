# Teams & Claims

## Primary systems

- FTB Teams
- FTB Chunks
- FTB XMod Compat
- Tensura Compat: FTB
- LuckPerms

## Responsibilities

**FTB Teams** — social/team grouping<br>
**FTB Chunks** — claims and force loading<br>
**Tensura Compat: FTB** — makes protections aware of Tensura abilities/interactions<br>
**LuckPerms** — server permission/rank authority<br>
**FTB XMod Compat** — bridge for supported cross-mod permission behavior

FTB Ranks is intentionally not installed because LuckPerms owns permissions.

Exact default claim limits and permission nodes will be documented after non-OP testing.
