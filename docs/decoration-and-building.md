# Decoration & Building

TSR includes enough decorative content to build distinct kingdoms without turning the pack into a duplicate-block catalog.

## Frozen building layer

- Domum Ornamentum
- Supplementaries
- Amendments
- FramedBlocks
- Macaw's Furniture
- Macaw's Doors
- Macaw's Windows
- Macaw's Roofs
- Macaw's Bridges
- Macaw's Lights and Lamps
- Farmer's Delight for kitchens/food spaces

## Design target

Players should be able to create:
- Tempest-style capitals,
- guild halls,
- castles and keeps,
- taverns,
- shops,
- estates,
- villages,
- bridges,
- city walls,
- functional MineColonies districts.

Mods such as Chipped, Handcrafted, and Beautify are excluded from v0.1 to reduce redundant decorative inventory and recipe bloat.
