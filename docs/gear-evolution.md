# Gear Evolution

**Tensura Gear Evolution is the authoritative equipment progression system in TSR.**

Apotheosis and other competing random rarity/affix progression systems are intentionally excluded.

## Intended equipment loop

**Normal equipment**<br>
→ **earn EP through use**<br>
→ **evolve**<br>
→ **Tensura material tiers**<br>
→ **advanced forging**<br>
→ **endgame specialist equipment**

## Integrated equipment systems

- Tensura Gear Evolution
- Tensura Metalworks
- Silent Gear
- Productive Metalworks
- Silent Gear Metalworks
- Sophisticated Backpacks
- Tensura Backpack Expansion

## Rule

Any additional equipment mod must complement this progression rather than provide an easier parallel path to stronger gear.
