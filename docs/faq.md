# FAQ

## What version is TSR built for?
Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

## Is Apotheosis included?
No. Gear Evolution is the authoritative equipment progression system.

## Does TSR use AE2 or Refined Storage?
No. Sophisticated Storage owns physical storage, Sophisticated Backpacks owns personal storage, and Tom's is retained only for terminal/wireless access.

## Why is a Nightmare utility library present if Nightmare content is excluded?
Tensura x MineColonies requires Nightmare's Tensura Utils as a dependency. The library is included only to satisfy that integration; Nightmare content itself remains excluded.

## Are high-tier races random starter options?
Not by design. Exceptionally powerful paths are intended to be progression-gated.

## Is the quest book finished?
No. The campaign architecture is frozen at 8 Acts / 32 Chapters / roughly 512 handcrafted quests, but the actual quest files are implemented after the assembled pack exposes verified registries and APIs.

## Can I play without building a MineColonies nation?
The campaign will make nation building a major branch, but TSR is intentionally designed with parallel adventure, race, magic, boss, and mastery progression.

## Are all test candidates guaranteed to ship?
No. Test candidates must pass their specific compatibility, balance, performance, or worldgen gates first.
