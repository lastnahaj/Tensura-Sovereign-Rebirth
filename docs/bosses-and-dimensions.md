# Bosses & Dimensions

Browse the unified [Tensura Boss Directory](tensura-reference/bosses/index.md) first. External TSR bosses retain their source mod and are never presented as native Tensura content.

## Encounter layers

| Layer | Content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Tensura encounters | Native bosses, Tensura: Not Enough Bosses, TenSura Boss Structure | Startup verified; encounter progression under validation |
| Integrated external bosses | Bosses' Rise with Tensura compatibility; Legendary Monsters with Tensura compatibility | Present in the profile; balance and reward behavior under validation |
| Adventure bosses | L_Ender's Cataclysm and Mowzie's Mobs | Present in the playable profile; encounter testing under validation |
| Dimension progression | Twilight Forest and The Aether | Present in the playable profile; portals, return paths, and progression under validation |

## Why these mods?

The external stack was selected because Ascension and dedicated compatibility layers can make much of it participate in Tensura's EP/skill/resistance scale.

Presence is not the same as integration. Dedicated compatibility exists for Bosses' Rise and Legendary Monsters; other external encounters are not called Tensura-aware without runtime evidence.

??? warning "Excluded encounter systems"
    Unique Monsters is deferred because of a reproduced startup-order failure. Ice & Fire and its Tensura bridge are jointly omitted from the playable beta because the bridge targets removed upstream registry classes. Neither is described as active content.

## Worldgen policy

TSR v0.1 intentionally avoids a broad biome-overhaul dependency. Tensura biomes and structures take priority over decorative biome count.

The Version 1 Beta terrain target and its current validation state are documented under [World Generation](world-generation.md), [TSR Structure Index](structures.md), and [Adventure, Travel & Loot](adventure-travel-and-loot/index.md).
