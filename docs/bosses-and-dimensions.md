# Bosses & Dimensions

Browse the local [Base Tensura Boss Directory](tensura-reference/bosses/index.md) first. External TSR bosses retain their source mod and are never presented as native Tensura content.

## Frozen adventure stack

- Native Tensura bosses
- Tensura: Not Enough Bosses

- L_Ender's Cataclysm
- Mowzie's Mobs
- Ice & Fire Community Edition
- Twilight Forest
- The Aether
- Unique Monsters

## Why these mods?

The external stack was selected because Ascension and dedicated compatibility layers can make much of it participate in Tensura's EP/skill/resistance scale.

## Controlled test candidates

- When Dungeons Arise
- Bosses' Rise + Tensura compatibility
- Legendary Monsters + Tensura compatibility
- Block Factory's Bosses

These are not promoted into the production baseline until worldgen density, combat balance, and performance tests pass.

## Worldgen policy

TSR v0.1 intentionally avoids a broad biome-overhaul dependency. Tensura biomes and structures take priority over decorative biome count.

The Version 1 Beta terrain target and its current validation state are documented under [World Generation](world-generation.md) and [TSR Structure Index](structures.md).
