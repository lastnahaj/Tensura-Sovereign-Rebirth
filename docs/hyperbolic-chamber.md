## How to enter

1. Master **Gate** and **Sacred Haki** to obtain [Hyperbolic Passage](tensura-reference/skills/ascension.md#hyperbolic-passage).
2. Equip Hyperbolic Passage and use it to open the route into the chamber.
3. Bring enough Magicule to withstand the environment and a dependable way home.

## Why train there?

EP earned inside the chamber is multiplied by **3**, making it a focused late-game training option for race evolution and the **5,000,000 Max EP** Ultimate-awakening threshold.

!!! danger "Magicule Poisoning"
    The chamber requires sufficiently high Magicule to exist safely. Characters below its safety threshold take **Magicule Poisoning** damage. Treat the first visit as a readiness check, not a guaranteed training session.

## Before opening the passage

- Make sure Hyperbolic Passage is equipped and usable.
- Enter with full Magicule and reliable regeneration or escape options.
- Keep a normal-world fallback training route until you know your character can survive the chamber.
- Faster EP gain does not reduce race, status, essence, or Ultimate-specific requirements; those gates still apply.
