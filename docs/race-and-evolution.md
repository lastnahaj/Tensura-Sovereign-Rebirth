# Race & Evolution

TSR treats race choice as a major progression path.

## Core principles

- Race evolution should be **earned**, not bypassed by random rewards.
- High-end races must not trivialize the opening campaign.
- Racial progression should remain meaningful across repeated characters/prestige.
- The Evolution UI is included to make branching paths understandable in-game.

## High-tier starting restrictions

Tensura: Origins is included, but exceptionally powerful paths such as True Dragon/Primordial/Origin Angel-style outcomes are planned as **late-game gated progression**, not normal random starting options.

Exact gating is finalized during assembly after the installed race registries and conditions are verified.

## Race Prestige

SlimeThrone Extras tracks prestige by race and uses racial completion as a major source of long-term Soul Grade growth.

See [Prestige & Soul Grade](prestige-and-soul-grade.md).
