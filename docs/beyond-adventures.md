## What the installed mod adds

<section class="beyond-feature-grid">
  <article><span>01</span><h3>Missions</h3><p>Daily, weekly, and repeatable objectives with tracked requirements and claimable rewards.</p></article>
  <article><span>02</span><h3>Contracts</h3><p>Character-focused contracts and a dedicated interface for obtaining and managing recruits.</p></article>
  <article><span>03</span><h3>Currency</h3><p>A coin balance used by the add-on's mission and contract economy.</p></article>
  <article><span>04</span><h3>Rewards</h3><p>Definitions in the installed file support coins, items, skills, souls, MXP, and characters.</p></article>
  <article><span>05</span><h3>Characters</h3><p>Character missions and deployment systems, including content associated with Rimuru, Hinata, and Luminous.</p></article>
  <article><span>06</span><h3>Boss objectives</h3><p>Mission definitions can point to boss encounters already supplied by the compatible installed content.</p></article>
</section>

## A practical first route

<ol class="prestige-steps prestige-steps--four">
  <li><b>1</b><div><strong>Open the Beyond interface</strong><span>Use the current keybind shown in Controls; customized bindings can differ between players.</span></div></li>
  <li><b>2</b><div><strong>Read the active mission</strong><span>Check its target, progress, refresh timing, and reward before leaving base.</span></div></li>
  <li><b>3</b><div><strong>Claim completed rewards</strong><span>Mission completion and reward claiming are distinct states in the add-on's interface.</span></div></li>
  <li><b>4</b><div><strong>Review contracts and deployment</strong><span>Use earned resources deliberately and keep the current three-character deployment limit in mind.</span></div></li>
</ol>

## TSR's current settings

| Setting | Current value | Player-facing effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Maximum characters deployed | 3 | Up to three characters may be deployed through the add-on's system. |
| Daily popup | Enabled | The daily mission interface may appear automatically. |
| Character griefing | Disabled | Deployed characters are configured not to grief the world. |
| Target mode | 3 | The pack uses the add-on's configured targeting mode. |
| Cosmetic characters | Disabled | Characters use their functional behavior rather than a cosmetic-only mode. |

## What is not part of this guide

!!! warning "Tensura: Dungeon is not installed"
    TSR's 1.21.1 pack includes **Beyond Adventures 1.1.9**, not the separate Tensura: Dungeon mod. Dungeon floors, Colosseum systems, Dungeon NPC shops, and Dungeon gamerules are therefore intentionally excluded from this wiki section.

The TR: Beyond website covers more than one project. A feature appearing elsewhere under that brand does not establish that it exists in this pack; this page is limited to classes, resources, metadata, and configuration found in TSR's pinned Beyond Adventures file.
