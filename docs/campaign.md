# Campaign

<span class="tsr-status">Planned</span>

TSR's handcrafted FTB Quests campaign targets **8 Acts**, **32 Chapters**, and approximately **512 authored quests**. The current tracked pack source does not yet contain the handcrafted campaign files, so none of those quest counts are presented as implemented.

## Planned acts

| Act | Title | Intended role |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome, Otherworlder | Onboarding, terminology, teams, claims, and progression map |
| 1 | Reincarnated | Race choice, early survival, base skills, EP, Magicules, and Aura |
| 2 | Paths of Power | Skills, Great Sage, magic, equipment, and specialization |
| 3 | Monsters Among Monsters | Guild work, Unique Monsters, hunting, and boss preparation |
| 4 | Birth of a Nation | Naming, subordinates, MineColonies, logistics, and settlement growth |
| 5 | Beyond This World | Dimensions, portals, exploration, and external boss branches |
| 6 | The Harvest Festival | Demon Lord/Hero progression, souls, and awakening requirements |
| 7 | Sovereign Rebirth | Ascension, prestige, Soul Grade, and sovereign endgame |

## Implementation gate

Quest authoring begins after the active mod set exposes verified item, entity, race, skill, task, and reward identifiers. Proposed conditions are classified as native FTB detection, Tensura compatibility detection, advancement/stat detection, KubeJS-detectable, bridge-required, or not reliably detectable.

## Quest Shop

The planned shop is progression-gated. It does not sell completed awakenings, Ultimate Skills, Soul Grade, or finished top-end equipment. Currency sources and every reward remain unverified until the quest and shop data exist in the active pack.

Base explanations should link to this wiki's [Tensura reference](tensura-reference/index.md), not require players to leave the TSR site.
