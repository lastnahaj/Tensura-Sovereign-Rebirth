# Rampage

<span class="reference-badge">Base Tensura reference</span> <span class="reference-category">Core Mechanics</span>

<div class="tensura-reference-article">
<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" dir="ltr" lang="en"><p><br/>
Rampage is an effect that makes the edges of your screen turn reddish with red lines, as if seeing red, and deals damage when the duration is low.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<ul><li>When under 10 seconds, deals 10% of max HP and max SHP every second, and leaves 1 HP/SHP remaining whenever it would deal fatal damage.</li>
<li>When hitting something or being hurt, it refreshes the duration to 30 seconds, or adds 3 seconds if already at 30 seconds.</li>
<li>Maximum duration replenished by being injured is 1 minute.</li>
<li>Certain skills have bonus effects tied to the Rampage status.</li>
<li>One of the few ways to obtain <a href="../../resistances/abnormal-condition-resistance/" title="Abnormal Condition Resistance">Abnormal Condition Resistance</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/spiritual-attack-resistance/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spiritual Attack Resistance">Spiritual Attack Resistance</a>.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mob_Notes">Mob Notes</span></h3>
<ul><li>Mobs with Rampage cannot be tamed until Rampage is removed or runs out of duration.</li>
<li>Mobs with Rampage will attack anything nearby that is not an ally.</li>
<li>Overrides the fleeing behavior from high <a href="../effects-fear/" title="Effects/Fear">Fear</a> levels; mobs will still rush toward targets as if attacking, but cannot deal damage.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Causes">Causes</span></h2>
<p>The following Unique Skills inflict Rampage:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="../../skills/unique/berserk/" title="Berserk">Berserk</a> – self-inflicted.</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/starved/" title="Starved">Starved</a> – only applies to subordinates.</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/wrath/" title="Wrath">Wrath</a> – only applies to non-subordinates.</li></ul>
<p>Other sources:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="../../bosses/mobs-charybdis/" title="Mobs/Charybdis">Charybdis's</a> <a href="../../mobs/mobs-megalodon/" title="Mobs/Megalodon">Megalodon</a> summons spawn with Rampage for 10 minutes, with no way to refresh Rampage.</li>
<li><a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-lord/" title="Mobs/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a> and <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster">Orc Disaster</a> occasionally give Rampage to all nearby <a href="../../mobs/mobs-orc/" title="Mobs/Orc">Orcs</a> for 10 seconds, refreshing periodically.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<ul><li>Milk does not remove the debuff.</li>
<li>Can be removed by waiting out the timer or by using skills such as <a href="../../skills/unique/gluttony/" title="Gluttony">Gluttony</a>'s Isolation mode.</li>
<li>Abnormal Condition Resistance halves the HP damage, while <a href="../../resistances/abnormal-condition-nullification/" title="Abnormal Condition Nullification">Abnormal Condition Nullification</a> nullifies it.</li>
<li>Spiritual Attack Resistance halves the SHP damage, while <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/spiritual-attack-nullification/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spiritual Attack Nullification">Spiritual Attack Nullification</a> nullifies it.</li></ul>
