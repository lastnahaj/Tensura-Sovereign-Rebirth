# EP, Magicule, Aura

<span class="reference-badge">Base Tensura reference</span> <span class="reference-category">Core Mechanics</span>

<div class="tensura-reference-article">
<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" dir="ltr" lang="en">
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="EP">EP</span></h1>
<p><b>EP</b> (short for <b>Evolution Points</b>) is a rough indicator of how strong a Mob, Player, or Item is. Both you and certain items — such as Monster Leather and Magic Steel gear — can evolve once they accumulate enough EP (see [<a class="text" href="../gear-evolution/">Evolution</a>]).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Gaining_EP">Gaining EP</span></h2>
<p>The primary way to gain EP is by killing mobs. The stronger the mob, the more EP you receive. Without any special skills (such as <i>Gourmand</i>), you gain <b>3% of the mob's EP</b> per kill.
</p><p>However, the EP gained is reduced if the mob is significantly weaker than you. The larger the EP gap in your favor, the smaller the reward down to a maximum reduction of <b>-90%</b> of the mob's EP.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death_Penalty">Death Penalty</span></h2>
<p>Dying causes you to <b>lose 5% of your current EP</b>, along with a reduction to your Magicule and Aura.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="EP_and_Stat_Growth">EP and Stat Growth</span></h2>
<p>Gaining EP also increases your <b>Max Magicule</b> and <b>Max Aura</b>. The split depends on your race:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Majin:</b> 2/3 Magicule, 1/3 Aura</li>
<li><b>Non-Majin:</b> 2/3 Aura, 1/3 Magicule</li></ul>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Magicule">Magicule</span></h1>
<p>Your Magicule Regeneration rate is fixed by default, but it can be increased through the following means:
</p>
<ul><li>Equipping armor with the <b>Magicule Absorption</b> engraving</li>
<li>Being in certain <b>Dimensions</b> or <b>Biomes</b></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Magicule-Boosting_Locations">Magicule-Boosting Locations</span></h2>
<p><b>Dimensions:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Hell</li>
<li>Labyrinth</li></ul>
<p><b>Biomes:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><a href="../../biomes/biomes-barren-lands/" title="Biomes/Barren Lands">Barren Lands</a></li>
<li><a href="../../biomes/biomes-ancient-forest/" title="Biomes/Ancient Forest">Ancient Forest</a></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Restoring_Magicule_to_Full">Restoring Magicule to Full</span></h2>
<p>Aside from natural regeneration, there are several ways to instantly restore your Magicule to 100%:
</p>
<ul><li>Consuming a <b>Magic Crystal</b> using <i><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/absorb-dissolve/" title="Absorb &amp; Dissolve">Absorb &amp; Dissolve</a></i> / <i><a href="../../skills/unique/predator/" title="Predator">Predator</a></i> (<i>Isolation Sub-skill</i>)</li>
<li>Activating <b><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/beast-transformation/" title="Beast Transformation">Beast Transformation</a></b></li>
<li><b>Dying</b> technically restores Magicule, but <i>not recommended</i> due to the EP loss</li></ul>
