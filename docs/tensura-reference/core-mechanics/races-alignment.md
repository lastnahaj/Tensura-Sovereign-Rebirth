<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Holy">Holy</span></h2>
<p>A term for non-majin life forms that have achieved Saint level
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Holy_FAQ">Holy FAQ</span></h2>
<ul><li>Can I still become a Majin if I'm Holy?
<ul><li><b>NO</b> You cannot become a Majin if you are Holy aligned</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining">Obtaining</span></h2>
<ul><li>Reach your third evolution on a race that isn't Majin by default = <a href="../../races/races-human-saint/" title="Races/Human Saint">Races/Human Saint</a> · <a href="../../races/races-elf-saint/" title="Races/Elf Saint">Races/Elf Saint</a> · <a href="../../races/races-dwarf-saint/" title="Races/Dwarf Saint">Races/Dwarf Saint</a> · <a href="../../races/races-merfolk-saint/" title="Races/Merfolk Saint">Races/Merfolk Saint</a> · <a href="../../races/races-hobgoblin-saint/" title="Races/Hobgoblin Saint">Races/Hobgoblin Saint</a> · <a href="../../races/races-mystic-oni/" title="Races/Mystic Oni">Races/Mystic Oni</a> · <a href="../../races/races-spirit-boar/" title="Races/Spirit Boar">Races/Spirit Boar</a> · <a href="../../races/races-spirit-bird/" title="Races/Spirit Bird">Races/Spirit Bird</a> · <a href="../../races/races-spirit-beast/" title="Races/Spirit Beast">Races/Spirit Beast</a> · <a href="../../races/races-true-dragonewt/" title="Races/True Dragonewt">Races/True Dragonewt</a></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Status">Status</span></h2>
<p><b>Holy is a status that is signified by the yellow race name in your stats menu</b></p><p>

</p><ul><li>No longer requires air to breathe underwater</li>
<li>Hunger stat does not go below 18 </li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tags">Tags</span></h2>
<ul><li>Holy Race is just a tag for race that gives you Holy status upon reaching your third evolution</li>
<li>Holy Race Tag will lock away becoming a <a href="../races-awakening/" title="Races/Awakening">True Demon Lord</a> unless you are Chaos-aligned, via you or another player who has the unique skill <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/unique/reverser/" title="Abilities/Skills/Reverser">Reverser</a></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Majin">Majin</span></h2>
<p>A catchall term for intelligent Monsters. They can be born naturally, be the result of monsters that have undergone mutation, or evolved from already intelligent non-magical beings that have undergone monsterization.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Majin_FAQ">Majin FAQ</span></h2>
<ul><li>Can I still get Egg despite becoming a majin?
<ul><li><b>YES</b> However it depends on <i><b>how</b></i> you became a Majin. If you became a majin through methods listed below (Except Spawning as a Majin) You can still obtain the <a href="../races-awakening/#True_Hero" title="Races/Awakening">Hero Egg</a>. <b>If</b> you became Majin through Race Evolution I.e <a href="../../races/races-kijin/" title="Races/Kijin">Kijin</a> -&gt; <a href="../../races/races-wicked-oni/" title="Races/Wicked Oni">Wicked Oni</a>, You can no longer obtain the Egg or Awaken to True Hero, unless you evolved back into a race that doesn't have the majin race tag.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining_2">Obtaining</span></h2>
<p><br/>
Majin can be acquired in 4 main ways
</p>
<ul><li>Use a <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/marionette-heart/" title="Items/Misc/Marionette Heart"></a></span> <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../items/marionette-heart/" title="Items/Misc/Marionette Heart"><span class="sprite-text"> Marionette Heart</span></a></span></li></ul>
<ul><li>Die to Magicule Poisoning (have more Magicules than your Max) (1%) (configurable)</li></ul>
<ul><li>Die to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/extra/demon-lord-haki/" title="Abilities/Skills/Demon Lord Haki">Demon Lord Haki</a> (5%)</li></ul>
<ul><li>Start as a Majin Race - <a href="../../races/races-slime/" title="Races/Slime">Slime</a> · <a href="../../races/races-ghoul/" title="Races/Ghoul">Ghoul</a> · <a href="../../races/races-wight/" title="Races/Wight">Wight</a> · <a href="../../races/races-lesser-daemon/" title="Races/Lesser Daemon">Lesser Daemon</a> · <a href="../../races/races-harpy/" title="Races/Harpy"> Harpy</a> · <a href="../../races/races-giant/" title="Races/Giant"> Giant</a></li></ul>
<p>It can also be acquired by some race evolutions. (<a href="../../races/races-vampire/" title="Races/Vampire">Vampire</a> (From Human), <a href="../../races/races-wicked-oni/" title="Races/Wicked Oni">Wicked Oni</a>, <a href="../../races/races-orc-lord/" title="Races/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a>)
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Status_2">Status</span></h2>
<p><b>Majin is a status that is signified by the purple race name in your stats menu</b></p><p>

</p><ul><li>No longer requires air to breathe underwater</li>
<li>Hunger stat does not go below 18 </li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tag">Tag</span></h2>
<ul><li>Majin Race is just a tag for race that gives you Majin status on acquiring the race</li>
<li>Majin Race Tag will lock away becoming a <a href="../races-awakening/#True_Hero" title="Races/Awakening">True Hero</a> unless you are Chaos-aligned, via you or another player who has the unique skill <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/unique/reverser/" title="Abilities/Skills/Reverser">Reverser</a></li>
<li>Acquiring Majin, rather than being one by default, will still allow you to acquire the Hero Egg</li></ul>
