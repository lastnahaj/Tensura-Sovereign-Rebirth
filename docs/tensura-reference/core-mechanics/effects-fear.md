<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>Effect is given to entity's using <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/common/coercion/" title="Abilities/Skills/Coercion">Coercion</a> or any form of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/extra/haki/" title="Abilities/Skills/Haki">Haki</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span></h2>
<ul><li>At level 1, the targets will only have heart beat sound effect and a subtle screen overlay. From level 2 onward, the targets get -5% speed for each level (so level 2 = 95% and level 20 = 5%)</li></ul>
<ul><li>From level 2-5, the targets will get pushed away by the Haki being used, affected by Knockback Resistance.</li></ul>
<ul><li>From level 5-9, the targets will be actively avoiding the user. For mobs without Rampage, they will be running away. For players, they will get Insanity damage (higher level higher damage) if they get in 7x7 radius of the user to force them to get away. Entities with the "No Fear" tag, including all bosses, will not be affected by this</li></ul>
<ul><li>From level 10, the targets will start taking 3 armor-ignoring damage for each level EACH 2 SEC. (so level 10 taking 3 and level 20 taking 30 EACH 2 SEC)</li></ul>
