<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" dir="ltr" lang="en"><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<p><big> Allow your Weapons, Armor and Tools to be enchanted with specific effects </big>
</p><p>Your Gear will grow stronger as you do, it has its own EP, You can check by hovering over it in your inventory.
At Certain EP Milestones, it will have a chance to get a random engraving from the list below, beware there is also a chance to get no engraving at all <b>You cannot choose what engraving you get, IT IS RANDOM</b>
</p><p>By Default, the EP requirements for Engravings are as follows
</p><p>50,000 EP for First Engraving<br/>
250,000 EP for Second Engraving<br/>
1,000,000 EP For Third Engraving
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Common_Engravings">Common Engravings</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Swift">Swift</span></h3>
<p><i>Increases the weapon's damage, attack range and attack speed</i><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sturdy">Sturdy</span></h3>
<p><i>Increases the weapon's damage and equipment's durability, and makes the item invulnerable to environmental damage. <b>(One of the two engravings avaliable to shields, with the <a href="../../items/items-gear/#Shield" title="Items/Gear">Tempest Scale Shield</a> featuring <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Magic_Interference">Magical Interference</a> as other avaliable engraving)</b></i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Crushing">Crushing</span></h3>
<p><i>Increases the weapon's damage along with damage dealt to Armor at the cost of durability</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Uncommon_Engravings">Uncommon Engravings</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Magic_Weapon">Magic Weapon</span></h3>
<p><i>Imbues the weapon with Magic Damage</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Holy_Weapon">Holy Weapon</span></h3>
<p><i>Imbues the weapon with Holy Damage</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Slotting_1">Slotting 1</span></h3>
<p><i>Allows for elemental cores to be inserted</i><br/>
Right click with an elemental core on the weapon with slotting to equip it. <br/>
One elemental core per level of slotting. Hold Right Click to do Special Attack. Multiple combinations of cores can be slotted. Adds 30 elemental damage to the weapon.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Breathing_Support">Breathing Support</span></h3>
<p><i>Stops air bubbles from draining</i><br/>
Only appliciable with Helmets. Stops air bubbles from draining
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Elemental_Boost_1">Elemental Boost 1</span></h3>
<p><i>Increases damage output of natural effects for each piece of gear equipped</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Increases 10% of output damage of natural effect per level for each piece equipped.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Elemental_Resistance_1">Elemental Resistance 1</span></h3>
<p><i>Decreases damage input of natural effects for each piece of gear equipped</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Decreases 10% of input damage of natural effect per level for each piece equipped.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Severance_Protection">Severance Protection</span></h3>
<p><i>Decreases severances effect on the wearer</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Reduces severance effect on the wearer by 10%
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Magicule_Absorption_1">Magicule Absorption 1</span></h3>
<p><i>Increases magicule regen</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Increases magicule regeneration by 100%
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Rare_Engravings">Rare Engravings</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Energy_Steal">Energy Steal</span></h3>
<p><i>Drains a small percent of EP from the target</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Slotting_2">Slotting 2</span></h3>
<p><i>Allows for elemental cores to be inserted</i><br/>
Right click with an elemental core on the weapon with slotting to equip it. <br/>
Can hold 2 elemental cores. Hold right click to do Special Attack. Multiple combinations of cores can be slotted. Adds elemental damage to the weapon: +30 for the first core and +15 for each additional core equipped.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Barrier_Piercing">Barrier Piercing</span></h3>
<p><i>Breaks through any barrier the weapon touches.</i><br/>
Instant break of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/common/ranged-barrier/" title="Abilities/Skills/Ranged Barrier">Ranged Barrier</a> &amp; <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/extra/multilayer-barrier/" title="Abilities/Skills/Multilayer Barrier">Multilayer Barrier</a>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Energy_Protection">Energy Protection</span></h3>
<p><i>Lowers energy loss</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Reduces the amount of energy stolen from the wearer by 10%
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Magicule_Absorption_2">Magicule Absorption 2</span></h3>
<p><i>Increases magicule regen</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Increases magicule regeneration by 200%
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Epic_Engravings">Epic Engravings</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Severance">Severance</span></h3>
<p><i>The target will receive damage which cannot be healed by regular means</i><br/>
Will make all damage from the weapon permanent, +3 damage per level of severance.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Soul_Eater">Soul Eater</span></h3>
<p><i>Imbues the weapon with Spiritual Damage</i><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Slotting_3">Slotting 3</span></h3>
<p><i>Allows for elemental cores to be inserted</i><br/>
Right click with an elemental core on the weapon with slotting to equip it. <br/>
Can hold 3 elemental cores. Hold Right Click to do Special Attack. Multiple combinations of cores can be slotted. Adds elemental damage to the weapon: +30 for the first core and +15 for each additional core equipped.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Elemental_Boost_2">Elemental Boost 2</span></h3>
<p><i>Increases damage output of natural effects for each piece of gear equipped</i><br/>
Same as Elemental Boost 1, 20% Max
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Elemental_Resistance_2">Elemental Resistance 2</span></h3>
<p><i>Decreases damage input of natural effects for each piece of gear equipped</i></p><p>Same as Elemental Resistance 1, 20% Max</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Intangibility">Intangibility</span></h3>
<p><i>Armor? I see no armor</i><br/>
</p>
<p>Makes the weapon completely ignore enemies armor and shields.</p><p> <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Dead_End_Rainbow?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Dead End Rainbow (page does not exist)"> Dead End Rainbow</a> will automatically gain this engraving.</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Spiritual_Protection">Spiritual Protection</span></h3>
<p><i>Reduce spiritual damage taken</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Reduce the amount of spiritual damage taken by the wearer by 10%
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Magic_Protection">Magic Protection</span></h3>
<p><i>Your puny magic has no effect on me</i><br/>
Armor exclusive only. Reduce the amount of magic damage taken by the wearer by 10%
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Curses">Curses</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining_Curses">Obtaining Curses</span></h3>
<ul><li>3% chance to gain upon reaching ep threshold to gain engraving</li>
<li>If engravings are artificially applied by <a href="../../skills/unique/godly-craftsman/" title="Godly Craftsman"> Godly Craftsman</a> or other skills, there is a 3% chance for it to apply per existing engraving and new engraving added.</li>
<li>Curse effect will not apply on <a href="../../armor/holy-armaments-boots/" title="Holy Armaments Boots"> Holy Armaments Boots</a>, <a href="../../armor/holy-armaments-chestplate/" title="Holy Armaments Chestplate"> Holy Armaments Chestplate</a> and <a href="../../armor/holy-armaments-leggings/" title="Holy Armaments Leggings">Holy Armaments Leggings</a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Curse_of_Enervation">Curse of Enervation</span></h3>
<p><i>Drains Saturation from nearby beings</i><br/>
</p>
<p> When on an weapon/tool, every non-undead entity(including the user) will lose 2 saturation each second per level.</p><ul><li><ul><li>Level 1 = 2 saturation</li>
<li>Level 2 = 4 saturation</li>
<li>Level 3 = 6 saturation <br/></li></ul></li></ul>
<p> When on armor, every non-undead entity(including the user) will lose 5 saturation each second per armor piece, per level.</p><ul><li><ul><li>Level 1 = 5, 10, 15, or 20 saturation total per 1, 2, 3, 4 armor pieces</li>
<li>Level 2 = 10, 20, 30, or 40 saturation total per 1, 2, 3, 4 armor pieces</li>
<li>Level 3 = 15, 30, 45 or 60 saturation total per 1, 2, 3, 4 armor pieces<br/></li></ul></li></ul>
<p> The user of items with Curse of Enervation will have a flat decrease of damage taken of 10 per piece, per level.</p><ul><li><ul><li>Level 1 = 10, 20, 30, or 40 damage reduction per 1, 2, 3, 4 armor pieces</li>
<li>Level 2 = 20, 40, 60, or 80 damage reduction per 1, 2, 3, 4 armor pieces</li>
<li>Level 3 = 30, 60, 90, or 120 damage reduction per 1, 2, 3, 4 armor pieces<br/></li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Curse_of_Lethargy">Curse of Lethargy</span></h3>
<p><i>Slows your ep gain</i>
<br/>
</p>
<p> Items with this curse will have a 20% decrease in ep gain, as well as decreasing the users ep gain by 20%</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Curse_of_Sealing">Curse of Sealing</span></h3>
<p><i>Blame Vic</i>
<br/>
</p>
<p> Items with this curse will be unable to gain any other engravings or enchantments.</p><p>Enchants and engravings on affected items will be unable to be taken by <a href="../../skills/unique/researcher/" title="Researcher"> Researcher</a>, <a href="../../skills/unique/godly-craftsman/" title="Godly Craftsman"> Godly Craftsman</a> or <a href="../../skills/unique/degenerate/" title="Degenerate"> Degenerate</a> </p><p> Items also cannot be decrafted or grindstoned while this curse is applied.</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Curse_of_Stagnation">Curse of Stagnation</span></h3>
<p><i>No breakthrough for you</i>
<br/>
</p>
<p> Items with this curse will be unable to evolve to the next material
</p><ul><li><ul><li>For example, <a href="../../tools/high-magisteel-axe/" title="High Magisteel Axe"> High Magisteel Axe</a> won't be able to evolve into <a href="../../tools/pure-magisteel-axe/" title="Pure Magisteel Axe"> Pure Magisteel Axe</a> </li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Curse_of_Ruination">Curse of Ruination</span></h3>
<p><i>No durability regen</i>
<br/>
</p>
<p> Items with this curse will no longer be able to regenerate durability through EP.</p><p> XP gained by killing mobs will be instantly consumed</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessing">Blessing</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining">Obtaining</span></h3>
<p>Mastered <a href="../../skills/unique/reverser/" title="Reverser"> Reverser's</a> Inverted Fusion(Buff) subskill is able to reverse curses with mastery</p><p>Each curse costs 10000 magicule to reverse</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessing_of_Vitality">Blessing of Vitality</span></h3>
<p><i>Reverse Curse of Enervation</i><br/>
</p>
<p>Regenerates Saturation if non-undead.</p><p>Applies the same damage reduction as Curse of Enervation</p><p>Damage reduction will not stack with Curse of Enervation</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessing_of_Vigor">Blessing of Vigor</span></h3>
<p><i>Reverse Curse of Lethargy</i><br/>
</p>
<p>Items with this blessing will have a 20% buff to ep gain rate, and will also buff the users ep gain by 20%</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessing_of_Transcendence">Blessing of Transcendence</span></h3>
<p><i>Reverse Curse of Sealing</i><br/>
</p>
<p>Allows the item to gain a random engraving at 1.5 million EP</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessing_of_Growth">Blessing of Growth</span></h3>
<p><i>Reverse Curse of Stagnation</i><br/>
</p>
<p> Item Ep gain is doubled</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessing_of_Restoration">Blessing of Restoration</span></h3>
<p><i>Reverse Curse of Ruination</i><br/>
</p>
<p>Doubles item durability regeneration when using EP as durability</p><p>Grants Mending effect. If mending is on the weapon already, treats it as mending 2</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Note">Note</span></h3>
<p>Curse of binding and curse of vanishing will be removed if reversed.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Exclusive_Engravings">Exclusive Engravings</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dead_End_Rainbow"><b>Dead End Rainbow</b></span></h3>
<p><i>Ideally reduce the target's Spiritual Health to 0 after 7 hits.</i></p><p>Deals very High Spiritual Damage.</p><p>Obtainable as a drop from <a href="../../bosses/mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi">Hinata Sakaguchi</a>, as '<a href="../../items/items-gear/#Misc" title="Items/Gear">Dead End Rainbow</a>' (Weapon).</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Magic_Interference"><b>Magic Interference</b></span></h3>
<p><i>Reduces Magic Damage from targets weaker than you, and partially bypasses Magic Barriers of targets.</i></p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Holy_Coat"><b>Holy Coat</b></span></h3>
<p><i>Multiplies the damage against Monster targets</i></p><p>Does 25% more damage for each level on any Monster that drops crystal or Majin players</p><p><span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/silver-ingot/" title="Silver Ingot"></a></span> <a href="../../items/silver-ingot/" title="Silver Ingot"><span class="sprite-text">Silver</span></a></span> items have Holy Coat I and <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/mithril-ingot/" title="Mithril Ingot"></a></span> <a href="../../items/mithril-ingot/" title="Mithril Ingot"><span class="sprite-text">Mithril</span></a></span> items have Holy Coat II</p><p>
<b> For Armours </b>
</p><p>Take 25% Less Damage for each level from any Monster that drops crystals or <a class="mw-redirect" href="../races-alignment/" title="Races/Majin">Majin</a> Players</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Magic_Capacity_1-3"><b>Magic Capacity 1-3</b></span></h3>
<p><i>Increase the amount of spell the item can hold.</i></p><p><i>Appear only one magic staff and grimore.</i></p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tsukumogami"><b>Tsukumogami</b></span></h3>
<p><i>Makes only the owner of the weapon be able to use its full potential</i></p><ul><li>Creative players can use the active mode.</li>
<li>Items will gain Tsukumogami upon reaching "God Grade" (1 million EP) or by default as a Hihii'rokane item.</li>
<li>Tsukumogami cannot be removed with grindstone.</li>
<li>Tsukumogami will not have its full power if wielded by anyone with less EP than its EP (even if the player was the owner prior to resetting)
<ul><li>EP requirement is in stages; the stats of the item lower for every 10% ep lower than the item the player has, capped at 10% of the items stats</li>
<li>For example: If the weapon has 100k EP and the user has 90k EP, the weapon will only have 90% of its stats, and so on till 10%.</li>
<li>HihiIrokane items will now only turn black when the owner has under 50% of the weapons EP</li></ul></li></ul>
