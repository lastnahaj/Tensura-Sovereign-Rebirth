<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span></h2>
<p>Chant speed is the amount of time it takes to cast spells. This cast time can be reduced and varies between spells.<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Items">Items</span></h3>
