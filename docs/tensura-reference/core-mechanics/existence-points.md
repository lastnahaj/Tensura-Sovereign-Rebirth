<h2><span id="What_are_Existence_Points.28EP.29.3F"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="What_are_Existence_Points(EP)?">What are Existence Points(EP)?</span></h2>
<ul><li>To calculate a player's EP the game simply adds your Magicules (MP) and your Aura (AP), very simple right? You will need EP to evolve, awaken, be able to learn certain skills, and other things.</li></ul>
<h2><span id="How_does_EP_gain_work.3F"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="How_does_EP_gain_work?">How does EP gain work?</span></h2>
<ul><li>The base EP gain is 3% which means that if you kill something with 100 EP for example you will get 3 EP from killing them, this can be changed with Game Rules and in the mod's Config's.
<ul><li>The 3% is distributed as 1% MP + 1% AP + 1% X
<ul><li>X = 1% AP if player doesn't have any alignment</li>
<li>X = 1% MP if player is Majin/Holy/Chaos aligned</li></ul></li></ul></li>
<li><b>Note that the stronger you are the less EP you will gain from killing things that are way weaker than yourself</b>, the formula is as following:
<ul><li>[Targets EP * (100% of their EP - Any % Reduction Penalties for being stronger)] * EP Gain (3% Default)</li>
<li>-1% for how many times your EP is stronger than the entity killed, the maximum reduction possible is 90%
<ul><li>Example: If an entity has 1000 EP and you have 3000 EP instead of calculating 3% of their 100% it will calculate 3% of their 97% (subtracting 3% from their total EP since you are 3 times stronger).</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span id="How_does_EP_loss_work.3F"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="How_does_EP_loss_work?">How does EP loss work?</span></h2>
<ul><li>Every time you die you lose certain amount of EP which reduces your maximum MP and AP, the base % EP loss on death is 5%, this can be changed in the world's game rules</li></ul>

## In Tensura: Sovereign Rebirth

<span class="tsr-status">Under Validation</span>

EP remains a primary TSR progression measure. Installed compatibility and Ascension systems are used where they explicitly place external content on Tensura's scale, but TSR does not publish unverified custom thresholds or unsupported boss values.
