<p>Infection is an effect that makes your health blood red and gets stronger effects the longer you have it, culminating in instant death.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h4>
<ul><li>Infection 1 - Makes health a dark red blood color. After 30 seconds, it applies a new instance of Infection 2 with 17 second duration.</li>
<li>Infection 2 - Does 1 damage every 3 seconds. After 15 seconds it increases to Infection 3 with 17 second duration.</li>
<li>Infection 3 - Does 2 damage every 3 seconds, and inflicts Nausea 1. After 10 seconds to Infection 4 with 17 second duration.</li>
<li>Infection 4 - Does 4 damage every 3 seconds, inflicts Nausea and <a href="../effects-fragility/" title="Effects/Fragility">Fragility</a> 1. After 10 seconds it increases to Infection 5 with 17 second duration.</li>
<li>Infection 5 - Does enough damage to kill you instantly.</li>
<li>If you survive using methods such as <a href="../../skills/extra/infinite-regeneration/" title="Infinite Regeneration">Infinite Regeneration</a>, the Infection effect continues getting higher and increases Fragility each level.</li>
<li>Being/becoming spiritual will prevent infection from having any effect</li>
<li>Unaffected by <a href="../../resistances/abnormal-condition-nullification/" title="Abnormal Condition Nullification"> Abnormal Condition Nullification</a> or resistance</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Causes">Causes</span></h2>
<ul><li>5% chance of getting the effect from eating <a href="../../items/raw-giant-bat-meat/" title="Raw Giant Bat Meat">Raw Giant Bat Meat</a>.</li>
<li>3% chance of getting the effect from eating <a href="../../items/cooked-giant-bat-meat/" title="Cooked Giant Bat Meat"> Cooked Giant Bat Meat</a>.</li>
<li>Otherworlder <a href="../../mobs/mobs-shinji-tanimura/" title="Mobs/Shinji Tanimura">Shinji Tanimura</a></li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/healer/" title="Healer">Healer</a> Unique Skill</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<ul><li>Milk does not remove the debuff</li>
<li>Abnormal Condition Resistance prevents the Fragility and Nausea debuffs, disables the damage ticks, and makes the status decrease by 1 instead of increasing each time.</li>
<li>Undead are unaffected by Infection</li></ul>
