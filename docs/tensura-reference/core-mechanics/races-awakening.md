</p>

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="True_Demon_Lord"><span class="animated-text-gradient-demon">True Demon Lord</span></span></h1>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Seed">Seed</span></h3>
<p>To Awaken, you need a Demon Lord Seed which is gained by having 200k EP and being a <a class="mw-redirect" href="../races-alignment/" title="Races/Majin">Majin</a>.
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner"><a class="image" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:TDLEvo.png"><img class="thumbimage" data-file-height="276" data-file-width="408" decoding="async" height="216" loading="lazy" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/misc/tdlevo-84455d98bf.png" width="320"/></a> <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a class="internal" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:TDLEvo.png" title="Enlarge"></a></div>True Demon Lord Evolution</div></div></div>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Souls">Souls</span></h3>
<p>Once you have the seed, you are able to gain souls.
To gain souls, kill any Mob with EP,
2k EP is equivalent to 1 soul (configurable)
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Awakening">Awakening</span></h3>
<p>For the awakening you need 10,000 Souls (Default Value configurable via Gamerules), you can choose to awaken whenever you want but once your soul amount reaches 20,000 you will be forced into awakening (Default Value configurable via Gamerules)
</p><p>A new Button will appear inside your Status Menu. Once clicked, it will announce in the chat that you have awoken as a True Demon Lord<br/>
<a class="image reference-overview-duplicate" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:TDLAwakening.png"><img alt="TDLAwakening.png" data-file-height="21" data-file-width="530" decoding="async" height="21" loading="lazy" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/misc/tdlawakening-109254fb07.png" width="530"/></a>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Harvest_Festival">Harvest Festival</span></h3>
<ul><li>A player evolving to True Demon Lord will go through a Harvest Festival upon activation, which will last 3 minutes and affect subordinates in a 30 block radius.
<ul><li>Within the first minute, the player will be unable to use any abilities and have limited vision/movement, all mob subordinates within range will be automatically set to Follow and Protect.</li>
<li>Reaching the second minute, the player will be sent into Sleep Mode instantly, unable to move or use any ability.</li>
<li>In the last minute, players subordinate and mobs subordinates (if within range) will be also automatically sent into Sleep Mode.</li></ul></li>
<li>Dying during Harvest Festival will lead to the loss of all acquired souls
<ul><li>Players now also get forced into Harvest Festival automatically when the Soul count reaches 20000 - Changeable with  <a href="../../gamerules/config-gamerules/" title="Config/Gamerules">gamerule forceHarvestFestival</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Grants an 3x EP buff</li>
<li>Double stats for subordinates (has to be npc subordinates, will not work on player)</li>
<li>An Evolution to player races  and eligible NPCs</li>
<li>Evolution of all applicable/current Resistances to Nullifications</li></ul>

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="True_Hero"><span class="animated-text-gradient-hero">True Hero</span></span></h1>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment_2">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessed">Blessed</span></h3>
<p>There is a 5% Chance on Reincarnation/Reset as a non-majin race to be Blessed. This grants you all Elemental Spirits Plus Either Light or Dark when you pray,
granting you the Hero Egg Instantly.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Egg">Egg</span></h3>
<p>Acquire all 5 base element Greater Spirits (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth, Space) + a Greater Light or Greater Darkness spirit to Gain the Hero Egg (Configureable) - <b> Note - You must not be a Default <a class="mw-redirect" href="../races-alignment/" title="Races/Majin">Majin</a> race</b>
</p><p>It will announce to you that you have obtained the Hero Egg.<br/>
<a class="image" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:HeroEggObtain.png"><img alt="HeroEggObtain.png" data-file-height="22" data-file-width="243" decoding="async" height="22" loading="lazy" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/misc/heroeggobtain-07d420aedc.png" width="243"/></a>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Egg_-_Ability">Egg - Ability</span></h4>
<p>You will also gain <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/intrinsic/eye-of-truth/" title="Abilities/Skills/Eye Of Truth">Eye Of Truth</a>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Hatching">Hatching</span></h3>
<p>To Hatch said Egg and become a True Hero, you will need to regenerate HP while under 25% of your max HP while fighting a boss which meets all the requirements below.
</p>
<ul><li>The boss must have at least 400k EP.</li>
<li>The boss must be below 50% HP.</li>
<li>The mob must be tagged as a valid boss for True Hero awakening. A list is provided below.</li></ul>
