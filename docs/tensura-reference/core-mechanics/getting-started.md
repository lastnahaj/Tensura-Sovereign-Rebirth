<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="The_very_beginning">The very beginning</span></h1>
<p>When spawning in, a menu will pop up, showing <a href="../../races/races/" title="Races">races</a> you can pick. Depending on what race you pick, the difficulty of your progression will change.
</p><p>When spawning in, you will acquire a <a class="mw-redirect" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Unique_Skills" title="Unique Skills">Unique Skill</a>.
</p>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Progression">Progression</span></h1>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Early_Game">The Early Game</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Basic_info">Basic info</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">Click to show info</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>To become stronger in the game one must kill monsters or <a href="../../mobs/mobs/" title="Mobs">mobs</a> to obtain "<a href="../existence-points/" title="Existence Points">EP</a>" and more "max magicules".
</p><p>The stronger the "monster" you kill, the more EP one obtains. By default, and without any skills modifying it, you will gain 3% of a mobs ep when you kill it.
</p><p>As a Non-Majin, 2/3 of gained EP will be converted to AP, with 1/3 going to MP. This is reversed as a <a class="mw-redirect" href="../races-alignment/" title="Races/Majin">Majin</a>, gaining 2/3 MP and 1/3 AP.
</p>
</div>
<h3><span id="How_to_get_armor.2Fweapons.3F"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="How_to_get_armor/weapons?">How to get armor/weapons?</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">Click to show info</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>If you killed a few monsters, you can also obtain something called <a href="../../items/items-mob-drops/" title="Items/Mob Drops">"Monster Leather"</a> which you can use to make different kinds of <a href="../../items/items-schematics-monster-leather-gear-schematic/" title="Items/Schematics/Monster Leather Gear Schematic">Monster Leather Armor</a> which <a href="../gear-evolution/" title="Gear Evolution">evolves as you gain EP</a>.
</p><p>...But to make Monster Leather Armor you must obtain a <a href="../../blocks/blocks-smithing-bench/" title="Blocks/Smithing Bench">Smithing Bench</a>.
This special crafting table requires 2 iron, 3 paper, a crafting table and 2 planks.
</p>
</div>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="About_skills">About skills</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">Click to show info</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="How_to_train_skills">How to train skills</span></h4>
<div class="collapsible-header">Click to show info</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>To train or use skills you must equip them by opening your character menu (Defaulted to B), going to the section of skill you want to equip, and then click on the skill and press one of the three empty slots at the bottom.<br/>
To train a skill, simply press the skill button and it will say "You have become closer to mastering [SKILL NAME]". <br/>
(see <a href="../mechanics-ability-usage/" title="Mechanics/Ability Usage">Ability Usage</a> to see how to use skills)
</p>
</div>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="What_are_some_useful_skills">What are some useful skills</span></h4>
<div class="collapsible-header">Click to show info</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>There is a skill called <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../magic/magic-sense/" title="Abilities/Skills/Magic Sense">Magic Sense</a> which grants night vision. It additionally allows you to see mobs through walls, which can be used to locate caves by the concentrations of mobs that spawn within. <br/>
For offensive skills, eating 100 Rotten Flesh to obtain <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/common/corrosion/" title="Abilities/Skills/Corrosion">Corrosion</a> or 100 Spider Eyes to obtain <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/common/poison/" title="Abilities/Skills/Poison">Poison</a> offer easy-to-use upgrades.
</p>
</div>
<h3><span id="How_do_I_continue.3F"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="How_do_I_continue?">How do I continue?</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">About Magic Ore</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>Now that you understand how to get Monster Leather Armor, your next major step in progression sits after vanilla diamond gear. <br/>
This is because now you need to obtain a diamond pickaxe to get obsidian.
</p><p>Tip: get around 19 <a class="external text" href="https://minecraft.wiki/w/Obsidian" rel="nofollow">Obsidian</a> (10 for Nether Portal, 4 for enchantment table, 5 for <a href="../../blocks/blocks-kiln/" title="Blocks/Kiln">Kiln</a>) <br/>
<i>Or if you like speedrunning, get 9 obsidian for the <a href="../../blocks/blocks-kiln/" title="Blocks/Kiln">Kiln</a> and <a class="external text" href="https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanting_Table" rel="nofollow">Enchanting Table</a> and make the portal with a lava lake :D</i>
</p><p>Now why would you need a Diamond Pickaxe? Well, you will need to obtain a Netherite Pickaxe and a <a class="external text" href="https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherite_Ingot" rel="nofollow">Netherite Ingot</a>! <br/>
However if you use ice and fire, you can make a <a class="external text" href="https://ice-and-fire-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Bone" rel="nofollow">Dragon Bone</a> Pickaxe
</p><p>After you've gotten your netherite pickaxe, it is highly recommended to get fortune to get more "magic ore" while mining.<br/>
You can find <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../magic/magic-ore-shard/" title="Magic Ore Shard"></a></span> <a href="../../magic/magic-ore-shard/" title="Magic Ore Shard"><span class="sprite-text">Magic Ore</span></a></span> between -25 and -31, however the "best" y level on average is -29.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mid_game">Mid game</span></h2>
<p>Now that you've gotten your magic ore and netherite ingot you can make a <a href="../../blocks/blocks-kiln/" title="Blocks/Kiln">Kiln</a> and smelt all the magic ore to obtain a low magisteel ingot.
</p><p>However, you will need a lot more to get the full magisteel armor... <br/>
</p><p>Now you can focus on getting evolutions, as a tip in mid game you can go into the nether and slay piglins, zombie piglins and wither skeletons.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">About "gaining more power"</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>If you have skills like predator, you can steal skills like <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/extra/sage/" title="Abilities/Skills/Sage">Sage</a> to be quicker in leveling up your skills.
Or you can also get Sage by mastering 20 magics/battlewills (for example: 9 magic mastered, 11 battlewills mastered)
</p><p>However if you do not have any predatory skills or you want even MORE power, you can also pray at the <a href="../../structures/structures-labyrinth-tree/" title="Structures/Labyrinth Tree">Labyrinth Tree</a> (<i><b>for location, see labyrinth tree page</b></i>)
</p><p>Then you can pray at the spirit tree to gain spirits, you can summon them if they are medium or above (Exceptions: dark spirit and light spirit) using the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../magic/summon-medium-elemental/" title="Abilities/Magics/Summon Medium Elemental">Summon Medium Elemental</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../magic/summon-greater-elemental/" title="Abilities/Magics/Summon Greater Elemental">Summon Greater Elemental</a>.
</p><p>Tip: if you have a predatory skill you can steal the spirits their skills (and maybe resistances, depends on your skill).
</p>
</div>

<h3><span id="Bosses_.28Part_1:_Early.2FMid_game.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Bosses_(Part_1:_Early/Mid_game)">Bosses (Part 1: Early/Mid game)</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">About the beginning bosses</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>Bosses are mobs that can spawn anywhere in the world. It can be a boss that you can encounter in any biome like the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-supermassive-slime/" title="Mobs/Supermassive Slime">Supermassive Slime</a>, <a href="../../bosses/mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi">Hinata</a>, <a href="../../bosses/mobs-shizu/" title="Mobs/Shizu">Shizu</a> (and more!)... Or bosses that live in specific biomes like the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-lord/" title="Mobs/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a> that can transform into the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster">Orc Disaster</a> <br/>
Below are all the bosses you can fight at early to mid game
</p><p><br/>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Orc_Lord">Orc Lord</span></h4>
<p>An evolved royal orc with increased strength, the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-lord/" title="Mobs/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a> is an early midgame boss. It is able to spawn in orcs as subordinates, and its attacks deal <a href="../effects-corrosion/" title="Effects/Corrosion"> Corrosion,</a> which can easily shred through armor if you are not careful. When killed, it has a chance to drop <a href="../../items/royal-blood/" title="Royal Blood"> Royal Blood</a> and Pork. You will also be able to learn the common skill <a href="../../skills/common/corrosion/" title="Corrosion"> Corrosion.</a><br/>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Shizu">Shizu</span></h4>
<p>An otherworlder who has the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-ifrit/" title="Mobs/Ifrit"> Greater Spirit Ifrit</a> bound to her. <a href="../../bosses/mobs-shizu/" title="Mobs/Shizu"> Shizu</a> is a late midgame boss, while her first phase is simple with little offensive or defensive capabilities, her second phase upon "dying" spawns <a href="../../bosses/mobs-ifrit/" title="Mobs/Ifrit"> Ifrit,</a> who is highly resistant to physical attacks. It is advised to have <a href="../../skills/other/bypass-degrade-skills/" title="Bypass/Degrade Skills"> Bypass/Degrade Skills</a> or <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../magic/abilities-magics/" title="Magic"> Magic</a> for this fight. Upon defeating Ifrit, you will get ifrits drops, and a dying <a href="../../bosses/mobs-shizu/" title="Mobs/Shizu"> Shizu,</a> who will drop the <a href="../../magic/anti-magic-mask/" title="Anti-Magic Mask"> Anti-Magic Mask</a> and <a href="../../magic/items-schematics-anti-magic-mask-schematic/" title="Items/Schematics/Anti-Magic Mask Schematic"> Anti-Magic Mask Schematic.</a><br/>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Supermassive_Slime">Supermassive Slime</span></h4>
<p>The <a href="../../bosses/mobs-supermassive-slime/" title="Mobs/Supermassive Slime"> Supermassive Slime</a> has a chance to spawn in place of a normal slime. It is an early midgame boss, which deals corrosion damage(not the corrosion effect). If you have <a href="../../resistances/corrosion-resistance/" title="Corrosion Resistance"> Corrosion Resistance</a> or <a href="../../resistances/corrosion-nullification/" title="Corrosion Nullification"> Corrosion Nullification,</a> you will remove its main method of attack. It also has <a href="../../skills/extra/ultraspeed-regeneration/" title="Ultraspeed Regeneration"> Ultraspeed Regen,</a> so it is ideal to use a skill that attacks its Spiritual HP or an item/skill that can apply severance to prevent <a href="../../skills/extra/ultraspeed-regeneration/" title="Ultraspeed Regeneration"> Ultraspeed Regen</a> from fully working. <a href="../../bosses/mobs-supermassive-slime/" title="Mobs/Supermassive Slime"> Supermassive Slime</a> will drop <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../items/slime-core/" title="Items/Mob Drops/Slime Core">Slime Core</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../items/slime-chunk/" title="Items/Mob Drops/Slime Chunk">Slime Chunk</a> when killed. <br/>
</p><p>You can also create your own pet <a href="../../bosses/mobs-supermassive-slime/" title="Mobs/Supermassive Slime"> Supermassive Slime</a> by feeding a <a href="../../mobs/mobs-slime/" title="Mobs/Slime"> Slime</a> <a href="../../items/slime-core/" title="Slime Core"> Slime Cores.</a> <br/>
</p>
</div>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Late_game">Late game</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_final_grind">The final grind</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">About awakenings</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>Awakenings are evolutions players can undergo to get even more power... <b><big>It is heavily reccomended to do this once you have a million or more ep. This is because you will need this boost very VERY badly in LATE game, not when you got 400k ep, not when you got 600k, do it after a million or more EP.</big></b><br/>
<big>See what awakenings do in <a href="../races-awakening/" title="Races/Awakening">Awakenings</a></big>
</p>
</div>
<div class="collapsible-header">"I want to get stronger faster!" (about EP farming late game)</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>There are multiple ways to grind for EP in the mid-to-late game. None are particularly fast- as with the rest of the mod, EP takes time and grinding to get. <br/>
<big><b>Known EP farms:</b></big>
</p>
<ul><li>Enderman farm
<ul><li>An ordinary enderman farm in the end, this is slow but good for AFK farming.</li></ul></li>
<li><a href="../../mobs/mobs-sissie/" title="Mobs/Sissie">Sissie</a> farming
<ul><li>Here you simply go into a broad ocean and start hunting for sissie's. Due to their immense amount of EP you can easily get EP</li></ul></li>
<li>Boss fighting
<ul><li>Like the name implies, fighting any boss rewards relatively high amounts of EP. See the above list for examples.</li></ul></li></ul>
</div>
<h3><span id="About_Bosses_.28Part_2:_Mid.2FLate_Game.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="About_Bosses_(Part_2:_Mid/Late_Game)">About Bosses (Part 2: Mid/Late Game)</span></h3>
<div class="collapsible-header">When am I ready to fight end game bosses?</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<p>It's reccomended to farm EP until you have roughly 2 million or more EP, as most bosses include stat-check attacks to kill underleveled players and prevent unintended progression skips. Additionally, ensure that the skills you use are mastered- the power increase is notable. <br/>
For some healing items, you can always look into <a href="../../items/items-consumables/" title="Items/Consumables">Consumables</a> and <a href="../mechanics-hipokute-farming/" title="Mechanics/Hipokute Farming">Hipokute</a> -both offer healing if your abilities can't keep up.<br/><br/>
End game bosses include <a href="../../bosses/mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi"> Hinata Sakaguchi,</a> <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster"> Orc Disaster</a> and <a href="../../bosses/mobs-charybdis/" title="Mobs/Charybdis"> Charybdis.</a>
</p>
</div>
<div class="collapsible-header">About Bosses</div>
<div class="collapsible-content">
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Orc_Disaster">Orc Disaster</span></h4>
<p>One of the first end game bosses one can fight is the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster">Orc Disaster</a>. <br/>
This boss is one of the weaker end bosses but one of the strongest mid game bosses on the list but is a nice warmup to what the endgame has in store.
</p><p>To find this boss, one must find an <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-lord/" title="Mobs/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a> and make it "gain" 200k ep.
An <b>easy way</b> to do this is feeding it subordinates with high ep count to reach that number (for example: <a href="../naming/" title="Naming">Endowed</a> <a href="../../mobs/mobs-knight-spider/" title="Mobs/Knight Spider">Knight Spiders</a>). However, make sure that the subordinates do not kill the Orc Lord (you can prevent this by making them stay at their position) <br/>
After this has been done, the Orc Lord will evolve into the Orc Disaster, and then you can fight this boss. <br/>
</p><p><br/>
<b>See the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster">Orc Disaster</a> page for rewards/more information</b>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Charybdis">Charybdis</span></h4>
<p>Charybdis can either be the first or last of the endgame bosses you fight, depending on how lucky you get while searching for the <a href="../../items/charybdis-core/" title="Charybdis Core"> Charybdis Core</a>. You can find these in <a href="../../structures/structures-charybdis-cave/" title="Structures/Charybdis Cave"> Charybdis Cave</a>. <a href="../../items/charybdis-core/" title="Charybdis Core"> Charybdis Cores</a> will enable you to spawn <a href="../../bosses/mobs-charybdis/" title="Mobs/Charybdis"> Charybdis</a> by placing it and killing mobs to charge it with EP. You can quickly charge this core by endowing a knight spider or giant ant and then killing it to fill the core to almost full instantly. Once the core is charged, right click on it to spawn <a href="../../bosses/mobs-charybdis/" title="Mobs/Charybdis"> Charybdis</a>. It is recommended to spawn <a href="../../bosses/mobs-charybdis/" title="Mobs/Charybdis"> Charybdis</a> inside of a cave to prevent her from flying away, or using a ranged attack with high damage such as <a href="../../magic/darkness-cannon/" title="Darkness Cannon"> Darkness Cannon</a>. Once you kill <a href="../../bosses/mobs-charybdis/" title="Mobs/Charybdis"> Charybdis</a> she will drop the <a href="../../items/items-schematics-charybdis-scalemail-gear-schematic/" title="Items/Schematics/Charybdis Scalemail Gear Schematic"> Charybdis Scalemail Schematic</a>, <a href="../../items/charybdis-scale/" title="Charybdis Scale"> Charybdis Scale's</a> and <a href="../../items/raw-charybdis-meat/" title="Raw Charybdis Meat"> Raw Charybdis Meat.</a><br/>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Gazel_Dwargo">Gazel Dwargo</span></h4>
<p><a href="../../bosses/mobs-gazel-dwargo/" title="Mobs/Gazel Dwargo"> Gazel Dwargo</a>, the king of the dwarves, is a mid to late late game boss, being relatively weaker than <a href="../../bosses/mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi"> Hinata Sakaguchi.</a> In order to fight <a href="../../bosses/mobs-gazel-dwargo/" title="Mobs/Gazel Dwargo"> Gazel Dwargo</a> you must attain either 100 positive reputation through trading with and protecting <a href="../../mobs/mobs-dwarf/" title="Mobs/Dwarf"> Dwarfs</a> or attain 100 negative reputation by attacking/killing dwarfs. While negative reputation is faster and simpler to achieve, it locks you into a single fight against him, as well as locking dwarf trades. Positive Reputation allows you to fight him an unlimited amount of times, as well as making dwarf trades cheaper. Once you have fulfilled the requirement to fight him, you must use the royal warp pad that spawns rarely in <a href="../../structures/structures-dwarf-village/" title="Structures/Dwarf Village"> Dwarf Villages</a> to enter his arena. He has two phases, his first phase focusing on using magical attacks and his weapon, the second phase focusing on utilising his exclusive battlewills. When defeated, Gazel will drop his weapon <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Items/Gear/Rhuk?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Items/Gear/Rhuk (page does not exist)"> Rhuk</a> and his exclusive battlewill <a href="../../battlewill/plum-blossoms-five-petals-thrust/" title="Plum Blossoms - Five Petals Thrust">Plum Blossoms - Five Petals Thrust.</a>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Hinata_Sakaguchi">Hinata Sakaguchi</span></h4>
<p><a href="../../bosses/mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi"> Hinata</a> is a boss that spawns in specific biomes, summoning all <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../structures/structures-labyrinth-tree/" title="Spirit Tree"> Greater Spirits</a> to assist her in battle. She also activates <a href="../../skills/extra/spatial-domination/" title="Spatial Domination"> Fault Field</a> to become temporarily unhittable by non-spiritual/spatial/mental damage. She will clear the severance effect with <a href="../../skills/extra/spatial-domination/" title="Spatial Domination"> Spatial Cleanse</a>. She attacks using her <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Dead_End_Rainbow?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Dead End Rainbow (page does not exist)"> Dead End Rainbow</a> during her first phase. If she uses her Melt Slash ability, she will stop using <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Dead_End_Rainbow?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Dead End Rainbow (page does not exist)"> Dead End Rainbow</a> and use <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Moonlight?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Moonlight (page does not exist)"> Moonlight</a> instead. She may also activate Disintegration, which will instakill no matter what if it hits you. Hinata has a lot of resistance and nullification skills, so it is ideal to fight her with <a href="../../skills/other/bypass-degrade-skills/" title="Bypass/Degrade Skills"> Bypass/Degrade Skills.</a> When she is defeated, she has a chance to drop her weapons and Holy Armorments boots, chestplate and leggings. More information can be found on <a href="../../bosses/mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi"> Hinata Sakaguchi.</a>
</p>
</div>
