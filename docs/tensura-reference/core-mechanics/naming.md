<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Naming">Naming</span></h2>
<p><big><b>!!! Evolution through naming a player can only be granted if: The target player is prior to their second to last evolution, and the target player does not require an awakening for their next evolution !!!</b></big><br/>
Default Key to Name is N (Rebindable)<br/>
When named, the 'owner' (who named you) will siphon souls for <a href="../races-awakening/" title="Races/Awakening"> TDL awakening</a> from the named player.
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner"><a class="image reference-overview-duplicate" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Naming.png"><img class="thumbimage" data-file-height="186" data-file-width="283" decoding="async" height="186" loading="lazy" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/misc/naming-6d265286af.png" width="283"/></a> <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a class="internal" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Naming.png" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The Naming GUI</div></div></div>
<p>Naming requires the target to have HP lower than 25% of Max HP (configurable) or have the Fear effect (caused by the Namer). These are ignored in creative mode or if the target for naming is below 1% of the namer's EP.
</p><p>Also ignored if the target is tamed by the player. Through normal Minecraft <a class="extiw" href="https://minecraft.wiki/w/Taming" title="mcw:Taming">Taming</a> or via Mind Control Skills. i.e. <a href="../../skills/intrinsic/charm/" title="Charm">Charm</a>
</p><p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><a class="image" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Subdue.png"><img alt="Subdue.png" data-file-height="160" data-file-width="160" decoding="async" height="40" loading="lazy" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/misc/subdue-7026908fb3.png" width="40"/></a> - [Subdue] (-) - Costs 0.5 × the target’s EP in your <b>current</b> Magicule.
<ul><li>– e.g. naming a 1 000 EP creature costs 500 Magicule.</li>
<li>– Boosts the target’s EP by +50 % (1 000 → 1 500 EP), tames/subordinates it, and heals it to full.</li>
<li>– <b>No</b> chance to permanently drain your <b>max</b> Magicule.<br/></li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><a class="image" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Evolve.png"><img alt="Evolve.png" data-file-height="160" data-file-width="160" decoding="async" height="40" loading="lazy" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/misc/evolve-0922f974dc.png" width="40"/></a> - [Evolve] (=) - Costs 1.5 × the target’s EP in your <b>current</b> Magicule.
<ul><li>- e.g. naming a 1 000 EP creature costs 1 500 Magicule.</li>
<li>- +150 % EP (1 000 → 2 500 EP), tames/subordinates it, heals to full, and **evolves** if an evolution exists.</li>
<li>- <b>20% chance</b> to permanently reduce your <b>max</b> Magicule by <b>exactly</b> the naming cost.<br/></li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><a class="image" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Endow.png"><img alt="Endow.png" data-file-height="160" data-file-width="160" decoding="async" height="40" loading="lazy" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/misc/endow-8bd34da08b.png" width="40"/></a> - [Endow] (+) - Costs 9 × the target’s EP in your <b>current</b> Magicule.
<ul><li>- e.g. naming a 1 000 EP creature costs 9 000 Magicule.</li>
<li>- +900 % EP (1 000 → 10 000 EP), tames/subordinates it, heals to full, and evolves if possible.</li>
<li>- EP gain is capped by the config’s `maxEPGain` (default 1,000,000).</li>
<li>- <b>50% chance</b> to permanently reduce your <b>max</b> Magicule by <b>exactly</b> the naming cost.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li>Endow works differently with Players, Each skill unlearnt has 50% to gain a certain amount of learning points [50% to get 25 - 25% to get 50 - 20% to get 75 - 5% to get 100]</li>
<li>Non-magicule based alignments (not Holy/Chaos/Majin) will also use aura for naming if there is not enough magicule.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
To allow yourself to be nameable - Default keybind is Shift+N
</p><p><br/>
Forced Evolutions from Naming: Ogre-&gt;Kijin-&gt;Mystic Oni
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Named_By_The_Masses">Named By The Masses</span></h2>
<p>Mass naming (also known as Self Naming) multiplies your current maximum EP by 1.5x, with the default maximum gain being 1 million EP
</p><p>The name given will be your username, there is no way to choose it.
</p><p>To be mass named you need to fulfill <b>one</b> of the following requirements:
</p>
<ul><li>To kill 1000 humans <b>in one life</b>. Humans are Villagers, Pillagers/Evokers/etc, Witches, and Otherworlders.</li>
<li>Or Complete 25 raids across any number of lives</li></ul>
<p>You also need to allow naming with Shift + N (by default).
If this is done after the requirements, killing something should cause it to update and activate the mass naming.
</p>
