# Hipokute Farming

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" dir="ltr" lang="en"><p><big>Grow that Grass</big>
</p><p>Place down <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/hipokute-seeds/" title="Hipokute Seeds"></a></span> <a href="../../items/hipokute-seeds/" title="Hipokute Seeds"><span class="sprite-text">Hipokute Seeds</span></a></span><br/>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="1.19.2">1.19.2</span></h2>
<p>On random tick, in version 1.19.2, a hipokute seed has 10% chance to be grow into hipokute "small grass"<br/>

</p><p><i>If planted in Hell, it becomes a 25% Chance to Grow into small grass</i>
</p><p>In other 90%, it grows into vanilla <a class="extiw" href="https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wheat_Seeds" title="mcw:Wheat Seeds">Wheat</a> and grows like in vanilla.
<i>75% In Hell</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="1.21.1">1.21.1</span></h2>
<p>The probability that a hipokute seed turns into a small grass rather than wheat is dependent on the current magicule of the chunk and is given by the following formula
</p>
<dl><dd><math class="mwe-math-element mwe-math-element-inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mstyle displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"><mi>P</mi><mo stretchy="false">=</mo><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mfrac><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mn>1</mn></mrow><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mi>max</mi><mo>⁡</mo><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>,</mo><mn>1</mn><mn>0</mn><mo stretchy="false">−</mo><mo stretchy="false">⌊</mo><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mfrac><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mi>M</mi></mrow><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mn>5</mn><mn>0</mn><mn>0</mn></mrow></mrow></mfrac></mrow><mo stretchy="false">⌋</mo><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow></mrow></mfrac></mrow></mstyle></mrow></math></dd></dl>
<p>where M is the chunk's current magicule. This means that for hell and magicule-rich biomes where <math class="mwe-math-element mwe-math-element-inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mstyle displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"><mi>M</mi><mo stretchy="false">≥</mo><mn>4</mn><mn>5</mn><mn>0</mn><mn>0</mn></mstyle></mrow></math>, the seed will always become small grass, and in normal chunks where <math class="mwe-math-element mwe-math-element-inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mstyle displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"><mi>M</mi><mo stretchy="false">=</mo><mn>1</mn><mn>0</mn><mn>0</mn><mn>0</mn></mstyle></mrow></math>, a hipokute seed has a <math class="mwe-math-element mwe-math-element-inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mstyle displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mfrac><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mn>1</mn></mrow><mrow data-mjx-texclass="ORD"><mn>8</mn></mrow></mfrac></mrow><mo stretchy="false">=</mo><mn>1</mn><mn>2</mn><mo stretchy="false">.</mo><mn>5</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">%</mi></mstyle></mrow></math> chance to turn into small grass.
<br/>
</p>
<hr/>
<p>As "small grass", it has 50-50 chance to become Hipokute Grass or Hipokute Flower, regardless of the version.<br/>
</p><p>

</p><p><b>Hipokute Grass cannot become Hipokute Flower</b><br/>
</p><p><br/>
Right-clicking a Hipokute Flower will drop a <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/hipokute-flower/" title="Hipokute Flower"></a></span> <a href="../../items/hipokute-flower/" title="Hipokute Flower"><span class="sprite-text">Hipokute Flower</span></a></span>  and set the plant back to "small grass" which will restart the 50-50 Chance again. <br/>
However, if the plant "failed" to grow, you can destroy it to gain back more seeds and gain <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/hipokute-grass/" title="Hipokute Grass"></a></span> <a href="../../items/hipokute-grass/" title="Hipokute Grass"><span class="sprite-text">Hipokute Grass</span></a></span>
</p><p>Hipokute seeds can only be planted on Grass/Dirt/Farmland, but it can spawn naturally on stones.
</p>
