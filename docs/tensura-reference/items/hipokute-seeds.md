<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>This item can be obtained by destroying/harvesting hipokute grass sprouts in the world. This whether naturally generated in the wild or grown by hipokute farming. Hipokute cannot be bonemealed or pollinated by bees.
</p>
