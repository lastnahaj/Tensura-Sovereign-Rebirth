<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>This item can be obtained by destroying/harvesting hipokute grass sprouts in the world. This whether naturally generated in the wild or grown by hipokute farming. Hipokute cannot be bonemealed or pollinated by bees.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p>This can be used to plant hipokute <br/>
(see more in <a href="../../core-mechanics/mechanics-hipokute-farming/" title="Mechanics/Hipokute Farming">hipokute farming</a>) <br/>
Hipokute is used to make different potions that heal your health and magicules <br/>
