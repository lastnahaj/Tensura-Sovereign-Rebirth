<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Crafting">Crafting</span></h3>
<p>Can be used to create an armor of Monster Leather of Rank-B.
The recipe is the same as the one of the leather of the vanilla game. It can also be used in a Smithing Bench after unlocking the Monster Leather Gear Schematic.
