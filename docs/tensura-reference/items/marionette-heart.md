<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<p>A rare magic item that can turn the user into a <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../core-mechanics/races-alignment/" title="Races/Majin">Majin</a> with the cost of half their max HP in damage and most of their current Magicule. <br/>
<b>This does NOT mean that the player will lose half their max hp, rather they will take this in damage (so you can heal this back up)</b>
<br/><b>Same for the Magicule, it will regenerate.</b>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h3>
<p>-99% Attack Damage <br/>
<b>This effect is only when you have the item in your hand/offhand.</b>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<ul><li>Found in Ancient City's (0.5% chance)</li>
<li>Bastion treasures and End City's (both 1% chance)</li></ul>
