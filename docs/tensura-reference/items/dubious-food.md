<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p>When eaten, the Dubious Food can give various effects based on an 80% chance per effect. <br/>
These effects include:
</p>
<ul><li>Fatal Poison (0:10)</li>
<li>Poison III (0:10)</li>
<li>Paralysis (0:05)</li>
<li>Hunger II (0:10)</li>
<li>Slowness II (0:10)</li>
<li>Weakness (0:10)</li>
<li>Blindness (0:10)</li>
<li>Nausea (0:10)</li>
<li>Mining Fatigue II (0:10)</li></ul>
