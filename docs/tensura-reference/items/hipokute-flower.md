<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>This item can be obtained by right clicking a hipokute grass or destroying it. <br/> This whether in the wild or whether by <a href="../../core-mechanics/mechanics-hipokute-farming/" title="Mechanics/Hipokute Farming">hipokute farming</a>.
</p>
