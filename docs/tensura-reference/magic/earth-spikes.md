<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a 1x3 spike from the ground underneath a looked-at mob (on ground and in 20 block radius) that deals 30 damage and knocks them into the air with knockback</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Target extends to all mobs in a 5x5 radius of the targeted mob/block, creating multiple spikes</li></ul>
