<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Combines the effects of Magic Barrier and Barrier into one, reducing magical and physical damage below 1/5th of the user's hp to 0. If attack is above 1/5th of the user's hp reduces damage by 1/10th. Lasts for 30 seconds. Cannot be used while Barrier or Magic Barrier or modes are active.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Adds two new modes.</li>
<li>Magic Defense which drops all physical damage protection for 1/4th negation and 1/8th reduction value.</li>
<li>Physical Defense which has the same values as Magic Defense but drops all magic damage protection for physical damage protection.</li>
<li>Both modes last for 15 seconds and have a 45 second cooldown.</li></ul>
