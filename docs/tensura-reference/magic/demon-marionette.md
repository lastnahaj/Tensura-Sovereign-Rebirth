<p>Completely dominate foes strong enough to be considered demon lord seeds and put them under your control
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Ineffective against Spiritual beings or targets with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/spiritual-attack-nullification/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spiritual Attack Nullification">Spiritual Attack Nullification</a>. While casting, summon a magic circle below the looked-at target (range 20 blocks) and wrap the target with marionette lines, slowing them down by 50% speed. When successfully cast on a target that remains in range and has an EP below 400,000 (200,000 with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/spiritual-attack-resistance/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spiritual Attack Resistance">Spiritual Attack Resistance</a>), forcibly tames the target for 10 minutes.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Works on <a href="../../bosses/mobs-supermassive-slime/" title="Mobs/Supermassive Slime">Supermassive Slime</a> and <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-lord/" title="Mobs/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a>/<a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster">Disaster</a></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Timer becomes permanent till death, untamed or force-tamed by others, and increases the cap to 800,000 EP. (400,000 if aiming at targets with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/spiritual-attack-resistance/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spiritual Attack Resistance">Spiritual Attack Resistance</a>)</li></ul>
