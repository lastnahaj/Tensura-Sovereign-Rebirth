<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Unleashes a barrage of 10 light arrows from behind the user to looked-at target (max range 20 blocks). Each arrow does 30 damage but deals triple damage against undead. 5 seconds cooldown</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increase arrow amount to 20</li>
<li>Increase max range to 30</li>
<li>Cooldown reduced to 3s</li></ul>
