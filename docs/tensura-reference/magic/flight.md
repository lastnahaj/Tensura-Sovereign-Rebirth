<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Slightly pushes the caster in the direction they are looking at while granting them slow fall for 2 seconds. Lasts for 10 seconds. Doesn't need a magic circle.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
