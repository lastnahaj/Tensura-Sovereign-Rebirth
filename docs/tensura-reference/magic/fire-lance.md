<p>Throw a lance made of flames at your enemy
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Shoots a fire projectile that behaves like an arrow and deals 50 points of magic damage and 20 points of fire damage. Does not set blocks on fire and does not explode. Should have a speed the same as or slightly faster than an arrow.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Unlocks Repeat Mode that, when held down, shoots a fire lance with the same property and cost every second.</li></ul>
