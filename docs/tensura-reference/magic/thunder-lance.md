<p>Creates a lance made of magic lighting
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Shoots a fast lightning projectile that behaves like an arrow and deals 50 points of magic damage and 30 points of lightning damage. The projectiles can pierce through multiple entities.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Unlocks Rain Mode that releases 10 thunder lances from the sky in a 10x10 radius.</li>
<li>Each lance does 30 magic damage and 30 lightning damage.</li></ul>
