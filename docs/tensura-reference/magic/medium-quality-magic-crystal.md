<h3><span id="Absorb_.26_Dissolve"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Absorb_&amp;_Dissolve">Absorb &amp; Dissolve</span></h3>

Consuming this Magic Crystal regenerates 2500 Magicules instantly.
