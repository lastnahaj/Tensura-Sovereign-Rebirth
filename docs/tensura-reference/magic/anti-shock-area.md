<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a 15x15 zone (radius 7.5) where all physical damage taken (normal, slashing, pierce, blunt) regardless of source is reduced to 1. Lasts for 2 minutes. 5s cooldown.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases radius to 30x30 (radius 15). Increases time to 3 minutes. 3s cooldown</li></ul>
