<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Deals either 100 points or 10% of the target's max SHP (whichever is higher) in spiritual damage (half if target resists) against a target within 6 blocks and inflicts 2 levels of fragility for 15 seconds. Has a 25% chance to inflict 1-3 levels of insanity for 30 seconds (50% if target's SHP is below 50%). Cooldown 3s.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Deals double damage against targets below 50% of their max SHP.</li></ul>
