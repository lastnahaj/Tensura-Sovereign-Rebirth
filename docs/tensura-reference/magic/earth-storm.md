<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>While held down, has a 30% chance to apply 40 ticks of levitation to all targets in a 10x10 radius every 20 ticks. All targets in the 10x10 radius have a rock projectile that deals 20 damage fall on them every 20 ticks. Maximum time is 15 seconds</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Maximum time extends to 30 seconds</li></ul>
