<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Applies Confusion effect (nausea, slowness, and darkness debuffs) to enemies in a 20x20 radius for 15 seconds. The level depends on the targets existence value compared to the user, with 75% ~ 150% dealing 1 level, 50% ~ 74% dealing 2 levels, and 1% ~ 49% dealing 3 levels. Cannot be healed with milk/antidote.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increased to 20 seconds. Enemies below 25% get an additional level (level 4).</li></ul>
