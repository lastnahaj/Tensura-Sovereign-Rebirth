<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Shoots a fire projectile that behaves like a fire charge and deals 20 points of magic damage and 10 points of fire damage on contact with a mob. Does not set blocks on fire, and does not explode. Should have the same speed as a fire charge.
<ul><li>will set looked at mobs on fire for 5 seconds and will trigger creeper, tnt and minecart tnt explosions</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Shoots three projectiles instead of one, identical to multishot on crossbows.</li>
<li>Casting time reduced to 20 ticks.</li></ul>
