<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>In a 15 block straight line, deals 200 damage to all mobs/players. If a player/mob is being looked at when activated, they are teleported towards the user 15 blocks. If a player/mob isn’t being looked at, teleports the player forward 15 blocks. 5 seconds cooldown</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases damage to 300</li>
<li>Increases range to 20</li>
<li>Cooldown reduced to 3s</li></ul>
