<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a pawn (skeleton or zombie, random) with 20HP, 5 attack damage, no weapon and burn during sunlight. Doesn’t cost any magicule to maintain but only last 5 minutes. 10s cooldown.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>5s cooldown instead.</li>
<li>Can summon 5 pawns at once when sneak.</li>
<li>Can learn <a href="../create-greater-undead/" title="Create Greater Undead"> Create Greater Undead</a> as Wight.</li></ul>
