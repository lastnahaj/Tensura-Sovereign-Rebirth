<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Shoots a purple energy projectile that on contact inflicts 30 seconds of burden III.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Additionally deals 30 seconds of slowness II.</li></ul>
