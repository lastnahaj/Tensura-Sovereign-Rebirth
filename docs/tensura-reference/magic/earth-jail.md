<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Single target ability that deals 50 damage to a looked-at target (in 20 block radius) then applies Slowness IV, Burden II, and Fragility II for 60 seconds. If resistance is held, Slowness becomes halved to Slowness II.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Applies mining fatigue II</li>
<li>Increases radius to 30.</li></ul>
