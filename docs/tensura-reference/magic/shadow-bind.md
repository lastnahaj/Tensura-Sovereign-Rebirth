<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>If the target (in 10 block radius) is standing in shadows and touching the ground, binds them in place for 10 seconds and deals 10 SHP damage a second. If target has <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/darkness-attack-resistance/" title="Abilities/Skills/Darkness Attack Resistance">Darkness Attack Resistance</a>, reduces speed to 20% instead,</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Changes 10SHP to 10SHP + 1% of their maximum SHP</li>
<li>Can be used even if the target isn't standing in a dark area</li></ul>
