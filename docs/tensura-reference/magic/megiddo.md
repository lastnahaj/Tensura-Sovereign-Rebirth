<p>Unleash powerful sun blasts using water to kill any nearby foes. It can also be used manually to concentrate fire for any stronger foes
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Single Target: On use, creates a giant water bubble 20 blocks above the player (follow the player) with 10 “charges”, removes when out of charge or lasted for 5 mins. On subsequent uses (only during day), shoots a light ray at a looked at mob/player within a 50x50 range that deals 150 heat damage</li>
<li>Auto Target: On use, creates 9 water bubbles 20 blocks above the player that stay put and last 30s, with 5 charges. These bubbles will automatically target any non-ally entities and shoot them with the 150 heat damage light ray in the range of 20x20x20.
<ul><li>both modes ignore armor and barriers such as <a href="../../skills/extra/multilayer-barrier/" title="Multilayer Barrier">Multilayer Barrier</a> and <a href="../../skills/common/ranged-barrier/" title="Ranged Barrier">Ranged Barrier</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Only Usable in Overworld and when it's sunny/clear skies</b>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Charges increase from 10 to 20 (5 to 10 for Auto)</li>
<li>Reduce Auto Target’s cooldown to 10s</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
