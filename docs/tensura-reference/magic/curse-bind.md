<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Inflicts a looked at target with paralysis III for 20 seconds, dealing 100 magic damage every 40 ticks and inflicting 1 level (+30 seconds) of curse and corrosion every 5 seconds.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>100 ticks cast time.</li>
<li>150 damage instead of 100.</li></ul>
