<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Makes dirt, mud, grass, mycelium, etc (terrain/soil blocks) in a 5x5x5 radius from the block the user is looking at (range 10 blocks) have a powdered snow/quicksand effect - replaces the blocks with "loose variant" (loose dirt, quickmud, quicksand with same texture).</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Casting time reduced to 60 ticks.</li></ul>
