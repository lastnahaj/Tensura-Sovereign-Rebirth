<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Grants the user a buff 'Wind's Protection' for 120 seconds that grants a speed bonus equivalent to speed I and makes the user immune to all physical projectiles (same as 100% projectile protection).</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases duration to 180 seconds and grants 'Wind's Protection' level III (Speed 3, Immunity to being lit on fire, +0.2 knockback resist, and increases all sources of fire damage from the player by 20%)</li></ul>
