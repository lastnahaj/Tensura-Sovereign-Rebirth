<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates magic fire on all blocks on the groundin a 30x30 radius, which deal 20 magic damage and 20 fire damage every 10 ticks (cannot activate if the caster is more than 20 blocks above the ground) . Once every 30 ticks, the flame surges 10 blocks high in the radius, dealing 200 magic damage and 50 fire damage. Lasts for 7.5 seconds.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Adds a Condensed mode change that limits the range to 5x5 blocks and lasting only 2 seconds, dealing 300 magic damage and 100 fire damage every second.</li></ul>
