<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Summons black clouds that last for 30 seconds, striking all mobs in a 35x35 block radius with lightning that deals 300 damage every 80 ticks. Cooldown 10s.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Cast time reduced to 100 ticks.</li>
<li>Coating mode unlocked. For the same cast time, applies a buff to the user's weapon that adds an extra 100 magic damage for up to 5 attacks.</li></ul>
