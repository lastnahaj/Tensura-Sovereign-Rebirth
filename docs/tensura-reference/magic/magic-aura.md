<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
Grants the player a buff that effects their physical attacks and Battlewill attacks for 5 minutes. <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/physical-attack-resistance/" title="Abilities/Skills/Physical Attack Resistance">Physical Attack Resistance</a> is ignored and Nullification is treated as Resistance. 7 Modes, can not change mode once the 5-minute effect starts. Will not apply additional damage if your attack is already buffed by Element Core <a href="../../core-mechanics/engravings/#Slotting_1" title="Engravings">Slotting</a>.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Default</b>: 500 MP. Adds <a href="../../core-mechanics/damage-types/#Magic" title="Damage Types"><span>Magic</span></a> Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user</li>
<li><b>Holy</b>: 1000 MP. Adds <a href="../../core-mechanics/damage-types/#Holy" title="Damage Types"><span>Holy</span></a> Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user.</li>
<li><b>Fire</b>: 1000 MP. Adds <a href="../../core-mechanics/damage-types/#Flame" title="Damage Types"><span>Flame</span></a> Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user.</li>
<li><b>Earth</b>: 1000 MP. Adds <a href="../../core-mechanics/damage-types/#Earth" title="Damage Types"><span>Earth</span></a> Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user.</li>
<li><b>Water</b>: 1000 MP. Adds <a href="../../core-mechanics/damage-types/#Water" title="Damage Types"><span>Water</span></a> Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user.</li>
<li><b>Wind</b>: 1000 MP. Adds <a href="../../core-mechanics/damage-types/#Wind" title="Damage Types"><span>Wind</span></a> Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user.</li>
<li><b>Space</b>: 1000 MP. Adds <a href="../../core-mechanics/damage-types/#Space" title="Damage Types"><span>Space</span></a> Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user.</li></ul>
