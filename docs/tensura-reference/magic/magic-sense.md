<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
as of 2.0.0.4
</p>
<ul><li>When pressed, grant the user Night Vision effect (Presense Sense level 0.5, no entity-highlight) till pressed again, the skill slowly gains mastery passively in this duration. When mastered, the ability becomes toggleable and Presence Sense level increases to 1.5 (sees invisible mobs and highlight entities in 30-block radius). Costs 5MP per 20 Ticks</li></ul>
