<p>Darker than black, darker than darkness, combine with my intense crimson! The time of awakening has come... Dance, dance, dance! Desire for the surge of my magic, the power of collapse with no equal, all phenomena turn to dust, flowing forth from the abyss! This is the ultimate attack magic, Explosion!
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>While held down, goes up in level every 50 ticks. For each level, the Magic damage increases by 100, and the blast radius and depth increase by 5 blocks. Gain another magic circle vertically each level, needs to reach at least level 3 to be casted and the targeted position must be not blocked from the sky. The maximum level is 5, no item drops.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases max level to 10.</li>
<li>Unlocks a mode that allows charged explosions to be set as a trap on a looked-at block (similar to Fusionist) with a maximum level of 3, recast while looking at the landmine to trigger it, sneak to remove it instead</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Side_Note">Side Note</span></h2>
<ul><li>Explosion cost adds when you cast higher levels, so level 1 would be 10k, level 2 would be 30k (10k from level 1 + 20k from level 2), level 3 would be 60k, etc up to 550k at level 10</li></ul>
