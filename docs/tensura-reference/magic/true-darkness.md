<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a 15x15 radius around the user where all players and mobs are inflicted with warden blindness and all light sources cannot produce light. Deals 100SHP in damage every second and inflicts one level of <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-insanity/" title="Effects/Insanity">Insanity</a> (for 10s) every 3 seconds. Lasts while held for up to 10 seconds</li></ul>
