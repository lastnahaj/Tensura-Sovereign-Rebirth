<p>Creates a wall of ice to protect the user
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a wall of ice 8x8 at the block the player is looking at. The wall of ice will last for 20 seconds and emit particles in a 5 block radius around it, dealing 50 aoe magic damage every 20 ticks. Direct contact with the ice inflicts 30 magic damage and 10 seconds of Chill I each second.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>When aimed at an entity instead of a block, it will now encase the target in a block (8x4x8) of ice blocks.</li>
<li>Contact damage is increased to 64 and now inflicts 15 seconds of Chill II every 20 ticks. Upon the ice wall’s removal after 20 seconds, inflict every contacting entity with 10 seconds of Frost.</li></ul>
