<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Launches a ball of fire in a straight line that explodes on contact with an entity or block, dealing 100 magic damage and 30 fire damage to mobs in a 6x6 radius. Doesn’t start fire on explosion.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Cast time is reduced to 20 ticks but if held down to the full 60 ticks it becomes a charged version that deals 250 magic damage and 60 fire damage with a radius of 12x12 blocks. (This charged casting, 60 ticks, won’t be affected by chant annulment but is affected by thought acceleration.)</li></ul>
