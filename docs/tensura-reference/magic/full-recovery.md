<p>Just as new, could say you are <i>fully recovered</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Restores 100% HP to the user or the mob/player they're looking at. Additionally it increases their saturation to full and gives Absorption IV for 2 minutes (same as eating a god apple). 15s cooldown</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Reduces casting time to 80 ticks. Increase the Absorption IV duration to 4 minutes. 10s cooldown</li></ul>
