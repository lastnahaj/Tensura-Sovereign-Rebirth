<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Magic Water Transform</b> - Grants access to Greater level Water Spirit Magic. Poisons attacked targets for 10 secs, makes the user's water attacks deal 1.2X Damage and makes the user immune to physical attacks like spirits, it also allows the user to use <a href="../../skills/extra/water-domination/" title="Water Domination">Water Domination</a> and <a href="../../skills/extra/water-manipulation/" title="Water Manipulation">Water Manipulation</a> without a water source. It lasts for 3 minutes, and upon ending inflicts the player with <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-paralysis/" title="Effects/Paralysis">Paralysis</a> 2, <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-fragility/" title="Effects/Fragility">Fragility</a> 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes. Costs 10,000 MP -&gt; Total cooldown: 3|6 + 10 = 13|16 minutes
<ul><li>Can't be activated along with the following skills
<ul><li><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/beast-transformation/" title="Beast Transformation">Beast Transformation</a></li>
<li><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/dragon-mode/" title="Dragon Mode">Dragon Mode</a></li>
<li><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/ogre-berserker/" title="Ogre Berserker">Ogre Berserker</a></li>
<li><a href="../magic-flame-transform/" title="Magic Flame Transform">Magic Flame Transform</a></li>
<li><a href="../magic-wind-transform/" title="Magic Wind Transform">Magic Wind Transform</a></li>
