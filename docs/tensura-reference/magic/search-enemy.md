<p>You have no enemies, or <i>do you?</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Highlights enemies in a 30x30 block radius (hostile mobs are outlined with red). Lasts for 2 minutes, cast it again to turn it of. Doesn't need a magic circle.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Adds a new mode with 0 cast time and 5 MP per second cost that lasts as long as its held down.</li></ul>
