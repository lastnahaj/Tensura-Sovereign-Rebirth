<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Launches magma projectiles outwards from the player in a conical spread. Deals 200 damage to any target hit by it (Only hit once) and creates shallow not-source lava on the floor</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Damage increased to 350</li>
<li>Targets hit by magma projectiles are inflicted with Burden II for 10 seconds</li>
<li>5s cooldown instead</li></ul>
