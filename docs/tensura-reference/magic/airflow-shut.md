<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a 5x5 zone centered where the player's looking at that drains oxygen and inflicts 5 seconds of silence every 20 ticks. It will remain indefinitely while held down and will linger for 10 seconds after the button is released.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Casting time reduced to 80 ticks.</li>
<li>Access to the Expanded Mode with 8x8 radius instead.</li></ul>
