<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Grants the caster a Reinforcement buff effect for 2 minutes, which makes the armors and weapons of the user take 75% less durability damage. Applies on an entity in 10 block radius instead when sneaking.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases duration from 2 minutes to 10 minutes.</li></ul>
