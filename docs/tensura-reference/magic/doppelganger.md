<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates 2 clones just like the <a href="../../skills/extra/body-double/" title="Body Double">Body Double</a> skill with EP equal to 5% of the caster's MP each. 5 second cooldown and doesn't need a magic circle.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Casting time is reduced to 80 ticks and decreases cooldown to 3s. Sneak while casting to create 4 clones instead with EP equal to the 2.5% of the user’s MP each. Can learn the <a href="../../skills/extra/body-double/" title="Body Double">Body Double</a> skill without having defeated <a href="../../bosses/mobs-ifrit/" title="Mobs/Ifrit">Ifrit</a>.</li></ul>
