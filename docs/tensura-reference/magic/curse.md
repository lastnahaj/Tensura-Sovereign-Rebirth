<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Releases purple miasma fog in a 10x10 radius around the user for 20 seconds. Miasma Fog deals 40 magic damage on contact every 40 ticks and inflicts 30 seconds of Curse I.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Level of curse increases from I to II.</li>
<li>Damage increases from 40 to 60.</li>
<li>Can learn [Curse Bind] as Wight.</li></ul>
