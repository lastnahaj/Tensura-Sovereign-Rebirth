<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>50hp to the user if released while not looking at an entity. If looking at an entity while released, heals that instead. 5s cooldown</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Heals for 100hp OR 25% of the target's max hp (Whichever is higher). Reduces cast time to 30 ticks. 3s cooldown</li></ul>
