<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Completely removes Poison, Nausea, and Weakness debuffs on the user. If looking at another mob/player, apply to them instead.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Additionally, will reduce the level of fatal poison by 1 and reduce duration by 10 seconds.</li></ul>
