<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Releases 30 icicle lances from the sky in a 15x15 radius. Each lance does 60 magic damage.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Gains [Repeat] mode that can be held down and repeated with a delay of 100 ticks (isn’t affected by chant annulment) and 10,000mp. Up to a maximum of 3 bonus times total.</li></ul>
