<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] At the cost of 20 MP per second, Magics, Skill, and Battlewills deal only 50% (33% with Mastery) damage to the user. Additionally, mobs within a 20x20 (30x30 with Mastery) radius of the user are unable to use any flight-based skills, magic, or battlewills (except for wing-based ones]. With Mastery, disable transformation skills (Mimic, Transform, Dragon Mode, Beast Mode) for 30 seconds if physically attacking a transformed mob.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Counter">Counter</span></h2>
<p>However there is one counter to this, being that if another player has 3.5x ep than the person/mob(if you're fighting <a href="../../bosses/mobs-charybdis/" title="Mobs/Charybdis">Charybdis</a>) then Magic Jamming won't work. <br/>
<small>(for example 1 million EP (person who uses magic jamming) -&gt; 3.5 million EP and magic jamming doesnt work)</small>
</p>
