<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates 3 rock projectiles that are launched spread-out from the user and sent where they are aiming. Each rock deals 150 magic damage and 50 earth damage.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Adds “Chain Mode” that fires the 3 projectiles out consecutively in a straight line instead of all at once spread out.</li></ul>
