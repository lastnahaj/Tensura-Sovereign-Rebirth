<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>A “kamehameha” style beam attack of black light with a range of 20 blocks. Inflicts 250 HP damage and adds 1 level of <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-insanity/" title="Effects/Insanity">Insanity</a>. Deals 10x the regular durability damage against armor. Inflicts a hunger II and wither II status effect for 30 seconds</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Decreases cast time to 40 ticks</li></ul>
