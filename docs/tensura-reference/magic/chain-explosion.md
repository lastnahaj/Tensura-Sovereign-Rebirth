</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates 3 explosions at the point looked at, each one dealing 200 damage in 10 block radius (the same as Explosion Lvl 2). The explosions are 30 ticks apart and released in a triangle (where the overlap of the three explosions is where the target block was).</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>The explosion damage increases to 300 damage and 15 block radius. (the same as Explosion Lvl 3)</li></ul>
<p><br/>
