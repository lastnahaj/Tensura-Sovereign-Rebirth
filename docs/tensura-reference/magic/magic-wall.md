<p>Use pure magic to create a wall in front of you
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a 4x4 wall made of barrier blocks that slows down entities that move through them by 50%. Reduce/negate magic damage projectiles that pass through that have damage lower than 30. Lasts for 10 seconds.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases duration to 25 seconds, projectile damage reduction to 50.</li>
<li>Cast time to 100 ticks.</li></ul>
