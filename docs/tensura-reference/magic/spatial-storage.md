<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Create a Spatial Storage tied to the magic with 6 rows and items can stack to 100 instead 64. When activated, creates a special spatial bag item that accesses this spatial storage; it will disappear if dropped, cannot be stored in a chest.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Items now stack to 200, cast time is 0.</li>
<li>Activating now instant open the spatial storage without any spatial bag (sneak to recreate the spatial bag instead).</li>
<li>The gui now has 4 additional armor slots (head, chest, legs, feet), with a new mode [Dress Change] that will hot-swap the player's currently equipped armor with the armor in the storage.</li></ul>
