</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates snow particles in a 35x35x35 radius around the player, dealing 200 magic damage and inflicting 30 seconds of Chill II. While held down will consume 5,000MP every 100 ticks to deal an additional 80 magic damage. Each subsequent damage will add an additional 15 seconds of Chill and increase its level by 1. This can be repeated up to a maximum of 3 times, after which the spell ends.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases the maximum blizzard surges from 3 to 5.</li></ul>
