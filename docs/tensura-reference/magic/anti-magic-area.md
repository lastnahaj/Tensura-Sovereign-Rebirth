<p>Completely stop anyone inside the area to use aspectual or summoning magic, with the sole exception of Satoru Go- <i>cough</i> people with Law Manipulation
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a 20x20 zone (radius 10) where Aspectual magic becomes impossible to use/activate, doing nothing when cast. Spirit Magic can still be used. Summoning magic cannot be used. Any magic already in effect stays in effect. Lasts for 1 minute. 5s cooldown.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases radius to 50x50 (radius 25). 3s cooldown.</li></ul>
