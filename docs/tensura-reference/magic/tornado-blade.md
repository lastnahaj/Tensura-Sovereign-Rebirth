<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Launches a ball/tornado of wind that explodes on contact to deal 150 magic damage and 25 wind damage to mobs in a 6x6 radius.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Increases radius of effect to 8x8 and pulls mobs in to the center on explosion. Deals double damage.</li>
<li>On explosion, release blades of wind from different directions from the center that each deal 50 magic damage and pierce through multiple enemies.</li></ul>
