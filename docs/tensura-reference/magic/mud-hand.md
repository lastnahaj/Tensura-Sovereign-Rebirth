<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Inflicts the mob/player the user is looking at with a bound status [immobilized] effect for as long as the button is held down, up to 5 seconds. Cannot be used on targets more than 4 blocks in the air.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Can be held for up to 10 seconds.</li>
<li>When the time runs out or the button is released, inflict the target with an additional 5 seconds of slowness II.</li></ul>
