</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Ineffective against Spiritual beings or targets with Spiritual Attack Nullification. When started casting, summon a magic circle below the looked-at target (range 6 blocks). When released on finishing casting while the target is in range, forcibly tames the target (works on Supermassive Slime, Orc Lord/Disaster) with an EP below 10,000 (5,000 if aiming at targets with Spiritual Attack Resistance) for 10 minutes.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Timer becomes permanent till death, untamed or force-tamed by others.</li></ul>
