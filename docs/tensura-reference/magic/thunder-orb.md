<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Shoots a lightning sphere that, every 10 ticks, strikes lightning on every enemy in a 5-block radius in its path. Each lightning does 80 magic damage and 40 lightning damage.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Radius increased to 10 blocks.</li>
<li>Cast time reduced to 80 ticks.</li></ul>
