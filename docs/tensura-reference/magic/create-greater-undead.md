<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates 1 pawns (zombie or skeleton, random), or 3 at once when sneak, each has 100HP, 5 base attack, wield sharpness 1 iron swords and power II bows, they also don't burnt in sunlight. Doesn’t cost any magicule to maintain but only last 5 minutes. 10s cooldown.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>5s cooldown instead</li>
<li>Pawns now wear chainmail armor.</li>
<li>Will summon 6 at once when sneak.</li></ul>
