<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Pierces the looked at target with spikes from the ground that inflict 250 magic damage and 50 earth damage on activation. Can be cast if Mud Hand is already cast (and being held down), reducing the cast time to 40 ticks and dealing an extra 50 magic damage.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Add a “Spread Mode” in which spikes are scattered in a 10x10 radius around the caster and will deal 20 points of contact damage (piercing) each second when touched, lasting for 30 seconds.</li></ul>
