<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Reduces physical damage done below 1/6th of the user's hp to 0 and if attack is above 1/6th of the user's hp reduces the damage by 1/12th the user's hp.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Values become 1/5th and 1/10th respectively.</li>
<li>Lasts for 20 seconds instead of 10.</li></ul>
