<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Grants access to Greater level of Space Spirit Magic. The user's space attacks deal 1.2X Damage and apply severance. The user also becomes immune to physical attacks like spirits. It lasts for 3 minutes, and upon ending inflicts the player with <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-paralysis/" title="Effects/Paralysis">Paralysis</a> 2, <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-fragility/" title="Effects/Fragility">Fragility</a> 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes. Costs 10,000 MP -&gt; Total cooldown: 3|6 + 10 = 13|16 minutes
<ul><li>Can't be activated along with the following skills
<ul><li><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/beast-transformation/" title="Beast Transformation">Beast Transformation</a></li>
<li><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/dragon-mode/" title="Dragon Mode">Dragon Mode</a></li>
<li><a href="../../skills/intrinsic/ogre-berserker/" title="Ogre Berserker">Ogre Berserker</a></li>
<li><a href="../magic-flame-transform/" title="Magic Flame Transform">Magic Flame Transform</a></li>
<li><a href="../magic-water-transform/" title="Magic Water Transform">Magic Water Transform</a></li>
