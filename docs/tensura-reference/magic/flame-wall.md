<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Creates a wall of fire identical to the fire wall spell, but it deals only half a heart of contact damage of magic and little knockback. For people who can see through illusions, the fire instead appears to be blue. (Still uses the Flame Magic Circle)</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Mimics mastered fire wall spell instead (double duration but no knockback resist negation).</li></ul>
