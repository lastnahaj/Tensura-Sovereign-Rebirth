<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Grants access to the Greater levels of Darkness Spirit Magic. Gives attacked targets darkness for 10 secs, makes the user's darkness attacks deal 1.2X Damage and makes the user immune to physical attacks like spirits. It lasts for 3 minutes, and upon ending inflicts the player with <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-paralysis/" title="Effects/Paralysis">Paralysis</a> 2, <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-fragility/" title="Effects/Fragility">Fragility</a> 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes. Costs 10,000 MP -&gt; Total cooldown: 3|6 + 10 = 13|16 minutes
