<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Silent Movement - The player makes zero noise, undetectable by Sculk or Sense Sound Wave.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive_2">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] Remove - The user instantly breaks Barriers. 5 Second Cooldown</li>
<li>[Active In-Slot] Dispel - The user automatically removes certain conditions from the user. The user removes various conditions from the target on it. 3 Second Cooldown. (configurable in config)</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Eraser - The user's attacks on enemies damage their Current MP. If the target has less than 15% of the user's EP, they are instantly killed, other wise each attack reduces their Current MP by 5% (2.5% if they have an ultimate skill). 2 Second Cooldown.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Necrosis - This applies the Necrotic Effect to a target. Necrotic makes it so they cannot regenerate Magicules and Regeneration Skills do not work. This has a 30 Second Cooldown and the condition lasts 25 Seconds.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Judgement - When used, Remove, Dispel, Eraser and Necrosis are all used together, but enhanced.
<ul><li>Dispel now deals bonus damage directly to the target based off the Barrier Points destroyed (1 Barrier Point = 5 Damage)</li>
<li>Dispel now bypasses Resistances and Nullifications</li>
<li>Eraser will instantly kill any target target with less than 50% of the user's EP by reducing their Current EP to 0%, otherwise reduce their Current EP by the difference between the User and Target's Current EP (0 if the target has higher EP) + 35% of the target's current EP.</li>
<li>Necrosis will apply the Necrotic Effect for 1 minute.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>This has a 1,440 second cooldown.
</p>
