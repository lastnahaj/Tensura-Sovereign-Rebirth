<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] All of Creation - Learn all schematics and aspcetual Magics</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] Analytical Appraisal - The Apprasial Level is 15 (30 on mastery)</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Pursuit of Truth: Pursuit of Truth: Investigate every 10 seconds, roll a 1/2 chance to gain 1 point of data on someone up to 10 points each one granting new details. Use /trnightmare investigate to get the info. While In-Slot the user gains the following benefits:
<ul><li>The user's Mastery and Learn Speed are increased by +10</li>
<li>The user's Melee Dodge Chance is at 50%, the user's Projectile Dodge Chance is at 25% and the user's Critical Hit Chance is at 30%, while it as a 40% Dodge Negation.</li></ul></li>
<li>When shift pressed, it will cycle between Pursuit Modes.
<ul><li>Pursuit of Surprise: This highlights nearby traps. Lava, Pointed Dripstone, Tripwire, Sculk Sensor, Sculk Shrieker, Cobweb, Powder Snow, Trapped Chest, TNT, TNT Minecart, End Crystals</li>
<li>Pursuit of Treasure: Chest, Barrel, Vault, Dragon Egg, Ancient Debris and Magic Ore, Charybdis Core</li>
<li>Pursuit of Battle: Highlights nearby mobs in colors, yellow for neutral, red for hostile</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press] Pursuit of Tutelage (Requires Mastery) - When used, the user can open up a Skill Creation Menu and teach their known Spells, Battlewills, Common, Extra and Resistance Skills to someone.</li>
<li>[Active, Toggle] Probability Manipulation - While this is toggled on, the user
<ul><li>'s physical attacks always deals damage to their target</li>
<li>always critically hits</li>
<li>'s Critical Hit Damage is increased by 2x</li>
<li>has Luck X</li>
<li>acts as if they have Fortune 4 on their tools.</li></ul></li></ul>
