<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] Magic Alteration: When toggled, the user can cast any Magic that's mastered within an Anti-Magic Area. If the player's EP is above 1.000.000, their mastered magic and battlewill are capable of bypassing</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Spatial Domination: Increases Spatial Damage by 4x. Mastery grants Spatial resistance/nullification degrade if the user has 800k ep. This doesn't stack with Spatial Domination or Spatial Manipulation.</li>
<li>[Passive, In Slot] Homeostasis: Remove any abnormal condition, Magic Interference, Severance or Cook’s Chaotic Fate effect of the user or looked-at entities.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press]  Imprison: At the cost of 10,000MP, imprisons the mob/player the player is looking at. Seals all movement, skill usage and healing. Drains MP from the trapped target at a rate of 500 per 10 ticks. Lasts for 300 seconds. Increases to 600 seconds with mastery - 10s cooldown - Goes on CD if you Miss (5s with Mastery).</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Universal Barrier: On use, set the player's "Barrier Points" equal to their Max HP x2.5. Damage taken under the Barrier Point threshold subtract MP from the user at a rate of 3 MP per 1 HP. Attacks above the threshold reduce the barrier points to 0 and have their damage reduced by the amount of barrier points the player has. (Can be shift activated to use on another player. This will give them "Barrier Points" equal to their max hp x1.75, and will run off their energy. Cannot be used if they already have Barrier Points active.)</li>
<li>[Active, Press]  Insulated Prison: This is an AOE version of Imprison, it cost 50,000 MP + 30% of the EP as MP for each affected entity to use, but imprisons all mobs in a 60x60 block radius. This has a 700 second cooldown. The duration of the imprison is 100 seconds (200 when mastered)</li></ul>
