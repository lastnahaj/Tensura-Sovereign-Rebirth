<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] Ultraspeed Regeneration - The user gain Ultraspeed Regeneration at 1/4th of the cost.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Guardian - The player takes damage in place of their subordinates</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Guardian:
<ul><li>Grant Protection - Grants all nearby subordinates/allies 30 extra armor points and 300 barrier points for 180 seconds. Costs 100 MP</li>
<li>Iron Wall - Grants the player an additional 40 (80 with Mastery) Armour Points and 1 (3 with Mastery) Knockback Resistance.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Held] Predation - On use, creates a black mist (breath) in a 2 block radius in front of the player. If it comes into contact with a mob, deals 10 damage and drains 100MP, with a 10% chance to gain one random Intrinsic, Common, Extra, or Resist skill from the target. If an enemy is killed by Predation, gains all of their Intrinsic, Common, Extra, and Resist skills. With Mastery, the mist is capable of eating projectiles. Targets killed have 30% (Configurable) of their ep added to the players instead of the usual 5%[a]. The other 50% will be added to Stomach's energy storage. Shift-activate to toggled on/off consume-block-mode, which will automatically be added to Stomach. (EP absorb only applies for the first target of that type).</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Corrosion - When activated while looking at nothing, costs 200 MP and deals 5 corrosion damage every 10 ticks to all enemies in a 10x10 radius. Enemies killed by this have their Intrinsic, Common, and Extra Skills taken by the player as well as have 30% (Configurable) of their ep added to the player's maximum.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Military Power:
<ul><li>Recieve - This mode lets the user open a menu to obtain skills from subordinates.</li>
<li>Provide - This mode lets the user open a menu to give skills from subordinates.</li>
<li>Stomach -  When activated, grants access to a spatial storage with the size of a chest with 28 rows. Items are able to be stacked to 400 instead of 64.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press/In-slot] Chaos Control - When Press, it fires several Chaos Eater projectiles at a target. It fires three projectiles, which each deal 400 Corrosion Damage. This counts as Predation when killing and attacking targets. When this skill is used 100 Times, if the user is holding a Meat Cleaver, it gains the following benefit: The Meat Cleaver does 200 Predation Damage on hit and predates skills like Prddation would.</li></ul>
