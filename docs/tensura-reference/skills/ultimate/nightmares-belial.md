<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] All of Creation - Learn all schematics and aspectual magics</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Spacetime Manipulation</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Life Domination: Soul Bank - The user is able to collect Souls, even without being a Demon Lord or having a Demon Lord Seed. If the user can already collect souls, they can now collect 25% more.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Paradox Flame - All of the user's Flame Magics become White. They apply the Freeze Effect when dealing Fire Damage. This now applies to Black Flame as well.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Nihilistic World - The user pulls all nearby entities into the Nihilistic World, a totally white dimension devoid of color, where concealment abilities do not work. While in this dimension, the user's Flame Magics deal Spiritual Damage as well. Anything killed inside of the After World will give the user Soul Points. The user gains 1.5x EP Regeneration inside of Nihilistic World for every 1,000 Souls they possess.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] White Flare (Requires learning) - The user begins to create ultra hot flame that they can fire out, this white flame is massive, but doesn't destroy the enviornment. It deals both 250 Nuclear Damage and 50 Spirit Damage for every 1,000 Souls they possess up to 500 Spiritual Damage. (So, 10,000 Souls).</li></ul>
<p>If something is killed by White Flare, they instead have their 5% of their EP consumed to create bonus Souls for the user.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Nihility Supply (Requires Mastery) - While held, the user can begin to burn their souls away to create Nihility. Their Nihility can scale up to 10,000 points before the user begins to take 50 Void Damage a second until it is decreased below 10,000. The user can have a maximum of 25,000 Nihility.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Nihilistic End (Requires Mastery, Requires Learning) - While the user is in Nihilistic World, they can use Nihilistic End, to deal 10 Nuclear and Void Damage for every 250 Nihility you have. Dealing a maximum of 1,000 Nuclear Damage and 1,000 Void Damage.</li></ul>
