<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Spiritual Manipulation - The user is immune to Charm and adjacent effects.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] Instant Kill - Makes the player's attacks deal 1500 (2000 with mastery) Spiritual Damage at the cost of 30,000 MP, 12s Cooldown, (6 with mastery)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Ultraspeed Action - Moves the player to where they're looking at high speed, effectively teleportation, 10 block radius, deal crit damage (+270 with Mastery) to anything on the way.  It cost 2,000 MP</li>
<li>[Active, Toggle] Eye Of The Moon - When this mode is toggled on, the user can shift and teleport to a shadow in sight. While this modes is toggled on, the user gains 80% Dodge Chance and 50% Dodge Bypass.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] (requires Instant Kill to be used atleast 500 times) Assassinate - This move while held will turn the user invisible if the is a low light level, as they hold the skill, they can move freely without being detected by sources with sensing below 8. The next target they it, will be hit by 2,000 SHP (4,000 SHP when mastered). The damaged is reduced by 50% if the Y level is at natural light or higher. This bypasses Resistances and Barrier Points.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] (requ Mastery and learning) Thousand Shadow Death -When held, dozens of shadows will be sent to grapple at the target and rip them to pieces. The target is similarly to Shadow Binding, but the longer they're bound, the more shadows will come to grab them and rip them to pieces. This does 250 SHP Damage and 250 Darkness Damage</li></ul>
