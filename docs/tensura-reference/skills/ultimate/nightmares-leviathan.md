<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Envious hero: If you are a True Hero, and awaken Leviathan, Lord of Envy, you will obtain the Unique Skill - <a href="../../unique/nightmares-stasis/" title="Stasis"> Stasis</a></li>
<li>[Passive, In Slot] Serpent's Jealousy: While in-slot, this skill allows you to randomly siphon Magicules and Aura from your target on hit. There is a 25% chance when you siphon Magicules and Aura to applied "Jealous" to their MP and AP bar. Preventing it from regenering in a similar fashion to Cook's Chaotic Fate.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Envious Fortune: While toggled on, you passively have Luck X and will automatically remove effects from yourself (Based off the UltimateNightmareSkillConfig's "LeviathanEffectPurge": Config). for 1,000 MP.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Power Siphon: When pressed, the looked at target suffers Magicule and Aura Drain, losing 2.5% and applying a 1.5% Max MP and AP stunt with Jealous.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Festival: In a 60x60 block radius around the user (Can be resisted if the user has 60% (80% with mastery) of the magicules of the user or more), applies Fragility, Paralysis, Chill and Slow Heal to the target. All nearby targets suffer 15% MP and AP drain with a 5% Jealous stunt on their MP and AP.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Dragon Festival: All non-allies in a 20 block radius gain Unluck while the user gains a level of Resistance for each nearby mob given Unluck.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Inferior: When used, for the next 60 seconds, (100 when mastered), you become empowered, gaining increased Attack Damage and Armor, as you reflect the properties of Absorption. During the duration you have the Envious Mirror effect, all attacks become "Inferior" making them only capable of doing a maximum of 1,000 Damage.</li>
<li>[Active, Toggle] Crushing Jealousy: When this mode is selected, toggle the Crushing Jealousy on, and any target with higher MP or AP than you will gain "Envied" Effect. Targets with this suffer a small amount of MP or AP Drain (Random) on being damaged by the user. If the user's MP or AP is lower than the target, they will recieve the "Stolen Luck" effect. Stolen Luck will reduce the effected target's dodge chance and crit chance by 50% per level.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Violent Envy: The immediate area prevents the usage of magic. Any attempts of casting Magic will result their MP being rendered by Jealous.</li></ul>
