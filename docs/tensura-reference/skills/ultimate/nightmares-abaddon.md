<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Reality Breaker - The user reduces Spatial Damage by 30% (50% when mastered). The user's adds Spatial Damage equal to 0.75x the attack damage of the user. This doesn't stack with Magic Aura.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-SLot] Limiter Breaker - While this skill is in-slot, the user can push skills over their maximum powerscale for Battlewills and Magics by 5. When mastered, it is by 10.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Acitve, Press] Bullet Break Storm - The user can charge a series of Magic Bullets and fire it at a target. It cost 50 AP and 50 MP per 1.5 seconds and has a maximum scale of 15 (This can be effected by Power Break). It deals 25 Spatial Damage and 25 Mystic Damage per level. It requires a scale of 3 to be able to gain mastery points. The attack starts with three magic bullets, and gains an additional magic bullet every 5 Power Scale.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Limitless World - The user pulls themselves and all non-allied targets into their Limitless World. Within this Limitless World, the world prevents teleportation. The user deals bonus physical damage, increased by 7.5% every time they hit a target. After increasing by 30%, the user begins to have 500 Magicules x the Boosted Amount each time they hit a target. If the user has Nihility, the target will instead be taking Void Damage in place of the bonus physical damage. This will take 1/2 the damage dealt as the Nihility Price per hit.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Dimensional Rupture - While this mode is in-slot, the user reduces all Spatial Damage by 50%, can bypass Distortion Field and Fault Field and becomes immune to severance.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot]Nihility Supply (Requires Mastery)  - While the user can deal Spatial Damage to generate Nihility. Their Nihility can scale up to 10,000 points before the user begins to take 50 Void Damage a second until it is decreased below 10,000. The user can have a maximum of 25,000 Nihility.</li></ul>
