<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Controlled Order - The user cannot become a Chaos Being. This skill cannot be copied.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Parallel Processing - Increases the amount of Learned Points and Mastery Points gained when learning/using Skills, Magic, Battlewill, and Arts by 14 (15 Total). The user can also cast more than one spell at a time.</li>
<li>[Passive, True] All of Creation - The user instantly learns all Aspectual Magic. The user instantly learns all Recipes.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Chant Annulment - For Magic, casting becomes instant. User will be able to cast magic even when air level is under max or silence effect is active. Enchantment particles are also reduced.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot]Immortal Army - All of the user's undead subordinates gain Ally Boost 3 (6 when mastered).</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Alignment Change - The user can change their alignment between Holy and Demonic.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Legion Change - The user can change all of their nearby subordinates to Holy or Demonic.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Dominate Death - The user can hold this button and after ten seconds, the convert all Undead Entities with less than 90% of the user's EP (Players and Mobs) without a name into their Temporary Subordinates for 30 minutes (15 if you have Spiritual Attack Resistance).</li>
<li>[Active, Press]Release Death - The user instantly kills all Undead Entities under their commands, converting them to Soul Points. This only works on Players with less than 50% of the user's EP. (75% if they have Spiritual Attack Resistance).</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Create Undeath - The user can convert Soul Points into the new Undead Life, creating one of three new Undead Mobs: "Death Knight", "Imitation Lich" and "Death Reaper".</li>
<li>[Active, Press]Command Undead - The user can use all of the various commands for their undead subordinates.
<ul><li>Neutral: Will only attack whatever attacks their owner.</li>
<li>Passive: Will not attack anything.</li>
<li>Aggressive: Will attack all non-allies.</li>
<li>End All Life:  Will attack all non-Undead Entities besides their Owner.</li></ul></li></ul>
