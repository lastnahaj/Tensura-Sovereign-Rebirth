<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Spacetime Manipulation - The user obtains Spacetime Manipulation.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Amplification and Thought Acceleration - The user regenerates Magicules x4 as fast. 8x as fast on mastery. The user gains +4 Learning and Mastery Points. Increase magic casting speed multiplier by 3, increase movement speed by 0.02 (0.04 with Mastery), increase attack speed by 0.3 (0.6 with Mastery)</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Cardinal Acceleration (Only a mode if you have <a class="new" href="https://tensuranightmares.wiki.gg/wiki/Acceleration.?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Acceleration. (page does not exist)">Acceleration</a>) - When used shifting, the user fires a powerful beam that deals 250 Flame Damage (350 when mastered) and melts blocks when it hits them.</li></ul>
<p>When used normally, the user accelerators forward at high speeds dealing a massive amount of damage in a straight line. 350 (500 Flame Damage when mastered) + Attack Damage.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Parallel Existence - The user creates a Body Double Entity of themselves with sentience, their skills, and the same EP as the user.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Encore - This is the same as Endorse' Encore, but it increases barriers by 90% (180% when mastered).</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Stockpile - This is the same, but it can store up to 225% unmastered and 500% when mastered, and deals bonus 50 Damage for every 100% over 200.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Multidimensional Barrier - The user creates a Barrier with x4 HP (6x when mastered) as well as the benefits of Fault Field while the user has the Barrier Points.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Scorch Excitation - The user begins to massively increase the Magicules of all nearby allies in the area, they begin to gain massive strength, gaining a level of Ally Boost every 10 seconds, along with a level of insanity every fifteen seconds, and makes it so the user is able to boost their Magicules post their cap, increases it by 10% a second.</li></ul>
<p>When shift used, this is used only on the user, and instead works at Ally Boosting yourself every 10 Seconds and gaining Insanity every 20 seconds, with your Magicules increasing by 15%.
</p>
