<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Tempt Control - The user gains Spacetime Manipulation on obtainment of Azazel. When Azazel and Spacetime Manipulation are mastered, Spacetime Manipulation evolves into Spacetime Domination.</li>
<li>[Passive, True] All of Creation - The user instantly learns all Aspectual Magic. The user instantly learns all Recipes.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Solicitation - While this skill is in Slot, the user will be able to copy sub-skills similarly to Pride.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration - Increases the amount of Learned Points and Mastery Points gained for Magics by 4. Thought Acceleration (Like Normal) .Chant Annulment (Like Normal)</li>
<li>[Passive, In Slot]</li>
<li>[Passive, True]</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Multidimensional Barrier - The user creates a Barrier with x4 HP (6x when mastered) as well as the benefits of Fault Field while the user has the Barrier Points.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Charm Domination - Ineffective on targets with an EP over 60% (40 if target has Spiritual Resist) of the user’s Max EP. Turns Targets between 25% and 50% Neutral (5 - 30 if target has Spiritual Resist), and turns targets below 25% (5 if target has Spiritual Resist) Max EP into temporary allies for 5 minutes (10 with Mastery). Costs 80 MP + Target’s EP [Looking at Target in 5 block radius, Shift to undo]</li>
<li>[Active, Press]  Temptation World - The user warps all nearby non-allies into the Temptation World with the user. All targets are inflicted with Magic Jamming.</li>
<li>[Active, Press]  Solicitation - The user opens their Solicitation Menu, which allows them to use the modes of copied skills from Tempter, only in the Temptation Dimension.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Severed Realm - The user can throw an enemy into the Temptation World. This is similar to Infinity Prison, so if you miss, it goes on cooldown, after four minutes, the enemy is release from the Temptation World. (5 cd on failure, 25 on success)</li>
<li>[Active, press] End of World - The user collapses the Temptation Dimension in on itself, with a white flash. This deals 3,000 Spiritual Damage to all entities inside of it, including the user. This is reduced to 1,500 if your EP is within 50% of the user's EP. If your EP is less than 50% of the users, this will instantly kill you. (500 cd)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] End of World: Requiem (Requires 100 uses of End of World and Mastery over Azazel) - The user simulates the collapse of the temptation world inside of a target, It causes a massive explosion from the target and deals 5,000 SHP damage and 5,000 Darkness Damage. It instantly kills a Charmed Target or target under 50% of the user's EP.  (760 cd)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Punitive Domination - When used all of the user's nearby allies glow green, targets which fear them glow red and mind controlled targets glow blue. When the user activates this ability a second time, it will reveal the percentage of HP the targets are at.
<ul><li>When shift used, the user will kill all Mind Controled Targets and targets with Fear 4 or higher (so long as they do not have more than 60% of the user's EP) instantly. If they can resist this effect, it instead damages them for half of their SHP by half (25% if they have Spiritual Resistances). This doesn't bypass Nullification. (320 cd).</li></ul></li></ul>
