<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Unyield:
<ul><li>Once a mob is named by the user, it will gain 1 (2 with Mastery) Unyielding Point each 5s that it’s 30 blocks around the player, this will decrease if the mob is too far away. If a subordinate dies to a damage source not from the owner, the user will gain 10% of the sub’s EP for each 60 points (5 minutes) it has - The maximum amount of EP can gain is 100% = 600 points (50 minutes).
<ul><li>To gain the fallen Subordinates’ skills/magics/battlewills, the Subordinate needs to have at least 120 points for Skills, Battlewills, Magic, and 600 points for Unique Skills. 6,000 for Ultimate Skills.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, In-Slot] Life Domination: Natural Domain
<ul><li>While this skill is In-Slot, the user can absorb the magicules in the surronding air. The user's SHP and AP regenerate much faster. (4x faster by default).</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Hope's Gift:
<ul><li>When Toggled on, the user and their subordinates gain 100% crit chance and a 25% Dodge chance</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Life Domination: superior life force
<ul><li>The user cannot have their energy drained by an attack that has less than 75% of their EP. any draining effects on the user are cut in half(75% when mastered)</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Backup:
<ul><li>Upon activation, at the MP cost equal to 25% of the user’s max MP, spawn an immobile Body with all your EP and no skills at where the user is. When activated again, transfer the user’s soul to where the Body is and spawn a new Body where the user is, swap their Equipments in Armor and Hand slots (other slots are untouched) - Shift activate to remove the Backup Body. If the user dies when there’s a Backup available, automatically transfer them to that Backup (If KeepInventory is off, items in Armor and Hand slots of the user will spawn where they’re supposed to die, else those will spawn where the Backup is). Only works Multi-Dimensionally with Mastery.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Life Domination: Empower
<ul><li>When used,you and all nearby subordinates gain Resistance degrade, akk if the subordinates and the user gain Strengthened 5 and Resistance 2</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, In-slot]Fortitude
<ul><li>While this mode is in slot, the user's sword is coating in pale blue flames, and the user drains the life force of anyone they strike, to various degrees based off several factors. This art does have a 7 Second Cooldown.
<ul><li>If the target has less than 30% of your EP, this skill will drain 80% of their HP and SHP in a single strike.</li>
<li>The the target has less than 50% of your EP, this skill will drain 60% of their HP and SHP in a single strike.</li>
<li>The the target has less than 70% of your EP, this skill will drain 40% of their HP and SHP in a single strike.</li>
<li>The the target has less than 50% of your EP and has no Ultimate Skill, this will drain 99% of their HP and SHP in a single strike.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Life Domination: Reconstruction
<ul><li>While this mode is held, the user begins to regenerate their HP and SHP alongside their subordinates HP and SHP at 50 a tick. Every second held, costs the user 10,000 Magicules.</li></ul></li></ul>
