<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Thought Acceleration: Increase magic casting speed multiplier by 4, increase movement speed by 0.01 (0.02 with Mastery), increase attack speed by 0.2 (0.4 with Mastery)</li>
<li>[Passive, True] All of Creation: The user instantly learns all Aspectual Magic. The user instantly learns all Recipes.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Parallel Processing: Increases the amount of Learned Points and Mastery Points gained when learning/using Skills, Magic, Battlewill, and Arts by 14 (15 Total).</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Chant Annulment: For Magic, casting becomes instant. User will be able to cast magic even when air level is under max or silence effect is active. Enchantment particles are also reduced.</li>
<li>[Passive, In Slot]  Ego / Manas: On attacking or being attacked, ignores every Dodge, Crit Chance, and Dodge Avoidance abilities. This becomes Manas after you name the Ego. ( W.I.P benefits post Manas ).</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Alteration: Open the Skill Alteration Menu. The Skill Alteration Menu allows you to select a skill and evolve it forcefully, however the requirements must be met.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Integrate: When used while staring at a Body Double, the user can project the consciousness of Raphael inside of the Body Double. When the Ego of Raphael is inside of the Body Double, it will be capable of using the Aspectual Magics you know. When the Manas of Raphael is inside of the Body Double, it will be capable of using the Skills you know as well.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Report &amp; Converse: When used normally, Raphael will scan the immediate area, and detect any nearby players which aren't your subordinates. It will affect them with glowing, and assess their threat level by classifying it. When used while shifting, Raphael will speak to you, with a chance of gaining more mastery, when mastered this instead will increase the Sociality of your skill's Ego. When raised enough, you may name the Ego.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Analysis: When used while looking at a mob, copies 1 of the Magic, and Skills (Except Unique Skills &amp; Resistances) of a target. Can copy from up to 20 blocks away while in line of sight of entity or magic circle and won't work on any spirit related magic.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Refining: Opens a GUI that can be used to craft things, brew potions and process magic ore</li></ul>
