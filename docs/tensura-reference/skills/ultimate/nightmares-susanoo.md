<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passives">Passives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Certain Outcome - When Toggled, all attacks made by the player become critical hits automatically and when In-Slot attacks made by the player shatter all barrier points unless the target has Law Manipulation or an Ultimate Skill and has an EP more than 4X the player's.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Space-Time Manipulation - The user is immune to the effects of Infinity Prison and can bypass Absolute Guard. The user's Spatial Damage is increased by 3.5x. This doesn't stack with Dominations.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Action Alteration - The player's attacks ignore all forms of dodge. Learning and Mastering abilities grants +8 learning points. Nullification is treated as resistance and resistance is bypassed.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] True Chaotic Fate - When in-slot, the damage of the player's attacks reduce the target's max hp by the amount of damage dealt. Using True Chaotic Fate will undo all permanent damage done with True Chaotic Fate to the user, looking at a mob will do it to them. This attack will bypass all forms of defenses and if a target is brought to 0 HP, they are killed.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Multidimensional Barrier - The user creates a Barrier with x4 HP (6x when mastered) as well as the benefits of Fault Field while the user has the Barrier Points.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Minus Break - The user counters an attack, reflecting it back on the attacker to deal damage. Minus Break will apply True Chaotic Fate's max Health Damage.</li></ul>
