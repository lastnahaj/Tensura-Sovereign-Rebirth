<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Spiritron Collection - The user gains a new Energy bar below their MP and AP Bars, this containts the user's Spiritron Point Bar. This bar will passively fill up so long as the user's MP is at 80% or higher. The Spiritron bar will regenerate 5 Spiritrons a second (10 when mastered) and has a maximum size of 200 by default, 500 on mastery. If the user's SP bar is above 150, they pay 20% less magicules when needed.</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Interference Prevention - All spells can be used in an anti-magic area and the user is immune to magic interference. Magic jamming damage mitigation is bypassed.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Purified Attacks - While the user's SP Bar is at 80 or higher, the user does 40 Holy Damage (60 when mastered) on hitting an entity with physical damage. If the user's SP bar is at 200 or higher, this applies 20 holy damage to spells.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Holy Miracle - When toggled, the user will increase all Holy Damage by +75 (+90 when mastered), but the user 25 (15 when mastered) damage whenever they deal Holy Damage.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Chant] Disintegration - The user begins to chant the Disintegration spell, using 75 Spiritrons to cast, along with 25,000 MP the user uses Disintegration, which does 150 Holy Damage, 200 on mastery. Entities within 10 blocks of the user or the target will take 70 holy damage, 50 on mastery.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Melt Cut - The user coats their blade in Holy Magic, allowing them to deal Holy Damage by attacking an enemy. This deals 90 Holy Damage on hit, with a 1 second cooldown.</li></ul>
