<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passives">Passives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Evershining Glory - User gains extra learning and mastery points, immunity to severance, And gains immunity to many bad effects, such as black burn, Holy burn, Spin, Infinite prison, and more.</li>
<li>[Passive, In Slot] Offensive-Defensive Accord - 30% universal damage resistance, 50% on mastery. All attacks on the user are absorbed and added onto the next attack the user unleashes as double at the cost of 15 configurable magicules per point. This does not stack. (If you get hit by a 10 damage attack, the next hit will add 20, but if you get hit by a 5 damage attack, it won‘t be 30, it will go down to 10)</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Ever Victorious - User gains retains 1/3 cost infinite regeneration and attacks degrade resistances 1 extra level (if you have 0 degradation, it becomes level 1, if level 1, it becomes level 2). 75% dodge chance for melee and 100% for projectile and 90% dodge denial.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Danger Sense</li></ul>
