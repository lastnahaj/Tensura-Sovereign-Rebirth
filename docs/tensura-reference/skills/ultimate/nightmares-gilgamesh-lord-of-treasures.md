<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] True Golden Rule - Grants a permanent Luck 10 effects</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Enkidu - At the cost of 25,000MP, imprisons the mob/player in holy chains the player is looking at. Seals all movement and skill usage. Enkidu scales its effect based on factors such as Divinity, TDL/TD/TH, TDL and TD seed/Hero Egg. Enkidu can only be used on targets with equal or more EP. Enkidu has a base duration of 20 seconds, with having the Hero Egg or Demon Lord Seed increases to 30, and being awakened makes it 50 seconds. This has a cooldown of 60 seconds, with it being 10 seconds if you fail to use it.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] King’s Storehouse - Open a Spatial Storage for both weapons and shields Use Shift Press to swap between Weapon Storage and Shield Storage.
<ul><li>Weapon Storage -  This storage is exclusively for weapons, with each item individually placed without stacking. This storehouse fuels the Gate of Babylon. This has a maxium storage of 45 items, 54 when mastered.</li>
<li>Shild Storage - This storage is exclusively for shields, with each item individually placed without stacking. This storehouse fuels the Gate of Babylon. This has a maximum of 45 items, 54 when mastered.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press/Held]  Gate of Babylon - For an initial cost of 25,000MP and a continuous drain of 5,000MP per second, summon golden portals that can be deployed behind the users back or surrounding a target on mastery. Use the same function as predation mist targeting to chose the deployment. Using your scroll wheel will allow you to open more portals up to 12
<ul><li>Direct Fire - Small portals appear behind the user, firing weapons from the King’s Storehouse directly at where the user is aiming, each strike utilizes the damage and attributes of the weapons for damage calculation, however it is all dealt as Light Damage. If there is no weapon in the slot, it will default to doing 35 Light Damage (70 when mastered). When mastered, it will increase the damage dealt by all projectiles by 45.</li>
<li>Surround Attack - Deploy portals in a circular formation around a targeted area or enemy, allowing for a simultaneous barrage from multiple angles.</li>
<li>Shield Wall - Deploy shields from kings storehouse in front of the user based on how many shields are stored. This mode gives the user Barrier Points equal to the number of Shields Stored x 10.
<ul><li>This can fire a maximum of 45 Projectiles (54 when mastered) before going back on cooldown.</li></ul></li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press](Requires Mastery)Ea: Sword of Rupture - For the cost of 1,000,000 max AP summon Ea: Sword of Rupture.The sword has a unique damage attribute. The sword can not be placed in kings storehouse and can not be used on entities that aren’t similar in strength to the user. Ea can also use Enuma Elish as a beam on right click, heavy cooldown and heavy MP cost.</li>
<li>[Active, Press]Requires Mastery) Golden Mythic Armor - For the cost of 1,000,000 max AP summon Gilgamesh’s golden armor.</li></ul>
