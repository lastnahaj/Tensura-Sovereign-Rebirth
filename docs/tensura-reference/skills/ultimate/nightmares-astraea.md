<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passives">Passives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Spiritual Blessing - The user becomes Blessed and a Holy Being.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Blessing Creation - This is similar to the Gift Creation, however the user can produce skills from a larger list. Shift Use to open a GUI allowing the deletion of Blessings</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Blessing Bestowal - The user can bestow one of the Blessings they can create as a skill to a subordinate.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Divine Protection - This mode when in slot gives the user the effects of the Divine Blessing or Boon they made.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Sword Saint - While a sword is held, the user gains dodge chance negation of 50% and critical hit chance of 100%.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Sword Saint - This will engrave a weapon with Holy Weapon or improve the Holy Weapon engravement up to level 3.</li></ul>
<p>The additional Blessing obtainable are:
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li>Phoenix Blessing</li>
<li>Phoenix Next</li>
<li>Death God</li>
<li>Sword Saint</li></ul></li></ul>
