<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passivey In-Slot] Earth-Type Magic - The user gains the Earth Magics. Also boosts their Earth and Gravity Damage by 50%.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Earth Dragon Summon - The user holds down this skill for 1 minute (Goes down by 10 Seconds every 100 Mastery). After the duration is met, the user summons Velgaia in her human body. (When shift used, and the boss is added, summon Dragon Body Velgaia)</li>
