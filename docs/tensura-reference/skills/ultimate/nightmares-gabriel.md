<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Patient Demon - If you awaken as a True Demon Lord while in possession of 「Gabriel, Lord of Patience」, you will obtain the Unique Skill <a class="external text" href="../../unique/envy/">Envy</a>. [Bugged as of version 1.0.3.2.5]</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Status Quo - The user's maximum Health cannot be reduced by the likes of Severance or Cook.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Render Stillness - The user can walk over liquid blocks as if they were a solid.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Air Wall - The user produces a solid 5x5 air wall in front of them. This air wall destroys any projectiles that come in contact with it and cannot be walked through.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Time Control - The user can alter the flow of time in a localized 8x8 area around them, slowing time down by up to 50% (80% when mastered)</li>
<li>[Active. Press] Time Freeze (Requires learning) - Stop time in the localized area of 8x8 for all things but yourself and other beings with Time Manipulation.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] White Lock (Requires learning and mastery) - Stop one specific target in time for 30 seconds.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Heat Death - Instantly kill any nearby mob without Thermal Nullification.  Or any mob with less than 70% of your EP. Any target that survives is affected by Heat Death effect, which makes them take more Cold Damage and Spatial Damage.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Counter Freeze - For the next 3 seconds, if anyone hits you, initiate a counter. When an entity is countered, they will be unable to attack for 5 seconds. On mastery, the time for an entity being countered extends to 10 seconds. Apply Chill to any targets that hit you.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Eternal World - The user's Magicules and Aura are not expended while in Eternal World. The user's allies also obtain this benefit, and all regeneration (HP, SHP, MP and AP) is stopped in Eternal World, but nothing can die either (Left at 1 HP). This effects all things in a nearby 16x16 radius</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Snow Crystal - Produce a giant 10x10x10 ice crystal around you, any mob that touches it become frozen in place, this ice structure is unbreakable and prevents all damage from touching the user.</li></ul>
