<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Divine Blessing of Faith - The user's max Spritirons is 1,500. You passively generate 15 Spiritrons a second.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Divine Blessing of Light - The user's attacks deal 50 Light Damage on hit. 100 when mastered.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot]  Divine Blessing of Future Sight - The user gains access to precognition, allowing them to randomly gain access to the Future Sight effect for five seconds.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Divine Creation - The user can open the Divine Creation Menu to open a list of blessings and create any blessing they want as a skill instead of an in-slot mode. When they shift use this, they delete any of the blessings instead. They can have a max of 5 blessings at a time. (Check <a class="text" href="https://tensuranightmares.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Blessings">Blessings</a>)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Divine Gift - The user can give 2 blessings to each of their subordinates. If they shift use the menu, they may revoke it. If this is used on an entity, not a player, the entity also gains the sentient skill.</li>
<li>[Active, In-slot/Press] Divine Blessing of The Paladin - While this mode is in-slot, the user deals 5% of their Spiritrons as Holy Damage and reduces all Holy Damage taken by their Spiriton amount while in slot.
<ul><li>When this mode is pressed, it instead gives Holy Weapon 5 to the weapon.</li>
<li>When the user has Divine Blessing of Gathering Spirits, they also reduce all the Elemental Damages by their Spiritron bar.</li></ul></li></ul>
