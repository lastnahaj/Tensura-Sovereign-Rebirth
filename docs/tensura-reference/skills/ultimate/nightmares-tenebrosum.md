<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Soul Collection - The user deals bonus Spiritual Damage equal to 5% of their Souls on their Physical Attacks.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Soul Shield - When the user would die, the user can expend a portion of their Souls in order to revive themselves. This requires 5,000 Souls.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Soul Transmogrification - The user holds down this ability for 30 seconds to convert 100,000 Souls and 20,000,000 MP to make it so their Skill Storage Skill will not reset on Character Reset, and they will obtain it again at 2,000,000 EP after awakening into a True Demon Lord. This Storage Transference would carry over all Skills, excluding those on the Tenebrosum Blacklist.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Soul Storage - When in hardcore mode, this allows the user to open a menu containing the list of all of the user's dead player subordinates, and allows them to be revived at the cost of 20,000 Souls. They are revived without their skills, and on the revival, all of their previous skills are stored inside of the owner's Skill Storage. When the game is not in Hardcore Mode, this skill doesn't exist.</li></ul>
