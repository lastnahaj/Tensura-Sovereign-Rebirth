<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Believer System: The user gains +1 Mastery and +1 Learning for every subordinate they have.</li>
<li>[Passive, In Slot]Absolute Guard:  Blocks all attacks with a formula of C = A * 15. C = Cost, A = Attack Damage. Attacks from an opponent with 80% or higher the user's EP can bypass it.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Imprison: At the cost of 50,000MP, imprisons the mob/player the player is looking at. Seals all movement, skill usage and healing. Drains MP from the trapped target at a rate of 500 per 10 ticks. Lasts for 600 seconds. Increases to 1200 seconds with mastery - 15s cooldown - Goes on CD if you Miss (10s with Mastery).</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Absolute End: Costs 20,000 MP, launches a projectile that deals 350 (450 with Mastery) Damage and has a severance effect. Projectile is 8 blocks (12 with mastery). 3s Cooldown</li>
<li>[Active, Press]  Nova Break: Cost 75,000 MP, the user swings down with a sword onto the enemy, dealing both 100 Space and 200 Severance Damage. This has both its Spatial and Severance Damage increased by +1 for every Subordinate the user has over the enemy. This has a 20 Second Cooldown.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Imaginary Space : Open a Spatial Storage with the size of a chest with 9x10 rows. Items are able to be stacked to 999 instead of 64. Stores the energy (MP Cost) of attacks blocked by Absolute Guard</li></ul>
