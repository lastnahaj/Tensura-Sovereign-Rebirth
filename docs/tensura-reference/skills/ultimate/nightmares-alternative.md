<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span></h3>
<p>When this is Bestowed to you, u get one of the seven different abilities
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] Absolute Superiority:
<ul><li>The user reduces their Magicule Cost by 50%. The user's Magic and Unique Skills have their cooldowns reduced by 50%.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] Complete Concealment
<ul><li>The user cannot be analyzed or viewed through analytical appraisal. The user gains 25% Dodge Chance and Critical Hit Multiplier.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Dominion Bullet:
<ul><li>Fires an mental bullet at any target. Dealing large amounts of SHP damage based off factors;
<ul><li>If less than 50% of your EP;+ 2,500</li>
<li>If not a Hero Egg or Demon Lord Seed; +1,000</li>
<li>If not a Spiritual Being; +2,000</li>
<li>If not a Divine Being; +1,500</li>
<li>Max total SHP Damage, 7,000</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Enemy Identification:
<ul><li>The user can press this on a target, and it will reveal the target's unique and ultimate skills to the user as long as they do not possess more than x2 the user's EP.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Parallel Existence
<ul><li>The user creates a Body Double Entity of themselves with sentience, their skills, and the same EP as the user.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] Soul Protect
<ul><li>The user is immune to all Spiritual Damage, but this goes on cooldown every 30 seconds for 10 seconds. The user is immune to Mind Control, Insanity, Fear and Rampage (Unless they have Wrath or Satanael).</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Subjugation Conquest:
<ul><li>The user emits a powerful fighting spirit infused with the holy alignment. This works just like Sacred Haki's release, however it also does 50 Holy Damage (100 when mastered).</li></ul></li></ul>
