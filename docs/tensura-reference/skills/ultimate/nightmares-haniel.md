<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Evershining Glory - User does not lose health to spatial attacks and User masters skills faster.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Danger Sense</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Hostility Identification - Sense all nearby entities and traps.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Automatic Recovery - Infinite regeneration at 1/2 cost</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Accord of Energy - Nullifications and resistances are degraded 1 level. Dodge chances are ignored 70% and crit chances are 80%, guaranteed on mastery. 60% dodge chance and 60% projectile dodge chance, with 100% projectile chance on mastery</li>
<li>[Passive, In Slot] Offense-Defense Swap - When In slot, half of the damage the user takes is stored and added to the next attack. this amount is changed each time the user takes damage</li></ul>
