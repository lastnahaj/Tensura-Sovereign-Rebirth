<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Spiritron Collection - The user gains a new Energy bar below their MP and AP Bars, this containts the user's Spiritron Point Bar. This bar will passively fill up so long as the user's MP is at 60% or higher. The Spiritron bar will regenerate 10 Spiritrons a second (15 when mastered) and has a maximum size of 500 by default, 800 on mastery. If the user's SP bar is above 150, they pay 35% less magicules when needed.</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Interference Prevention - All spells can be used in an anti-magic area and the user is immune to magic interference. Magic jamming damage mitigation is bypassed.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Purified Attacks - While the user's SP Bar is at 60 or higher, the user does 40 Holy Damage (60 when mastered) on hitting an entity with physical damage. If the user's SP bar is at 150 or higher, this applies 25 holy damage to spells.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Holy Miracle - When toggled, the user will increase all Holy Damage by +90.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Chant] Disintegration - The user begins to chant and Disintegration spell, using 60 Spiritrons to cast, along with 25,000 MP the user uses Disintegration, which does 250 holy damage, 300 on mastery. Chant time is 5 seconds instead of 10.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Melt Cut - The user coats their blade in Holy Magic, allowing them to deal Holy Damage by attacking an enemy. This deals 90 Holy Damage on hit, with a 1 second cooldown. Becomes Melt Breaker on mastery. (+90 true damage above 500 SP).</li>
<li>[Active, In Slot] Disintegrate Coat (mastered) - if the Spiritron meter is above 400 and the user is in melt cut mode, the user is coated in Spiritrons. This deals 50 damage on hit as normal, but it is absolute/true damage.</li>
<li>[Active, Toggle] Spiritron Coat - The user uses their Spiritron points as sacrificed barrier points. Upon being damage, the user loses an equivalent to 1/5 amount of Spiritron Points to block the damage (if they have dont have enough spiritrons to cover the damage, it takes the rest of the Spiritrons to cover what it can) and an amount of Holy damage equal to the number of SP spent is dealt back to the attacker.</li></ul>
