<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Actor - The user can use this on a player to begin learning them. This is based of Learn Speed. When at 100%, you can shift use Actor and select the name of the player to become them. This also works on entities. If a player has more than 50% of your EP, you cannot imitate them. You gain their stats, name, skills, and such, but not their inventory. While Acting as a player, you gain their known arts as well, and their magics and battlewills.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Stage Crew - The user can imprint an imitation onto a body double or clone they possess</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Gallery Fake - The user shift presses to select a move to mimic from their list of imitated entities, and they can now use that ability without acting as the target.</li></ul>
