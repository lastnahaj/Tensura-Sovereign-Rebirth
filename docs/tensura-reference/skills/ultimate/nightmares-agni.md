<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] Flame Authority - When this skill is toggled on, it increases Flame Damage to x6. (Doesn't stack with Flame Manipulation or Domination. ). And bypassess Flame Attack Resistance, and degrades Nullification to Resistance.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In Slot] Blast Blow - The user coats their arms in flame. They instantly destroy any weaker being they touch, anything lower than 50% of the users EP or without an Ultimate Skill only needing to be lower than 60% of their EP&gt;</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Blazeball - The user begin to move around and rush towards nearby mobs as a ball of flame. Dealing 75 Flame Damage.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Maximum Blazeball (Requires 1,000 mob kills with Blazeball and mastery.) - This is the same as Blazeball, but the user is a 4x4 ball of flame and it deals 200 Flame Damage.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Blaze Wave - When held, the user begin to use a superior version of Heat Wave,  all mobs around the player in a 10x10 Radius take 50 damage every second.</li></ul>
