<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Daemonic Heart - The user and all of their Subordinates become Majins.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Tainted Growth - The user and all of their subordinates gain 5 to 9% EP Gain. (Split evenly between Aura and Magicule. The user degrades all Nullifications and Bypasses Resistances.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Villain’s Intimidation - The player and their allies have a 100% critical hit rate, 2x Critical Damage Modifier and a 10% Dodge Chance that cannot be bypassed,</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Evil Charisma - When activated, all Passive and Neutral Mobs within a 20x20 radius will be treated as the player's allies and will assist them in combat for 2400s [Negated by Spiritual Attack Nullification, reduced to 120s by Resistance]</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Soul Crushing Haki - All mobs within a 30x30 radius are affected. If their EP is &gt;= 75% of the user's used EP, it does nothing. Otherwise, gives 2 Fear levels for each 20% difference with the targets' EP. 50 MP per 20t. This also does 100 Spiritual Damage per second for every 20% EP difference there is.</li>
<li>[Active, In-slot/Press] Soul Destruction - When used, the user will instantly kill all subordinated mobs and converts them into Soul Points using the same convertion from EP to Soul Gain. The user will gain their Skills Resistance, Common and Extra Skills if the user's Soul Count exceeds 25,000. The user's physical attacks deal Spiritual Damage equal to 1% of their Soul Count. This damage caps out at 250.</li></ul>
