<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In-Slot] Thought Acceleration</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggle] Light, Heat and Spatial Domination - The user increases Light, Heat, Flame and Spatial damage is increased by 3.5x (Mastery grants Light, Heat, Flame and Spatial attack resistance/nullification degrade if the user has 800k EP. The user becomes immune to Infinity Prison and bypasses Infinity Prison on mastery)</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Will Control - Nearby Subordinates gain Inspiration 1 (2 when mastered)</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Thought Communication</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Thought Domination</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Hundred Petals Rush (Requires Haze) - The user becomes cloaked in Haze, and then dashes forward, the dashing attack causes black flames to burn the trail behind them, and anything in the line get hit by many slashes</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Prominence Acceleration - The user dashes forward, releasing their Black Haki and Flames to emulate that of a black sun. This applies Black Burn to all targets within 30 blocks, and everything in the 12x12 dash area is hit.</li></ul>
