<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Fallen Lord - The user becomes a Majin Alignment, however they take 15% less damage from Holy and Spiritual. The user gains the Extra Skill <a href="../../extra/nightmares-hand-of-creation/" title="Hand of Creation">Hand of Creation</a> and <a href="../../extra/nightmares-hand-of-destruction/" title="Hand of Destruction">Hand Of Destruction</a></li>
<li>[Passive,  In-Slot ] Phantasmal Style - Applies an effect that deals 2.5% of the target's spiritual HP (5 when mastered) to the user's attacks. The user also has a chance of applying a level of Drowsiness to the target, (15% Chance, 30 when mastered), up to level 8.  The user increases the Spiritual Damage they do by 50 for every level of Drowsiness the target has.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Inslot/Press] Fallen Edge - The user can shift press this to swap between Fallen Catastrophe and Fallen Strike mode.
<ul><li>Fallen Catastrophe - The user can attacks a target, and instantly kills any target that doesn't check 2/3 of the conditions. If 2/3 conditions are met, it gives them drowsiness 5, if 3/3 are met, it gives them 4 instead. Spiritual Attack Resistance or Nullification. Greater than 30% of the user's EP. Has an ultimate skill. This has a 15 second cooldown</li>
<li>Fallen Strike - The user's attacks deal 2.5% of the target's spiritual health damage (5% when mastered). This stacks with Phantasmal Style. Any target that doesn't possess an Ultimate Skill will be instantly killed upon their Spriitual HP reaching 30% of lower.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Held] Fallen Slumber - The user can shift press this to swap between Fallen Thanatos and True Sleep.
<ul><li>Fallen Thanatos - The target the all nearby entities are given Drowsiness every 7 seconds. Any nearby entities that have Drowsiness 10 near the user while Fallen Slumber is held, are killed.</li>
<li>Fallen Sleep - The user holds this button and dozes off, while held they're unable to move, attack or use any abilities, but begin to regenerate 100 HP and SHP  (+25% when mastered) a second, and they increase the user's MP and AP regeneration by 150x. If MP is at max, it will begin to create stored MP. If the user has more than 500,000 stored MP, they begin to regenerate the MP of nearby allies by 5,000 MP a second.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press] Fallen World - The User can Shift use this ability to swap between Modes.
<ul><li>World of Nightmares - When this mode is used, the user pulls all nearby targets into the Fallen World. While in this Fallen World, the user is immune to all Physical Damage, and Illusion Clones of the user are spawn around the victims of the World of Nightmares. When your Illusion Clones are attacked, they explode and deal 500 Spiritual Damage to their attacker.</li>
<li>World of Ruination - When this mode is used, the user pulls all nearby targets into the Fallen World. The World of Ruination prevents any of its victims from being able to heal inside of it. Inside of the World of Ruination, the user's Fallen Lord status also allows them to increase their Holy and Spiritual Damage Reduction to 50% and increase their Holy and Spiritual Damage by +50.</li>
<li>World of Creation - When this mode is used, the user's Magicule Regeneration is boosted massively, regenerating 15% of their Magicules per second.</li></ul></li></ul>
