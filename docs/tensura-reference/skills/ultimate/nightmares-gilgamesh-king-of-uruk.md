<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] Clairvoyance - This gives the user an additional Presence Sense 20, Presence Sense Raidus 20 and 10 Levels of Analytical.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Marduck’s Axe - For the cost of 1,000,000 max AP summon Marducks axe. With the unique engrave Size Control. The axe can alter its size at will increasing reach and sweep</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Mystic Codex - For the cost of 1,000,000 max MP summon the Mystic Codex. The item while held increase Chant Speed by 5x.</li>
<li>[Active, Press]  King’s Storehouse - Open a Spatial Storage for placing magic staffs and shields. The user can shift press to swap between the modes.
<ul><li>Staff Storage - This storage is exclusively for Staffs, with each item individually placed without stacking. This storehouse fuels the Gate of Babylon. This can store Low Magic Staffs, Medium Magic Staffs, High Magic Staffs and Staff of Slime.</li>
<li>Shield Storage - This storage is exclusively for shields, with each item individually placed without stacking. This storehouse fuels the Gate of Babylon.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press/Held] Gate of Babylon: For an initial cost of 25,000MP and a continuous drain of 5,000MP per second, summon golden portals that can be strategically deployed in various configurations: Using your scroll wheel will allow you to open more portals up to 16
<ul><li>Direct Fire - Small portals appear behind the user, off various spells at random, being Dimension Cutter, Fire Lance, Icicle Lance, Stone Shot, Thunder Lance, Water Cutter, Wind Cutters. The damage of them are either 100/175/325/400 based off if they're a Low Magic Staff, Medium Magic Staff, High magic Staff or Staff of Slime.</li>
<li>Shield Wall - Deploy shields from kings storehouse in front of the user based on how many shields are stored. This is the same as Treasure Lord Gilgamesh's.</li>
<li>Sky Gate (Mastery) - Summon a large portal in the sky and have it automatically target any non-allied entity in a large radius. Draws damage and shoots spells directly from the storehouse but all of the spells fired like Direct Fire has, has auto targeting.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press] Omniscient Omnipotent Star - Opens a GUI interface allowing the user to locate and scan any of the following: Player entities: Displays current EP, coordinates, dimension, and any Ultimate Skills possessed Cooldown: 5 minutes (3 minutes on mastery)</li>
<li>[Active, Toggle] Territory Creation - At the cost of 1,000,000 max MP, claim a territory of 128x128 blocks as your permanent territory. Within the territory the user and allies gain MP regeneration. ( There is an admin command which is "/trnightmare territorycreation &lt;playername&gt; remove" to force remove this )</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Melammu Dingir (requires learning and mastery)
<ul><li>Mode one - open a simple spatial storage similar to kings storehouse however, any weapon put inside instantly has its EP drained and stored and then destroy the weapon</li>
<li>Mode two - Fire off all of the stored EP as pure energy the more EP stored the more damage. Willfire all EP at once also required a short incantation prior to firing. Can only be used within users Territory</li></ul></li></ul>
