## In Tensura: Sovereign Rebirth

<span class="tsr-status">Controlled rewards</span>

TSR uses Tensura Skill Books as its controlled skill-reward layer. Natural Skill Books loot injection is disabled in the reviewed beta configuration; authored quest and boss rewards are added only after registry and detector validation.
