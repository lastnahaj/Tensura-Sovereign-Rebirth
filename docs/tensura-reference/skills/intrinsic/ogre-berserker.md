<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Grants an effect equivalent to Strength 10 and gives the user an additional 10 Armour Points and +0.03 Speed. It lasts for 3 Minutes and upon ending inflicts the player with Paralysis 2, Fragility 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes. Costs 5,000 MP
