<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Hold] - At the cost of 30 MP per 20 ticks held down and 50% speed, deal 2 water damage and apply poison on all mobs within a 10x10 radius.</li></ul>
