<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Consumes the held item if it's flagged as a valid target for the skill, instantly consuming the item without any delay. Restores HP and MP. (See List Below)
<ul><li>Consuming <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../../items/slime-core/" title="Slime Core"></a></span> <a href="../../../items/slime-core/" title="Slime Core"><span class="sprite-text">Slime Cores</span></a></span> as a <a href="../../../races/races-slime/" title="Races/Slime">Slime</a>, You are able to change your size. Shift to Shrink - Non Shift to Grow. Growing increases your maximum HP by 5</li>
<li>Consuming 100 <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../../magic/magic-ore-shard/" title="Magic Ore Shard"></a></span> <a href="../../../magic/magic-ore-shard/" title="Magic Ore Shard"><span class="sprite-text">Magic Ore</span></a></span> as a <a href="../../../races/races-slime/" title="Races/Slime">Slime</a> is needed to evolve into a <a href="../../../races/races-metal-slime/" title="Races/Metal Slime">Metal Slime</a></li>
<li>Gain status effects from consumed food(No mastery bonus)</li></ul></li></ul>
