<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - If used while possessing a body, leaves the body (which will despawn in 5 minutes to avoid lag unless its the original body) and its intrinsic skills behind and turns the player into Spiritual Form. While in spiritual form if the player can possess a target:<b>([Ineffective against target with <a href="../../../resistances/spiritual-attack-nullification/" title="Spiritual Attack Nullification">Spiritual Attack Nullification</a> toggled)</b>
<ul><li><b>Non-player mob</b>: At below 10% HP, Below 10% Spiritual HP, or below 25% Maximum EP (Relative to the user) - Reduce to 5% HP, 5% SHP or 12.5% EP if the target has Spiritual Attack Resistance toggled.</li>
<li><b>Player</b>: Require 2 out of the 3 optional requirements above. If possessed, the target player will be put into Spiritual form and will die eventually without having [Possession].</li></ul></li>
<li>When possessing a target, the user turns back into Physical Form with the target’s physical stats and it's skills.</li></ul>
<p><b><big>WARNING:</big></b><big> <br/>
<b>Upon usage of this skill, hand slot items and armor will be left behind on the old body. <br/></b>
<b>Friendly reminder to acces the inventory and take these items from your old body back.</b></big>
</p>
