<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Fully heals the player and restores their ep, gives them a Strength V-equivalent damage increase, 10 armor points and +0.04 Speed, and temporarily increases their max MP and AP by 2x. It lasts for 3 Minutes and upon ending inflicts the player with Paralysis 2, Fragility 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. It can only be used once a day (so 20 minutes cooldown). Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes. 0MP
