<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Default: creates a blood mist in a 10x10 area where the user is looking. Mobs inside the bloodmist take 10 HP a second and for each enemy damaged in this way the player heals 10 HP. The Bloodmist lasts for 30 seconds and if activated again before the 30 seconds is over it deals 100 damage to all mobs in range. Costs 500 MP and deals 20 HP in damage to the player on activation.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - Blood Ray: (Requires Mastery + 500k EP): Shoots a blood beam from the player that deals 50 magic damage every 10 ticks (40 block range) and breaks blocks on contact while held down at the cost of 10 HP and 10,000 MP each time.</li></ul>
