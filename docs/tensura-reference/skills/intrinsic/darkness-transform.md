<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - At the cost of 30 MP per 20 ticks held down and 50% speed, deal 2 Dark damage and apply Darkness Effect for all mobs within a 10x10 area</li></ul>
