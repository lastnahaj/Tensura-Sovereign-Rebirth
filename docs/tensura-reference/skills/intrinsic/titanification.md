<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active] <b>Titanification</b>: When activated, increases the players height by 6 blocks. Lasts for 2 minutes and grants the player +60 Attack Damage and +20 Armor (Speed, Jump Strength and Attack/Mining reach increases according to size change).</li>
<li>While active the player is completely immune to all magic attacks unless they come from someone with over twice their ep.</li>
<li>When mastered, gain the ability to choose size modification among [0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 blocks] increase while still keep the buff effect. Sneak-activate to turn off</li></ul>
