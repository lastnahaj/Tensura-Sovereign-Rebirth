<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - On activation, grants the user a temporary 2x increase to their max Magicules and Aura; 20 Armor Points; a Strength 4 Effect and +0.05 Speed. It lasts for 3 Minutes and upon ending inflicts the player with Paralysis 2, Fragility 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. It can only be used once a day. Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes.
<ul><li>Can't be activated along with the following skills
