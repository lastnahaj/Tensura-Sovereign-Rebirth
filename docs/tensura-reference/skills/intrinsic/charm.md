<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Ineffective on targets with Spiritual Null or with an EP over 50% (30 if target has Spiritual Resist) of the user’s Max EP. Turns Targets between 25% and 50% Neutral (5 - 30 if target has Spiritual Resist), and turns targets below 25% (5 if target has Spiritual Resist) Max EP into temporary allies for 5 minutes (10 with Mastery). Costs 80 MP + Target’s EP [Looking at Target in 5 block radius, Shift to undo]</li></ul>
