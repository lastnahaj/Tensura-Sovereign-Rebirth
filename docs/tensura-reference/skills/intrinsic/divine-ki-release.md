<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - Increases the user’s battlewill damage by 50 damage (100 with Mastery) and deals x3 durability damage (x5 with Mastery) on targets’ armor at legend grade or below when dealing Physical or Battlewill. If copied, cannot be used without Divinity. Grants Physical Resistance/Nullification degradation.</li>
<li>Can attack Spiritual Entities</li></ul>
