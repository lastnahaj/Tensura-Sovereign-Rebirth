<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>On Obtainment - Grants access to the intermediate level of Earth Spirit Magic</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - At the cost of 30 MP per 20 ticks held down and 50% speed, deal 2 earth damage and apply Burden for all mobs within a 10x10 radius</li></ul>
