<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Shoots a blade-shaped water projectile that deals 40 HP in damage and moves at 120 blocks per second and costs 10 MP. Requires the user to be underwater or have a spatial storage-type skill with water inside of it, which consumes one bottles worth of water on each use (1 water point).</li></ul>
