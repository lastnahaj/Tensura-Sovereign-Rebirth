<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>Release a shriek attack that does 20 sound damage (30 with mastery) on non-spiritual beings in a 15-block range (20 with mastery), costs 500 MP. Cannot be activated if the user has Silence or doesn’t have max air/bubble.</li></ul>
