# Common Skills

Common skills and broadly available abilities.

**16 upstream articles indexed.**

## C

- [Coercion](coercion.md) — Unleash a deafening roar in front of you scaring any afflicted entities.
- [Corrosion](corrosion.md) — Empower your attacks with the deadly effect of Corrosion, with mastery you can do this automatically.

## F

- [Farsight](farsight.md) — Focus your eyes and become able to see things far away.

## G

- [Gravity Field](gravity-field.md) — Weaken gravity around yourself to make movement easier or create a variously sized sphere granting previous effects while debuffing enemies.
- [Gravity Flight](gravity-flight.md) — Manipulate gravity to allow flight, your momentum from before will be continued.

## H

- [Hydraulic Propulsion](hydraulic-propulsion.md) — Propel yourself at high speeds underwater.

## P

- [Paralysis](paralysis.md) — Empower your attacks with the effect of Paralysis, with mastery you can do this automatically.
- [Poison](poison.md) — Empower your attacks with the effect of Poison, with mastery you can do this automatically.

## R

- [Ranged Barrier](ranged-barrier.md) — Place down differently sized barriers which block enemies in or out. Strong attacks or special effects can still destroy it.

## S

- [Self Regeneration](self-regeneration.md) — Speed up your body’s natural regeneration to increase your survivability.
- [Strength](strength.md) — Use magicules to strengthen your muscles.

## T

- [Telepathy](telepathy.md) — Give orders to tames when looking at them through commands.
- [Thought Communication](thought-communication.md) — Send commands to nearby allies and become able to have them attack players.

## V

- [Voice Cannon](voice-cannon.md) — Unleash a powerful roar decimating all weak enemies in the way.

## W

- [Water Blade](water-blade.md) — Shoot out a blade of water which flies in a straight line.
- [Water Current Control](water-current-control.md) — Manipulate water around you to propel yourself in any direction.
