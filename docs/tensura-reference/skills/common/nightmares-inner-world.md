<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Mindscape - The user enters their inner world. The dimension is seemingly empty.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Commune - When the user is inside of the Inner World, it can be used to communicate with those within there. When used outside of it, one of the those within will randomly say something. This is used to gain bond points with the entities within.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="True_Dragon_Interactions">True Dragon Interactions</span></h3>
<ul><li>Defeating a True dragon boss will make it drop a seed, when eatting the seed it will make a new skill mode apper with the dragon's name.</li>
<li>When their respective Sub-Mode is used within the Inner World, it will summon the true dragon which allows you to fight it to gain Bond Points upon defeating it. When the Sub-Mode is used outside of the Inner World, you will attempt to talk to your true dragon, which will give you a chance to obtain its innate skill with a low chance.</li>
<li>At 100 Bond Points, you can make a Shared Name with the True Dragon, acting like a Self-Name but costs you 150,000 MP. This can give 100 bond points.</li>
<li>At 250 Bond Points, you can obtain their respective uniques. (<a href="../../unique/nightmares-divide/" title="Divide">Divide</a> from Veldora. <a href="../../unique/nightmares-acceleration/" title="Acceleration">Acceleration</a> from Velgrynd. <a href="../../unique/nightmares-cessation/" title="Cessation">Cessation</a> from Velzard.)</li>
<li>At 500 Bond Points, You can obtain their respective ultimates. (<a href="../../ultimate/nightmares-veldora-lord-of-storms/" title="Veldora">「Veldora, Lord of Storms」</a> from Veldora. <a href="../../ultimate/nightmares-velgrynd-lord-of-scorch/" title="Velgrynd">「Velgrynd, Lord of Scorch」</a> from Velgrynd. <a href="../../ultimate/nightmares-velzard-lord-of-frost/" title="Velzard">「Velzard, Lord of Frost」</a> from Velzard. <a href="../../ultimate/nightmares-velgaia-lord-of-earth/" title="Velgaia">「Velgaia, Lord of Earth-Star」</a> from Velgaia)</li></ul>
