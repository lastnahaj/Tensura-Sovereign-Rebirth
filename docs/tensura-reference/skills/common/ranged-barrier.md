<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Creates a barrier centered on the user’s cursor on activation. The barrier acts as a physical barrier that needs to be broken to be escaped from. The size can be changed from 5x5, 10x10, and 20x20 with mode change, with the 20x20 only becoming available with Mastery. Cost 10 MP - 25 MP - 50 MP respectively.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="How_to_master">How to master</span></h2>
<p>By placing a Ranged Barrier around a mob, it will increase mastery.
