<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Creates a barrier that inflicts hostile mobs with a slowness 2 effect and grants allied mobs and the player a speed 2 effect. The range of the barrier can be changed by changing the mode, with a bigger range becoming available once mastery is achieved
<ul><li>The ranges are Self, 10x10, and 20x20. Lasts for 120 seconds and costs 50MP. Doubled to 240 Seconds with Mastery.</li>
<li>Can be removed by shift activating while looking at the field within a 15 block radius.</li></ul></li></ul>
