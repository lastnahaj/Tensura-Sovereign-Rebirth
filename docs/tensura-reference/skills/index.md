# Skills & Abilities

Browse skills by their formal type, with entries from TSR's Tensura mods combined into the same directories. A race-granted skill can still be Extra-class; its obtainment method does not change its type.

Individual skill pages include **How to obtain**. Source-described routes are distinguished from pinned-build checks, partial prerequisites, and unknown methods. An MP cost is not an obtainment method, and a registry entry is not proof of a normal gameplay unlock.

The directories combine entries from the recorded pack inventory with Nightmares' 1.21.1 reference build. Nightmares pages are marked **Server build match pending** until the installed release and configuration are confirmed. Historical skill pages remain accessible through existing links but are excluded from current directories, skill progression, and site search. Source-only or explicitly non-gameplay Nightmares entries are not listed as normal progression choices.

## Follow a progression path

Skill pages show **previous stages and prerequisites** and **next stages and unlocks** when a documented connection exists. A combination or awakening may need more than mastery alone; read every listed condition before planning the next upgrade.

- [Energy Charge](../battlewill/energy-charge.md) → [Maximum Charge](../battlewill/maximum-charge.md).
- [Magicule Attunement](extra/magicule-attunement.md) → [Magicule Resonance](extra/magicule-resonance.md) → [Magicule Dominion](extra/magicule-dominion.md).
- [Great Mage](unique/great-mage.md) → [The Timeless Mage](ultimate/the-timeless-mage.md), with its [awakening gates and altar ritual](../../ascension-and-awakening.md).

For resource fundamentals, see [Skills, EP & Magicules](../../skills-ep-and-magicules.md). Race-specific skills are linked from the [race evolution maps](../races/evolution-trees.md).
