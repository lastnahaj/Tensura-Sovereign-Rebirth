## Unique skills

<details class="ascension-entry" id="deus-sanguis" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/deus_sanguis.png" alt="Deus Sanguis icon"><span><strong>Deus Sanguis</strong><small>Unique · Vampire</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Passives

- **Night toggle:** Gain **Strengthen III**, **Speed II**, and **Haste II**.
- **Bleed toggle:** Melee hits apply **Bleeding VI for 4 seconds**, or **Bleeding X** when mastered.

### Bite

- Bites the target's neck, applying **Paralysis IV**, **Blindness IV**, and **Darkness IV**.
- Deals **20 blood damage**, or **25 when mastered**.
- **Cooldown:** **3 seconds**.

### Shift

- Become half normal size, lose **50% Max HP**, and gain creative flight.
- Reuse the ability to restore normal size and HP.
- **Cooldown:** **20 seconds**, or **10 seconds when mastered**.

### Health Drain

- **Held AOE:** Drains every nearby enemy's **current HP by 10% per second** as blood damage.
- **Mastered:** Drains **20% current HP per second**.
- **Cooldown:** **30 seconds**, or **10 seconds when mastered**.

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="trash-gamer" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/trash_gamer.png" alt="Trash Gamer icon"><span><strong>Trash Gamer</strong><small>Unique · Mastery and glitches</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Passive

While the skill is in your skill bar, every ability gains **+4 mastery gain**.

### Mode 1 — Duplication Glitch

- Duplicates one item held in your hand.
- Costs **50% of current MP**, reduced to **25% when mastered**.
- **Cooldown:** **600 seconds**.
- Configurable exclusions and extra safety checks block containers such as shulker boxes, bundles, and supported backpacks.

### Mode 2 — Buff Glitch

- Applies a random positive potion effect for **3 minutes** with a random **1–10** amplifier.
- **Cooldown:** **60 seconds**, or **10 seconds when mastered**.

### Mode 3 — Prodigy

- For **10 minutes**, set Max HP and current HP to **1**, dodge chance to **99%**, and movement speed to **+20%** while ignoring opponent resistances and nullifications.
- When it ends, Max HP and current HP are restored, **Paralysis X** is applied for **3 minutes**, and all Aura and MP are consumed.

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="sealer" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/sealer.png" alt="Sealer icon"><span><strong>Sealer</strong><small>Unique · Capture and stored power</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Passive

**Toggle:** Gain **+100 Max SHP**.

### Mode 1 — Seal

- Target a living mob within **16 blocks** while your EP is at least **1.5×** its EP.
- Captures the exact mob into a Sealing Stone dropped at its position.
- Using the stone restores that mob with its full stats, equipment, and NBT.

### Mode 2 — Suppress

- Seal **20% of your Max EP** into a stone.
- Right-click the stone later to redeem the EP.

### Mode 3 — Stored Strength

- Adds **+0.2 attack damage per use**, up to **+20**.
- The stored bonus resets on death.

**Awakening:** [The One Who Seals](ultimate/ascension-ultimates.md#the-one-who-seals)

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="bubble-majin" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/bubble_majin.png" alt="Bubble Majin icon"><span><strong>Bubble Majin</strong><small>Unique · Reflection and absorption</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Passive

**Toggle:** Reflect all basic projectiles toward their source.

### Mode 1 — Storage

Opens spatial storage similar to Gluttony.

### Mode 2 — Candy Beam

- Fires a forward beam that converts a target into candy when you are at least **2× stronger** than a normal target or **5× stronger** than a boss.
- The candy stores **10% of target EP** and all Common, Extra, and Intrinsic skills from the target. Eating it grants those rewards.
- Player targeting is controlled by a config option and is **off by default**.
- Does not work on the **Elemental Colossus** or **Ender Dragon**.

### Mode 3 — Regeneration

Consume **5% Aura** to restore HP to full.

**Awakening:** [The Evil Majin](ultimate/ascension-ultimates.md#the-evil-majin)

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="king-of-curses" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/king_of_curses.png" alt="King of Curses icon"><span><strong>King of Curses</strong><small>Unique · Cursed offense</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Toggle passives

- Multiply melee attack damage by **1.15**.
- **Reversed Cursed Technique:** Slowly restores health every **2 seconds** and stacks with regeneration skills.

### Abilities

- **Dismantle:** Fires a black damaging slash.
- **Cleave:** Sends multiple slashes around you.
- **Fuga:** Launches concentrated fire energy that explodes at the aimed location.
- **Malevolent Shrine:** Continuously creates slashes in the surrounding area.

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="imprisoned-jester" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/imprisoned_jester.png" alt="Imprisoned Jester icon"><span><strong>Imprisoned Jester</strong><small>Unique · Chaos and spiritual damage</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Passives

- **In slot — Shark to Shark:** **+20% magic damage**, **+20% physical damage**, and **+10% crit chance**. Mastery adds another **+10% physical damage** and **+10% crit chance**.
- **Toggle:** Reflect **25% physical damage** to the attacker.
- **Always-on:** Forces alignment to **Chaos**.

### Mode 1 — Chaos Spades

Fires spreading white flames in the aimed direction that deal EP-scaled Soul Damage. Scaling caps at **10M EP**.

### Mode 2 — Explosive Goodbye

- Can activate below **25% HP**.
- Explodes based on every point of missing HP and damages both normal HP and SHP.

**Awakening:** [The Unbound Jester](ultimate/ascension-ultimates.md#the-unbound-jester)

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="dragon-slayer" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/dragon_slayer.png" alt="Dragon Slayer icon"><span><strong>Dragon Slayer</strong><small>Unique · Dragon progression</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Toggle passive

- Ice & Fire dragons become neutral toward you.
- Ice Breath, Fire Breath, and Thunder Breath bypass enemy resistances and nullifications.

### Granted skills

**Ice Breath · Fire Breath · Thunder Breath · Dragon Skin · Dragon Ear · Dragon Eye**

### Dragon Essence

Each Dragon Essence eaten adds **+20 Max HP** without a cap. This bonus resets on death.

### Mode 1 — Dragon Infusion

Infuse a Dragon Heart held in your hand to gain **+20,000 EP**.

### Mode 2 — Dragon Rage

Gain **Strength X for 30 seconds**. **Cooldown: 60 seconds**.

**Awakening:** [The Slayer of Dragons](ultimate/ascension-ultimates.md#the-slayer-of-dragons)

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="eye-of-mind" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/eye_of_mind.png" alt="Eye of Mind icon"><span><strong>Eye of Mind</strong><small>Unique · Analysis and hostile gaze</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

**Related race:** [Gazer evolution](../races/gazer.md)

### Toggle passive

Adds **+9.0 Ability Learning** and removes it cleanly when toggled off or forgotten.

### Analytical Appraisal

Toggles a level-2 analysis overlay with a **20-block** range, increased to **30 blocks when mastered**.

### Analysis

- Attempts to steal one copiable skill from a living target within **20 blocks**.
- **Success:** **25%**, or **50% when mastered**.
- Copies only Common, Extra, or Intrinsic skills; skips temporary and `NO_PLUNDERING` skills and anything you already know.
- **Cooldown:** **10 seconds on success**, **5 seconds on failure**.

### Detrimental Gaze

- Applies Mining Fatigue, Weakness, and Slowness to a target within **20 blocks** for **60 seconds**.
- Amplifier 0, or amplifier 1 when mastered.
- **Cooldown:** **60 seconds**.

### Ray of Death

- **Held black beam:** **20-block** range; consumes **2% Max Magicule per second** and ends when empty.
- Damage per hit: `3 + cbrt(EP) × 0.5`; mastered damage is multiplied by **1.5**.
- Approximate damage: **3 at 0 EP**, **26 at 100k**, **53 at 1M**, and **111 at 10M**.

</div>
</details>

<details class="ascension-entry" id="great-mage" markdown="1">
<summary><img src="../../../assets/ascension/skills/great_mage.png" alt="Great Mage icon"><span><strong>Great Mage</strong><small>Unique · Advanced magic study</small></span></summary>
<div markdown>

### Toggle passive

Grants **Chant Annulment** and **+19 bonus skill/magic mastery**.

### Mode 1 — Create Tome

Open a menu, insert a book, and select any magic you know to create its magic tome.

### Mode 2 — Study

Look at a magic spell's incantation circle to learn and master that magic instantly.

### Mode 3 — Magic Chant

Manipulate world mana, spend **30 XP levels**, and learn one random magic you do not know.

**Awakening:** [The Timeless Mage](ultimate/ascension-ultimates.md#the-timeless-mage)

</div>
</details>
