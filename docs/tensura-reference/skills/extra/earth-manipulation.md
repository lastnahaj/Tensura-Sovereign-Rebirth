<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Extra_obtainment_method">Extra obtainment method</span></h2>
<p>Mastering 3 earth skills, the following skills can be mastered to obtain the skill:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="../../intrinsic/earth-transform/" title="Earth Transform">Earth Transform</a></li>
<li><a href="../../../magic/magic-earth-transform/" title="Magic Earth Transform">Magic Earth Transform</a></li>
<li>??? (No third "earth" skill has been added yet.)</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - Increases damage dealt by Earth related attacks by 1.5x. Doesn't stack with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../earth-domination/" title="Abilities/Skills/Earth Domination">Earth Domination</a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<p><b>Manipulation only works on weak earth blocks like Dirt, Sand, Gravel</b>
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Wall</b>: Creates a 4x5 wall of earth blocks (lasts for 1 min) in front of the user at the cost of 5 MP</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Wall Break</b>: Break 3x3 walls in front of the user at the cost of 10 MP</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Pit</b>: Creates a 5x5x5 hole in the ground at the cost of 20 MP</li></ul>
