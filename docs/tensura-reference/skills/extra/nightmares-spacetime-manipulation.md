<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Spacetime Infliction - Increases Spatial Damage by 4x.</li>
<li>[Passive, in-slot] Warp Shot - non-magic projectiles that are shot/thrown by the player (Arrows, Spear, Kunai, Sniper Bullet, Web Bullet, Potions, Thrown Item [from Thrower], Egg, Ender Pearl, Firework Rocket, Snowball, Trident) will automatically warp to the back of the looked-at target's head and shot at the speed of the original speed multiplied by the warp shot level. Sneak to not warp shot while shooting. Cost 50 MP each warp.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] Dimension Ray - Spawn a ray ranging 30 blocks that deals 50 (100 with mastery) damage per second to anything hit. Maximum held time is 3s followed by 10s cooldown.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] Dimension Storm - deals 50 (100 with mastery) Damages all mobs within a 10x10 radius of where the player looks.</li>
<li>[Active - Hold] Fault Field - While held, makes the user immune to all non-ultimate skill attacks that don't deal spatial damage or mental/spiritual damage.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] Space-Time Cleanse - Remove severance with a cost of 100 mp per Severance value. 3 second cooldown. Shift activate to use on others.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] Spatial Transfer
<ul><li>Mode 1: The user instantly teleports to a location up to 60 blocks away</li>
<li>Mode 2: The user instantly warps to the coordinates. (Cost 5 MP per block)</li></ul></li></ul>
