<summary>Skill information</summary>
<div markdown>

- **Toggle:** Increases MP regeneration by **5% unmastered** or **10% mastered** in the pinned configuration.
- **Obtainment:** Master [Energy Charge](../../battlewill/energy-charge.md), possess **Haki** without mastering it, and reach **20,000 EP**.
- **Evolution:** Master this skill to obtain [Magicule Resonance](magicule-resonance.md).
