<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Clone Creation</b>: Costs 10% of the User's Max MP and creates a Body Double entity with identical stats to the player (EP equal to user’s 10% MP) and copy set of gears (won’t drop) but with a permanent Fragility 2 debuff and passively regenerate 2 HP each second consuming 20 of its own EP. The entity acts as a friendly mob to the player similar to a dog and will attack enemies. Lasts until deactivated, it runs out of energy or is killed. Remaining energy is returned to the player</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Clone Control</b>: Crouch + Activate while looking at a clone to remove it. If activated while looking at a created clone in 50 block radius, switch body with that clone, leaving the original body behind, when this original body is killed, the user dies. While controlling a clone, the user will not generate MP and going without the original body in 50 block radius, the user takes 10% of both physical and spiritual damage. If killed or out of magicule while in a clone, the user won’t die and will be moved back to the original body.</li></ul>
