<summary>Skill information</summary>
<div markdown>

- **Ability:** Applies [Regeneration Suppression](../../../ascension-and-awakening.md#regeneration-suppression) to an enemy.
- **Effect:** Force-disables the target's **Self Regen**, **Ultraspeed Regen**, and **Infinite Regen**, making high-regeneration PvP fights contestable.
- **Obtainment:** Master [Magic Jamming](../../magic/magic-jamming.md) and kill **30 Vexes**.
