<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - Increases damage dealt by Earth related attacks by 3x. Mastery grants Earth attack resistance/nullification degrade if the user has 800k EP. Doesn't Stack with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../earth-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Earth Manipulation">Earth Manipulation</a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<p><b>Domination will work on stronger blocks like stone and deepslate</b>
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Wall</b>: Creates a 4x5 wall of earth blocks (lasts for 1 min) in front of the user at the cost of 5 MP</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Wall Break</b>: Break 3x3 walls in front of the user at the cost of 10 MP</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Pit</b>: Creates a 5x5x5 hole in the ground at the cost of 20 MP
<ul><li>Earth breaking skills break all ores excluding diamond, netherite and magic ore</li></ul></li></ul>
