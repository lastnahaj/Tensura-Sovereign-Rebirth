<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
Increases Water Damage by 1,5x. Doesn't stack with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../water-domination/" title="Abilities/Skills/Water Domination">Water Domination</a>.
</p>
<ul><li><b>When sneaking</b>: Shoot a water ball dealing 12 damage with knockback if in water or has water points in spatial storage (1 water point each) - Cost 100MP.</li>
<li><b>Not Sneaking</b>: “Breath Water” (deal 8 damage each sec) if the user is under rain, the user is in water or has water points in spatial storage (1 water point each 5s) - Cost 10MP each sec.
<ul><li>Can turn concrete powder into concrete on contact</li></ul></li></ul>
