<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<p>[True Passive] Faithful - The user's owner is always their God, even if they were killed by their God, as long as they possess this skill and have their Faith, their owner cannot be changed.
</p><p>[Passive, In-slot] Holy Manipulation - While this is in slot, the user's faith in their God improves their Holy Damage. This increases all Holy Damage by 2x. When this skill is mastered, it also grants the user Holy Attack Resistance degradation.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<p>[ Active - Hold ] Devotion
While this is held, the user begins to chant, this gains Mastery for Secret of Faith.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Faith Directory - Opens a Menu to display a list of all faiths, can be used to view the list of people in faiths and their roles.</li></ul>
