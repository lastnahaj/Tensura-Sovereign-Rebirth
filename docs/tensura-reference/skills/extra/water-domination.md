<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Increases damage dealt by Water related attacks by 3x. Mastery grants Water attack resistance/nullification degradation if the user has 800k EP. Doesn't stack with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../water-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Water Manipulation">Water Manipulation</a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[ Active - Press/Hold ] - <b>When sneaking</b>: Shoot a water ball dealing 12 damage with knockback if in water or has water points in spatial storage (1 water point each) - Cost 100MP.
<ul><li><b>Not Sneaking</b>: “Breath Water” (deal 8 damage each sec) if in water, in rain or has water points in spatial storage (1 water point each 5s) - Cost 10MP each sec.</li>
<li>Using on concrete powder will instantly convert to concrete.</li></ul></li></ul>
