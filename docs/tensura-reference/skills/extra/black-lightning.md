<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<p>4 Modes. 1 Art.  Anything besides Default requires <a class="mw-redirect" href="../molecular-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Molecular Manipulation">Molecular Manipulation</a>
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Default Power</b>: Consumes 500 MP and fires a large bolt of lightning down on their cursor, dealing 50 Damage. Lights blocks on fire and turns sand into glass in a 20 block radius. 5 second cooldown.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Decreased Power</b>: Consumes 100 MP and fires a smaller bolt of lightning down that deals 25 Damage. Lights blocks on fire and turns sand into glass in a 10 block radius. 3 second cooldown.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Increased Power</b>: Consumes 1000 MP and fires a giant bolt of lightning that deals 150 Damage. Lights blocks on fire and turns glass into sand in a 35 Block Radius. 7 second cooldown.</li>
<li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Blast</b>: Requires Mastery. Unleashes electricity in a beam-like radius up to 30 Blocks in-front of the user. Deals 50 Damage and costs 100 MP each second. Inflicts targets with paralysis II for 5 seconds on contact.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Death Storm</b> (Requires <a class="mw-redirect" href="../wind-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Wind Manipulation">Wind Manipulation</a> + Learning): When activated, changes the weather to stormy and any neutral or hostile mob within a 50x50 radius is considered a target. Every 10 seconds, every mob within range is hit with either a Black Lightning Strike or a Tornado automatically. Black Lightning deals 50 Lightning Damage and Tornado last for 10 seconds, moves the target up and deal 10 wind damage each sec. Lasts for a total of 60 Seconds and costs 5,000 MP. Stops if user is deep under a roof.</li></ul>
