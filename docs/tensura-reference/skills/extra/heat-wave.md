<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Projectile</b>: At the cost of 40 MP, launch a projectile where the player is looking. The projectile does 30 damage on contact and does 10 damage every second to everything in a 5x5 radius around it while in motion. 3 second cooldown(1 second with mastery)</li>
<li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Storm</b>: At the cost of 20 MP per second, all mobs around the player in a 10x10 Radius take 10 damage every second.</li></ul>
