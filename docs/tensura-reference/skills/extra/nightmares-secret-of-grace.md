<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In-slot]  Faith Management -</li></ul>
<p>While this skill is in slot, the user increases their Subordinates Prayer skill's spiritron generation.
(Certain skills made this a passive skill, such as; <a href="../../ultimate/nightmares-raphael-knowledge/" title="Raphael">Raphael</a>, <a href="../../ultimate/nightmares-asmodeus/" title="Asmodeus">Asmodeus</a> and <a href="../../ultimate/nightmares-sariel/" title="Sariel">Sariel</a>)
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Faith Directory - Opens a Menu to display a list of all faiths, can be used to view the list of people in faiths and their roles.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Priest Management - Opens a menu to display a list of your faithful, and lets you turn up to 3 into priests</li>
<li>[Active - Hold/Scroll] Spiritron Control - The user can gather Spiritrons much faster than through the usage of Prayer. The user is able to generate a number of Spiritrons equal to their Learning Speed x Subordinate Number per second. This is doubled if they possess a skill such as Saint.
<ul><li><small>(The user can scroll to modify the output of their Spiritrons. This permits the god to reduce their Holy Damage or increase their Holy Damage up to 4 times by scrolling them to 0% or up to 400%.</small></li><small>
</small><li><small>The Spiritron Costs of the Holy Magics increased or decrease by the percentage.)</small></li></ul></li></ul>
