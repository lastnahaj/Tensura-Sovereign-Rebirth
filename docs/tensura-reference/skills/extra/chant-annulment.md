<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - For Mastered Magic, casting becomes instant. User will be able to cast magic even when air level is under max or silence effect is active. Enchantment particles are also reduced.</li></ul>

<ul><li>Doesn't work for the following spells:
