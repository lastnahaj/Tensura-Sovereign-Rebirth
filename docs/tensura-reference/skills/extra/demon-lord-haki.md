<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Default</b> - With mastery, scroll while holding down the skill button to change the percentage of EP used for calculation. When activated 5% Speed (10% with mastery), all mobs within a 30x30 radius are affected. If their EP is &gt;= 75% of the user's used EP, it does nothing. Otherwise, give 2 Fear level for each 20% difference with the used EP. 50 MP per 20t.</li>
<li>[Active - Toggle] - <b>Coating</b> - Applies a buff to the users physical and battlewill attacks for 2 minutes, allowing their attacks to bypass Physical Attack Resistance (Nullification is also treated as Resistance) and damage Spiritual Lifeforms. 100 MP.</li></ul>
