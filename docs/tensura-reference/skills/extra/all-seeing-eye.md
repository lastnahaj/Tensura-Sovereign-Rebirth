<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - When holding down, locks the play into a 360° View, abit further zoomed out than regular 3rd person. While active, grants the player +0.02 Speed, +0.5 Swim Speed, + 0.1 Attack Speed &amp; Mining Speed [equal to Haste 1] buff, increases Presence Sense Level by 1 if there’s any and increases the block radius of Presence Sense skills by 10 blocks. When mastered, this becomes toggleable and gives 10% dodge chance, but if used by holding down, double the boost on speed and presence sense radius. Costs 10 MP per 20 ticks.</li></ul>
