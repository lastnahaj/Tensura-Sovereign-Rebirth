**Obtainment:** Granted by the **Gravity Soul Insect** race. The pinned race configuration lists Gravity Flux in that form's skill grants. This does not establish other learning routes.

## Modes

| Mode | Effect | Mastered effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fluctuate | Gravity Fluctuation I and Nausea I for 10 seconds | Both effects become level II; duration stays 10 seconds |
| Hamper | Burden I for 10 seconds | Burden II for 10 seconds |

Aim at a living target within **15 blocks**, increasing to **20 blocks** when mastered. The configured durations are 200 game ticks, or 10 seconds at normal tick speed.

</div>
