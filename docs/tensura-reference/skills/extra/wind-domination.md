<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Increases damage dealt by Wind related attacks by 3x. Mastery grants Wind attack resistance/nullification degradation if the user has 800k EP. Doesn't stack with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../wind-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Wind Manipulation">Wind Manipulation</a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[ Active - Hold/Press ] <b>When sneaking</b>: Shoot a wind sphere dealing 12 damage with strong knockback - Cost 200MP.
<ul><li><b>Not Sneaking</b>: “Breath Wind” dealing 8 damage and costing 20MP each sec.</li></ul></li></ul>
