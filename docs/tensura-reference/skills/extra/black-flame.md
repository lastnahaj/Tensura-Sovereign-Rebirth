<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <b>Coating</b>: On tools it grants an autosmelt effect (ores into bars, wood into charcoal); on melee weapons it grants a fire aspect effect; and on Bows it lights their arrows on fire. With Mastery, apply a debuff that blocks regeneration for 30 Seconds to the user's attacks. [5MP each Block-mined/Attacked-Hit]</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Default</b>: Unleashes Black Flames outward from the user in a breath/cone attack. Lights blocks on fire. Costs 50 MP per second and deals 20 damage per second.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Projectile</b>: Fires a Flame Charge-esc projectile that explodes on contact and deals 50 Damage. Costs 100 MP. [1 second cooldown]</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Hell Flare</b>: (Requires <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../common/ranged-barrier/" title="Abilities/Skills/Ranged Barrier">Ranged Barrier</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../../battlewill/ogre-flame/" title="Abilities/Battlewills/Ogre Flame"> Ogre Flame</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../flame-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Flame Manipulation">Flame Manipulation</a>. Requires Learning): Launches a projectile on activation that creates a 30x30 Barrier where it lands. The barrier is filled with Black Flames that deal 300(800 with mastery) damage on spawn. The barrier and the flames last for 60 Ticks. Costs 2000 magicule to activate. [2 seconds cooldown]</li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Limited Hell Flare</b>: (Requires Hell Flare Mastery): Launches a projectile that creates a 5x5 barrier where it lands. The barrier is filled with black flames that deal 2500 damage on spawn. The barrier and flames last for three seconds. [1 second cooldown]</li></ul>
