<summary>Skill information</summary>
<div markdown>

- **Toggle:** Gain **+3 attack damage** and **+0.4 attack speed**.
- **Mastered:** Gain **+5 attack damage** and **+0.6 attack speed**; HP drain is halved.
- **Cost:** Drains HP each tick and disables itself before the drain would become lethal.
- **Killing blow:** Heals **10% of the target's Max HP**, or **15% when mastered**.
- **Obtainment:** Bloodfiend race.
