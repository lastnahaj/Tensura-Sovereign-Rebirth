<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/> 3 Modes. Costs 1000 MP to use. Needs to be directly under the sky. 10 second cooldown, 5 when mastered.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Clear Weather</b>: Clears any weather and makes the sky clear.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] <b>Rain</b>: Sets the weather to Rain.</li>
<li>[Active - Press] <b>Storm</b>: Sets the weather to Thunderstorm.</li></ul>
