</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>When toggled, the user can cast any Magic that's mastered within an Anti-Magic Area. If the player's EP is above 1.000.000, their mastered magic and battlewill are capable of bypassing Resistances and Nullifications.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>Abnormality Cleanse: Remove any abnormal condition, Magic Interference, Severance or Cook’s Chaotic Fate effect of the user or looked-at entities.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Takeover (Requires mastery): When looked a magic circle being casted, destroy the magic circle and put that magic into cooldown of 3s for the caster. When looked at a Magic entity (field, barrier, projectile, etc) instead, takeover that entity and the user is now the owner of the effect, if used on a warp portal, force close it. Ineffective against players that have law manipulation and have EP above 75% of the player's.</li></ul>
