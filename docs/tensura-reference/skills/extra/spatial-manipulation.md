<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - Increases Spatial Damage by 1.5x. Doesn't stack with <a href="../spatial-domination/" title="Spatial Domination">Spatial Domination</a>.</li>
<li>[Passive - In Slot] - <b>Warp Shot</b>: (Requires Learning) - When active, non-magic projectiles that are shot/thrown by the player (Arrows, Spear, Kunai, Sniper Bullet, Web Bullet, Potions, Thrown Item [from Thrower], Egg, Ender Pearl, Firework Rocket, Snowball, Trident) will automatically warp to the back of the looked-at target's head and shot at the speed of the original speed multiplied by the warp shot level. Sneak to not warp shot while shooting. Cost 50 MP each warp.
<ul><li>When activated, grant the user warp shot level of 0.5. Press again to deactivate.</li></ul></li></ul>
