<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] On use, set the player's "Barrier Points" equal to their Max HP x1.5. Damage taken under the Barrier Point threshold subtract MP from the user at a rate of 5 MP per 1 HP. Attacks above the threshold reduce the barrier points to 0 and have their damage reduced by the amount of barrier points the player has.
<ul><li>Can be shift activated to use on another player. This will give them "Barrier Points" equal to their max hp x0.75, and will run off their energy. Cannot be used if they already have Multilayer Barrier active.</li></ul></li></ul>
