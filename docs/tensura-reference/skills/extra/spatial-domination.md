<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/> Doesn't stack with <a class="mw-redirect" href="../spatial-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spatial Manipulation">Spatial Manipulation</a>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Increases Spatial Damage by 3x. Mastery grants Spatial resistance/nullification degrade if the user has 800k ep</li>
<li>[Passive - In Slot] <b>Warp Shot</b>: When active, non-magic projectiles (Arrows, Spear, Kunai, Sniper Bullet, Web Bullet, Potions, Thrown Item (from Thrower), Egg, Ender Pearl, Firework Rocket, Snowball, Trident) will automatically warp to the back of the looked-at target's head and shot at the speed of the original speed multiplied by the warp shot level. Sneak to not warp shot while shooting. Cost 50 MP each warp.
<ul><li>When activated, grant the user warp shot level of 1. Press again to deactivate. (Doesn't stack with Manipulation).</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Dimension Ray</b> (Requires Learning)  - Costs 5,000 MP each second. Spawn a ray ranging 30 blocks that deals 50 (100 with mastery) damage per second to anything hit. Maximum held time is 3s followed by 10s cooldown (7s with Mastery).</li>
<li>[Active] <b>Dimension Storm</b> (Requires Learning Dimension Ray) Costs 50,000 MP, deals 50 (100 with mastery)  Damages all mobs within a 10x10 radius of where the player looks (20 block range) - 20s Cooldown (10s with mastery).</li>
<li>[Hold] <b>Fault Field</b> (Requires Learning) While held, (costs 2000 MP per 20 Ticks), makes the user immune to all non-ultimate skill attacks that don't deal spatial damage or mental/spiritual damage (MP Cost = Damage Taken * 50).</li>
<li>[Active] <b>Spatial Cleanse</b> (Requires Learning) - Remove severance with a cost of 100 mp per Severance value. 3 second cooldown. Shift activate to use on others.</li></ul>
