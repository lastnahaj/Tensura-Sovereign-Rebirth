<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
If activated and the player is standing on a block with a light level of 10 or lower, then grant them true invisibility, immunity to damage, and +0.1 Speed and +1 Step Height. This lasts as long as the button is held down and no attacks can be made nor can blocks be broken. While held down, the player will lose oxygen unless they don't need to breathe, ie Majin or Saint.  Consumes 10 MP per 20 Ticks.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Shadow Step</b> (Require Mastery): Instantly teleport behind the looked-at entity [Max distance: 20 blocks], costing 200 MP.</li>
<li><b>Shadow Storage</b> (Require Mastery): While looking at a tamed subordinate, save the mob into a shadow item that doesn't break to environmental damage except Void damage. [Only works on mobs that don't need air - monsters or majin, doesn’t work on summoned spirits]. Right-click on the ground again to respawn the mob.</li></ul>
