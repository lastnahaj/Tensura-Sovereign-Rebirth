<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
2 Modes.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Blink</b>: Teleports the player to where their cursor is looking.  Costs 10MP per block distance. - Max: 30 Blocks (50 Blocks if mastered). (3 Second Cooldown).</li>
<li><b>Warp</b>: Prompts the player to enter coordinates (X, Y, Z) and teleports them there after 100 ticks. Becomes instant with Mastery. (Costs 10 per block distance)
<ul><li><b>Portal</b>: Same as Warp but opens a portal instead of teleporting that other entities can also pass through and lasts for 20 Seconds. (Costs 50 per block distance)</li></ul></li></ul>
