<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - The player's MP essentially becomes their HP. Even if their HP reaches 0, as long as their regeneration isn't impeded they'll regen to max instantly - Costs 100 MP (60 MP with Mastery) per 1 HP. Also instantly regenerates all lost SHP - Cost 200 MP (160 MP with Mastery) per 1 SHP.</li></ul>
