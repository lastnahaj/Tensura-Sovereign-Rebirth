<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Sticky</b>: Throws a projectile that unleashes sticky thread in an area. Costs 50 MP - 3s Cooldown (1s with Mastery)<br/></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Steel</b>: Throws a projectile that unleashes sticky-steel thread in an area. Costs 100 MP. Costs 50 MP - 3s Cooldown (1s with Mastery)<br/></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Slinger</b>: Sends out a web projectile to the block the player is looking at.
<ul><li>If it lands, the web string will be attached to that landed block, the player can crouch to get pulled to the attached block, there will be no fall damage as long as they are below the Y level of the attached block.<br/>
<ul><li>The string will dissolve after 60s, when the length exceeds 50 blocks, when the player reaches the attached block or when the player shift activates this mode again.</li></ul></li>
<li>If the web projectile hits an entity instead, attach it to that entity and this will work like a Lead but the entity doesn’t take fall damage as long as its Y level is lower than the user’s, crouch to pull the entity closer to the user, the string will snap if it exceeds 30 blocks length (this will reverse if the target is 3 times bigger than the user or have higher EP than user). Cost 200 MP.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Arcane Thread Fetters</b> <i><b>(Requires Learning)</b></i>: Encases the looked at mob in 10 block radius (20 with mastery) in sticky-steel thread for 25 seconds.
<ul><li>Once activated again, during 5s (10s with Mastery) since encasing it, while looking at an encased mob, deal 30 (60 with Mastery) damage to the mob, remove the webbed effect. Costs 200 MP - 5s cooldown (3s with Mastery)</li>
<li>This Art is affected by Abilities that apply effects on attack like Corrosion, Poison, [Shadow Striker]'s Insta-kill, etc.</li></ul></li></ul>
