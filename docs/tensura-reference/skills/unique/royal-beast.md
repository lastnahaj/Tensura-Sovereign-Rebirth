<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - Fully heals the player and restores their MP and AP, gives them a Strength 10-equivalent damage increase, 20 armor points and +0.08 Speed, and temporarily increases their maximum MP and AP by 2x. Grants the player creative flight. It lasts for 3 Minutes and upon ending inflicts the player with <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-paralysis/" title="Effects/Paralysis">Paralysis</a> 2, <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-fragility/" title="Effects/Fragility">Fragility</a> 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. 20 Minute Cooldown [1 Day]. Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes. 0 MP</li></ul>
