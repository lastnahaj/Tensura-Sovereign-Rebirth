<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] - With <b>mastery</b>, makes the player immune to severance.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/thought-acceleration/" title="Abilities/Skills/Thought Acceleration">Thought Acceleration</a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Spatial Suppression</b>: Inflicts a status debuff to all mobs within a 50x50 radius that prevents them from using teleportation abilities for 60 seconds. Costs 300 MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Swap</b>: When activated, the player and the mob/enemy/entity that they're looking at swap places [Max range 30 blocks]. Costs 100 MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Spatial Motion</b>: Blink if not shifting (Costs 10 per block distance), Spatial GUI if shifting (Costs 10 per block distance for instant warp, 50 per for portal)</li></ul>
