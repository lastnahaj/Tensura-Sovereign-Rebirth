<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Instinctual Avoidance: 20% Chance to auto-dodge melee attacks. 100% chance to auto-dodge thrown projectiles (arrows, kunai, etc)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot in this mode] Danger Detection: Like Danger Sense</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active] Presence Detection: Upon holding down, boosts the user’s Presence Sense Level by 1 and Presence Sense Distance by 20 blocks. If activated while sneaking down, cycle through the available Presence Sense mode:
<ul><li>All: Like any Presence Sense ability - Highlights all mobs in sensing radius in blue, even invisible ones or ones under the effect of concealing abilities.</li>
<li>Monsters: Highlights any monsters in sensing radius in certain colors: [Neutral: Yellow, Enemy: Red]</li>
<li>Trap: Highlights any trap blocks in halved sensing radius in red. [Trap blocks: Lava, Pointed Dripstone, Tripwire, Sculk Sensor, Sculk Shrieker, Cobweb, Powder Snow, Trapped Chest, TNT, TNT Minecart, End Crystal].</li>
<li>Treasure: Available with Mastery, highlights any treasure blocks in halved sensing radius in gold. [Treasure blocks: Chest, Barrel, Vault, Dragon Egg, Ancient Debris and Magic Ore, Charybdis Core].</li></ul></li></ul>
