<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Abundance - Your Magicule Regeneration increases by x2 when below 85% Magicules.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Family Account - Your Magicules are shared with all nearby subordinates.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Unique Law Ignoring - The user bypasses Resistances and degrades Nullifications. The user ignores Barrier Points.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Predation - When used, the user uses a predation mist, similar to Gluttony in function, however with a different appearance. The damage 100 instead of 50.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Recovery - The user can remove any negative conditions, including the likes of Cook and Severance. They also fully restores all HP at the cost of 90 MP (45 with Mastery) per HP. Fully restores all Spiritual HP at the cost of 70 MP per HP (Requires Mastery)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Stomach - When activated, grants access to a spatial storage with the size of a chest with 9 rows. Items are able to be stacked to 666 instead of 64.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Snack For Later! - While held, boost your Magicule Regeneration by 5x (10x when mastered), if your Magicules are at 100%, you instead begin to generated Stored Magicules.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Family Bonds! - When used, open a skill menu of any ally, select any skill they have, and by both parties paying the MP Cost + The Stored Magicules of the cost being used, the user gains that skill. When mastered, this can be used to copy Unique Skills, however, only up to four.</li></ul>
