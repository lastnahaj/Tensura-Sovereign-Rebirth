<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Engorge</b>: When toggled on, increases the player's size by 1.5 times and grants various passive buffs (0.4 Knockback Resist, Knockback 1, 10 Bonus Damage, 2X Speed, 10 Armour, +1 Attack Range Boost). Makes the players hunger permanently fully saturated.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Amplify</b>: When activated (need Engorge toggled on), double all the buffs from Engorge for 2 minutes at the cost of 300 MP. If activated again while currently buffed, causes the user to dash forward in the direction they're looking and deals Current Weapon Damage + 50 (100 with Mastery) Damage to all mobs that they touch during the dash at the cost of 150 MP [Sneaking will half the dash distance] - Toggling off Engorge will stop this mode.</li></ul>
