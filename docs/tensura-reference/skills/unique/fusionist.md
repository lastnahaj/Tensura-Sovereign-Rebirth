<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Disassemble</b>: Breaks the block the player is looking at, but instead of dropping the block it adds 1 "Matter". Shift activate to consume the block items in hand instead.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Fuse</b>: Turns the block the player is looking at into a "landmine" at the cost of 5 Matters. When its touched by an unallied player/mob or when the skill button is pressed again, creates a TR Explosion the size of 25 blocks, dealing 300(500 with mastery) damage instantly. Costs 1000 MP.
<ul><li>When Fusionist is mastered, Fuse can be charged again by activating the mode again while looking at the mine at close range. Each charge increases dmg by 50 and radius by 5, costing 10 matter per charge with a maximum of 5 charges.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Stone</b>: At the cost of 1 Matter, throws a projectile that explodes on contact and deals 100 damage. Explosion level 6, damage around 77. Costs 200 MP. Can deal the same damage even to enemies hiding behind blocks.</li></ul>
