<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Pasive">Pasive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Pasive, Toggle] Space Breaker - The user reduces Spatial Damage by 10% (20% when mastered). The user's adds Spatial Damage equal to 0.5x the attack damage of the user. This doesn't stack with Magic Aura.</li>
<li>[Pasive, In-SLot] Power Break - While this skill is in-slot, the user can push skills over their maximum powerscale for Battlewills and Magics by 2. When mastered, it is by 4.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Bullet Break - The user can charge a Magic Bullet and fire it at a target. It cost 50 AP and 50 MP per 1.5 seconds and has a maximum scale of 10 (This can be effected by Power Break). It deals 25 Spatial Damage and 25 Mystic Damage per level. It requires a scale of 3 to be able to gain mastery points.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Limitless Space - The user creates a virtual zone around the user which nullifies teleportation. In this Limitless Space, the user's physical damage increases by 5% every time they land a blow on a target. Every time a blow is landed above a 15% Damage Boost, the user expends 500 Magicules x the amount boosted. The Limitless Space is a 48x48 Block Radius that last for three minutes. (6 when mastered.)</li></ul>
