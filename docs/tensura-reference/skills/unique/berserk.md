<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Flame Aura</b>: Adds Flame Damage to the player's physical and aura attacks equal to 50% of their base damage. Costs 200 MP per attack. Increases Flame Damage by 2x.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Rage</b>: Grants the player <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-strengthen/" title="Effects/Strengthen">Strengthen</a> 10 (20 with Mastery) buff for 5 Minutes on activation. Costs 300 MP</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Mad Ogre</b>: Grants the player a strength 15 (30 with Mastery) buff (doesn't stack with rage), doubles their size (+2 reach &amp; +1 step height), gives them +0.4 (0.8 with Mastery) Knockback Resistance, +0.04 Speed, and gives them 20 (40 with Mastery) armors for 10 minutes. During the duration the player is inflicted with the <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-rampage/" title="Effects/Rampage">Rampage</a> Debuff. After the duration gives the player <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-fragility/" title="Effects/Fragility">Fragility</a> 2, weakness 2, and <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-paralysis/" title="Effects/Paralysis">Paralysis</a> 2 for 10 minutes. Costs 5,000 MP.
<ul><li>With Mastery, 6 spheres of fire spawn behind the player and on activation again, one will be sent flying towards where the player is looking, exploding and dealing 100 damage. When sneaking, the player can no longer send the orbs flying, but direct attacks from others landing on the player will trigger an orb to explode instantly and halve the damage that the player would take. The Flame Orbs will regenerate 20 seconds after being used in any way.</li></ul></li></ul>
