<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Opens a GUI with access to a Spatial Storage. Stackable items in here stack to 128.</li>
<li>Has a slot that instantly smelts entire stacks of objects.</li>
<li>Has a built in crafting table and a custom enchanting table allowing the player to freely add whatever they wish from the list of analyzed enchantments.</li>
<li>Enchanted books have their effects stored to be selected in the enchantment menu.</li>
<li>Maximum bonus level is 1
<ul><li><b>Gain Mastery by Enchanting or Learning Enchants (Should take 800 enchants/enchanted items to master, without skills that increase mastery gained)</b></li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Once mastered, the user automatically gains <a class="mw-redirect" href="../godly-craftsman/" title="Abilities/Skills/Godly Craftsman">Godly Craftsman</a>, reducing their magicules accordingly.
</p>
