<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] When used while not looking at a target, remove all negative status effects from the user and restore their hp to full including Severance damage. When looking at a mob, remove all their negative status effects and restores their hp to full. Costs 60 MP (40 with Mastery) each healed HP and 100 MP each effect level removed.</li></ul>
