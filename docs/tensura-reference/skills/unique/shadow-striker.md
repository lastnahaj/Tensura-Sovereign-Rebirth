<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/thought-acceleration/" title="Abilities/Skills/Thought Acceleration"><b>Thought Acceleration</b></a></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] <b>Instakill</b>: Makes the player's attack deal 500 (Configurable) Spiritual Damage (1000 Mastered, Configurable) at the cost of 3000 MP (Configurable), 10s Cooldown (Configurable) (5s Mastered, Configurable)
<ul><li><b>Must be in Instakill Mode for Effect to be active!!!</b></li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Ultra Acceleration</b>: Moves the player to where they're looking at high speed, effectively teleportation, 10 block radius, deal crit damage (+70 with Mastery) to anything on the way. Costs 100 AP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Espionage</b>: Grants presence concealment 1(2 with mastery) which makes the player invisible and immune to being sensed by <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../../magic/magic-sense/" title="Abilities/Skills/Magic Sense">Magic Sense</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/sense-heat-source/" title="Abilities/Skills/Sense Heat Source">Sense Heat Source</a>, and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/sense-soundwave/" title="Abilities/Skills/Sense Soundwave">Sense Soundwave</a>. Becomes toggleable with mastery.</li></ul>
