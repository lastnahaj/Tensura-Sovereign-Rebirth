<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In-Slot] Joy In Service - The user halves all Spiritual Damage taken. While the user has the Insanity Effect, any mobs killed will give the user 1 Sacrifice Point.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Insanity - The user gains Insanity 3.</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Greater Good (requires mastery) - The user's subordinates when willed by a source outside of the user, gives the user of this skill 15% of their EP.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Praise - The user will damage them selves for 10 HP and 20 SHP. This will generate 4 Sacrifice Points.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Carnage - The user's Sacrifice Points are used to deal Spiritual Damage on hit at a 2:1 Ratio. This as a configurable cap.</li></ul>
