<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] Scorching Flames - This increases Flame Damage by +25 (50 when mastered).</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Burning Breath - The user fires a beam of scalding flames so compressed it resembles a beam of light. This beam deals 25 (50 when mastered) Flame and 50 Spiritual Damage (100 Spiritual Damage when mastered)</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Bloody Lava - The user begins to emite a massive burning aura that begins to boil the ground and turn it to lava. It deals 50 Flame Damage all around the user.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Superheating - The user begins to generate Magicules much faster (x4 faster / 8x mastered) and can hold up to 256% of their MP as Max Magicules. On mastery the user becomes immune to Magicule Poisoning.</li></ul>
