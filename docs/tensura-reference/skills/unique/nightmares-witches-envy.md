<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Witch's Gaze - This skill cannot be copied.</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Return By Death -  You cant die from physical dmg (act like instant regen at 1hp and by-pass any sure kill hit or anti « counter death » ability)
<ul><li>The user has a "Savepoint" created at the last locate they slept and any time they would die, they would instead "respawn" at the savepoint, only losing EP equal to the difference between the Save Point and the EP at "Death", but retaining skills.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Learning By Death - You gain 1 Learning and Mastery points each time you die, up to a max of 20. 1 Minute Cooldown between stack gain.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-slot] Witch's Curse - This applies a stack of the Witches Curse Effect to the attacker for each time they'd kill the user of this skill, the accumulation of stacks is limited to 5 (10 with mastery). On mastery, this has a 10% Chance to apply the Witches Curse effect each time they damage the user.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Witch’s wrath - Allows you to consume the stacks applied by the witch's curse, each stack deals 10 Corrosion Damage and Spiritual Damage (25 when mastered).</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Envious Life - Allows the user to copy any of the other Sin Factor skills by killing the wielder of the skill. ( This also lets them copy Extra Skills and Common Skills)</li></ul>
