<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Divine Release</b>: Grants an effect equivalent to Strength 20, gives the user an additional 20 Armour Points, and +0.06 Speed. It lasts for 3 Minutes and upon ending inflicts the player with <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-paralysis/" title="Effects/Paralysis">Paralysis</a> 2, <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-fragility/" title="Effects/Fragility">Fragility</a> 2, and Weakness 2 for 10 Minutes. Mastery increases the duration to 6 Minutes  and double the stat boost. During the duration, all damage taken is treated as physical damage. Every battlewill attack the user does in this mode will deal x6 damage (x8 with Mastery). Costs 10,000 MP and deals 10HP in damage every second while active. [Activate again to turn off the effect but still gets the debuff and cooldown]</li></ul>
