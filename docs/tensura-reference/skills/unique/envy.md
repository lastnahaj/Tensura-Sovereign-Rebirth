<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Jealous Luck</b> : 10% auto-dodge chance, Luck III, immune to Weakness and Slowness.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Absorb</b>:
<ul><li><b>Requirement:</b> Can only be activated on a target with a higher EP than the player.</li>
<li><b>Success Rate:</b>
<ul><li>User EP &lt;10% of target EP → 10%</li>
<li>User EP 10–20% of target EP → 20%</li>
<li>User EP &gt;20% of target EP → 30%</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Effect:</b>
<ul><li>Attempts to reduce the target's EP to the user's current EP.</li>
<li>The difference is returned to the user.</li>
<li>50% of absorbed EP → <b>Maximum EP</b></li>
<li>Remaining 50% → <b>temporary MP/AP</b></li>
<li>Maximum absorption: <b>1,000,000 EP</b></li></ul></li>
<li><b>Effectiveness:</b>
<ul><li>60% chance → absorbs half of available EP</li>
<li>40% chance → full effect</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Cooldown:</b> 30s (10s on Mastery)</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Strength Sap</b>:
<ul><li>30×30 block radius around the user. Can be resisted if the user's magicules are ≥60% of the target's (≥80% with mastery).</li>
<li><b>Effect:</b>
<ul><li>Applies <b>Weakness</b> and <b>Slowness</b> for 10s.</li>
<li>Debuff level is based on the user's magicules relative to the target's:
<ul><li>50–60% → Level 1</li>
<li>40–50% → Level 2</li>
<li>30% and below → Level 6</li></ul></li>
<li>Drains MP/AP from affected entities at 0.1% per second. (Drained MP/AP does not go to the user.)</li>
<li>20% chance to increase the user's <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-insanity/" title="Effects/Insanity">Insanity</a> by 1 level every 10s. (Increased Insanity level lasts 12s.)</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Cost:</b> 1,000 magicules/s</li></ul></li></ul>
