<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Fractured Mind - The user Insanity at level 3, this becomes level 5 when mastered.</li>
<li>[Passive, In-SLot] Hysterical Strength - The user gains a level of Strengthened for each level of Insanity they have. This is made to be two levels of Strengthened when mastered.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Lucid Mind - The user can decrease their Insanity by 1 Level to restore 50 HP and 100 SHP. (100 and 200 on mastery). When used while shifting, will consume all levels of Insanity and heal based off the Level, using the same ratio above.</li>
<li>[Active, In-Slot] Induce Delirium - The user's attacks have a chance of apply Delirium to the target on hit, so long as the user's Insanity is at 1 or higher.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Psychosis - The user releases their aura, and all surrounding creatures will slowly gain a level of insanity every 30 seconds.</li></ul>
