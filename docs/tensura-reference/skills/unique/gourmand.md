<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>[Passive - Toggle]</b> — When enabled, increases the amount of permanent EP gained from killing a target by <b>3%</b> for both Aura and Magicules (<b>6% total</b>). At Mastery, this bonus increases to <b>7.5%</b> each (<b>15% total</b>).</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>[Passive - In Slot]</b> — Grants the user's attacks a <b>50% chance</b> (<b>75%</b> with Mastery) to steal <b>1%</b> of the target's current MP and restore that amount to the user.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>[Active - Press]</b> — Instantly kills a target if they either:
<ul><li>Are affected by <b>Fear V</b> or higher.</li>
<li>Have current EP (not maximum EP) below <b>10%</b> of the user's current EP.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>Upon a successful execution, the user restores EP equal to the target's current EP, split evenly between Aura Power (AP) and Magicule Power (MP) (<b>50% AP / 50% MP</b>).</li></ul>
<ul><li>This effect can increase AP and MP beyond <b>100%</b>. Exceeding 100% may cause negative effects, such as <b>Insanity</b> from excess Aura or <b>Magicule Poisoning</b> from excess Magicules.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Range: <b>3.5 blocks</b> directly in front of the user.</li></ul>
