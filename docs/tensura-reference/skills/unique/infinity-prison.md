<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] - <b>Absolute Guard</b> : Blocks all attacks with a formula of C = A * 25. C = Cost, A = Attack Damage. Attacks from an opponent with 75% or higher the user's EP can bypass it.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Imprison</b>: At the cost of <b>50,000MP + 50% of the Targets EP</b>, imprisons the mob/player the player is looking at at a range of 30 blocks. Seals all movement, skill usage and healing. Drains MP from the trapped target at a rate of <b>500 per 10 ticks</b>. Lasts for <b>300 seconds</b>. Increases to <b>600 seconds with mastery</b> - 20s cooldown - Goes on CD if you Miss (10s with Mastery).
<ul><li>50,000 MP + 250,000 EP = 300,000 Total MP Cost (Configurable)</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Imaginary Space</b> : Open a Spatial Storage with the size of a chest with 9x10 rows. Items are able to be stacked to 999 instead of 64. Stores the energy (MP Cost) of attacks blocked by Absolute Guard</li></ul>
