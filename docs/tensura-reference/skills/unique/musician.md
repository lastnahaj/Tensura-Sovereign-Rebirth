<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/sense-soundwave/" title="Abilities/Skills/Sense Soundwave"><b>Sense Soundwave</b></a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Sonic Blast</b>: Fires a sonic boom that deals 75 (150 with Mastery) damage ignoring armor at the cost of 50 MP - 1s Cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Sound Wave</b>: Deals 40 damage (70 with mastery) to everything in a 10x10 radius ignoring armor at the cost of 100 MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Mind Requiem</b> (Requires Mastery): Fires a sonic boom that does 150 Damage and 100 Spiritual Damage ignoring armor at the cost of 200 MP, this move is able to damage spiritual beings unlike the other 2 modes. Cooldown 3s.</li></ul>
