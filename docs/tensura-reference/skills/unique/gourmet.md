<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Predation</b> : On use, creates a black mist (breath) in a 2 block radius in front of the player. If it comes into contact with a mob, deals 10 damage and drains 100MP, with a 10% chance to gain one random <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a>, or <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Resistances" title="Category:Resistances">Resist</a> skill from the target. If an enemy is killed by Predation, gains all of their <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a>, and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Resistances" title="Category:Resistances">Resist</a> skills. With Mastery, the mist is capable of eating projectiles. Targets killed have 30% (Configurable) of their ep added to the players instead of the usual 5%<sup class="reference" id="cite_ref-1"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>a<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. The other 50% will be added to Stomach's energy storage. Shift-activate to toggled on/off consume-block-mode, which will automatically be added to Stomach. (EP absorb only applies for the first target of that type)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Corrosion</b>: When activated while looking at nothing, costs 200 MP and deals 5 corrosion damage every 10 ticks to all enemies in a 10x10 radius. Enemies killed by this have their <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>, and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a> Skills taken by the player as well as have 30% (Configurable) of their ep added to the player's maximum.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Stomach</b> : When activated, grants access to a spatial storage with the size of a chest with 7 rows. Items are able to be stacked to 200 instead of 64.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Receive</b>: When activated while looking at a subordinate: <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Resistances" title="Category:Resistances">Resistances</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a> and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a> skills owned by it are given to the player.</li></ul>
