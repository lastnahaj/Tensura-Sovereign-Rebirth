<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] - The player's attacks have a 50% chance to seize 1% of the enemies MP, which restores the player's expended MP.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Rob</b>: Only works on opponents with an EP higher than the user. <b>Steals</b> 1 of their skills (Only <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills"> Common</a> and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills"> Extra</a> skills) or Aspectual Magics. 25% chance at success, which raises to 50% with Mastery. Costs 1,000 MP each attempt - 10s cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Copy</b>: <b>Copies</b> 1 of their skills (Only <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills"> Common</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills"> Extra</a>, and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills"> Intrinsic</a> skills) or Aspectual Magics of a target. 25% chance at success, which raises to 50% with Mastery. Costs 1,000 MP each attempt - 10s cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Force Takeover</b> (Requires Mastery): Works like Rob, but works on targets weaker than the user and has a 100% success rate. When used on a summon owned by someone with 75% the user's EP or lower, <b>steal</b> that summon. Costs 5,000 MP - 10s cooldown.</li></ul>
