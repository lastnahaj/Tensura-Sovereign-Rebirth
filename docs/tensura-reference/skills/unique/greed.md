<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Spiritual Domination</b>: When held down and looking at a mob, put it under control after a certain amount of time passes. Base Value = 60 seconds. Being in a distance of 5 blocks decreases base value by 30 seconds and being at a distance over 20 blocks increases the value by 30 seconds [Max distance is 30 blocks]. When used against a mob rather than a player, it increases value by another 30 seconds. When used against players, it decreases the time by 1 second per 1 desire value. 1 desire value = 10 villager trades. Control time decreased by 2 seconds for each trade a trader mob has. [You must be looking at the target for the whole time or the timer will go down; Shift activate can release/free the controlled target]</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Toggle] <b>Greed Flare</b>: Must be sneaking to deactivate
<ul><li>If used while looking at nothing, grants the player a buff that increases the damage of their physical attacks equal to 5% (10% with mastery) of their current spiritual hp, 20s (10s with mastery) cooldown. [Activate again to turn off, consume 10 mp * boosted damage each attack]</li>
<li>If used while looking at an ally within 20 blocks, grant the buff to them instead, equal to 2.5% (5% with mastery) of that ally’s current SHP, 20s (10s with mastery) cooldown. [Consume 50 mp * damage on activation]</li>
<li>If used while looking at a mob within 20 blocks, deals damage to that mob equal to the 2.5% (5% with mastery) of the user’s current SHP, 20s (10s with mastery) cooldown.  [Consume 20 mp * boosted damage each attack]</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Death Wish</b>: If held down for 200 ticks while looking at a mob within 5 blocks, instantly kills them. Slows target by 60%. Costs 1000 MP [You must be looking at the target for the whole time or the timer will go down]</li></ul>
