<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - Gain 2 Extra points towards Mastery and Learning</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/thought-acceleration/" title="Abilities/Skills/Thought Acceleration">Thought Acceleration</a></b></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <b>Predictive Calculation</b> : 25% Chance to dodge an attack. 50% Chance to ignore dodge on enemies. 75% to deal Crit damage. Nullification is treated as resistance and resistance is bypassed. Magic Jamming's damage mitigation is ignored.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/analytical-appraisal/" title="Abilities/Skills/Analytical Appraisal">Analytical Appraisal</a></b>: Analysis level 1 (2 with Mastery), distance 10 blocks (20 with Mastery), If a target has greater than 5x (7x with Mastery) the player's EP, then only its base stats are shown (Name, HP, SHP, Armor).</li></ul>
