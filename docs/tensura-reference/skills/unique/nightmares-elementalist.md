<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Spiritually One: The user obtains the Blessed Status.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Elemental Boost: The user's Elemental Damage is boosted by 30%. 40% when mastered.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Elemental Conversion: The user enters a meditative state where they cannot move, and converts any elemental damage taken to Magicules.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Elemental Connection: The user selects one of the elements to connect themselves too, they become immune to this element and boost the Elemental Damage by 20 Damage, 40 Damage when mastered.</li></ul>
