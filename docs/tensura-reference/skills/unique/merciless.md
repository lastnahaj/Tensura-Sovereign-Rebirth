<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - In Slot] - <b>Soul Consume</b>: Applies a debuff to the player's attacks that drains the afflicted mob/player's spiritual health at 10 per 10 ticks (this drain lasts 5s). Costs 100 MP for each attack by user [required to be in active skill slots]</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Soul Steal</b>: Instantly kills all mobs within a 30x30 radius that are below 10% HP, have an EP below 10% of the users maximum, or have at least a lvl 5 <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-fear/" title="Effects/Fear">Fear</a> debuff. Costs 100 MP</li></ul>
