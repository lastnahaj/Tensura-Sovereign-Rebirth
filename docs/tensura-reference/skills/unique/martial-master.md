<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/> <b><a href="../../extra/heavenly-eye/" title="Heavenly Eye">Heavenly Eye</a></b> is acquired upon obtaining Martial Master. If Martial Master is created with Creator, Heavenly Eye will not be obtained.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] - Grants 5 learning and mastery points with Battlewill instead of 1.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] <b>Secret</b>: Makes all Battlewill and Melee attacks deal 1.5X damage.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <b><a href="../../extra/thought-acceleration/" title="Thought Acceleration">Thought Acceleration</a></b></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Foresight</b> : Makes the player able to dodge projectiles with 100% accuracy. Able to dodge melee attacks with 25% accuracy.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Ultra Acceleration</b> : Moves the player to where they're looking at high speed, effectively teleportation, 15 block radius, deal crit damage (+75 with Mastery) to anything (in radius of player attack range) on the way. Costs 100 AP.</li></ul>
