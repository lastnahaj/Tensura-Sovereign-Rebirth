<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Substitution</b> : The player takes damage in place of their subordinates [Not working for Spiritual Damage or damage dealt by the owner]</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Grant Protection</b> : Grants all nearby subordinates/allies 10 extra armor points and 30 barrier points for 180 seconds. Costs 100 MP</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Toggle] <b>Iron Wall</b> : Grants the player an additional 15 (40 with Mastery) Armour Points and 0.4 (1 with Mastery) Knockback Resistance.</li></ul>
