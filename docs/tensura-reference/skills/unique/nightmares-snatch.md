<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Fountain of Youth - Gain Costless regeneration, Thought Acceleration, a 50% to Steal Mp from a hit entity and as these immunities:
<ul><li>Abnormal Conditions</li>
<li>Chill</li>
<li>Rest</li>
<li>Sleep</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Physical Hunt - When used while looking at a target in a 20 block range, apply fragility to it and Strengthen, Haste and Resistance to the User. The user also gains an attack damage bonus equal to 75% of the Targets attack damage</li></ul>
<p>Sneak Usage lets this Mode affect all Entities in a 20 block radius with longer CD
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Fox Hunt (requires learning) - If a looked at Entity has less EP than the User, kill the entity and gain Its EP (like Reapers Infinite Eater). If used against the same entity type again, instead gain 25% instead.</li></ul>
<p>Sneak Usage makes this be an Aoe with longer Cooldown
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Zero Sign - On holding, Gain presence Concealment aswell as a +5 attack boost per entity in a 15 block radius</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p><p><br/>
(Costless Regeneration has a MP to HP conversion rate of 1HP-80(40)MP and 1SHP-240MP {&lt;-1SHP-50MP with Infinity})
</p>
