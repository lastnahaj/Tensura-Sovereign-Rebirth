<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
All activations are accompanied by a screech sound effect - Doesn’t work on Spiritual Lifeforms or those under the effects of Rampage.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Target</b>: Turns the looked at mob into an ally for 10 Minutes. Costs 50 MP, negated by Spiritual Attack Nullification. Reduced to 5 Minutes by Resistance.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Area</b>: Turns all mobs within a 20x20 (30x30 with Mastery) radius into allies for 5 Minutes. Costs 80 MP per mob. Negated by Spiritual Attack Nullification. Reduced to 2.5 Minutes by Resistance.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Charm</b>: Applies a "Hero of the Village V" effect for 2 Minutes to the player. Costs 200 MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Kill</b>: Kills all mobs (that is under mind control) within a 20x20 (30x30 with Mastery) Radius. Costs 100 MP per mob. Negated by Spiritual Attack Nullification. Deals half hp in damage with resistance. - Cooldown: 5s (3s with mastery)</li></ul>
