<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
<a href="../../extra/law-manipulation/" title="Law Manipulation">Law Manipulation</a> is acquired upon mastering the skill.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>When toggled, increases the amount of Learned Points and Mastery Points gained by 2.</li>
<li><a href="../../extra/thought-acceleration/" title="Thought Acceleration">Thought Acceleration</a> (Like Normal)</li>
<li><a href="../../extra/chant-annulment/" title="Chant Annulment">Chant Annulment</a> (Like Normal)</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[ Active - Press] - <b>Analytical Appraisal</b>: Analysis Level 18 (28 with mastery), distance 40 blocks (50 with Mastery)</li>
<li>[ Active - Press] - <b>Analyze</b>: When held down for 5s (3s with mastery) (affected by Chanting Speed) while looking at a Magic Circle or Magic Entity (barrier, field, projectile, etc.) within 30 blocks radius, copies the magic. <b>It can't copy any spirit magic or elemental summoning magic</b></li></ul>
