<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Heal</b>: Fully restores all HP at the cost of 80 MP (40 with Mastery) per HP. Fully restores all Spiritual HP at the cost of 60 MP per HP (Requires Mastery). If used while not looking at a target, applies to the player [Also remove Virus from the user], if used while looking at a target it heals them instead (require sneaking to heal others). [Physical Healing is done first, with MP cost - then to spiritual HP]</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Infection</b>: Applies the <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-infection/" title="Effects/Infection">Infection</a> debuff [Does not work on spiritual lifeforms] to the looked at target [Cure Virus if the target already has the effect]. Costs 100 MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Plague</b> [Requires Mastery]: Applies the Infection debuff to everything in a 15x15 radius. Costs 300 MP.</li></ul>
