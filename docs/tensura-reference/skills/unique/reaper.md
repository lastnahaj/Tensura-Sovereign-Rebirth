<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Recon - Cannot toggle when Attack is activated. Reduces player size by half, gives the user +1 Presence Sense level, + 10 Presence Sense Radius (20 with Mastery), highlights all mobs within the sensing radius area in white. Grants a 10% (20% with Mastery) Physical Attack auto-dodge chance.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] -  <b>Attack</b> - Cannot activate when Recon is toggled. While holding down, scroll mouse to choose the number of clones to summon, up to 5 clones. Upon release the skill button, reduces player size by half, reduces EP by 50% (lasts until disabled) and spawns the chosen number of clones (shift to have Alex model, else Steve model) with identical stats to the player and 50% EP divided evenly to every clone. Activate again to disable, having EP back to 100%</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Infinite Eater</b> (Requires Learning) - <b>Requires both Recon and Attack Mode to be inactive</b>. When activated while looking at a target, if their EP is equal to or lower than the user's, they're insta-killed and 50% of the target’s EP is consumed (EP gain only applies for the first target of that type). Costs as much MP as the target’s EP. Will recover magicule when succeeded. Cooldown 10s (5s with Mastery)</li></ul>
