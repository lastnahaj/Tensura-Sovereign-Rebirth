<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration</li>
<li>[Passive, In-Slot] Detect Weakness - While this skill is in slot, entities you hit will progress the production of your Lethal Dose.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actives">Actives</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] Bloody Bite - The user strikes at an enemy, spending some of their MP to inject them with their Poison. If the Detect Weakness is at 30% of less, this move only deals Physical and Spiritual Damage. (Halved by Abnormal Condition Resistance, reduced by 25% with Nullification). If Detect Weakness is at 50%, it infects them with Magicule Poisoning. If Detect Weakness is at 80%, it becomes Lethal Poison. If it is at 100%, it becomes Cursed Poison.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Poison Refinement - While Held, this move will slowly drain the user's MP and begin producing 2.5% (5% on mastery) of the progress for your Lethal Dose a second.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Deadly Domain - This can only be used when your Lethal Dose is at 100%. All Non-Spiritual Beings will take 500 SHP damage (1000 on mastery), and all Spiritual Lifeforms take 250 (500 on mastery) and your Lethal Dose is reduced to 0.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Cursed Poison (Requires Mastery, Requires Learning) - The user can infect a target with Cursed Poison for 5 minutes.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cursed_Poison_Effect">Cursed Poison Effect</span></h3>
<p>Cursed Poison is a Status Effect that deals both 25 HP and SHP damage per second each level. If you kill a entity while they are infected with Cursed Posion, you gain 10% more EP from it instead
</p>
