<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Dummy Sword</b>: At the cost of 100 MP, creates a Spatial Blade (with Severance V - X with mastery) in an empty hand slot. Blades infinitely regenerate at the cost of 20 MP per blade.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Blade Storm</b>: Require Spatial Blade to be in 1 of the user’s hand. At the cost of 2000 MP, unleashes 5 (10 with mastery) Severance Blades from behind the user to looked-at target (max range 20 blocks - 30 with Mastery). Cooldown 5s (3s with mastery).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Severance</b>: Applies a severance effect and a 10 (30 with Mastery) damage boost to the player's attacks for 120 seconds. Costs 200 MP.</li></ul>
