<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Breeder Reactor</b>: While held down, gives the player MP equal to 2% of their maximum every 5 seconds. Each usage has a 3% chance to add 1 level of <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-rampage/" title="Effects/Rampage">Rampage</a> which lasts for 30 seconds, which rises to 7% if the player's MP is past their natural maximum. Duration of Rampage increases by 3 seconds upon hitting an enemy. Rampage can only reach level 10 (20 with Mastery)[Configurable]. With Mastery, becomes a toggle ability instead of hold.</li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>Rampage Effect - +5 Armor, +30 Atk, +0.02 Atk Speed, +0.01 Speed, +0.01 Swim Speed, + 0.1 Knockback Resist per Rampage level</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Acitve - Hold] - <b>Enrage</b>: While held down, gives every mob in a 15x15 radius Rampage effect (+1 level each 10 seconds) for 20 seconds. With Spiritual Attack Resistance, reduced to a 25% chance. Costs 100 MP a second.</li></ul>
