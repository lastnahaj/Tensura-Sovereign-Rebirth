<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/> Makes <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/gravity-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Gravity Manipulation">Gravity Manipulation</a> Learnable.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Makes the player immune to fall damage and simple projectile (arrow, trident, kunai,...) with mastery [Toggle]. Repel, Attract, and Flicker are able to have their ranges manually selected with keys with mastery (0 = Hold like normal)</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Repel</b>: Repels the mob the player is looking at, pushing them away. The longer its held before releasing, the bigger the distance. Costs 50 MP</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Attract</b>: Attracts the mob the player is looking at, pulling them towards the player. The length its held down before releasing determines the distance pulled. Costs 50 MP</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Oppress</b>: Traps the mob the player is looking at inside a gravity field that reduces their speed and jump strength to 5% as well as applying Burden 2 for 30 seconds.  Each 10 seconds, add 1 level of insanity. If activated again while a mob is trapped, deals 100 (200 with Mastery) Damage (can be done repeatedly). Trapping a Mob costs 300 MP, dealing damage costs 700 MP. Cooldown 10s (5s if mastered)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Bleve</b>: Deals 100(200 with mastery) damage at the cost of 1000 MP to the mob the player is looking at and knocks them 10 blocks into the air.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Flicker</b>: Pushes the player forward at high speed. The longer the button is held the farther they go when the button is released. Costs 50 MP.</li></ul>
