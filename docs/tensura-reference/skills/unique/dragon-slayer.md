### Toggle passive

- Ice & Fire dragons become neutral toward you.
- Ice Breath, Fire Breath, and Thunder Breath bypass enemy resistances and nullifications.

### Granted skills

**Ice Breath · Fire Breath · Thunder Breath · Dragon Skin · Dragon Ear · Dragon Eye**

### Dragon Essence

Each Dragon Essence eaten adds **+20 Max HP** without a cap. This bonus resets on death.

### Mode 1 — Dragon Infusion

Infuse a Dragon Heart held in your hand to gain **+20,000 EP**.

### Mode 2 — Dragon Rage

Gain **Strength X for 30 seconds**. **Cooldown: 60 seconds**.

**Awakening:** [The Slayer of Dragons](../ultimate/the-slayer-of-dragons.md)
