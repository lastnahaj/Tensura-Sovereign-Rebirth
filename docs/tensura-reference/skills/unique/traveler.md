<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle + In Slot] - <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/spatial-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spatial Manipulation">Spatial Manipulation</a></b>: x2 Spatial Damage [Toggle] &amp; Warp Shot [In Slot while in this Mode]
<ul><li>When active, non-magic projectiles (Arrows, Spear, Kunai, Sniper Bullet, Web Bullet, Potions, Thrown Item (from Thrower), Egg, Ender Pearl, Firework Rocket, Snowball, Trident) will automatically warp to the back of the looked-at target's head and shot at the speed of the original speed multiplied by the warp shot level. Sneak to not warp shot while shooting. Cost 50 MP each warp.</li>
<li>When activated, grant the user warp shot level of 0.5. Press again to deactivate. (Stackable)</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Instant Motion</b>: Teleports the player to where they're looking instantly. Costs 5 MP per block distance. Range 200 Blocks. 5 Seconds Cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Teleport</b>: Opens Spatial Motion GUI. Costs 5 MP per block distance for instant warp, 25 per for portal. 10 second cooldown(5 with mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Stardust Arrow</b> (Requires Learning): Launches a singular energy arrow where the player is looking that deals 30 damage and applies severance [Sneak to apply warp shot on the arrow]. Costs 100 MP and 100 AP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Stardust Rain</b> (Requires Mastery + Learning): Launches energy arrows from 360⁰ in a sphere around the looked at enemy [Radius: 20 Blocks]. Costs 5000 AP and 5000 MP. Each arrow does 30 Damage and applies a Severance effect [Around 10-12 arrows at once]. 5 second cooldown(4 seconds with mastery)</li></ul>
