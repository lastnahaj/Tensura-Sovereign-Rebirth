<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Status Quo: The user's maximum Health cannot be reduced by the likes of Severance or Cook.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Render Stillness: The user can walk over liquid blocks as if they were a solid.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In Slot]  Fixed State: The user's Magicules and Aura are not expended while in Fixed State.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Air Wall: The user produces a solid 5x5 air wall in front of them. This air wall destroys any projectiles that come in contact with it and cannot be walked through.</li>
<li>[Active, Hold] Time Control: The user can alter the flow of time in a localized 8x8 area around them, slowing time down by up to 50% (80% when mastered)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Time Freeze (Requires learning): Stop time in the localized area of 8x8 for all things but yourself and other beings with Time Manipulation.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] White Lock (Requires learning and mastery): Stop one specific target in time for 30 seconds.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Block Acceleration (Requires learning): Place a block of Block Acceleration Glass, which speeds up the tick rate of blocks around it (Crops and Spawners included).</li></ul>
