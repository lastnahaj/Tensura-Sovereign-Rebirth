<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Predation</b>: On use, creates a black mist (breath) in a 2 block radius in front of the player. If it comes into contact with a mob, deals 10 damage and drains 100 MP, with a 10% chance to gain one random intrinsic, common, extra, or resistance skill from the target. If an enemy is <b>killed</b> by Predation, <b>gains all</b> of their intrinsic, common, extra, and resist skills. With Mastery, the mist is capable of eating projectiles. If a magic projectile is eaten, then the player gains that magic (consumed attacks have their ep added to stomach's energy storage). 50% chance to copy eligible magics when consuming a target. Targets killed/corpses eaten have 30% (Configurable) of their ep added to the players, instead of the usual 5% (EP absorb only applies for the first target of that type using Predator) The other 50% will be added to Stomach's energy storage. Shift-activate to toggled on/off consume-block-mode, which will automatically be added to Stomach.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Stomach</b>: When activated, grants access to a spatial storage with the size of a chest with 7 rows. Items are able to be stacked to 128 instead of 64.(Use Alt+skill key to change different modes)</li>

<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Isolation</b>: Consumes held item and restores MP similar to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../intrinsic/absorb-dissolve/" title="Abilities/Skills/Absorb &amp; Dissolve">Absorb and Dissolve</a>, but with twice the restored MP. If nothing is held and the player is looking at a target, remove all negative status effects from that target. If used while empty handed and not looking at a mob, removes all negative status effects from the player [Costs 100MP each effect level removed].</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Analysis</b>: Opens an analysis GUI functioning similarly to <a href="../great-sage/" title="Great Sage"> Great Sage's</a> refinement subskill excluding the function for automation and saving item on closing GUI.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - ???] - <b>Mimicry</b>: Not implemented yet.</li></ul>
