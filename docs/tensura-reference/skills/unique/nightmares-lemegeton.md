<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Magical Prodigy - Instant learning and mastery of obtained spells. Concentrator is obtained upon acquiring.</li>
<li>[Passive, In slot] Magic Cancel - (Requires Mastery) Upon being hit by an ability, every mode of that ability will be set to a minimum of 300 seconds of cooldown (5 minutes, configurable). This ability has a 60 second cooldown.</li>
<li>[Passive, Toggled] Arcane Blessing - Magic, holy, and spiritual damage and multiplied by 2 when dealt by the user.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press]Demon Seal - At the cost of 50k Magicules, the user can tame a spiritual player with 40% or less of their EP temporarily, OR tame a spiritual mob with 80% or less of their EP temporarily.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Key’s Defense - While held, the user pays 50 Magicules per point of damage blocked. Physical, fire based, and water based attacks are blocked using Magicules. Player’s movement speed is divided in 2 for this duration.</li></ul>
