<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><big><b>!!! Toggle has no effect ATM !!!</b></big>
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Predation</b> : On use, creates a black mist (breath) up to a 10 block (shifting and using the ability will change the block consumption between only blocks, liquids or both, slideable range with scroll mouse. Max 15 with mastery) radius in front of the player If it comes into contact with a mob, deals 50 damage, applies <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-corrosion/" title="Effects/Corrosion">Corrosion</a> for 5 seconds, and drains 1000 MP, with a 30% chance to gain 1-3 random <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a>, or <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Resistances" title="Category:Resistances">Resists</a> skill/s from the target. If an enemy is <b>killed</b> by Predation, gains <b>all</b> of their <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a> and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Resistances" title="Category:Resistances">Resists</a> skills. With Mastery, if a <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Magics" title="Category:Magics">Magic</a> projectile is eaten, then the player gains that magic . <b>For the first time killing a type of mob</b>, targets killed have 50% (Configurable) of their ep added to the players max instead of the usual 3%.  This can be toggled to consume blocks using shift + ability key while on the predation mode, which will automatically be added to Stomach.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Stomach</b> : When activated, grants access to a spatial storage with the size of a chest with 9 rows. Items are able to be stacked to 666 instead of 64.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] <b>Corrosion</b>: Costs 200 MP and deals 10 <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-corrosion/" title="Effects/Corrosion">Corrosion</a> damage every 10 ticks to all enemies in a 10x10 radius. Enemies killed by this have their <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Resistances" title="Category:Resistances">Resists</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>, and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a> Skills taken by the player.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Receive</b> : <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>, <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>, and <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a> skills owned by the player's subordinates are given to the player.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Isolation</b> : Consumes held item and restores mp similar to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../intrinsic/absorb-dissolve/" title="Abilities/Skills/Absorb &amp; Dissolve">Absorb and Dissolve</a>, but with twice the restored mp. If nothing is held and the player is looking at a target, remove all negative status effects from that target. If used while empty handed and not looking at a mob, removes all negative status effects from the player [Costs 100 MP each effect level removed].</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Footnote">Footnote</span></h2>
<p>When requirements to obtain are met <b>Gluttony</b> becomes learnable with 99% learning points, rather than being automatically learnt.
</p>
