<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Silent Movement</b> : The player makes zero noise, undetectable by Sculk or Sense Sound Wave.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Presence Concealment</b> : When held, makes the player completely invisible and undetectable by any sensory abilities until the player runs out of energy. While in slot, dealing extra 50 (150 with mastery) physical damage while concealed. Costs 50 MP per 20 ticks. Becomes toggleable with mastery.</li></ul>
