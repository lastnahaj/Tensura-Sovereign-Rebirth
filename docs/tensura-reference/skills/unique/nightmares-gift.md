<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h1>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h2>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Gift Creation - The user can open a menu and produce any of the Divine Protection Skills as a Sub-Skill for Gift. Only works on the user, no gifting other entities divine protections. Shift Use to open a GUI allowing the deletion of Blessings</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - In-Slot / Mode] Divine Protection - The user swaps to the Divine Protection Skill to gain the benefits of the Divine Protection created by Gift Creation.</li></ul>
