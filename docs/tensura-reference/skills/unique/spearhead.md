<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
<big><b>!!! You can NOT gain Skills from Players UNLESS the world is in Hardcore Mode (ONE LIFE) !!!</b></big><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True]: <b>Skill Collection</b> : When an ally dies within 20 blocks of the user, the user can collect their <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Resistances" title="Category:Resistances">Resistance</a>/<a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Intrinsic_Skills" title="Category:Intrinsic Skills">Intrinsic</a>/<a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a>/<a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a>/<a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Unique_Skills" title="Category:Unique Skills">Unique</a> Skills.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot]: Allies do 10 extra damage and +0.02 Speed, +1 Swim Speed, +5 Armor (Double buff with Mastery)</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Thought Domination</b> : Allows the player to command their subordinates, including a "Meat Shield" Option.</li></ul>
