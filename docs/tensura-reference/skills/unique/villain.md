<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] - When dying from Magicule Poisoning, the option to become a Majin appears 100% of the time.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - Increases EP gained from defeating enemies from 3% to 6% (Split evenly between Aura and Magicule. 1.5% each). Grants 25% dodge negate. When mastered gain Haki Coat on tick.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] - <b>Villain’s Intimidation</b> : The player and their allies have a 50% critical hit rate and a 10% auto-dodge chance.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Villain’s Charisma</b> (200 MP): When activated, all Passive and Neutral Mobs within a 20x20 radius will be treated as the player's allies and will assist them in combat for 120s [Negated by Spiritual Attack Nullification, reduced to 60s by Resistance]</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Demon Lord’s Haki</b>: All mobs within a 30x30 radius are affected. If their EP is &gt;= 75% of the user's used EP, it does nothing. Otherwise, gives 2 Fear levels for each 20% difference with the targets' EP. 50 MP per 20t.</li></ul>
