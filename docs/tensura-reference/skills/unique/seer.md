<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <b>Precognition</b>: 25% (50% with Mastery) Chance to auto-dodge a physical/projectile attack. Attacks that land will be reduced to 70% damage (50% with Mastery). Gain 33% (50% with Mastery) Crit chance on attack.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Future Sight</b>: When activated, grants the player perfect dodge, perfect dodge negation and perfect crit chance for 10 seconds (20s with Mastery). After the duration, it cannot be used again for another 10 seconds. [Activate again to turn off the effect].</li></ul>
