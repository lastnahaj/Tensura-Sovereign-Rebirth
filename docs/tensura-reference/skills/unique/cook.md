<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive] Certain Outcome: When **toggled**, all attacks made by the player become critical hits automatically. When **in-slot**, attacks made by the player shatter all barrier points unless the target has Law Manipulation or an Ultimate Skill and has an EP more than 2X the player's.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Optimal Action: The player's attacks ignore all forms of dodge. Learning and Mastering abilities grants +4 learning points. Nullification is treated as resistance and resistance is bypassed.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - toggle] <b>Chaotic Fate</b> (Requires Learning): When activated, makes the damage of the player's next attack reduce the target's max hp by the amount of damage dealt. Crouching and using chaotic fate will undo all permanent damage done with chaotic fate to the user, looking at a mob will do it to them. When activated with mastery, it will turn into a active - press ability</li></ul>
