<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Crafting</b>: Opens a crafting GUI that also has an option to Decraft items into their base components. Second tab is a combination of anvil (without renaming) and grindstone (be able to select what enchantments to remove). Using the anvil function to combine enchantments allows it to bypass the vanilla cap of obtainable levels of an enchant <b>By a max of 2 Levels above Vanilla Max</b>. (Neither options costs experience points.)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Separate</b>: When used on mobs with an EP below 75% of the player, steal <b>one</b> skill of type <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Common_Skills" title="Category:Common Skills">Common</a> / <a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Extra_Skills" title="Category:Extra Skills">Extra</a>. When used on oxidized copper, strip it. When used on ore blocks, it gives the player the ore and leaves a stone/deepslate block in its place. When used on suspicious sand/gravel instantly separate item. When used while not looking at anything, remove status effects (unless it’s a beneficial skill effect) from the user. When used while sneaking and looking at subordinate/ally, remove status effects from them instead including severance [Costs 100 MP each effect level removed].</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Synthesize</b>: When used, if the user is looking at a Charybdis Core, gain skills and MP based on the Core’s state. If the player is looking at a mob tagged Spiritual that is at 25% hp or lower, the mob perishes and the player gains all the skills and EP of the mob, only works once per mob type. (<i>Cannot be a summoned spirit, has to be found in the wild</i>)</li></ul>
