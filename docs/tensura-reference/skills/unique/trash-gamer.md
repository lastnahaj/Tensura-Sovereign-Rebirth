### Passive

While the skill is in your skill bar, every ability gains **+4 mastery gain**.

### Mode 1 — Duplication Glitch

- Duplicates one item held in your hand.
- Costs **50% of current MP**, reduced to **25% when mastered**.
- **Cooldown:** **600 seconds**.
- Configurable exclusions and extra safety checks block containers such as shulker boxes, bundles, and supported backpacks.

### Mode 2 — Buff Glitch

- Applies a random positive potion effect for **3 minutes** with a random **1–10** amplifier.
- **Cooldown:** **60 seconds**, or **10 seconds when mastered**.

### Mode 3 — Prodigy

- For **10 minutes**, set Max HP and current HP to **1**, dodge chance to **99%**, and movement speed to **+20%** while ignoring opponent resistances and nullifications.
- When it ends, Max HP and current HP are restored, **Paralysis X** is applied for **3 minutes**, and all Aura and MP are consumed.
