<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Earth Bound - The user gains all of the Aspectual Earth spells.</li>
<li>[Passive, True] Multi-Tooled - Depending on EP, (0-20,000 diamond tier, 20,001-500,000 netherite, 500,000-2,000,000 pure magisteel, 2,000,000+ Hihi’Irokane) your bare hands will be able to be used as multi tools, upgrading as you get stronger, also increasing punch damage. While you have this skill, holding a tool/weapon will give Paralysis V and Fragility V, cutting you off from using tools with engravings/enchantments.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Giant’s Requiem - Call out to your brethren, summoning two clones with 1/2 power compared to you, copying your armor and such Costs 2000 (1500 mastery) magicules with a cooldown of 200 seconds (120 mastery,) only 2 clones can exist at a time. The clones are both considered sentient.</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Cowardice - Become slightly intangible for 30 seconds, increasing step height and speed, and giving a 50% chance to dodge attacks while active 2 minute cooldown, 1000 magical cost, no bonus for mastery</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Mineral Shifting - When used on ore blocks, it gives the player the ore and leaves a stone/deepslate block in its place. When used on suspicious sand/gravel instantly separate item. When this skill is mastered, it doubles it instead of the normal amount.</li></ul>
