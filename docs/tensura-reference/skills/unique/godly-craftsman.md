<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
The same as <a class="mw-redirect" href="../researcher/" title="Abilities/Skills/Researcher">Researcher</a>, but can store <a href="../../../core-mechanics/engravings/" title="Engravings">Engravings</a><sup class="reference" id="cite_ref-1"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and take <a class="external text" href="https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanting#Summary_of_enchantments" rel="nofollow">enchantments</a> from enchanted or engraved gear. Additionally, items can be stacked to 256 in Spatial Storage. The max level of enchantment stored can be 2 levels higher than enchantments cap
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li>i.e. Sharpness 7 compared to Vanilla Sharpness 5 (Will need a book or item with said enchantment to store at that level)</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Engravings can never be raised above their own maximum level, no matter how many bonus levels the skill grants. Curse and Blessing engravings can neither be learned nor applied by the skill: they are on its blacklist and can only be obtained or removed through the means described below.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Curse_Engravings">Curse Engravings</span></h2>
<p>Curse Engravings are hostile <a href="../../../core-mechanics/engravings/" title="Engravings">engravings</a> that attach themselves to gear on their own. They cannot be chosen, and <b>Godly Craftsman</b> can neither apply nor remove them.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining">Obtaining</span></h3>
<p>There are two ways an item can be cursed:
</p>
<ul><li><b>While engraving with the skill.</b> Each time an item leaves the skill's enchanting menu having actually cost experience, there is a <b>3% chance per engraving already on the item</b> of a random Curse being added. An item carrying five engravings therefore has a 15% chance of picking up a Curse on every further edit. Curses gained this way always arrive at level I.</li>
<li><b>While the gear gains EP.</b> Gear that gains EP from kills receives a random engraving upon crossing the Unique (50,000 EP), Legend (250,000 EP) and God (1,000,000 EP) thresholds. Every one of those rolls carries a flat <b>3% chance</b> of also granting a Curse. The Curse arrives at the same base level as the engraving that triggered it: level I for a Common or Uncommon roll, level II for a Rare roll and level III for an Epic roll, capped by the Curse's own maximum level.</li></ul>
<p>Items in the <code>tensura:no_curse</code> tag can never be cursed. That tag currently points at <code>#tensura:holy_armaments_items</code>, so <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Holy_Armaments?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Holy Armaments (page does not exist)">Holy Armaments</a> gear is permanently immune.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Removal">Removal</span></h3>
<p>Curses cannot be removed with a Grindstone, with <a href="../degenerate/" title="Degenerate">Degenerate</a> or with Godly Craftsman. The only cure is <a href="../reverser/" title="Reverser">Reverser</a>, which converts each Curse into its opposing Blessing at the same level, for a Magicule cost per Curse:
</p>

</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Configuration">Configuration</span></h3>
<p>The chance and the pool of Curses are configurable in <code>tensura/enchantment_config</code> through <code>curseChance</code> (default 3.0) and <code>curseEngraving</code>. The per-engraving chance used by the skill's own menu is set separately in <code>tensura/unique_skill_config</code> under <code>GodlyCraftsman.curseChance</code> (default 0.03).
</p>
