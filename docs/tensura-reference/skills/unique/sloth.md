<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <b>Phantasmal Style</b> (Requires Learning): When activated, applies an effect to the player's attacks that deals 10 (+1% of target’s max SHP with Mastery) Spiritual damage.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Deep Hypno</b>: While looking at a mob, adds one level of Drowsiness every 10 seconds (15 with spiritual resistance, 20 with nullification) and 5 (10 with Mastered) seconds of duration. Each level of sleep reduces movement speed by 25% and has an additional effect identical to weakness and fragility. On lvl 5, it kills the target instantly. When used on a magic circle, will destroy the magic circle and place it on a 3 second cooldown (5 seconds with mastery).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Fallen Hypno</b>: Deep Hypno but within an Aoe radius of 10x10 (20x20 if mastered) and has double the casting time - 20 seconds (25 with spiritual resistance, 30 with nullification).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Deprive</b>: Drains 1000 MP (+0.3% target’s max MP with Mastery) and deals 10 (+1% of target’s max SHP with Mastery) Spiritual Damage every second while looking at a mob within 5 (15 with Mastery) blocks. If used on a mob with spiritual HP below 50%, they become tamed/subordinate to the player.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] - <b>Rest</b>: While held down, makes the player unable to move, attack, or activate other abilities. Regen 10 (+1% of target’s max with Mastery) HP and Spiritual HP per sec and increases the players MP, and AP Regen by 10X. If MP is at max, it fills up a temporary MP storage at the same rate. If there is enough MP in the temporary storage (1000 x Y = X [Y = Number of Allies. X = Cost]), right clicking will restore 1000 MP, 1000 AP, 50 HP and 10 Spiritual HP to all allies within a 30x30 radius.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Fallen Strike</b> (Requires Phantasmal Style. Requires Mastery.): At the cost of 5,000MP, deals 500 Spiritual Damage to the looked at target. Ignores Spiritual Attack Resistance and Nullification. [Cooldown 10s]</li></ul>
