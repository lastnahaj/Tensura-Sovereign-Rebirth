### Passive

**Toggle:** Reflect all basic projectiles toward their source.

### Mode 1 — Storage

Opens spatial storage similar to Gluttony.

### Mode 2 — Candy Beam

- Fires a forward beam that converts a target into candy when you are at least **2× stronger** than a normal target or **5× stronger** than a boss.
- The candy stores **10% of target EP** and all Common, Extra, and Intrinsic skills from the target. Eating it grants those rewards.
- Player targeting is controlled by a config option and is **off by default**.
- Does not work on the **Elemental Colossus** or **Ender Dragon**.

### Mode 3 — Regeneration

Consume **5% Aura** to restore HP to full.

**Awakening:** [The Evil Majin](../ultimate/the-evil-majin.md)
