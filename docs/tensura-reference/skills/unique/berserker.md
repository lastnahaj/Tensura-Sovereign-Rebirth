<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h1>
<p><br/>
Mastery is gained passively.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Death Benefit</b> - Upon killing an enemy, receive 4% of its EP. 2% as Magicule and 2% as Aura. This stacks with the base 3% given to ordinary players on killing a mob, for a total of 7%</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] <b>Equipment Destruction</b> - The user's attacks passively deal double damage to armour durability. Attacks against the user take up double durability from their weapon.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Diamond Body</b> - When activated, grants the user an Armor and ** Physical Damage (Hand, Sword, Spear, Axe) buff temporarily. The buff scales to the user's current max ep and does not consume any of the user's ep to activate:
<ul><li>Attack Damage (x2 with Mastery): 5 base then add one 1 more for each 40k EP (stops at 2M EP)</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>Armor: 1 for each 20k EP (stops at 2M EP)</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>Speed: 0.01 for each 25k EP (stops at 1M EP)</li></ul></li></ul>




</div>
