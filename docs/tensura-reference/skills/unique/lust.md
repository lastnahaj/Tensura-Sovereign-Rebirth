<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active] - <b>Drain</b>: When used while not looking at a mob, adds an effect to the users attacks that drains 200 (200 + 0.1% with Mastery) MP and AP (each) and gives it to the user (temporary, not max) for 10 seconds. When used while looking at a mob, it drains 200 (200 + 0.5% with mastery) MP and AP (each) and gives it to the user. Costs 100 MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active] - <b>Invigorate</b>: When used while not looking at a mob or when sneaking, fully heals the player and removes all status effects. When looking at a mob while sneaking, fully heals them and removes all status effects [Cost 80 MP (40 with Mastery) per HP and 100 MP per effect level]. If used on a mob that can breed while its at full hp, will cause them to enter hearts mode.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active] - <b>Embracing Drain</b> (Requires Learning): Requires the player to be looking at a mob and be within 2 blocks. Locks the player and the mob in place and drains 200 (0.5% with Mastery) MP and AP per second. Lasts for a total of 5 seconds. Causes pain effects even with resistance/nullification. Cost 500 MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Held] Death Blessing (Requires Mastery + Learning): When holding down, a pair of divine hands reaching from the sky toward the position/target that the user is looking at (in 10 block range), while being casted, every entities in 6x6 block radius around the targeted position are slowed down by 60%. Once reaching 5s casted, all of the affected entities are instantly killed if their EP is lower than 50% of the player's and restores the player's expended MP and AP by the target mobs EP (75/25). If the target is between 50% and 75% Drains 25% EP and deals half their HP in damage. If above 75% drains 5% and deals half their HP in damage. Cost 7500 MP - 5s cooldown.</li></ul>
