## Toggle passive

While toggled on, Fourth Wall adds **4.0** to each of the ability learning gain and ability mastery gain attributes. Toggling it off removes those modifiers.

This is an attribute bonus, not four mastery points automatically awarded per action. Other modifiers can affect the resulting gain.

## Availability

The skill is registered in the pinned build and appears in the exported skill inventory. No reincarnation condition, mastery chain, or racial grant has been confirmed for this entry; the inventory alone does not prove a normal gameplay obtainment route.
