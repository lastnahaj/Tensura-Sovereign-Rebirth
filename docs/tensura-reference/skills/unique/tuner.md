<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] - <b>Destiny Change</b>: If the player would die, instead restore them to full HP, remove negative status effects, and restore their MP and AP up to 50% (75% with Mastery) of their maximum total. This applies for EACH type of damage once a Minecraft day (20 minutes). Once this ability is triggered, the skill will start its 20 mins cooldown, the skill can still be used during the cooldown, when the 20 mins cooldown runs out, the user can again revive from the death type that they already endured during the last 20 mins.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] - <b>Unexpected Result</b>: (Becomes Toggleable with Mastery) If the player's HP is below 25%, grants a Strength 10 Buff, + 0.2 Attack Speed, + 0.04 Movement Speed, + 1 Swim Speed , and 15% physical attack auto-dodge chance. (double all buffs with Mastery)</li></ul>
