<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] Thought Acceleration</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Pursuit of Tutelage (Requires Mastery)
<ul><li>When used, the user can open up a Skill Creation Menu and teach their known Spells and Battlewills to someone.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Hold]Pursuit of Truth: Investigate every 10 seconds, roll a 1/3 chance to gain 1 point of data on someone up to 10 points each one granting new details. Use /trnightmare investigate to get the info
<ul><li>While In-Slot the user gains the following benefits.
<ul><li>The user's Mastery and Learn Speed are increased by +10</li>
<li>The user's Melee Dodge Chance is at 50%, the user's Projectile Dodge Chance is at 25% and the user's Critical Hit Chance is at 30%, while it as a 40% Dodge Negation.
<ul><li>When shift pressed, it will cycle between Pursuit Modes.</li>
<li>Pursuit of Surprise: This highlights nearby traps. Lava, Pointed Dripstone, Tripwire, Sculk Sensor, Sculk Shrieker, Cobweb, Powder Snow, Trapped Chest, TNT, TNT Minecart, End Crystal [  Add a config to add other blocks in ]</li>
<li>Pursuit of Treasure: Chest, Barrel, Vault, Dragon Egg, Ancient Debris and Magic Ore, Charybdis Core. [ Add a config to add other blocks in ]</li>
<li>Pursuit of Battle: Highlights nearby mobs in colors, yellow for neutral, red for hostile</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Toggle] Analytical Appraisal:
<ul><li>The Appraisal Level is 5, 15 on mastery.</li></ul></li></ul>
