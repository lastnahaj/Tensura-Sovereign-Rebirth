<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] - <b>Coating</b>: Costs 1,000 MP, adds a 50 (200 if mastered) damage boost, as well as a severance effect to the player's attacks for 60 seconds.
<ul><li><b>Resist Bypass</b>: Bypasses Resistances and treats Nullifications as Resistances while coat is active.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active - Press] - <b>Projectile</b>: Costs 20,000 MP, launches an invisible projectile that deals 50 (300 with Mastery) Damage and has a severance effect. Projectile is 5 blocks(10 with mastery). 3s Cooldown</li></ul>
