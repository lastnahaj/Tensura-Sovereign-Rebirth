### Toggle passives

- Multiply melee attack damage by **1.15**.
- **Reversed Cursed Technique:** Slowly restores health every **2 seconds** and stacks with regeneration skills.

### Abilities

- **Dismantle:** Fires a black damaging slash.
- **Cleave:** Sends multiple slashes around you.
- **Fuga:** Launches concentrated fire energy that explodes at the aimed location.
- **Malevolent Shrine:** Continuously creates slashes in the surrounding area.
