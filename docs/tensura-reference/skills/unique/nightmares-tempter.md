<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In-Slot] Solicitation (Requires Mastery):
<ul><li>While this skill is in-slot and mastered, the user will be able to copy sub-skills similarly to Pride.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Charm:</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Temptation World:
<ul><li>The user traps all nearby entities with Charm or Fear in their Illusion. The world becomes dark around them, and they can only see a couple blocks in front of them. Entities with Spiritual Nullification and higher EP than the user can ignore this effect.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press] Reality Exchange (Requires Mastery):
<ul><li>The user pulls all targets inflicted by Temptation World into the Temptation Dimension. The dimension is a dark void, with very little ground to move in.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press] Solicitation (Requires Mastery, Requires Learning):
<ul><li>The user opens their Solicitation Menu, which allows them to use the modes of copied skills from Tempter, only in the Temptation Dimension.</li></ul></li>
<li>[Active, Press] End Of World (Requires Mastery, Requires Learning):
<ul><li>The user collapses the Temptation Dimension in on itself, with a white flash. This deals 1,000 Spiritual Damage to all entities inside of it, including the user. This is reduced to 500 if your EP is within 50% of the user's EP. If your EP is less than 30% of the users, this will instantly kill you.</li></ul></li></ul>
