<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle + In Slot] <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/spatial-manipulation/" title="Abilities/Skills/Spatial Manipulation">Spatial Manipulation</a></b>: x2 Spatial Damage &amp; Warp Shot [In Slot while in this Mode]
<ul><li>When active, non-magic projectiles (Arrows, Spear, Kunai, Sniper Bullet, Web Bullet, Potions, Thrown Item (from Thrower), Egg, Ender Pearl, Firework Rocket, Snowball, Trident) will automatically warp to the back of the looked-at target's head and shot at the speed of the original speed multiplied by the warp shot level. Sneak to not warp shot while shooting. Cost 50 MP each warp.</li>
<li>When activated, grant the user warp shot level of 0.5. Press again to deactivate. (Stackable)</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../../magic/magic-sense/" title="Abilities/Skills/Magic Sense"><b>Magic Sense</b></a> : Same as normal. Treated as Mastered.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] - <b>Predictive Calculation</b> : 10% Chance to auto-dodge an attack. 50% Chance to ignore dodge on enemies. Nullification is treated as resistance and resistance is bypassed. Magic Jamming's damage mitigation is ignored.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Create Weapon</b> : If used when not looking at a mob, creates a Walther P99 Pistol in the player's empty hand [Sneak Right-clicking to change Bullet Mode: Magic and Physical. Physical Bullet consumes 100 AP and deals 30 Physical Damage - 1s (0s with mastery) cooldown, Magic Bullet consumes 100 MP and deals 100 (200 with Mastery) Magic Damage - 3s (0s with mastery) cooldown. If Sniper is actively in a slot and is mastered, the bullets will teleport behind a looked at enemy when fired instead]. If activated while looking at an entity or already having a pistol in 1 hand, throws a grenade at the mob which explodes and deals damage in a 5x5 block radius - 3s cooldown (1s with mastery).
<ul><li>Magic Bullet will apply severance if Warp Shot is active</li></ul></li></ul>
