<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../extra/thought-acceleration/" title="Abilities/Skills/Thought Acceleration"><b>Thought Acceleration</b></a></li>
<li>[Passive - Toggle] <b>Inspire Forces</b> Boosts the stats of all allies in 30 block radius by 30% (60% with Mastery) and gives them 30% Crit chance (60% with Mastery).</li>
