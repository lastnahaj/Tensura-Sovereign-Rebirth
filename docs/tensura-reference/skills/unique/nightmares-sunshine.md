<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h1>
<ul><li>[Passive Toggle] Grace of the Sun - Depending on the time of day, increase Damage, HP, Armour and speed depending on the time of day, with no boost at night and full boost at noon (Works even at night with Rhitta)</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h2>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Solar Burst - Shoot a flame orb into view direction, dealing Fire damage to all entities hit (100s cd)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Cruel Sun - Shoot a Stronger and Destructive flame orb into view direction, dealing Fire damage to all entities hit and breaking blocks in a 6 block radius (160s cd)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] (learnable) Burn to Ash - Create a Fire Ring around a Place aimed at, dealing fire and magic damage to all inside(200s cd)</li>
<li>[Active, Press] Spiral of Creation - Call forth a flame pillar at your location in a 4 block square, trapping you and anything caught inside for a few seconds and dealing Damage to anyone else caught inside Exept the user(800s cd)</li></ul>
