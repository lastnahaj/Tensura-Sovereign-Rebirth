<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<p>[Passive - Toggle] 24 FPS -
Summon 24 copies of your self mimicking your previous movements that hold your momentum. As you move more and more your attack and movement speed pile up, however, getting hit at high speed or suddenly stopping can end your momentum or get you stuck in 1 frame
</p><p>Skill must be toggled on to use the rest of the moves properly
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] 360 Barrage - Using built up momentum and after images, whichever target is selected willl have all of your afterimages surround them as you deal 2.5x melee damage (5x on mastery) and teleport around the target to wherever your afterimages are around them</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Speed Blitz - You and your projections simultaneously jump the target, hitting them at once dealing 4x melee damage, 8x in mastery.</li>
<li>[Active, Held] Projection Breaker - Give up all of your momentum and trap your target in a frame for 1 second (3 on mastery) then smash them through it, breaking the projection and dealing full melee damage and exploding the target while launching them backwards.</li></ul>
