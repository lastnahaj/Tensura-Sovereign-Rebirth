<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><i>To activate, you need to right click the magic engine to work.</i><br/>
</p><p>While active will reduce the surrounding area's Magicule by 1000 in a 16 block radius, which makes it 32x32x32 area of lower magicule level. <br/>
<i><b>(Configurable)</b></i><br/>
</p><p>Mobs will not spawn in that if the area magicule level is under 10 (configurable). <br/>
<b>Higher Magicule areas will require more Magic Engine at once to stop monsters from spawning.</b><br/>
</p><p><br/>
Mobs also will not target enemies if they are in an area that has under 10 magicule, <i>unless already aggroed, attacked or already inside the lowered area.</i> <br/>
</p><p><b>This lower Magicule level will also affect the players/mobs magicule regeneration (since the default rate is area magicule / 100 per 10 ticks).</b>
</p>
<h2><span id="How_to_see.2Fvisualise_the_range"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="How_to_see/visualise_the_range">How to see/visualise the range</span></h2>
