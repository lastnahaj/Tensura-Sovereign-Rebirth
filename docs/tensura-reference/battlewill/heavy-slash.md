<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] When activated, performs a melee attack with 1.5x the normal damage. If the attack hits a mob in 6 (10 with Mastery) blocks ahead, it acts like it has knockback 5. If the attack misses, it knocks all mobs in a 3 block radius in front of the player into the air and deals 0.5x damage. Cost 80 AP.</li></ul>
