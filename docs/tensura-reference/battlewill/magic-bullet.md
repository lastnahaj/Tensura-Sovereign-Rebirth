<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] While held down, increases value by 1 every 1.5 seconds (1s with Mastery) up to 15 seconds. Each level costs 25 AP and deals 10 Damage. Mastery increases the maximum to 30 seconds. If charged past level 5, explodes on impact without destroying blocks, dealing 100 damage at the impact zone (increases every 5 levels, damage is 2x at level 20, 3.5x at level 30) in a 15 block radius (increases radius by one for each level, spikes every 5 levels, at level 20 has a radius of 30 blocks).</li></ul>
<p>(Needs to be charged to at least 2.0 power for mastery increase)
</p><p><br/>
</p>
