<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] On activation, sends a cut-like projectile out from the player (does the regular attack animation) that deals slashing damage equal to double their weapons damage. With mastery, the slash does double the weapon's damage and if they're holding a weapon in their off-hand it sends out two slash projectiles. Costs 50 AP - 2s cooldown (1s with Mastery).</li></ul>
