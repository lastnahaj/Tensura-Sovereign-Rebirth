<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Held] While held down, increases power by 1 every 1.5 seconds (1 second with Mastery) up to power 120 (power 160 with mastery) can only be held down for 30s (60s with mastery).</li></ul>
<p>. Each power costs 100 AP and deals 25 Damage. If charged past power 5, explodes on impact without destroying blocks, dealing 100 damage at the impact zone (increases every 5 power, damage is 2x at level 20, 3.5x at power 30) in a 15 block radius (increases radius by one for each power, spikes every 5 power, at power 20 has a radius of 30 blocks).
</p><p><br/>
(Needs to be charged to at least 2.0 power for mastery increase)
</p><p><br/>
<br/>
</p>
