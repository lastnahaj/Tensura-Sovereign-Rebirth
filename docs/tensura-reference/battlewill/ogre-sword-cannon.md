<p>(Warning destroys some blocks)
<br/>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] Every 2 Seconds (1s with Mastery) charged up to 5 (10 with Mastery), increases value by 1 costing 200 AP each. For every 1 value, increase the size and range of the attack. When released, launches a piercing projectile with damage equal to x(1.5 + 0.5 * charged value) user’s damage.</li></ul>
