<summary>Skill information</summary>
<div markdown>

- Creates a shadow clone that copies your stats and fights back.
- Its M1 attacks bypass your resistances and nullifications.
- If it travels too far away, it teleports back to you.
- It despawns after **2 minutes** or after killing you.
- Defeating it grants an EP reward plus bonus EP.
