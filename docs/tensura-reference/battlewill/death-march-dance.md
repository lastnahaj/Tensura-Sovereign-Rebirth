<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] While held down, charges a meter that increases the size of a Magic Bullet above the player's head. The magic bullet increases in power every 2 seconds (1s with mastery), and each increase costs 100AP, with a maximum power level of 5 (10 with mastery). When released, sends small magic bullets toward the direction the user is looking at, with the amount depending on the power level (1 power level = 5). Each magic bullet deals 25 damage.</li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>will slowly home in on a looked at target if withn 50 blocks of the user when shot</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
(Needs to be charged to at least 2.0 power for mastery increase)
</p><p><br/>
