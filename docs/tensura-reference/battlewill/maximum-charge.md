<summary>Skill information</summary>
<div markdown>

- **Held ability:** Consume **6% of maximum Magicules per second** to restore **10% of maximum Aura per second**.
- **Mastered:** Restores **15% of maximum Aura per second**, with the same Magicule cost.
- **Channel limit:** Up to **5 seconds**, followed by a **5-second cooldown**, including early release. Applies **Slowness IV** while channeling.
- **Obtainment:** Master [Energy Charge](energy-charge.md).
- **Previous:** [Energy Charge](energy-charge.md). This is the final documented Battlewill charge upgrade.
