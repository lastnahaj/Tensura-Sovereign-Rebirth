<p><br/>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] On use, creates an Aura Shield item that behaves like a vanilla Minecraft shield. Costs 80 AP.
<ul><li>[ Active - Held ] When held creates a 3x3 Aura Shield ahead of the user with 100 HP, lasts till release, the shield can block entities and projectiles. Costs 1000 AP and has a 5s cooldown (3s with mastery)</li></ul></li></ul>
