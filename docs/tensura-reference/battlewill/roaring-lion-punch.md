<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/> Requires being barehanded or using a fist-type weapon(i.e. Dragon Knuckle/Armosaurus Gauntlet).
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] : When activated, the player makes a punch attack that consumes 10% of their AP up to 2000 (4000 with Mastery) total AP and has damage that scales to the amount of AP. 10 AP = 1 damage and with the user's attack damage, also applies enchantment's post effect.</li></ul>
