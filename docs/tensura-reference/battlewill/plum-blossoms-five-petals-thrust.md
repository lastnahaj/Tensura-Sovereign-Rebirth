<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Plum Blossoms - Five Petals Thrust</b>: When held down with a sharp weapon in either hand, forms a pink blossom behind the user and reduces the user’s speed by 75%, and after 4s and release, the user instantly move forward in 15 blocks and deal 100 damage (+ the user’s attack damage) ignoring armor and shield to any target in the line. After the dash, the flower will linger behind the user for 1 minute, during this time, each direct/melee damage the user takes is negated, for 5 times till the flower is out of petals. Cost 8.000 AP. Shift activate again to remove the flower.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Master_Effect">Master Effect</span></h3>
<p>The user can charge attack upto 12s (8s more), and the attack gains 25 more damage each second and cost 1500 AP each.
</p><p><br/>
</p>
