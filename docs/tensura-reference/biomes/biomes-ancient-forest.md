<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span></h2>
<p>A rare biome with high magicule count, and home of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../structures/structures-labyrinth-tree/" title="Spirit Tree">Spirit Tree</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span></h3>
<ul><li><a href="../../items/hipokute-flower/" title="Hipokute Flower">Hipokute Flower</a> is more likely to spawn here, and can also be farmed in this biome</li>
<li>Medium spirits that spawn have a slight chance to be spawned as a greater spirit instead</li>
<li>Giant Trees in this biome have a chance to spawn with a portal to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../structures/structures-labyrinth-tree/" title="Spirit Tree"> Labyrinth</a> they appear with soul lanterns for reference</li></ul>
