<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span></h2>
<p>A land of death, swarmed with undead. Wizard towers from long ago can be found here.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span></h2>
<ul><li>Undead mobs and races are not affected by sunlight in this biome and can spawn during the day.</li>
<li><a href="../../items/hipokute-grass/" title="Hipokute Grass"> Hipokute Grass</a> is slightly more common.</li>
<li>Applies the curse effect rather than <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-magicule-poison/" title="Effects/Magicule Poison"> Magicule Poison</a> if entering this biome without enough EP as a non-undead.</li></ul>
