<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<p>Goblins can only spawn in a <a href="../../structures/structures-goblin-village/" title="Structures/Goblin Village">Goblin Village</a> and cannot spawn outside a village. Upon naming, will evolve into a hobgoblin.<br/>
Goblins can trade but will not restock.
</p>
