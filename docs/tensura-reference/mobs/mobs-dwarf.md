<p><br/>
Dwarves can only spawn in <a href="../../structures/structures-dwarf-village/" title="Structures/Dwarf Village">Dwarf Village</a> and cannot spawn outside of the village. Upon taming they will lose the capacity to trade.
Dwarves can trade and will restock just like villagers. They also have a reputation system based on the player's actions
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Drops">Drops</span></h2>
<ul><li>None</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Breeding">Breeding</span></h2>
<p>Can be bred in the same way as vanilla villagers, just need to drop them food in the floor and have enough beds for the 2 parents and the child, also make sure the parents are different gender
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Professions">Professions</span></h2>
<ul><li>Once a dwarf choses a profession they will never change profession</li>
<li>Dwarf will only work/restock AND TRADE during the worktime in the day (in Minecraft time from 2000 to 12000), this can be seen as they only wear Profession clothing during work time.</li>
<li>For Leatherworker/Fisherman, Dwarf will only take in empty cauldron or barrel as work station</li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Behavior">Behavior</span></h2>
<ul><li>During work time if their workstation block is intact, <b>farmer</b> dwarves will navigate to nearby farmland to harvest crops and replant them (including Hipokute grass), will use bonemeal to boost crop growth if given any (Goblin/Lizardman/Orc can also do this when given a hoe/sickle in main hand and set on wandering but not as fast as farmer dwarf). Farmer dwarves will also occasionally go back to the composter workstation to make bonemeal and craft bread if possible.</li></ul>
<ul><li>During work time if their workstation block is intact, <b>fisherman</b> dwarves will navigate to nearby water source to fish for loot which is affected by fishing rod enchantments if given any. (Goblin/Lizardman/Orc can also do this when given a fishing rod in main hand and set on wandering but dwarf has buff in fishing luck as Fisherman)</li></ul>
<ul><li>During work time if their workstation block is intact, <b>lumberjack</b> dwarves will seek for nearby available trees to chop down and replant saplings where the trees are previously chopped if there’s any in inventory. (Goblin/Lizardman/Orc can also do this when given an axe in main hand and set on wandering but not as fast as lumberjack dwarf).</li></ul>
<ul><li>During work time if their workstation block is intact, <b>butcher</b> dwarves will seek for nearby available cows/pigs/chickens to breed (if has enough breeding food in inventory) and will start killing the animals if the number exceeds 6 animals in the area. (Goblin/Lizardman/Orc can also do this when given a short sword in main hand, enough breeding food in inventory and set on wandering but not as fast as butcher dwarf).</li></ul>
<ul><li>During work time if their workstation block is intact, <b>shepherd</b> dwarves will seek for nearby available sheep to breed (if has enough breeding food in inventory), sheer wool and also start killing the animals if the number exceeds 6 animals in the area. (Goblin/Lizardman/Orc can also do this when given a sheer in main hand, enough breeding food in inventory and set on wandering but not as fast as shepherd dwarf).</li></ul>
<ul><li>Dwarves and NPCs will sometimes put their collected items into a chest that is next to their workstation, keeping only some of the needed items in their inventory (like seeds, saplings).</li></ul>
<ul><li>Dwarves also throw foods to each others if have too much while the other is in need.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Reputation_System">Reputation System</span></h2>
<ul><li>Players will start with 0 Dwarven Reputation. (DR for short)
<ul><li>When you hit any dwarf (even if tamed by you) while there's another untamed dwarf nearby (so there's a witness), you get -0.1 DR.</li>
<li>When you kill any dwarf (even if tamed by you) while there's another untamed  dwarf nearby, you get -1 DR.</li>
<li>When you kill a monster (not tamed by you) that is targeting an untamed dwarf (as in saving the dwarf), you get +1 DR.</li>
<li>When you get a dwarf to level their trader level, you get +0.1 DR.</li>
<li>When you trade with a dwarf in a buying trade (buying items with coins), you get +0.05 DR. When you trade with a dwarf in a selling trade (selling items for coins), you get +0.01 DR.</li>
<li>Dwarf Reputation won't increase if either the dwarf or the attacker is tamed by players. Players can't regain reputation once they reach -100 reputation.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>If you got positive DR, dwarves will trade with you for a discounted price for 0.5% each point.</li>
<li>If you got negative DR, dwarves will trade with you for an increased price for 2% each point. And if you reach -50 DR, they will avoid trading with you as well as guard dwarves will constantly target you.</li></ul>
<ul><li>You can use the warp pad toward Gazel's boss fights on Royal Towers when you either have -100 DR or 100 DR.
<ul><li>As -100 DR, you are using the warp pad as a criminal, getting executed by the king himself. You will have to defeat Gazel to get your hand on Five Petals Thrust battlewill manual. You will be unable to reuse the warp pad again after this.</li>
<li>As 100 DR, you are using the warp pad as a friend with the dwarven nation, getting greeted by the king himself. You can fight him as a training fight and if you win, he gives you the Five Petals Thrust for free. Players can come back to fight Gazel again even after winning against him (no more EP and drop).</li></ul></li></ul>
