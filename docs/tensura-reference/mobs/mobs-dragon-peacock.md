<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Additional_Features">Additional Features</span></h2>
<p>If wearing a Monster Leather helmet, a tamed Dragon Peacock can be right clicked to have it ride the player. Pressing crouch will let it off of your head. Holding crouch while right clicking it will allow you to change activity mode while wearing the Monster Leather helmet. If you fall while a Dragon Peacock is on your head, your falling rate will decrease.
</p>
