<p><br/>
A large spider type monster. It lurks underground or in caves. After being named, it can be equipped with a monster saddle and ridden. Using the Race/Mount Ability key (default R), while riding it causes it to shoot out its <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/extra/sticky-steel-thread/" title="Abilities/Skills/Sticky Steel Thread">Sticky Steel Thread</a> with a large arc. When ridden or pursuing a player, it will break leaves and logs in its way. Holding the jump key, much like a horse will charge a jump bar allowing the spider to go around 10 blocks in the air.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Special_Skins">Special Skins</span></h2>
<p>Naming a Black Spider "Kumoko" (case sensitive) changes the appearance of the Black Spider to one with white skin. This skin is a reference to <a class="external text" href="https://kumodesu.fandom.com/wiki/Kumoko" rel="nofollow">Kumoko</a> from the "So I'm a Spider, So What?" series.
