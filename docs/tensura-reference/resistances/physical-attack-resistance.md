<p>Physical Attack Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents damage from physical attacks if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.<br/>
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>Get hit by physical attacks that deal 20+ dmg (e.g. tool/weapon damage, a monster hitting you, fall damage ...) (1-4 points)
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_exceptions...">The exceptions...</span></h3>
<p>Not be fire damage. <br/>
Not be explosion damage. <br/>
Not be caused by thorns. <br/>
Not be Tensura magic. <br/>
Not be generic magic damage. <br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Just get hit by physical attacks just like it says above, by the time you get Holy Attack Resistance, Pain Resistance and Magic Resistance you will most likely have this already</li></ul>
<p><br/>
<br/>
</p>
