<p>Wind Attack Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, it resists Wind Attacks if they fall below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and let someone use Aerial Blade or Wind Blade on you</li>
<li>If you are playing alone then let a Feathered Serpent (Medium Wind Spirit) or Sylhp (Greater Wind Spirit) hit you</li></ul>
