<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, it resists Water Attacks if they fall below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and let someone use Water Cutter on you</li>
<li>If you are playing alone then let the Undine that Hinata summons hit you</li></ul>
<p><br/>
<br/>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining_from_mod">Obtaining from mod</span></h3>
<p>When Aqua Frog attack you, you will get water resistance</p>
