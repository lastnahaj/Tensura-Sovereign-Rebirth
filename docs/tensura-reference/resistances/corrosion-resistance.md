<p>Corrosion Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, it <b>nullifies</b> the effects of <b>Wither</b> and prevents <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-corrosion/" title="Effects/Corrosion"><b>Corrosion</b></a> damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go fight <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-lord/" title="Mobs/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a> and let him hit you, if that doesn't work, try with an <a href="../../bosses/mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster">Orc Disaster</a></li></ul>
