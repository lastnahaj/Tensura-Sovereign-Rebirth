<p>Spatial Attack Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, it resists spatial Attacks if they fall below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and get someone to hit you with Spatial Domination using the Dimension Ray mode or Swipe</li>
<li>If you are playing alone let Winged Cats Or Akash attack you with their projectiles</li></ul>
