<p>Cold Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, it nullifies the effects of Chill and prevents Cold damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>If you are playing alone get hit by Ice Dragon's Ice Breath (Ice and Fire Mod)</li></ul>
<p>Recommended that you should have a way to get Fragility to increase the damage taken.
</p><p>Ex: fragility potions or the Shrink Spell.
<br/>
<br/>
</p>
