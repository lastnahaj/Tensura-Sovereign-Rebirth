<p>Spiritual Attack Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>To obtain spiritual attack resistance you need to get hit by an attack that deals spiritual damage (damage that affects your SHP). <br/>
</p><p>The damage needs to be 15+
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents Spiritual damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and let someone attack you with a weapon that does spirit damage for example Dead End Rainbow, or they could use a weapon with the Soul Eater engraving.</li>
<li>If you are playing alone then enjoy being jumped by Hinata, or drop a weapon with Soul Eater on the ground for a mob to pick up, and let them hit ya with it.</li>
<li>Certain Darkness Magics, Darkness Cannon, Shadow Bind, and True Darkness, deal Spiritual Attack Damage too.</li>
<li>Insanity also can deal Spiritual Damage, meaning if get high level of it, can be used to help get the Resistances.</li>
<li>Using Body Double and moving further away from the main body also deals spiritual damage.</li></ul>
