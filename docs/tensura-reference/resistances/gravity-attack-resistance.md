<p><b>Gravity Attack Resistance</b> is a <b>Resistance Skill</b> in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents <b>Gravity</b> damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved. Also grants immunity to <a class="external text" href="https://minecraft.fandom.com/wiki/Levitation" rel="nofollow">Levitation</a>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to Spirit Tree and let another player hit you with Oppressor or with a weapon that has 3 earth element cores on it</li>
<li>If you are playing alone go fight the War Gnome, he has an attack that creates a purple ball, that attack does gravity damage</li></ul>
