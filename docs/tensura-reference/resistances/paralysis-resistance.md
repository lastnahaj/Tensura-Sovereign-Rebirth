<p><b>Paralysis Resistance</b> is a <b>Resistance Skill</b> in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>Can be obtained by having the <a href="../../core-mechanics/effects-paralysis/" title="Effects/Paralysis">Paralysis effect</a> and taking (5+) physical damage multiple times. (until you learned it)
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents Paralysis damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP. Also prevents paralysis, slowness and minimg fatigue effects
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Get someone to use Paralysing Breath on you</li>
<li>If you are playing alone just get one or multiple Evil Centipede to use their Paralysing Breath on you</li></ul>
