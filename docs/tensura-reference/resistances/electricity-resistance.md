</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents <b>Lightning</b> damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirt Tree and get someone else to hit you with Electro Blast</li>
<li>If you are playing alone you can get it from getting hit by Sylphide (Greater Wind Elemental) or using the Ice and Fire mod to get hit by the breath of a Lighting Dragon though you should have a way to get Fragility Effect as normally they don't do enough damage, can make fragility potions or use the Shrink Spell.</li></ul>
