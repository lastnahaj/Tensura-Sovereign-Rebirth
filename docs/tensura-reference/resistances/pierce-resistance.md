<p>Pierce Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents damage from piercing attacks if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
<br/>
<br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtainment">Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>It is caused by any of the follow projectiles:
arrow, <a href="../../items/items-schematics-spear-schematic/" title="Items/Schematics/Spear Schematic">spear</a>, <a href="../../items/items-schematics-kunai-schematic/" title="Items/Schematics/Kunai Schematic">kunai</a> or trident.
<br/> <br/>
It is caused by a living entity wielding a weapon in their main hand, these can be:
a spear or a trident.
<br/> <br/>
<b>The attack must deal 20+ damage</b>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and get someone to beat you up with a spear</li>
<li>If you are playing alone you could just let Lizardmen hit you for this since they have spears</li></ul>
