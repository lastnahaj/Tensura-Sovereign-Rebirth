<p>Flame Attack Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents <b>Fire</b> damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to Spirit Tree and let another player hit you with Hellfire or Flare Circle</li>
<li>If you are playing alone just fight Ifrit (Greater Fire Elemental) and let him hit you</li></ul>
