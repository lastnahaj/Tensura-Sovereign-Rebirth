<p>Holy Attack Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents <b>Holy</b> damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and let another player hit you with a weapon that has Holy Weapon engraving on it, or they could use the Magic Aura skill and change the damage to Holy. Or drop a weapon that does holy damagr to a mob, let the mob pick it up and let them hit you with it.</li>
<li>Go fight the scary mean woman (Hinata) and get hit by her Melt Slash</li></ul>
