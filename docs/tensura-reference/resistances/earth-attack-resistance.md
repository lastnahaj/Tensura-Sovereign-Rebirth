<p>Earth Attack Resistance is a Resistance Skill in Tensura: Reincarnated.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents <b>Earth</b> damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and get another player to hit you with Earth Spikes</li>
<li>If you are playing alone your best option would be to fight the War Gnome (Greater Earth Elemental) that Hinata summons or plunder it from a Beast Gnome (Medium Earth Elemental)</li></ul>
