<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents damage from <b>Heat</b> if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and let other player hit you with Heat Wave</li>
<li>If you are playing alone go fight Shizu, she can also use Heat Wave or fight Ifrit, as every Flame Attack, also deals heat damage.</li></ul>
