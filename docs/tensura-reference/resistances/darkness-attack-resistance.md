</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p>When toggled, prevents <b>Darkness</b> damage if it falls below 50% of your Max HP.
If the attack is not below 50% of your Max HP the damage will be halved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>Go to the Spirit Tree and let another player hit you with Darkness Cannon (don't use armor, it will get destroyed VERY fast by Darkness Cannon}</li></ul>
