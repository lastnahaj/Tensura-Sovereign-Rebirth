<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span></h2>
<p>The lowest level of the daemon race. They spontaneously come into existence within the Daemon Realm, where they slowly accumulate experience from fighting and being summoned before eventually evolving into Greater Daemons.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_Ability">Racial Ability</span></h2>
<p>Creative flight (unaffected by <a href="../../magic/magic-jamming/" title="Magic Jamming">Magic Jamming</a>)
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Evolution">Evolution</span></h2>
<p><a href="../races-greater-daemon/" title="Races/Greater Daemon">Greater Daemon</a> - 20,000 EP or Harvest Festival
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span></h2>
<ul><li>Daemon going to a physical world without a name or awakening will get their EP limited at 100k MP + Summon Body's EP (if any) and 40k AP. This will affect energy regeneration, naming, EP drop, gear, awakening/race evolution and many skills activation/acquirement</li>
<li>Evolutions while possessing a body will gain the best stat from the body and evolution</li>
<li>if you don’t have a Unique suitable for Fighting you Should learn and Master Fire (Aspectual) to kill Daemon</li></ul>
