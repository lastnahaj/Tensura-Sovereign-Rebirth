<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_Ability">Racial Ability</span></h2>
<p>You get the innate ability of water breathing. you have no need for air as long as you're underwater
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span></h2>
<p>A sprite race descended from water elementals. Their fish-like bodies give them an insurmountable advantage in water.
</p><p>Higher chance to form a contract with Water Spirits
</p>
