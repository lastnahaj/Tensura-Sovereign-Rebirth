<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span></h2>
<p>A Vampiric thrall brought about by Blood Raise, highly weakened by sunlight.
</p><p>Weakness V, Mining Fatige V, Slowness V and Burn in Sunlight
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li>Wearing a helmet will prevent burning, although the helmets durability will slowly decrease while in the sun.</li>
<li>The debuffs will not apply while under a roof or when raining</li></ul></li></ul>
