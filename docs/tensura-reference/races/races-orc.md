<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span></h2>
<p>A race of beastfolk who lost the ability to shift between man and beast, resulting in a permanent mix of the two. Their physical strength is greater than average but pails in comparison to their original strength as beastfolk.
</p>
