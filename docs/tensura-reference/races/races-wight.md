<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Traits">Traits</span></h2>
<ul><li>Positive
<ul><li>Undead mobs are passive.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>Negative
<ul><li>When in sunlight, gains <i>Fragility III</i>, <i>Weakness III</i>, <i>Mining Fatigue III</i>, <i>Slowness III</i>, and <i>Burn</i> effects.</li>
<li>Wearing a helmet will prevent burning, although the helmets durability will slowly decrease while in the sun.</li>
<li>The debuffs will not apply while under a roof or when raining</li></ul></li></ul>
