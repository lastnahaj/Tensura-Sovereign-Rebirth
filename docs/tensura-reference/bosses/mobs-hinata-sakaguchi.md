<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h1>
<p><big><big>'"Good Luck"<i></i></big></big>
</p><p>A Rare otherworlder, One of the <b>strongest</b> bosses in the game at that. <br/>
Hostile towards the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../core-mechanics/races-alignment/" title="Races/Majin">"majin"</a> race (because lore wise, hates monsters), neutral towards non majin
</p><p>Will passively regenerate 2 HP + 20 SHP per second, and uses healing magic that heals 100 HP + 200 SHP every 10 seconds.
</p><p><b>Hinata cannot be tamed</b>
</p><p>Hinata is immune to ALL negative effects with the exception of:
</p>
<ul><li><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/other/anti-skill/" title="Abilities/Skills/Anti-Skill">Anti-Skill</a></li>
<li><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/unique/infinity-prison/" title="Abilities/Skills/Infinity Prison">Infinity Prison</a></li>
<li><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/unique/lust/" title="Abilities/Skills/Lust">Lust</a>'s Embracement</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/oppressor/" title="Oppressor">Oppressor</a>'s Oppression</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/merciless/" title="Merciless">Merciless</a>'s Soul Drain</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/sloth/" title="Sloth">Sloth</a>'s Drowsy</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="How_Aggro_works">How Aggro works</span></h2>
<p>Whenever a player is near, she will perform a series of checks to determine if she should attack them. Those checks are in the following sequence:
</p>
<ul><li>If the player's EP is below 10000, don't attack.</li>
<li>If the player is a <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../core-mechanics/races-alignment/" title="Races/Majin">Majin</a>, attack.</li>
<li>If the player is a <a href="../../races/races-dwarf/" title="Races/Dwarf">Dwarf</a>, <a href="../../races/races-elf/" title="Races/Elf">Elf</a>, or <a href="../../races/races-human/" title="Races/Human">Human</a>, don't attack.</li>
<li>Attack everyone else.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Spawn">Spawn</span></h2>
<p>A Rare Boss that has a 1.45% chance to spawn instead of an otherworlder. <br/>
<i>(1/69 chance in place of a normal otherworlder)</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Phases">Phases</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Phase 1</b>
<ul><li>Summons 5 Greater Spirits and Enters Fault Field Mode, After Greater Spirits are defeated, Hinata will Enter Phase 2</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Phase 2</b>
<ul><li>During this Phase, Hinata will only use her passive-abilities along with Dead End Rainbow to attack, with some teleportation. When her HP gets under 75%, she will enter Phase 3</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Phase 3</b>
<ul><li>Hinata will still continue using her swordsmanship, along with Spiritual Magics, <a href="../../magic/abilities-magics-hellfire/" title="Abilities/Magics/Hellfire">Hellfire</a>, <a href="../../magic/blizzard/" title="Blizzard">Blizzard</a>, <a href="../../magic/earth-jail/" title="Earth Jail">Earth Jail</a>, <a href="../../magic/aerial-blade/" title="Aerial Blade">Aerial Blade</a>. When her HP gets lower than 50% she will also start using Disintegration. During her cast, the targets will be slowed down, to cancel the attack, attack her with enough damage, If the Disintegration lands, the target is insta-killed, Once the HP bar reaches 25%, she will turn to phase 4.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Phase 4</b>
<ul><li>Upon entering this phase, Hinata will heal to 50% of her HP and this will be her new max HP as her passive regeneration becomes 10 HP + 50 SHP each second. During this phase, Hinata will use every move from last phases, but this time she will equip the Holy armaments and Moonlight sword along with Melt Slash move. If Melt Slash lands on targets they will get inflicted with 10x Hinata's damage ignoring armor(min 1000 damage), resistance and severance, followed by an after-damage of 100 damage every 10 ticks for 5 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Drops">Drops</span></h2>
<ul><li>Chance for <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Dead_End_Rainbow?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Dead End Rainbow (page does not exist)"> Dead End Rainbow</a></li>
<li>Chance in Stage 4 for <a class="new" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Moonlight?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" rel="nofollow" title="Moonlight (page does not exist)">Moonlight</a> + Holy Armament Armor</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tips">Tips</span></h2>
<ul><li>You can teleport out of Disintegration with Instant-move.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Recommended to have Law Manipulation with 1 million EP to Ignore Her Resistances and Immunities.</li></ul>
