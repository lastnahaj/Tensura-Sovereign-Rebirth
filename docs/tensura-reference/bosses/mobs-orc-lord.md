<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h1>
<p><big><big><i>"Eat, kill, all to satisfy my hunger"</i></big></big>
</p><p>While Fighting the Orc Lord/Disaster, when he become low health he wil start to consume his summoned orcs. Regaining his HP in the process<br/>
</p><p>Orc Lord can be tamed by mind control spells <a href="../../magic/demon-dominate/" title="Demon Dominate"> Demon Dominate</a> and <a href="../../magic/demon-marionette/" title="Demon Marionette"> Demon Marionette.</a>
</p><p>Caseoh is that you?
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Spawn">Spawn</span></h2>
<p>A Rare Boss that has a 1 in 900 chance at spawning instead of a <a href="../../mobs/mobs-orc/" title="Mobs/Orc">Orc</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Gelmud_Method">Gelmud Method</span></h3>
<p>If you feed a <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../mobs/mobs-orc/" title="Royal Orc">Royal Orc</a> 10 <a href="../../items/royal-blood/" title="Royal Blood">Royal Blood</a> that:
</p>
<ul><li>has 80k ep</li>
<li>has the <a href="../../skills/common/self-regeneration/" title="Self Regeneration">Self Regeneration</a> skill</li>
<li>is <b>not tamed/named</b></li></ul>
<p>The Royal Orc will evolve into an <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Orc Lord</a>. This Orc Lord will be <b>Neutral</b> towards you.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Evolution_Method">Evolution Method</span></h2>
<p>The evolution of an Orc Lord into an <a href="../mobs-orc-disaster/" title="Mobs/Orc Disaster">Orc Disaster</a> occurs when it:
</p>
<ul><li>Possesses 200k EP.</li></ul>
<p>The Orc Lord will do a brief animation before fully evolving into an Orc Disaster.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining_EP">Obtaining EP</span></h2>
<p>Orc Lords gain EP when they kill nearby mobs.
</p>
<ul><li>Summoned Orcs give their Orc Lord EP and Armour Points upon killing mobs.</li>
<li>An Orc Lord consuming summoned Orcs will also <b>not</b> grant them any EP or Armour Points.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Quick_Evolution_Method">Quick Evolution Method</span></h2>
<p>Here are 5 easy steps to evolve an Orc Lord, into an Orc Disaster:
</p><p>1. Locate a handfull of <a href="../../mobs/mobs-knight-spider/" title="Mobs/Knight Spider">Knight Spiders</a> and <a href="../../core-mechanics/naming/" title="Naming">endow</a> them.
</p><p>2. Reduce their HP so they are less of a threat to the Orc Lord and allowing for swift kills.
</p><p>4. Kill the summoned Orcs so the Orc Lord is the one to kill the Knight Spiders.
</p><p>5. Bring the Knight Spiders to the Orc Lord so it can kill them all.
</p><p>Repeat those simple 5 steps until the Orc Lord evolves into an Orc Disaster.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skill_obtainment">Skill obtainment</span></h2>
<p>Upon killing an Orc Lord, one will obtain the skill <a href="../../skills/common/corrosion/" title="Corrosion">Corrosion</a> (<b>not learned</b>).
</p><p>Upon predating the Orc Lord using the unique skill <a href="../../skills/unique/predator/" title="Predator">Predator</a>, the player will also obtain the Orc Lord's own unique skill which is <a href="../../skills/unique/starved/" title="Starved">Starved</a>. Which when Starved is mastered along with Predator, will cause both to combine into the unqiue skill <a href="../../skills/unique/gluttony/" title="Gluttony">Gluttony</a>.
</p>
