<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Spawnrate">Spawnrate</span></h3>
<p>A rare mob that has a 1/1000 chance to spawn in place of a normal <a href="../../mobs/mobs-slime/" title="Mobs/Slime">Slime</a>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Damage_type">Damage type</span></h3>
<p>Deals Corrosion damage <b>(BUT NOT CORROSION EFFECT)</b> , so if you have <a href="../../resistances/corrosion-resistance/" title="Corrosion Resistance">Corrosion Resistance</a> or <a href="../../resistances/corrosion-nullification/" title="Corrosion Nullification">Nullification</a> you can be immune to slime damage.
</p><p>This can also be used to obtain <a href="../../resistances/corrosion-resistance/" title="Corrosion Resistance">Corrosion Resistance</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Note">Note</span></h3>
<p>Supermassive Slime won't eat Beam, Breath, Barrier or certain projectile attacks.<br/>
Supermassive Slime regains 100 MP for every block it breaks and regains MP equal to the EP of mobs killed by its attacks.<br/>
Supermassive Slime can be tamed with mind control spells <a href="../../magic/demon-dominate/" title="Demon Dominate"> Demon Dominate</a> and <a href="../../magic/demon-marionette/" title="Demon Marionette"> Demon Marionette</a>
</p>
