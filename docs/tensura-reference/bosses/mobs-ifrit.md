<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h1>
<p>The Greater Fire Spirit that is one of the many spirits that Hinata has, but also inhabits Shizu's body.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Spawn">Spawn</span></h2>
<p>This spirit spawns during the first phase of <a href="../mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi">Hinata Sakaguchi</a> <br/>
</p><p>This spirit also spawns during the second phase of <a href="../mobs-shizu/" title="Mobs/Shizu">Shizu</a>
</p><p>You can also summon this spirit (if you have it) yourself, using <a href="../../magic/summon-greater-elemental/" title="Summon Greater Elemental">Summon Greater Elemental</a><br/>
<i>However, this will not give any drops</i>
</p><p>Will not naturally spawn with Majin alignment.
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li>Being named by a Majin will change its alignment to Majin</li></ul></li></ul>
