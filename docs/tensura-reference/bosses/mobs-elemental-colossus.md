<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Respawn_Rules">Respawn Rules</span></h2>
<p>The Elemental Colossus can be respawned infinitely by throwing a <a href="../../blocks/blocks-block-of-pure-magisteel/" title="Blocks/Block of Pure Magisteel">Block of Pure Magisteel</a> at the middle of the <a href="../../structures/structures-labyrinth-tree/" title="Structures/Labyrinth Tree">Labyrinth</a> Arena. <br/>
Elemental Colossus now will respawn when the gamerule [ColossusRespawn] is on if the player reset character/race<br/>
Existence Points are awarded only once per player upon their <i><b>first</b></i> successful defeat of the colossus.<br/>
<b>Subsequent respawns defeated by the same player will not yield additional EP.</b>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Note">Note</span></h2>
<ul><li>The Elemental Colossus will only spawn when reaching the arena</li>
<li>The Elemental Colossus will only aggro  targets within 40 blocks from the center of the area. [Can be changed with /tensura worldData labyrinth colossusPosition areaRadius]</li></ul>
