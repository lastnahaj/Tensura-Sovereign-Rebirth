<p><br/>
<i>"The Fairy's not gonna like this..."</i>
</p><p>The <b>Elemental Colossus</b> is a powerful boss found within the <a href="../../structures/structures-labyrinth-tree/" title="Structures/Labyrinth Tree">Labyrinth</a>. It primarily specializes in close-range combat, using melee attacks, a spinning attack, grappling, and a powerful leap attack.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary">Summary</span></h2>
<p>The Elemental Colossus is a heavily armored melee boss that can both fight nearby targets and rapidly close large distances. Its attacks include melee strikes, a spinning attack, grabbing and throwing targets, and a leap that can end in a powerful slam.
</p><p>It also has several damage and status-effect immunities and regenerates health over time.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Combat">Combat</span></h2>
<p>The Elemental Colossus primarily fights using physical attacks.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Melee_Attack">Melee Attack</span></h3>
<p>The Colossus performs repeated melee attacks against nearby targets.
</p><p>Its base attack damage is <b>50</b>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Spin_Attack">Spin Attack</span></h3>
<p>The Colossus can perform a spinning attack that damages nearby targets and applies knockback.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Damage:</b> 1.5× attack damage</li>
<li><b>Area:</b> 4 blocks beyond its bounding box</li>
<li><b>Selection chance:</b> 30%</li></ul>
<p>The spin attack is performed from ticks 10 through 20 of its attack behavior when selected.
</p><p>For the Colossus, this means the attack deals <b>75 damage</b> before other modifiers.
</p>
<div class="mw-highlight mw-highlight-lang-java mw-content-ltr mw-highlight-copy" dir="ltr"><pre><span></span><span class="n">attackDamage</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">*</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mf">1.5</span>
</pre></div>
<p>Here, <b>attackDamage</b> refers to the Colossus's base attack damage of 50.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Grab_and_Throw">Grab and Throw</span></h3>
<p>The Colossus can grab nearby targets and force them to ride on its body before throwing them away.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Grab range:</b> 5 blocks</li>
<li><b>Grab damage:</b> 0.5× attack damage</li>
<li><b>Throw damage:</b> 2× attack damage</li>
<li><b>Throw velocity:</b> Magnitude of 5 in the direction the Colossus is facing, with an additional 1.0 vertical velocity</li></ul>
<p>With 50 base attack damage, the grab deals <b>25 damage</b> and the throw deals <b>100 damage</b> before other modifiers.
</p>
<div class="mw-highlight mw-highlight-lang-java mw-content-ltr mw-highlight-copy" dir="ltr"><pre><span></span><span class="n">doHurtTarget</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">target</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mf">0.5f</span><span class="p">);</span>
</pre></div>
<p>The multiplier causes the normal attack damage to be reduced to half for the grab.
</p>
<div class="mw-highlight mw-highlight-lang-java mw-content-ltr mw-highlight-copy" dir="ltr"><pre><span></span><span class="n">doHurtTarget</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">target</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mf">2.0f</span><span class="p">);</span>
</pre></div>
<p>The same attack function is instead given a multiplier of 2 to deal double damage for the throw.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Leap">Leap</span></h3>
<p>The Colossus can leap toward distant targets to close large gaps.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Minimum range:</b> 10 blocks</li>
<li><b>Maximum range:</b> 64 blocks</li>
<li><b>Attack interval:</b> 30 ticks</li></ul>
<p>The leap activates the Colossus's slamming state.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Slam_Attack">Slam Attack</span></h3>
<p>If the Colossus falls more than 3 blocks while in its slamming state, it performs a powerful slam attack.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Area:</b> 10 × 10 × 10 blocks</li>
<li><b>Damage:</b> 2× attack damage</li>
<li><b>Knockback:</b> Yes</li>
<li><b>Explosion radius:</b> 3 blocks</li>
<li><b>Block destruction:</b> Can destroy blocks tagged as Earth Skill Breakable when Mob Griefing is enabled</li></ul>
<p>With 50 base attack damage, the slam deals <b>100 damage</b> before other modifiers.
</p>
<div class="mw-highlight mw-highlight-lang-java mw-content-ltr mw-highlight-copy" dir="ltr"><pre><span></span><span class="n">isSlammingFall</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&amp;&amp;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">fallDistance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mf">3.0f</span>
</pre></div>
<p>This condition means the slam requires the Colossus to be in its slamming state and to have fallen more than 3 blocks.
</p><p>The slam also creates an explosion at the point of impact. The Colossus does not take normal fall damage when performing a slam.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Defenses">Defenses</span></h2>
<p>The Elemental Colossus possesses several resistances and immunities.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Damage_Resistances">Damage Resistances</span></h3>
<ul><li>Heat and Cold damage, due to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../resistances/thermal-fluctuation-resistance/" title="Abilities/Skills/Thermal Fluctuation Resistance">Thermal Fluctuation Resistance</a>.</li>
<li>Damage classified as a Natural Effect is reduced to 20% of its original amount.</li></ul>
<p>The Natural Effect reduction means that only one-fifth of the original damage is applied.
</p>
<div class="mw-highlight mw-highlight-lang-java mw-content-ltr mw-highlight-copy" dir="ltr"><pre><span></span><span class="k">if</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">TensuraDamageHelper</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">isNaturalEffects</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">source</span><span class="p">))</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">amount</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">*=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mf">0.2f</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</pre></div>
<p>Here, <b>source</b> is the damage source being checked, while <b>amount</b> is the amount of damage that would otherwise be dealt.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Damage_Immunities">Damage Immunities</span></h3>
<p>The Colossus is immune to:
</p>
<ul><li>Projectile attacks classified as Piercing.</li>
<li>IN_WALL damage, such as damage from being inside a block.</li>
<li>Poking damage, such as damage from berry bushes and cacti.</li>
<li>HOT_FLOOR damage, such as damage from standing on magma blocks.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Status_Effect_Immunities">Status Effect Immunities</span></h2>
<p>The Colossus cannot be affected by:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison</li>
<li>Fatal Poison</li>
<li>Paralysis</li>
<li>Infection</li>
<li>Burden</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Regeneration">Regeneration</span></h2>
<p>The Elemental Colossus regenerates health depending on its current state.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Normal:</b> 10 HP every 2 seconds</li>
<li><b>Sitting:</b> 20 HP every second</li>
<li><b>Sleeping:</b> 30 HP every second</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Behavior">Behavior</span></h2>
<p>The Elemental Colossus uses ground-based navigation and can detect targets within 64 blocks.
</p><p>Its actual aggression area is restricted by the configured Labyrinth Colossus area.
</p><p>During combat, the Colossus can use melee attacks, spin attacks, grab-and-throw attacks, and leap attacks.
</p><p>The Colossus possesses complete knockback resistance.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sleep">Sleep</span></h3>
<p>When naturally spawned, the Elemental Colossus initially begins in a sleeping state.
</p><p>While sleeping:
</p>
<ul><li>It regenerates <b>30 HP per second</b>.</li>
<li>Incoming damage is reduced to <b>10%</b> of its normal amount.</li></ul>
<p>The Colossus wakes when it becomes angry, becomes a vehicle or passenger, or should follow its owner.
</p>

<li>1 <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/elemental-essence/" title="Elemental Essence"></a></span> <a href="../../items/elemental-essence/" title="Elemental Essence"><span class="sprite-text">Elemental Essence</span></a></span> (around 10% chance)</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Respawn_Rules">Respawn Rules</span></h2>
<p>The Elemental Colossus can be respawned infinitely by throwing a <a href="../../blocks/blocks-block-of-pure-magisteel/" title="Blocks/Block of Pure Magisteel">Block of Pure Magisteel</a> at the middle of the <a href="../../structures/structures-labyrinth-tree/" title="Structures/Labyrinth Tree">Labyrinth</a> Arena.
</p><p>The Elemental Colossus will also respawn when the gamerule <b>[ColossusRespawn]</b> is enabled if the player resets their character or race.
</p><p>Existence Points are awarded only once per player upon their <b>first</b> successful defeat of the Colossus.
</p><p><b>Subsequent respawns defeated by the same player will not yield additional EP.</b>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Note">Note</span></h2>
<ul><li>The Elemental Colossus will only spawn when reaching the arena.</li>
<li>The Elemental Colossus will only aggro targets within 40 blocks from the center of the area. [Can be changed with /tensura worldData labyrinth colossusPosition areaRadius]</li>
<li>Although the Elemental Colossus inherits taming functionality, it has no direct method for being tamed.</li></ul>
