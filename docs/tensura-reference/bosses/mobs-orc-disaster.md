<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h1>
<h3><span id=".22EAT.2C_KILL.2C_ALL_TO_SATISFY_MY_LUST.22"></span><span class="mw-headline" id='"EAT,_KILL,_ALL_TO_SATISFY_MY_LUST"'><b>"EAT, KILL, ALL TO SATISFY MY LUST"</b></span></h3>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Spawn">Spawn</span></h2>
<p>A Boss that appears after an <a href="../mobs-orc-lord/" title="Mobs/Orc Lord">Orc Lord</a> reaches 200k EP
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skill_obtainment">Skill obtainment</span></h2>
<p>Upon killing an Orc Disaster, one will obtain the skill <a href="../../skills/common/corrosion/" title="Corrosion">Corrosion</a> (<b>not learned</b>).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Note">Note</span></h2>
<p>The Orc Disasters summoned subordinates will not gain ep themselves, but transfer it to the Orc Disaster on kill.<br/>
</p><p>The Orc Disaster is tameable through the mind control spells <a href="../../magic/demon-dominate/" title="Demon Dominate"> Demon Dominate</a> and <a href="../../magic/demon-marionette/" title="Demon Marionette"> Demon Marionette</a>
</p>
