<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h1>
<p><big><big>'"Scary dwarf"<i></i></big></big>
</p><p>The king of the dwarves, One of the <b>strongest</b> bosses in the game with the sole exception of <a href="../mobs-hinata-sakaguchi/" title="Mobs/Hinata Sakaguchi">Hinata Sakaguchi</a> of course. <br/>
Hostile towards those who enter his chamber to challenge him and those who are considered criminals among the dwarves
</p><p><b>Gazel cannot be tamed</b>
</p><p>Gazel is immune to ALL negative effects with the exception of:
</p>
<ul><li><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/other/anti-skill/" title="Abilities/Skills/Anti-Skill">Anti-Skill</a></li>
<li><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/unique/infinity-prison/" title="Abilities/Skills/Infinity Prison">Infinity Prison</a></li>
<li><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../skills/unique/lust/" title="Abilities/Skills/Lust">Lust</a>'s Embracement</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/oppressor/" title="Oppressor">Oppressor</a>'s Oppression</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/merciless/" title="Merciless">Merciless</a>'s Soul Drain</li>
<li><a href="../../skills/unique/sloth/" title="Sloth">Sloth</a>'s Drowsy</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="How_Aggro_works">How Aggro works</span></h2>
<p>Whenever a player enter his chamber, if the player has positive reputation you will have to right click Gazel for him to begin attacking, if the player has negative reputation he will attack the second you enter the chamber
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Spawn">Spawn</span></h2>
<p>A Boss that can be fought by having 100 or -100 reputation with dwarves and going through the warp pad in their village
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Phases">Phases</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Phase 1</b>
<ul><li>Attacks the player mainly using his magic, specifically Earth Spikes and his weapon, once you lower his HP enough he will enter his second phase covering himself in a red aura</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Phase 2</b>
<ul><li>During this Phase, Gazel will start using his battlewill more dealing great damage to the player and also teleporting to the player to attack with his sword</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Drops">Drops</span></h2>
