## Where it appears

The wind portal is one of the three elemental portals with a complete generation registration in Mysticism 2.1.2.

## What this reference confirms

Fire, space, and water portal templates exist in the artifact, but they have no matching worldgen structure definitions or structure sets and are therefore not listed as generated structures.

The structure, structure set, and applicable biome tag are present in the pinned artifact. Server configuration, pregenerated terrain, or an existing world border can still affect what players encounter.
