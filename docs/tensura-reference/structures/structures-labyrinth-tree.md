<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Utility">Utility</span></h2>
<p>The labyrinth tree is used to enter the <b>Labyrinth</b>
</p><p>To enter the labyrinth one must need <b>8000 EP</b>, because else they will die to magic poisoning inside the magic labyrinth and basically will get kicked out (does not take away EP)
</p><p>You will also regen magicules faster while being inside the Labyrinth
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Locating">Locating</span></h2>
<p>A <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="../../items/explorer-maps/" title="Labyrinth Explorer Map"></a></span> <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../items/explorer-maps/" title="Labyrinth Explorer Map"><span class="sprite-text">Labyrinth Explorer Map</span></a></span> can be acquired from a master level <a href="../../mobs/mobs-dwarf/" title="Mobs/Dwarf">Cartographer Dwarf</a>, which leads towards a nearby Labyrinth Tree.
</p>
<h1><span id="The_Labyrinth_.28dimension.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Labyrinth_(dimension)">The Labyrinth (dimension)</span></h1>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner"><a class="image" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Labyrinth_arena.png"><img class="thumbimage" data-file-height="1369" data-file-width="2543" decoding="async" height="172" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/structures/labyrinth-arena-8fd18214ea.png" width="320"/></a> <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a class="internal" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Labyrinth_arena.png" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Arena in which you can fight the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-elemental-colossus/" title="Mobs/Elemental Colossus">Elemental Colossus</a></div></div></div>
<p>Upon entry of the labyrinth one will face a big hallway, with a stair going upwards leading to the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-elemental-colossus/" title="Mobs/Elemental Colossus">Elemental Colossus</a> spawn platform. <br/>
</p><p>You <b>CANNOT</b> avoid the colossus and go behind it to get a spirit, a barrier will stop you
</p><p>There are 2 outcomes once you face the colossus, each giving you the option to enter the labyrinth.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Outcome_1:_Death">Outcome 1: Death</span></h2>
<p>Once you die to the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-elemental-colossus/" title="Mobs/Elemental Colossus">Elemental Colossus</a> you will get the advancement "Chill, its just a test" which will teleport you beyond the barrier, which will still allow you to get spirits.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Outcome_2:_Victory">Outcome 2: Victory</span></h2>
<p>Once you win against the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-elemental-colossus/" title="Mobs/Elemental Colossus">Elemental Colossus</a> you will get the advancement "The fairy wont be happy with this", upon it's death (<b>If the gamerule respawncolossus isnt active</b>) will grant you a pure magisteel block. This can be used to respawn the colossus by dropping the block in the middle of the arena.
</p><p>When the <code>respawncolossus</code> gamerule is active, this is not the case. You will <b>NOT</b> get a pure magisteel block for killing the colossus.
</p>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Obtaining_spirits">Obtaining spirits</span></h1>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner"><a class="image" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Labyrinth_crystal.png"><img class="thumbimage" data-file-height="1369" data-file-width="1204" decoding="async" height="364" src="../../../assets/upstream/tensura/items/labyrinth-crystal-754c2177dd.png" width="320"/></a> <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a class="internal" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Labyrinth_crystal.png" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The circular area on top is where the player can pray for spirits.</div></div></div>
<p>After defeating (or dying to) the <a href="../../bosses/mobs-elemental-colossus/" title="Mobs/Elemental Colossus">Elemental Colossus</a>, one can go to the direction of the stairs leading upwards to the Praying platform and sneak on the Praying Path for 20 seconds for a chance to get a Spirit.
</p><p>Once they either succeed or fail to get a Spirit, they will have a <b>20 minute cooldown on praying</b>, after the cooldown runs out, they can pray again, if players pray while they’re still on cooldown, they will get teleported to the entrance.
</p>
<ul><li>40% chance for a Lesser Elemental.</li>
<li>20% chance for a Medium Elemental.</li>
<li>10% chance for a Greater Elemental.</li>
<li>1% chance for an Elemental Lord if the player already has a Medium or Greater spirit of the Element.</li></ul>
<p><i>Some races have higher chance to obtain certain Elementals</i>
</p><p>100% Chance on Lesser, x2 chance on Medium &amp; Greater
</p>
<ul><li><a href="../../races/races-dwarf/" title="Races/Dwarf">Dwarf</a> - Earth</li>
<li><a href="../../races/races-elf/" title="Races/Elf">Elf</a> - Wind</li>
<li><a href="../../races/races-merfolk/" title="Races/Merfolk">Merfolk</a> - Water</li>
<li><a href="../../races/races-goblin/" title="Races/Goblin">Goblin</a> &amp; <a href="../../races/races-ogre/" title="Races/Ogre">Ogre</a> - Fire</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Spirit_tiers">Spirit tiers</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lesser">Lesser</span></h3>
<p>These spirits have low magical power, thus only giving you only manipulation (which you need to learn) and their lesser spell.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Medium">Medium</span></h3>
<p>These spirits have better magic than lesser, giving you multiple spells and giving you the manipulation (which you need to learn). <br/>
These can also be summoned using the <a href="../../magic/summon-medium-elemental/" title="Summon Medium Elemental">Summon Medium Elemental</a> skill.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Greater">Greater</span></h3>
<p>These are close to the strongest spirits which give you all the respective skills. This spirit type is also needed to unlock the <a href="../../core-mechanics/races-awakening/" title="Races/Awakening">True Hero</a> awakening. <br/>
These again can be summoned using <a href="../../magic/summon-greater-elemental/" title="Summon Greater Elemental">Summon Greater Elemental</a>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lord">Lord</span></h3>
<p>This is the strongest spirit, <i><b>as of now it is equal to greater spirit</b></i>. You will not get anything extra, however this type of spirit is very rare to get. <br/> <br/>
<big> <b>If one gains a lord or greater, they will also have the respective benefits of the lesser and medium.</b> </big> <br/>
<small>(aka it goes: Lord (has all benefits of all other SAME ELEMENT spirits) -&gt; Greater -&gt; Medium -&gt; Lesser (has only lesser spells and manipulation)<b></b></small>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Alternate_Obtainment">Alternate Obtainment</span></h2>
<p>Wild spirits can be found around the world. These wild spirits are neutral, nameable and wild greater spirits will not have a boss hp bar.<br/>
Small chance to obtain spirit level, magics and manipulation will become available to learn when naming a wild spirit:
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li>Medium Spirit - 15% chance</li>
<li>Greater Spirit - 5% chance</li></ul></li></ul>
