<div class="tensura-reference-article">
<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" dir="ltr" lang="en"><p>Tachi's are the combination of the <a href="../../items/items-schematics-japanese-schematic/" title="Items/Schematics/Japanese Schematic">Japanese Schematic</a> and the <a href="../../items/items-schematics-long-sword-schematic/" title="Items/Schematics/Long Sword Schematic">Long Sword Schematic</a> which do mid-range damage, have buffed attack reach, and have a 100% sweeping chance.
</p><p>- The Tachi is a 2 handed blade allowing it to benefit from not having any item in your offhand, increasing in damage by 1.
</p>
