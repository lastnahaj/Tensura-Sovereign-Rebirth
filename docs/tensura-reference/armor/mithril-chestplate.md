<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<p>Gives armor points to help defend the user against incoming attacks.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Unique_Engravement">Unique <a href="../../core-mechanics/engravings/" title="Engravings">Engravement</a></span></h3>
<p>As unique property it has <b>Holy Coat 2</b> <i>(due to it being made out of mithril)</i>. <br/>
<big><b>This will be passed down to its evolution.</b></big>
</p>
