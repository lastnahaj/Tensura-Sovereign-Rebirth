<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills_Gained">Skills Gained</span></h3>
<p>On learning Tsukuyomi, gain the Spatial Manipulation and Multilayer Barrier skills.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration - Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Universal Perception - Enhance your senses to the point of detecting heat, sounds and magicules at an enhanced level.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Spiritual Manipulation - Prevents the user from being affected by any mind control skills like [Charm]. Also grants the user [Spiritual Attack Nullification].</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, In-Slot] Insta-Kill - Attempts to instantly kill the target's Spiritual Body on the next attack. Costs: 50K MP.
<ul><li>If the target has Spiritual Attack Resistance, deals 10000 + 50% of their Spiritual Health.</li>
<li>If the target has Spiritual Attack Nullification, deals 5000 + 25% of their Spiritual Health.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>On mastery, the target's Spiritual Attack Resistance is treated as null, allowing you to instantly kill the target if they do not have Spiritual Attack Nullification.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] Eye of the Moon: Puts the user into an enhanced Shadow Motion. All of the user's skills still work while in Shadow Motion and the user can still do damage. Clones can also do the same. However, being exposed to bright light will draw you out of the shadows. Costs: 350K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Ultraspeed Action - Dash forward 20 blocks (25 on mastery), dealing critical damage + 75 as well as deal half of the target's spiritual health. On mastery, deal critical damage + 300 as well as attempt to instantly kill the target's spiritual body. Same restrictions of Insta-Kill apply. Costs: Nothing?! oh wait. 100K AP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Parallel Existence - Create clones that do not copy your equipment but can utilise your skills and deal damage to your target. Costs: 75K MP.</li></ul>
