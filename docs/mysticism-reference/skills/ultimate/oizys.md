<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Abilities/Skills/Thought_Acceleration">Thought Acceleration</a> - Speeds up thinking to an insane degree, giving you much greater reduced casting time, movement speed and attack speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Abilities/Skills/Universal_Perception">Universal Perception</a> - No Change.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Oizys' Sorrow - All players and mobs, regardless of EP, are slowed by the user's baneful aura. Affected entities gain Slowness 8 for 10 seconds while near the wielder. Wielder gains Resistance 3 passively. Additionally, all attacks will degrade immunities.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (100.0 MP)] Gust - Generate strong blasts of wind to throw and manipulate objects or deadly, sharpened wind. Holding crouch will make your wind blasts explosive. ITEMS WILL NOT BE EXPLOSIVE. No cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (10K MP.)] Baneful Requiem - Cry out, knocking back entities within 8 blocks with moderate knock back and inflicting them with blindness 2, weakness 4 AND Paralysis 4 their movements for 10 seconds. On mastery, the effects are applied for 20 seconds. Cooldown of 20 seconds. Costs 10K MP to use.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Turbulent Force - Pick up and control the movements of an entity you pick up. Maximum height of 10 blocks. (20 on mastery). While holding onto an entity, LEFT CLICK to CRUSH them (Costs: 500K MP), dealing massive Gravity and Wind damage. On crushing an entity, Oizys gains a 15 second cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] Circus Show - Make them laugh, make them cry, make them regret they were ever born. All entities entering your personal space of 10 blocks will forcibly turn their head towards you and be unable to move. Costs 10000 MP/second to use. Costs: 50K MP.</li></ul>
