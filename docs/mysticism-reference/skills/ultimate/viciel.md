<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] Ruling Authority - Gives Inspiration 1 to every subordinate under the user. Gives Inspiration 2 on mastery.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Hidden In Darkness - When toggled, your presence is removed from the TAB list and untraceable via players typing out your name and trying to fill it in via tab button. Mobs will ignore players presence as long as they aren't close.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Master Of Darkness - Learn and master skills/battlewill/magic related to darkness or hiding. Example, Shadow Motion, Spells from the Darkness Spirit. Formhide battlewill, etc.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] Sound of Silence - All sounds made by the user are silenced, mining, walking/running, etc.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] Shadow Dominance - User is buffed in shadows or at night, given an attack damage and movement speed boost</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] True Copy - Open a GUI. Become able to select one skill from the target to copy, same as <a href="../../unique/hidden-ruler/" title="Hidden Ruler">Hidden Ruler</a> but the targets Uniques, Nullification's and Resistance's are also able to be copied. Copying a skill will transfer it to a "copy" bank. Shift and Use to check your skill bank. This skill is temporary and will be lost on death.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] True Paste - Open a GUI. Become able to select one skill from your skill bank to paste it onto a target, this includes Nullification's and Resistance's. Pasting will remove the skill from your bank and temporarily give them the skill until they die.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Darkness Conscription - Forcefully tame/mob thats in the shadows or in darkness permanently. Will not work on mobs in high light levels. Costs: 100K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Shadow Jump - Shift and Use places a marker. It HAS to be a dark location, otherwise it fails.. USING again without shifting and inside a low light level allows you to teleport to your placed marker. If you crouch and use on an entity, the entity becomes marked and the user can jump to them, even through dimensions. Costs: 50K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Petrichor Stained Destiny - All subordinates under you immediately gain temporary Strengthen 5 and Speed 1 for two minutes. Crouching will make all subordinates jump to your location immediately, on top of the effects being bestowed. On mastery, the effects are doubled and the duration becomes five minutes. Costs: 150K MP.</li></ul>
