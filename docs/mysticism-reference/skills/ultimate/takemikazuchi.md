<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Martial Mastery: Instantly learn any Battlewill. Gain 20 mastery points for battlewills every time one is used.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration - Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Melee Master - Increases attack speed by flat 2.0F</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Lord of Combat - All melee attacks deal 5x damage (10x with mastery).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Precognition - Makes the player able to dodge projectiles with 100% accuracy. Able to dodge melee attacks with 50% accuracy. (80% with Mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Sacred Sword - All your melee attacks bypass resistances and degrade nullifications. Additionally, gain 1% of AP back every 5 seconds.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active] Lightspeed Acceleration - Teleport the player 20 blocks (25 on mastery) forward, slashing and dealing (75 + Critical Attack Damage) x 5 (via Lord of Combat). On mastery, the formula becomes (300 + Critical Attack Damage) x 10. to anything on the way. Costs 100K AP.</li></ul>
