<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Unyielding - Grant hope to your allies. All allies in a 15 block radius from the user receives the Inspiration and Ally Boost effect. Additionally, all nearby allies gain 3 Unyielding Points every 10 seconds. On mastery, nearby allies gain Inspiration 2 and Ally Boost 2, and gain 4 Unyielding Points instead.</li></ul>
<p>Allow it to go up to 1800 instead of 1200 and gain ally Ultimate Skills at 1800 points. 1201 - 1799 points don't do anything.
If the subordinate is a player, the owner can now copy the target's Extra Skills and below if it is not hardcore mode. Resistances are included, but Nullifications will not be copied.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] A Buff to the users physical and battlewill as well as allowing their attacks to bypass Physical Attack Resistance (Nullification is also treated as Resistance) and damage Spiritual Lifeforms.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Accel -  Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Sacred Haki - Inflict Fear on nearby entities as well as letting all allies within the activation radius healed by 1200HP every 5 seconds (100 ticks) and have every negative status effects removed. Costs: 10K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Backup - Only takes 10% to create a backup.  The rest remains the same (like crouch to dismiss and press again to switch between bodies). On mastery, your backup will gain ally boost 1 even when you're not nearby and it will only take 5% to create a backup.  Your backup will attack those nearby when the skill is mastered.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Return - Returns the number of Unyielding points that your subordinate has when looking at one.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Art, In Slot] Fortitude - Drain the life of the target by coating your next attack with the raw unfiltered life-reaping energy of [Sariel]. This mode applies a cooldown of 15 seconds to [Sariel]. Costs: 300K MP.</li></ul>
<p>If your target does not possess an Ultimate Skill, instantly kill the target.
If the target possesses an Ultimate Skill, they will be protected from instant death, but instead take heavy damage and receive Anti-Skill. Based on the following:
</p>
<ul><li>If the target has 100% to 110% EP from your EP, deal 90% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount.</li>
<li>If the target has 110% to 120% EP from your EP, deal 80% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount.</li>
<li>If the target has 120% to 130% EP from your EP, deal 70% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount.</li>
<li>If the target has 130% to 140% EP from your EP, deal 60% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount.</li>
<li>If the target has 140% to 150% EP from your EP, deal 50% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount.</li>
<li>If the target has more than 150% of your EP, deal 25% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount.</li></ul>
