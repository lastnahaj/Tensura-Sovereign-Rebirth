<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] Unbreakable Will – Grants immunity to psychological effects such as Insanity and Charm. Additionally, lethal damage (damage that would reduce HP to 0) is negated once every 10 minutes, granting 3 seconds of invulnerability and displaying an on-screen warning when HP reaches 0.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Conqueror's Might – The user's physical attacks ignore Physical Attack Resistance (Nullification is treated as Resistance). Damage to Spiritual Lifeforms is increased by 30%.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Thought Acceleration –  Increase magic casting speed multiplier by 2, increase movement speed by 0.01 (0.02 with Mastery), increase attack speed by 0.2 (0.4 with Mastery)</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>Active - Hold] Kaiser's Dominion – Releases an overwhelming aura that inflicts Fear on enemies within a 10-block radius for 10 seconds (when the skill button is released). Simultaneously, heals all allies/subordinates within range for 25 HP per second and removes all negative status effects from allies. Costs 50K MP per second and has a Cooldown of 30s.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Relentless Echo – Inflict Level 2 (Level 4 If Mastered) Weakness, Slowness, Fragility and Burden for 15 seconds (30 Seconds on mastery) to a single target that you look at in a 10 block radius. Costs: 30K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Invincible Victory – Grants the user invulnerability for 30 seconds. HP, MP, and AP regeneration are disabled during this time. The user suffers a weakened state for 10 seconds after the effect ends. Costs 75K MP and has a Cooldown of 45s.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Conquest – Unleashes a 10-block arc slash, dealing damage that equates to <i>(target max hp/2 + current damage)</i>. Bypasses Physical Attack Nullification and destroys all barriers on the target. Costs 150K MP and has a Cooldown of 20s.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press {Requires Mastery)] Glorious Verdict – Targets a single enemy within 20 blocks. If the target has less than 25% of the user's Max EP, they lose 30% of their remaining MP and AP. If the target has more than 25%, they lose 15% MP and AP instead. Only works on targets with 500K EP or above. Costs 500K MP and has a Cooldown of 20s. Same applies to mobs except EP instead of MP and AP.</li></ul>
