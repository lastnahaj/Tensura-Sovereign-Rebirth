<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>Counterforce</b> - 25% chance to reflect all incoming damage with double the output. (50% on mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] <b>Equilibrium Shift</b> - When having 15% hp or less, removes all debuffs and gives it to non allied entities in a 10x10 area as well as takes 30% of their current hp and use it to heal yourself.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Malice Exchange</b> - Takes yours or an entities debuffs and lets you put it onto other entities.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Duality Shift</b> -  Lets you swap the places of yourself with a different entity.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Vital Shift</b> - 25% chance to swap hp with target if they have more hp, chance raises to 50% when mastered, cant go above your maximum hp.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Absolute Antithesis</b> - Lets you counter any damage with 100% certainty for 2 minutes, has a 5m cooldown.</li></ul>
