<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>Instinctual Avoidance</b> - 80% (100% on mastery) chance to dodge melee attacks. 100% chance to avoid thrown projectiles (arrows, kunai, etc).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>True Sight</b> - All Blindness, Darkness or Nausea effects are unable to be applied to you.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>Journeyman's Instinct</b> - Ignore the dodge chance of any entity, as well as preserve your dodge chance should it be ignored.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Danger_Sense"><b>Danger Sense</b></a> - Alarm the player should a mob lock onto them with a bell noise.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <b>Presence Detection</b> - Highlights all mobs in a 50x50 area in blue, even invisible ones or ones under the effect of concealing abilities.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Thought_Acceleration"><b>Thought Acceleration</b></a> - Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Requires Mastery, Passive, Toggle] <b>Thought Acceleration</b> - Increases your reaction speed even further!</li></ul>
<p>If an entity attacks you while possessing [Unpredictability], you preserve a 25% chance of dodging them.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] <b>Multidimensional Barrier</b> - Combines Fault Field and Multilayer Barrier to provide an unbeatable defense. On mastery, increase the number of barrier points.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] <b>Eye of the All-Father</b> - When targetting an entity, become able to apply five different debilitating effects onto them that get progressively stronger the longer you look at the target. Costs: 50K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>The following are the available effects:</li>
<li>Blindness: Applied at +1 for 30 seconds. Meaning the initial effect will be Blindness 2 instead of Blindness 1.</li>
<li>Paralysis: Applied at +2 for 30 seconds. Meaning the initial effect will be Paralysis 3 instead of Paralysis 1.</li>
<li>Petrification: Applied at base value for 30 seconds.</li>
<li>Burden: Applied at base value for 30 seconds.</li>
<li>Darkness: Applied at base value for 30 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>This skill deactivates paralysis and abnormal resistances and nullifications from the target</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] <b>Enhanced Danger Detection</b> - See only traps and dangerous entities through walls, color coded by the aggression of the mob.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] <b>Enhanced Treasure Detection</b> - In addition to seeing chests through walls, become able to see Magic Ore, Silver Ore and Ancient Debris through walls when this mode is toggled on.</li></ul>
