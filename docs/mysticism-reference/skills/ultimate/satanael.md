<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, in Slot] Rage - Break armor much easier when Rampage is active (x3 durability damage, x5 with Mastery).</li></ul>
<ul><li>On MASTERY, chance for Rampage to increase is now 15% (below 100% MP) and 30% (when above 100% MP).</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Breeder Reactor - While held down, gives the player MP equal to 5% of their maximum every 100 ticks. Each usage has a 5% chance to add 1 level of Rampage and 1 minute of duration which rises to 10% if the player's MP is past their natural maximum. With Mastery, becomes a toggle ability instead of hold. [+10 Armor, +60 Atk, +0.3 Atk Speed, +0.1 Speed, +1 Swim Speed, + 0.2 Knockback Resist per Ramage level]</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Enrage - While held down, gives every mob in a 30x30 radius Rampage effect (+1 level each 10 seconds) for 20 seconds. With spiritual attack nullifcation, reduced to a 50% chance. Resistance is bypassed. Costs 5K MP a second.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Stampede - Burst forth with an uncontrollable fit of rage, instantly giving you Rampage X [+100 Armor, +600 Base Atk, +3 Atk Speed, +1.0 Speed, +10 Swim Speed, +2 Knockback Resist]. Cooldown of 1200 seconds (20 mins), reduced to 600 seconds with mastery (10 mins). This effect will last for 10 minutes. Costs 500k MP to use.</li></ul>
