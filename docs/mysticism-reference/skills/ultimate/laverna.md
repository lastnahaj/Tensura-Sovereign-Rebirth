<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration - Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Universal Perception - Enhance your senses to the point of detecting heat, sounds and magicules at an enhanced level.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Shadow Garden - All entities killed are automatically turned into Shadow Warriors.costs 5% of the target's EP (from mp) . On mastery, costs nothing.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Essence of Eclipse - Your shadows grow hungry. All Shadow Warriors gain increased stats when near the player.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Rewrite Fate - Any deaths that the user may take are rewritten at the cost of 15% of the user's MP (Lowers to 10% on mastery). The user will then teleport to a random location next to the target. Susano'o Fantasy Destruction overwrites this and outright kills you. Does not have a cooldown.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Blitz - Gain the abilities of all nearby Shadow Warriors at once. Using this ability permanently consumes all of your active Shadow Warriors and gives you their EP and skills PERMANENTLY in exchange. 3000 second cooldown on Laverna after.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Shadow Storage - Store any subordinate you are looking at into an item. Right click the ground to re-summon them.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Merge - While holding any of your shadow warrior subordinates in item form, pressing the skill key will allow you to harness their abilities. Their max EP is added to your EP while Merge is happening. Pressing Merge again while merged will give you the original subordinate back in item form. Dying will make you permanently lose this subordinate.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Euclidean Dystopia - Turn the surroundings into the Shadow Monarch’s domain. A dense, dark shadow looms over the user’s harrowing aura, crippling and slowing down all entities in the domain. Additionally, remaining in the domain will remove 2% Spiritual HP per second of all non-allied and non-friendly mobs. Cannot be resisted or nullified.</li></ul>
