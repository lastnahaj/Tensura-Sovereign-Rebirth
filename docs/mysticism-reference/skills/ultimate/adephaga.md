<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <b>Devourer</b> - When toggled on, increases the player's size by 1.5 times and grants various passive buffs (1.0 Knockback Resist, Knockback 3, 200 Bonus Damage, 3X Speed, 30 Armour).  Additionally makes the players hunger permanently fully saturated.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] <b>Hard Stomach</b> - You can eat otherwise unorthodox and inedible items, leading to some incredible boons.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Consume</b> - When holding an item, press the skill key to consume it. This will restore Magicules based on the item consumed.
<ul><li>If you are looking at a mob, consume them if their health is low and gain their skills for one hour. On mastery, this increases to two hours. Includes bosses.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Amplify</b> - When activated (need Devourer toggled on), double all the buffs from Devourer for 2 minutes at the cost of 10K MP. If activated again while currently buffed, causes the user to dash forward in the direction they're looking and deals Current Weapon Damage + 200 (500 with Mastery) Damage to all mobs that they touch during the dash at the cost of 10K MP [Sneaking will half the dash distance] - Toggling off Devourer will stop this mode.</li></ul>
