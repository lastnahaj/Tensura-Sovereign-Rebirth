<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] - Absolute Guard : Blocks all attacks with a formula of C = A * 25. C = Cost, A = Attack Damage. or from an opponent with 8 times or higher the user's EP can bypass it.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Spatial Domination: Increase spatial damage by 8.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Gravity Manipulation: Increase gravitational damage by 4.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] Law Manipulation: Nullification is treated as resistance and resistance is bypassed. Magic Jamming's damage mitigation is ignored.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Imprison: Like Infinity Prison, but takes away 1,000 MP per second. Cost 100,000 MP, cooldown 10s (5s with Mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Imaginary Space: 15 rows of storage, with each slot able to store 999 items.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Matter Manipulation. Costs: 5 MP.(..yeah you read that right.)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Gravity Manipulation: Flight unaffected by magic jamming.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Universal Barrier: barrier points are equal to the max HP x 4 (x8 with Mastery). Costs: 20K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press]  Absolute Severance: does 700 damage (1400 with Mastery). Costs: 100K MP, cooldown 5s (3s with Mastery).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Insulated Imprisonment: affects all entities not allied to the player with Spatial Blockade, Magic Interference, and Fragility, also reduces their attack power, attack speed, movement, and jump strength by half, and takes away 250 MP per second in a 20 blocks radius (30 with Mastery). Cost 200K MP, cooldown 40s (20s with Mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Fatal Imprison (requires Mastery): affects all entities not allied to the player with Infinite Imprisonment in a 30 blocks radius. Cost 250k MP, cooldown 20s.</li></ul>
<ul><li>([Active - Press] Nova Break (requires Mastery): inflict 5,000 holy damage and 3,000 spiritual damage to a single target. Cost 2M MP, cooldown 200s.</li></ul>
