<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Action Alteration - Break all barriers when you touch them. All hits become critical hits and all crits deal 2x damage instead of 1.5x. 2x EP threshold becomes 5x EP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Thought Acceleration - Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Universal Perception - Enhance your senses to the point of detecting heat, sounds and magicules at an enhanced level.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Fantasy Destruction - Regard all phenomena null, being able to bypass Infinity Prison/Uriel's Absolute Guard, and ignore any attempts to slow down the user, weaken them, or hamper them in any way.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Optimal Action - Attacks ignore all dodge. Gain 10 learning/mastery points instead of 1 (default). Nullifications are treated as non-existent.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press {Toggle on Skill Mastery}] True Chaotic Fate - Increases permanent health severance from the previous version.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Counter - Emit a loud guard noise. For the next 3 seconds, if anyone hits you, initiate a counter. When an entity is countered, they will be unable to attack for 5 seconds (Attack Speed set to 0).
<ul><li>While in the countering state, the user cannot deal any damage.</li>
<li>On mastery, the time for an entity being countered extends to 10 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
