<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] <b>Golden Rule</b>: Grants a permanent Luck 5 effect</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] <b>Territory Creation</b>: At the cost of 1,000,000 max MP, claim a territory of 250x250 blocks as your permanent territory. Within this territory, you and your subordinates gain infinite magicule regeneration, as well as infinite health regeneration. This is a one-time use ability; once established, the territory cannot be relocated or redefined.
<ul><li>(Infamy: Regenerate 3% MP, 3% AP, 5% HP every 3s to allies)</li>
<li>(Disrepute: Drain 1% MP and AP to everyone else every 3s)</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>Enkidu</b>: At the cost of 50,000MP, imprisons the mob/player in holy chains the player is looking at. Seals all movement and skill usage. Lasts for 240 seconds. Increases to 480 seconds with mastery. (20 second cooldown)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] <b>King’s Storehouse</b>: Open a Spatial Storage with the dimensions of a large chest featuring 4x5 rows. This storage is exclusively for weapons, with each item individually placed without stacking. This storehouse fuels the Gate of Babylon, providing a diverse and powerful arsenal.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press/Held] <b>Gate of Babylon</b>: For an initial cost of 25,000MP and a continuous drain of 5,000MP per second, summon golden portals that can be strategically deployed in various configurations:</li></ul>
<p>(Damage formula: Base Damage + Base Damage… / number of rows)
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li><b>Direct Fire</b>: Small portals appear behind the user, firing weapons from the King’s Storehouse directly at where the user is aiming, each strike utilizes the damage and attributes of the weapons for damage calculation</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>(Gate amount formula: Starts at 5 gates, gain additional gates as you master the skill up to 10 at mastery)
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li><b>Surround Attack</b>: Deploy portals in a circular formation around a targeted area or enemy, allowing for a simultaneous barrage from multiple angles.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>(Gate amount formula: Direct Fire gate + 2, 12 gates at mastery)
</p>
<ul><li>[Active - Press](Requires Mastery) <b>Ea: Sword of Rupture</b>: For the cost of 1,000,000 max AP summon Ea: Sword of Rupture. With a base damage of roughly 200 with the unique damage type Genesis. The sword inherently degrades nullifications to nothing as well as wounds from this sword can not be healed naturally.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Press]  <b>Create Axe</b>: Create an indestructible Axe that does 82 damage and that has the Genesis engraving. Can only be created once. MP cost, 1M MP</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Held] <b>Enuma Elish</b> (Only usable when you have EA in one of your hands); Fires a laser beam that does 2k genesis damage and drains 0.05% of the target's MP every second. Cost 100k MP per second.</li></ul>
