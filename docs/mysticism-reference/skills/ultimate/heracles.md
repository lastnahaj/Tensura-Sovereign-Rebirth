<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Bullet Time - x4 Spatial Damage &amp; Warp Shot are passively applied.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Universal Perception - Heat. Sound. Magic. All do not slip by your scope.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] True Sight - None can hide from the eyes of a sniper. See through all forms of Presence Concealment.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Create Weapon - Instead of creating a sniper pistol, generate an anti-material sniper rifle. Costs: 300K MP.</li></ul>
<p>While the sniper rifle is held, lose all your movement speed.
</p><p>Right click to scope in. Left click while scoped to fire. Bullet does a base 1000 Spatial Damage without any modifiers.
</p><p>While scoped in, highlight target weak points with a red cross. Piercing a weak point will deal 3x damage.
</p><p>Bullet speed is instantaneous and acts like a railgun, piercing all in the path.
</p><p>Scrolling while scoped will allow you to change the zoom.
</p><p>Once you shoot, a reload timer appears across the screen. Right click at the right time to execute a perfect reload. Perfect reloads grant the Sniper +50% damage on the next shot. Failing to execute the perfect reload leads to a slower reload of 10 seconds and doesn't boost your damage. Base damage is 25.
</p>
