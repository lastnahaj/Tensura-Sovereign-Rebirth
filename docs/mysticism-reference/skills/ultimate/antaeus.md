<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <b>Limitless</b> - All projectiles will be halted two blocks from the user, and the user is also immune to all projectiles provided they are not magic. Additionally, no entity can walk in a one block radius from the user, and the user becomes immune to fall damage. Susanoo’s Fantasy Destruction will bypass Limitless.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Thought_Acceleration"><b>Thought Acceleration</b></a> - Increase magic casting speed multiplier by 2, increase movement speed by 0.01 (0.02 with Mastery), increase attack speed by 0.2 (0.4 with Mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Universal_Perception"><b>Universal Perception</b></a> -  Enhance your senses to the point of detecting heat, sounds and magicules at an enhanced level.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] <b>True Gravity Domination</b> - Player’s flight is not affected by magic jamming.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Charge] <b>Repulsion: Red</b> - Reverse the powers of attraction at your disposal to create repulsion. Create a projectile that can grow in size, deal gravity damage (scales with power) and destroy more blocks the bigger it gets. The projectile, when spawned, deals about 400 damage and scales higher with the power scale. (Affected by Gravity Manipulation/Domination.) All entities will be pushed away from the projectile and take heavy gravity damage. Costs: 125K MP
<ul><li>When Antaeus is mastered, become able to launch the projectile (at 20.0 power scale).</li>
<li>Crouch before release to cancel the projectile's launch.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Charge] <b>Attraction: Blue</b> - Utilise the powers of attraction at your disposal to create the ultimate black hole. Create a projectile that can grow in size, deals gravity damage (constantly if you’re sucked into it), and gets stronger attraction the bigger it is. The projectile, when spawned, deals about 200 damage and scales higher with the power scale. (Affected by Gravity Manipulation/Domination.) Costs: 125K MP
<ul><li>Also sucks in blocks. All blocks that are sucked in will be deleted instantly. Ignores water (due to high amounts of lag) Not even Gravity Domination will save you from Blue.</li>
<li>When Antaeus is mastered, become able to launch the projectile (at 20.0 power scale).</li>
<li>Crouch before release to cancel the projectile's launch.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, requires Mastery, Charge] <b>Hollow: Purple</b> - Collide Red and Blue together for the ultimate destruction.  Creates a gigantic projectile that requires 5 seconds of charging. Releasing before reaching power scale of 5.0 will do nothing. Costs: 500K MP
<ul><li>This projectile has an extremely short lifespan of 5 seconds after creation due to the complicated nature of it's existence. Shoot it quickly before it disappears!It has a cost of ~1million MP. The projectile, when spawned, deals about 5000 damage and scales higher with the power scale. (Affected by Gravity Manipulation/Domination.)</li>
<li>When colliding with blocks, it destroys things in a huge radius and deals insane Gravity damage.</li>
<li>Crouch before release to cancel the projectile's launch.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active] <b>Bleve</b> - Use gravity to collapse your target, launching them with great force and allowing natural forces to deal 200 gravity damage (400 when mastered) to them. Puts Antaeus on a 10 second cooldown, 5 seconds when mastered. This is boosted by Gravity Manipulation/Domination.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Oppress</b> - Freeze your target in place. Using the skill a second time on it will deal decent gravity damage to the target. Puts Antaeus on a 10 second cooldown, 5 seconds when mastered. This is boosted by Gravity Manipulation/Domination. Costs: 125K MP</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Charge] <b>Flicker</b> - Become able to propel yourself at high speeds. Holding shift before pressing will bring up the Spatial Motion [Teleport] menu.</li></ul>
