<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Requirements">Requirements</span></h2>
<p>To evolve a unique skill to an ultimate skill, all of the following requirements have to be met
</p>
<ul><li>Be awakened as either True Hero or True demon Lord</li>
<li>Posses an evolvable unique skill</li>
<li>Possess every required skill and master it (applies to all)</li>
<li>Have at least the required amount of MP</li>
<li>(If Soulquality Gamerule is on) have atleast 1 Soulquality left</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
