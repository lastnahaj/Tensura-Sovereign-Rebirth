<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Dissasmble - Turn all blocks in a 3x3 radius into matter. Shift + Skill Key shows you how much matter you have. Holding a stack of any convertable item will allow you to convert the held item into matter at double the rate. So one stack of 64 = 128 matter. Costs: 30K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Stone - Hurl a Stone infront of you twice as strong and fast as its predecessor. Costs: 30K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Fuse - Create a landmine 3x stronger than its predecessor. Costs: 30K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Explosion Dash - Allow the user to choose how much matter to expend with the scroll wheel. Pushes you a certain amount of blocks in a direction. 3 second cooldown. 1 if mastered. Costs: 10K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Art, Press] Bites The Dust - Requires 2000 matter minimum. 1000 on mastery. Implant a bomb into your target but expend all your matter. The bomb needs to be primed over one minute and the target will not know until 15 seconds before priming. When primed, the bomb lasts 120 seconds.  If the wielder dies, the bomb inside the target will deactivate. Costs: 30K MP.
<ul><li>If the bomb's timer reaches 0, it will automatically go off.</li>
<li>Crouch and press to disarm.</li>
<li>Reactivate [Bites The Dust] when the bomb has primed to detonate a powerful bomb depending on how much matter was fused that kills your target instantly and blows up the surrounding area.</li></ul></li></ul>
