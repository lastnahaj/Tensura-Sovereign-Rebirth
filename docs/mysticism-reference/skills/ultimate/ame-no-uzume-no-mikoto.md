<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Compatibility_System">Compatibility System</span></h2>
<p>If you rolled the Unique Skills <a class="text" href="../../unique/captivator/">Captivator</a> or <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Bewilder">Bewilder</a>, you are compatible with the other skill in this set, giving you a 5% chance to obtain the other Unique Skill from the respective skill holder.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled, Idol Mode] <b>Shining Star</b> - Every 5 seconds, there is a 50% chance that a non-allied entity in a 10 block radius of you will receive the Dazzled effect for 3 seconds.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled, Acting Mode] <b>Center Stage</b> - Every 5 seconds, there is a 50% chance that a non-allied entity in a 10 block radius of you will receive the Spotlight effect for 3 seconds.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] <b>Standing Ovation</b> - Completing a [Performance] will cause you to switch between Acting mode and Idol mode. Additionally, gain Hero of the Village 10.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] <b>Performance</b> - Charm all non-allied entities in your presence in a 20 block radius, costing 175K MP/s to use. This Charm will wear off after 1 hour and cannot be re-applied. Upon completing your performance, [Standing Ovation] activates. Addtionally, go on a 5 second cooldown. The maximum time you can Perform for is 15 seconds.
<ul><li>If the target has Spiritual Attack Resistance, the effect will last 10 minutes.</li>
<li>If the target has Spiritual Attack Nullification, the effect will last 15 seconds.</li>
<li>All entities allied to you will gain Inspiration 2 instead, meaning newly Charmed foes will instantly gain Inspiration. On mastery, this Inspiration upgrades to Inspiration 4.</li>
<li>If the crouch key is held before activating, instantly kill all Charmed entities within a 20 block radius (25 on mastery) that are temporary subordinates at 175K MP per entity killed.</li>
<li>If the target has Spiritual Attack Resistance, they will instead take 50% of their health as damage.</li>
<li>If the target has Spiritual Attack Nullification, they will instead take 10% of their health as damage.</li>
<li>Cooldown of this is 5 seconds (3 when mastered.)</li>
<li>On mastery, the cost of this mode will be 100K MP, both crouching and non-crouching.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press, Idol Mode] <b>Encore</b> - Your second song, your second stage. Instantly Dazzle all nearby non-allied targets for 30 seconds. Cooldown of 60 seconds. Costs: 300K MP
<ul><li>All allies gain Inspiration 3 instead.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press, Acting Mode] <b>Spotlight</b> - The actors on stage are yours to use. Direct all aggro of nearby mobs and grant the target you are looking at the Spotlight effect for 20 seconds, which causes the target’s allies and non-allies to indiscriminately attack them, acting out a beautiful play to your script. Cooldown of 30 seconds. Costs: 250K MP
<ul><li>Other players will lose control of their character and will also be forced to attack the spotlit target.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Mastery, Active, Press] <b>Ornate Star</b> - You're at the peak of your career. One glance is enough to steal the show and spark jealousy amongst all your rivals. Costs: 500K MP
<ul><li>On targetting an entity and activating this mode, seal all their skills and lock their mouse from turning for 10 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
