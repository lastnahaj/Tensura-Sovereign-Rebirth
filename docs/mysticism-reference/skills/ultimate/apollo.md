<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <b>Auditory Sense</b> - Fine tune your hearing to hear all mobs within a 50x50 radius, much higher than Musician's.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Thought_Acceleration"><b>Thought Acceleration</b></a> - Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>Synesthesia</b> - All of Apollo's SOUND-BASED attacks are so powerful that they cause the other senses to be stimulated, incurring blindness on all affected</li></ul>
<p><br/>
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (50K MP)] <b>Sonic Blast</b> - Fire a sonic blast in the direction you are looking at, extending up to 12 blocks (16 blocks on mastery). Deals 150.0 True Damage (300.0 on mastery). Affected by Sound Manipulation/Domination.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (100K MP)] <b>Sound Wave</b> - Emit an ear piercing sound around you in a 15 block radius. Deals 80.0 True Damage (140.0 on mastery). Affected by Sound Manipulation/Domination.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (150K MP, 3s Cooldown, Mastery required.)] <b>Mind Requiem</b> - Your voice becomes able to affect the Spiritual Body. Emit a powerful, mind-wrenching scream reaching 10 blocks, dealing 500.0 True Damage and 200.0 spiritual damage. Affected by Sound Manipulation/Domination.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (300K MP, 10s Cooldown, Learning from [Mind Requiem] usage required.)] <b>Soul Render</b> - Scream in all cardinal directions around you, affecting the spiritual body. Deal insanely high damage with the downside of a high cost and long cooldown, reaching up to 10 blocks and dealing 900.0 True Damage and 300.0 spiritual damage. Affected by Sound Manipulation/Domination</li></ul>
