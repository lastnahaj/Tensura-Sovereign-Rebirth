<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] Ethereal Presence - 20% chance to dodge projectiles or physical attacks by phasing out of existence. Does not stack with upgrades or preceding skills.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] Regenerate - If you've been out of combat for 15 seconds (no damage taken), restore 3% of MP every 5 seconds. Does not stack with upgrades or preceding skills.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Life Domination - If a subordinate were to die near you (30 blocks), place a lingering soul for 30 seconds. See Life Domination - Active.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] Return by Death - Death is not the end. If the player had set a Dream before dying, instantly teleport to the location of the Dream, restore all stats, and cheat death. Places the entire skill on a cooldown of 20 minutes. Susano'o’s Fantasy Destruction bypasses this and will kill you regardless.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (1000.0 MP.)] Checkpoint - Save your current health, SHP, magicules, aura, and location. Reactivating Dream will restore your stats back to the saved values and instantly teleport you to the location you saved. When activated, go on a 600 second cooldown. Dying will also trigger [Checkpoint] if you've saved one. See [Passive: Return by Death]. Crouching and activating will cancel the dream.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Life Domination - Revive the Lingering Soul you are looking at, at the cost of the subordinate's Max EP before their death.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Art, Press] Restore Life - Forcibly evolve the target using your own Soul Points. Holding Shift will attempt to make the target go through an awakening (costing 20k Souls instead of 10k). Works in Hardcore Races. ONLY OTHER PLAYERS CAN GO THROUGH AN AWAKENING.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold (0 MP)] Hypnosis - Induce Drowsiness on nearby mobs for 10 seconds.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold (100 MP/s)] Dream Eater - Holding down the skill while looking at a mob that is Drowsy will deal 50 damage and 50 spiritual damage after a 5 second timer. Successfully consuming a dream will reduce [Return by Death] and [Checkpoint]'s cooldown by 30 seconds.</li></ul>
