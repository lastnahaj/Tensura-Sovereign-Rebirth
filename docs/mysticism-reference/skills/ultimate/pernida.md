<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Reconstruction - Should the body be destroyed, the user can completely reform and reconstruct themselves. 120 second cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Self Replication - Should user be less than 50% HP, generate a clone of the user. Up to five clones can be created. Your clones can also create clones this way, but have a decreasing clone cap the more generations it has.
<ul><li>For example, the player can make 5 clones.</li>
<li>Those 5 clones can make 3 clones.</li>
<li>Those 3 clones can make 1 clone.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Organic Evolution - When faced with stronger opponents, grant the user various buffs to keep them on par.
<ul><li>When the opponent is 20% EP higher than you, [Pernida, Lord of Compliance] grants you Strengthen 3 and Resistance 1.</li>
<li>When the opponent is 40% EP higher than you, [Pernida, Lord of Compliance] grants you Strengthen 6 and Resistance 2.</li>
<li>When the opponent is 60% EP higher than you, [Pernida, Lord of Compliance] grants you Strengthen 9 and Resistance 3.</li>
<li>When the opponent is 80% EP higher than you, [Pernida, Lord of Compliance] grants you Strengthen 12 and Resistance 4.</li>
<li>When the opponent is 100% EP higher than you, [Pernida, Lord of Compliance] grants you Strengthen 15 and Resistance 4.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Adaptive Evolution - The user gains more points for a Resistance the more they take damage.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Regeneration - When the user is out of combat, their cells regenerate, healing both HP and MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Adaptive Observation - The user gets 5 more learn and mastery points on all skills, magics and battlewill.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Organic Distortion - Warps and distorts the targets body dealing 500 damage and giving them Distortion 3 for 10 seconds
<ul><li>Distortion turns off flight, increases the target's Gravity by 0.6 per level, reduces their attack damage by 1.5 per level and reduces movement speed by -0.075 per level.</li>
<li>Default Human values for comparison: 1 Entity Gravity, 1.0 Attack Damage, 0.1 Movement Speed</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Anatomy Manipulation - Allows the user to change the size of their body. (Use the scroll wheel to select your size.)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] (Adaptive Replication) - When used on target, copy a random intrinsic, common or extra skill. If the random skill is a Unique, temporarily gain that skill for 180 seconds. This is extended to 300 seconds on mastery.
<ul><li>This cooldown is 120 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Abilities Costs: 100K MP.
</p>
