<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] <b>Angel of Death</b> - Any subordinates that die within a 20 block vicinity are instantly revived with 20% HP. Costs 10% of the target subordinate's EP to revive them.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Drain</b> - When used while not looking at an entity, adds an effect to the users attacks that drains 1000 + 0.5% (5000 + 1%) MP and AP (each) and gives it to the user (temporary, not max) for 10 seconds. When used while looking at an entity, it drains 1000 + 1% (5000 + 5% with mastery) MP and AP (each) and gives it to the user. Costs 100K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Invigorate</b> - When used while not looking at a mob or when sneaking, fully heals the player and removes all status effects. When looking at a mob while sneaking, fully heals them and removes all status effects [Cost 30 MP (10 MP on mastery) per HP and 100 MP per effect level]. If used on a mob that can breed while its at full hp, will cause them to enter hearts mode. Costs 50K MP.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] <b>Death Blessing</b> - Every 3 seconds, instantly kills all mobs within a 15x15 radius if their EP is lower than 75% of the player's and restores the player's expended MP and AP by the target mobs EP (75/25). If mob is between 75% to 100% of your EP, instead drain 25% EP and deal half their HP in damage. If the target is stronger than you, drain 5% and deals half their HP in damage. Costs 150K MP every time it is triggered.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Art, In Slot] <b>Memory End Requiem</b> - Drain the life of the target by coating your next attack with the raw unfiltered life-reaping energy of [Asmodeus]. This mode applies a cooldown of 15 seconds to [Asmodeus]. Costs 300K MP.
<ul><li>If your target does not possess an Ultimate Skill, instantly kill the target.</li>
<li>If the target possesses an Ultimate Skill, they will be protected from instant death, but instead take heavy damage. Based on the following:</li>
<li>If the target has 100% to 110% EP from your EP, deal 90% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount. Additionally, drain 75% of the target's EP, split into 75% MP and 25% AP for the user and inflict Weakness 6, Slowness 6 and Burden 6 for 15 seconds.</li>
<li>If the target has 110% to 120% EP from your EP, deal 80% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount. Additionally, drain 50% of the target's EP, split into 75% MP and 25% AP for the user and inflict Weakness 4, Slowness 4 and Burden 5 for 14 seconds.</li>
<li>If the target has 120% to 130% EP from your EP, deal 70% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount. Additionally, drain 40% of the target's EP, split into 75% MP and 25% AP for the user and inflict Weakness 4, Slowness 4 and Burden 4 for 13 seconds.</li>
<li>If the target has 130% to 140% EP from your EP, deal 60% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount. Additionally, drain 30% of the target's EP, split into 75% MP and 25% AP for the user and inflict Weakness 4, Slowness 4 and Burden 3 for 12 seconds.</li>
<li>If the target has 140% to 150% EP from your EP, deal 50% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount. Additionally, drain 20% of the target's EP, split into 75% MP and 25% AP for the user and inflict Weakness 2, Slowness 2 and Burden 2 for 11 seconds.</li>
<li>If the target has more than 150% of your EP, deal 25% of their health as damage and heal yourself for the amount. Additionally, drain 10% of the target's EP, split into 75% MP and 25% AP for the user and inflict Weakness, Slowness and Burden for 10 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
