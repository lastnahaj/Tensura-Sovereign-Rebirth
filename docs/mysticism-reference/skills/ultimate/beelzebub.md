<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Compatibility_System">Compatibility System</span></h2>
<p>If you rolled the Unique Skills <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Guardian">Guardian</a> or <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Gourmet">Gourmet</a>, you are compatible with the other skill in this set, giving you a 0.05% chance to obtain the other Unique Skill from Royal Orcs.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>Demon Lord Haki Coating</b> - Your attacks passively bypass resistances and halve the effectiveness of nullifications.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] <b>Iron Wall</b> - You gain 30 defense instead of 15. 45 on mastery. Does not work if Guardian is toggled. Additionally, all subordinates in a 20 block radius of you gain 15 defense, upgrading to 30 when mastered.</li></ul>
<p>Gain <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Spatial_Manipulation">Spatial manipulation</a>, <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Ultraspeed_Regeneration">Ultraspeed Regeneration</a>, <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Universal_Perception">Universal Perception</a>, and <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Godwolf_Sense">Godwolf Sense</a>.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] <b>Predation</b> - Works like <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Gluttony"><b>Gluttony</b></a> does.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Gluttony"><b>Stomach</b></a> - Bigger storage.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Predator"><b>Isolation</b></a> - Works like Predator's version.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Gluttony"><b>Provide</b></a> - Choose a skill from your skill list (from intrinsic to extra) to confer onto the target. The target be a PERMANENT subordinate through a NAME.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Gluttony"><b>Receive</b></a> - Choose a skill from the target's skill list to grant a permanent copy to yourself. The target must be a PERMANENT subordinate through a NAME.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Gluttony"><b>Corrosion</b></a> - From the preceding evolution, except stronger.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Guardian"><b>Substitution</b></a> - Take on the all the damage that any subordinates near you would take, but it is halved.</li></ul>
<p>All Abilities here cost 10K MP.
</p>
