<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Thought_Acceleration"><b>Thought Acceleration</b></a> - Speed up your reaction speed, increasing casting speed, attack speed, and movement speed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Universal_Perception"><b>Universal Perception</b></a> - Enhance your senses to the point of detecting heat, sounds and magicules at an enhanced level.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>Unstable Transmitter</b>  - Falling below 10% health gives you 75% of your maximum health as absorption. Furthermore, at the time of being damaged below 10%, all enemies nearby will be teleported away. Puts the skill on a cooldown of 120 seconds.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] <b>Void Infusion</b> - Infuse your fists with the power of the void, dealing extra armor damage and passively severing the target for a percentage of their damage dealt.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active] <b>Destroyer Haki</b> - Emanate the aura of the Destroyer, heralding the end as you inflict nearby enemies with unavoidable, non-resistable Void damage at 500 per second (1000 when mastered). Additionally, crush the souls of your enemies under the weight of their sins, reducing their movement speed exponentially based on how powerful you are above the opponent. Costs 50K MP per second to use.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] <b>Dimension Hopper</b> - Switch the dimensions, allowing you to scroll to select the dimensions (Overworld, Nether, End, Hell, Spirit Dimension) and hop between them.  On release, your dimension will change. Nothing will happen if you release and you are in the chosen dimension. Cooldown of 30 seconds. 15 seconds when mastered.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] <b>Turn: Null</b> - Radiate unstoppable Void energy from your fingertips, instantly deleting a 16x16x16 radius around the player. Caution must be exercised, destroyer. Puts Dionysus on a 300 second cooldown when used, 150 when mastered. Costs 500k MP to use. Fails if the skillGriefing gamerule is on.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] <b>Multilayer Barrier</b> - Equip a barrier that uses the features of your toggled resistances and nullifications to block attacks.</li></ul>
