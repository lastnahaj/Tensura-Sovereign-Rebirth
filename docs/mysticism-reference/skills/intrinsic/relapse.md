<p><b>[Relapse] is an Intrinsic Skill in Tensura: Mysticism. It requires the user to have a self-shattering sense of loss, and hence cannot be utilized by any other race other than the Forgotten line. It is the Intrinsic Skill of the Forgotten Race and its usage and functionality scales with the power of the owner.</b>
</p>
