<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Magisteel Body:</li></ul>
<div class="toccolours mw-collapsible mw-collapsed">
<div>Click here to show armor levels</div>
<div class="mw-collapsible-content">
<p>Every milestone in the user's EP will net them an increase in armor. The resulting armor points is rounded DOWN. Keep in mind that all of these armor points stack with normal armor.
</p><p>At 25,000 EP and lower, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Low Magisteel. This totals 20 armor points.
</p><p>At 50,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of High Magisteel. This totals 24 armor points.
</p><p>At 80,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Pure Magisteel. This totals 40 armor points.
</p><p>At 550,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Adamantite. This totals 48 armor points.
</p><p>And at 1,000,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set Hihi'irokane. This totals 56 armor points.
</p>
</div></div>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span></h3>
<p>To use this skill, you need to be either the <a href="../../../races/greater-doll/" title="Greater Doll">Greater Doll</a> Line or a spiritual being possessing a magisteel golem
</p>
