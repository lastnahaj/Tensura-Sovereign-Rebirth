<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Tenacity:</li></ul>
<p>Applies the Aura Healing effect. Instantly regenerate HP and SHP damage. Costs 100 AP per point of HP healed and 120 AP per point of SHP healed.Behaves like Infinite Regen when Mastered. Reduced to 60 and 80 AP respectively when mastered.
</p>
