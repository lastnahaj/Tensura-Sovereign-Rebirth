<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Exoskeleton, Every milestone in the user's EP will net them an increase in armor. The resulting armor points is rounded DOWN. Keep in mind that all of these armor points stack with normal armor.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<div class="toccolours mw-collapsible mw-collapsed">
<div>Click here to show armor levels</div>
<div class="mw-collapsible-content">
<p>At 0 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Monster Leather D, divided by 2. This totals 5 armor points
</p><p>At 25,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Monster Leather C, divided by 2. This totals 7 armor points.
</p><p>At 50,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Monster Leather B, divided by 2. This totals 10 armor points.
</p><p>At 80,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Monster Leather A, divided by 2. This totals 12 armor points.
</p><p>At 800,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Monster Leather Special A, divided by 2. This totals 22 armor points.
</p><p>At 4,000,000 EP, your armor will be equivalent to a full set of Hihi'irokane armor, divided by 2. This totals 28 armor points.
</p><p>If you are of the Ant race and it's evolutionary path "Hardshell Ant", your armor will NOT be divided by 2. Instead, the armor you will gain is as follows, and YES, this stacks with armor:
</p><p>0 EP: 11 armor points.
</p><p>25,000 EP: 15 armor points.
</p><p>50,000 EP: 20 armor points.
</p><p>80,000 EP: 24 armor points.
</p><p>800,000 EP: 44 armor points.
</p><p>4,000,000 EP: 56 armor points.
</p>
</div></div>
