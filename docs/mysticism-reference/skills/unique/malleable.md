<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<p>[Sub-Skill] Cold Clay - The user will learn Flame Attack Resistance when Malleable is acquired.
</p><p>[Passive, Toggle] Memory Material - All resistances are learned 5x faster, and so are earth type magics/abilities. They are also mastered 5x faster.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[[Active, Press] Alchemy - Cycle through 13 different modes; Physical, Fire, Ice, Lightning, Piercing, Earth, Water, Light, Dark, Holy, Poison, Corrosion, and Wind. Similar to Magic Aura, change any damage dealt from the player to this damage type. Upon mastery, becomes a toggle. 10 second cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Harden - While holding this skill, disable flight, movement, the ability to attack, and spatial moves, and give yourself regeneration 5 and resistance 2, alongside 10 armor points. After releasing, put the skill on cooldown for 120 seconds (60 seconds on mastery) and give weakness 2 and slowness 2 for 30 seconds.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Vessel - Summon a clay golem with 40 max health, with no AI, allowing it to be possessed by anyone who's able to. If another player possesses it, they become a temporary subordinate until they leave the body/die. Maximum of 2 may exist. On mastery, increases the max health of the Vessel to 200.</li></ul>
