<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Pressure:</li></ul>
<p>You emit a pressure when hit by a player, that player’s cost for abilities are double for 30 seconds.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Glaciate - Cost 500 MP per second you hold it. You surround yourself and the air with hail, slowing down everything in a 10 block radius, and dealing increasing cold damage, starting at 10, and going up by 5 every 10 seconds, stopping at 100 damage. Slows down the users as well, Like Demon Lord Haki and other such skills. CD 30 Seconds. When Mastered, Damage Cap is raised to 200.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Draconic Pulse - Cost 300 MP to fire. You launch a blast of draconic energy in the shape of a dragon from your mouth leaving destruction in its wake, if hit by the draconic energy it deals 40 Magic Damage. CD is 10 Seconds, lowered to 5 seconds when mastered.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Freeze Dry - Costs 500 MP per second while holding it. When looking at a mob or player, you rapidly freeze the air around them, dealing 20 cold damage every second for as long as you hold and look at them. It also slows them down a bit, with a small chance of leaving them frozen in place, 10% chance for 30 seconds. Cold Resistance makes it 5% and 15 seconds. Cold Nullification ignores the chance. 20 Second CD, lowered to 10 seconds when mastered.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Blizzard:</li></ul>
<p>Summon a storm of ice, pelting targets with heavy cold and water damage. Lasts until released, and goes on a 10 second cooldown after.
</p>
