<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] Courage of the Strong – Grants immunity to psychological effects such as Fear and Charm.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - Toggle] Relentless Barrage – Increases Attack Speed by 10%.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] Might – Releases an overwhelming aura that inflicts Fear on enemies within a 10-block radius for 10 seconds. Costs 2000 MP per second and has a cooldown of 40s</li>
<li>[Active - Press] Tenacious Resolve – Weakens the duration and strength (if strength surpasses Level 1) of negative effects not affected by Courage of the Strong and caused by other entities. Costs 500 MP and has a cooldown of 60s</li></ul>
