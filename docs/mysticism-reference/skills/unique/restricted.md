<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Heavenly Restriction:</li></ul>
<p>The user of this skill can no longer gain any magicules, nor gain any more skills (except for the ones provided to them on reincarnation by their race, OR if a skill was gifted to them, i.e. Hidden Ruler.)
They can, however, still gain <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Battlewill"></a></span> <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Battlewill"><span class="sprite-text">Battlewills</span></a></span>.
</p><p>The user also gains <b>3x</b> more Aura than a normal player, to compensate for their lack of Magicules.
</p><p>Since the user has little to no magicules, they cannot be picked up by any magicule sensing skills. They can, however, be picked up by auditory sense or heat sense.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Fearless:</li></ul>
<p>The user cannot be affected by <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Effects/Fear">Fear</a>, Slowness or <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Effects/Burden">Burden</a>. They also cannot feel any effects of <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Haki">Haki</a>.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Learned Techniques:</li></ul>
<p>The user’s <span class="nowrap"><span class="sprite-file"><a href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Battlewill"></a></span> <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Battlewill"><span class="sprite-text">Battlewills</span></a></span> do <b>5x</b> more damage than usual. This stacks with <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Divine_Ki_Release">Divine Ki Release</a> or <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Martial_Master">Martial Master</a>, if the user should SOMEHOW come into their possession.
Additionally, all of the user’s <b>PHYSICAL attacks</b> deal <b>3x</b> more damage.
</p><p>[Passive, True] Through the Odds (Requires Mastery):
On mastery of [Restricted], become able to pierce through all resistances and degrade them.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Decimate:</li></ul>
<p>Instantly dash 12 blocks ahead (16 blocks on mastery) and deal 20 + a weapon’s critical worth of damage.
</p><p>On mastery, instead deal 1.5 * (35 + Critical Hit damage). This attack can now damage spiritual beings and will bypass Resistances. Costs 500 Aura to use.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Counter:</li></ul>
<p>Consume 1500 aura instantly. Enter a <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-countering/" title="Effects/Countering">parrying stance</a> for 3 seconds (5 on mastery), accompanied by a loud sound to let opponents have time to react (no sound atm, sound guy was busy).
</p><p>Movement and Attack Speed are set to zero while this stance is active. Press again while active to cancel, and no cooldown will occur.
</p><p>The next physical attack that hits you will deal zero damage and disables your enemy’s attack speed for 5 seconds as they scramble to recover <i>(See <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-imbalanced/" title="Effects/Imbalanced">Imbalanced</a>)</i>.
If you are successful in a parry, go on a 15 second cooldown.
</p>
