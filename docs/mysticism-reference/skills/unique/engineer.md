<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Tools of the Trade - Nearby Sentries or Dispensers will upgrade passively near the player. The longer it lives, the stronger it gets.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Build [Sentry/Dispenser]:</li></ul>
<p>Sentry:
Holding down the skill for 5 seconds will see you build a sentry at that location. Sentries are stationary and copy the user’s attributes at the time of building, like armor, HP, SHP and more.
Sentries are autonomous and shoot at the nearest non-friendly target, including players.
Maximum of 2 sentries at a time. Cooldown of 30 seconds. Placing down a new sentry at the limit of 2 will destroy the oldest placed sentry.
Level 2 requires 120 seconds of being near the player. It will double the fire rate of the sentry.
Level 3 requires 300 seconds (total 300, so 5 minutes from level 1) of being near the player. It will increase the fire rate further and equip your sentry with missiles.
</p><p>Dispenser:
Place down a Dispenser that provides the nearest player with magicules, aura and health over time. It restores 0.5% of MP and Aura every 5 seconds at level one.
At level 2, after 120 seconds of being near the player, it starts to dispense health, healing 10 HP and restoring 1% of MP/AP every 5 seconds.
At level 3, after 300 seconds (total 300, so 5 minutes from level 1) of being near the player, it starts to heal 5% of HP and restore 3% of MP/AP every 3 seconds.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Bubble Shield - Throw down a bubble shield at the player's feet that lasts for 15 seconds. The bubble shield blocks all damage from the outside and prevents entities and projectiles from entering it. The owner and allies of the owner may enter or leave as they see fit. Puts the skill on a 25 second cooldown.</li></ul>
