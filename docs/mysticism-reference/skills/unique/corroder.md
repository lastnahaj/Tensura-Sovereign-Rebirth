<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>Passive, Toggle] Corrosive Touch - All your physical attacks now apply the corrosion effect. When unmastered, this effect is applied at Corrosion 2 for 10 seconds. On mastery, this upgrades to Corrosion 3 for 15 seconds. This is changeable in the config.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Corrosion Ward - Corrosion will NEVER apply to you.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Dissolve - Emit a dissolving aura around you, melting any targets nearby. This drains 100 MP/s, changeable in the config, and deals 10 Corrosive damage. It also additionally applies Corrosion 1 to all targets for 10 seconds.
<ul><li>On mastery, this upgrades to 15 Corrosion Damage, applying Corrosion 2 to all targets for 15 seconds,</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Art (requires learning), Press] Cross Corrosion - Instantly coat your blade in corrosive acid. On your next attack, slash for heavy, armor-ignoring damage. This attack uses 1000.0 Magicules and is configurable in the config.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
