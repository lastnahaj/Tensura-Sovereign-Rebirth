<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Generosity - Create a circle of amplification in front of the user that raises the damage of any projectiles passing through it by 3 times. If a player passes through this gate, they will experience heightened speed and step height for 10 seconds. Every entity or player that passes through this gate contributes to [Pay It Forward]. The user cannot use the gate to amplify themselves or their projectiles/attacks. Only one circle can be active at a time and persists until the user takes it down. Costs 1000 MP to spawn a circle and 500 MP every second it is active. Shift and Use to remove the circle.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] Pay It Forward - Every Contribution you have gained will be consumed to power up Pay It Forward. The duration of this mode depends on how many Contributions have been made, following this formula (Contributions x 10 seconds)</li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>Toggling off the mode preserves the remaining seconds for future use.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>When Pay It Forward is active, the user will gain Damage Amplification and hugely boosted Speed and Step Height depending on the number of Contributions counted.
</p><p>30 or more contributions - 2x Damage, +0.1F movement speed and +2 Step Height.
</p><p>50 or more - 3x Damage, +0.15F movement speed and +3 Step Height.
</p><p>100 or more - 5x Damage, +0.2F movement speed and +4 Step Height.
</p><p>500 or more - 10x Damage, +0.5F movement speed and +6 Step Height.
</p>
