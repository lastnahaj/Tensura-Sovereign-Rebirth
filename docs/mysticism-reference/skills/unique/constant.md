<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Requires Mastery, Passive, Toggled] On Mastery, prevents Cook and Severance from being applied to the player.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Constant: Health - Holding down the skill key will preserve your current health and spiritual health no matter how much damage you take. This mode drains 1000 magicules every second of is held down.  When releasing, you will have a cooldown of 10 seconds,</li></ul>
<p>After 15 seconds of holding this ability down, this mode automatically turns off with a longer cooldown of 15 seconds.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Constant: Physical - After pressing the skill key, begin a 5 second timer. It will record the highest physical hit you perform during this timer. After the timer has ended, [Constant: Destruction] becomes available to use. Puts the skill on a 10 second cooldown after the key is pressed.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held, Locked] Constant: Destruction - On the completion of Constant: Physical, become able to use Constant: Destruction. While holding down this skill mode, your damage will stagnate and always deal the highest hit you saved in Constant: Physical. This mode drains 3000 magicules every second it is held down. When releasing, you will have a cooldown of 10 seconds,</li></ul>
<p>After 15 seconds of holding this ability down, this mode automatically turns off with a longer cooldown of 15 seconds.
</p>
<ul><li>[Requires Mastery, Active, Press] Constant: Energy - For 10 seconds after pressing this mode, your Magicules and Aura will not deplete. However, it will begin a hidden counter. After the 10 seconds are up, the cost of all the abilities that you used will be added up and multiplied by two, then subtracted from each of your statistics accordingly. If the drain from this mode would exceed 0%, instead set your magicules to 25% and prevent regeneration of magicules for 3 minutes.</li></ul>
