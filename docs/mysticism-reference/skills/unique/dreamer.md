<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] Recall - Restore 0.5% of MP every 5 seconds. On mastery, this upgrades to 1%.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - True] Lucid Dream: Death is not the end. If the player had set a Dream before dying, instantly teleport to the location of the Dream, restore all stats, and cheat death. The (Dream) mode goes on a cooldown of 300 seconds when this happens. (On mastery, reduces to 120 seconds.)</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Dream:</li></ul>
<p>Save your current health, SHP, magicules, aura, and location.
Reactivating Dream will restore your stats back to the saved values and instantly teleport you to the location you saved.
When activated, go on a 300 second cooldown. (On mastery, reduces to 120 seconds.) Dying will also trigger Dream if you've saved one, see [Lucid Dream]. Crouching and activating will cancel the dream.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Hypnosis - Induce Drowsiness I (Drowsiness II when mastered) on mobs in a 10 block radius (15 when mastered). All values can be modified in the configuration files.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Dream Eater:</li></ul>
<p>Holding down the skill while looking at a mob that is Drowsy will deal 30 damage (50 when mastered) and 30 spiritual damage (50 when mastered) after a 5 second timer.
Successfully consuming a dream will reduce (Dream)'s cooldown by 60 seconds. Grants a mastery point as well. All values are configurable in the configuration files.
</p><p><br/>
</p>
