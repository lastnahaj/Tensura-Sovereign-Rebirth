<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Soulscape - Killing any entity will have a 25% chance for their soul to linger for a short period of time. 50% chance on mastery. The user has 30 seconds to choose whether or not to keep this entity or it will disappear forever. See [Shadow Warrior].</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Essence of Night - During the night, your shadows grow restless. All Shadow Warriors gain increased damage and speed. Does not affect the player, unless a specific condition is met. See [Shadow Merge].</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Shadow Warrior - Pressing the mode while looking at a lingering soul will create a Shadow Warrior version of the mob you killed, at the cost of the target's EP before you killed them PERMANENTLY. This REDUCES YOUR MAX MP BY THE TARGET'S EP. Includes Otherworlders but not bosses. Shadow Warriors are loyal to the player and act like a permanent subordinate.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Shadow Storage - Store any subordinate you are looking at into an item. Right click the ground to re-summon them.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Shadow Merge - While holding any of your shadow warrior subordinates in item form, pressing the skill key will allow you to harness their abilities, meaning their stats are added onto yours. Their max EP is added to your EP while Merge is happening. Pressing Shadow Merge again while merged will give you the original subordinate back in item form. Dying will make you permanently lose this subordinate.</li></ul>
<p>While Merged, Essence of Night applies to you as well.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Special_Interaction">Special Interaction</span></h3>
<p>Mastering plunderer makes shadow motion available to the user
</p>
