<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Freund Schild - Every 1k damage the user receives are stored as misfortune points which can be used to amplify the misfortune redirected against target. (if landed successfully this cannot be migitated or nullified)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Probability Manipulation - 50% dodge 45% crit (70% dodge on mastery) (65% crit on mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] The Balance - All crit damage agasint the damage is countered and carries over all buffs (i.e: cook users will cook themselves, target will sever themselves if they had severence.) Bypassed by Susano'o, follows same formula as absolute guard for infinity prison. Cannot directly counter skills that apply potion effects.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Fortune Mitigation - Your own schrift affects you causing you to deal 25% less damage</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Misfortune Release - Releases stored misfortune, can release upto 10 misfortune points doing 1k damage per point. Releases misfortune bypasses all barriers.</li></ul>
