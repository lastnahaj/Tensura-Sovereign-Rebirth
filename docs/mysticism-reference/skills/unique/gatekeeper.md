<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Golden Rule: Grants a permanent Luck 5 effect to the user.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Enkidu - At the cost of 25k MP, imprisons the target in holy chains the player is looking at. Seals all movement and skill usage. Lasts for 120 seconds. Increases to 240 seconds with mastery. Cooldown of 20 seconds. When mastered, the cooldown becomes 10 seconds. Missing will incur a cooldown as well.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] King's Storehouse - Open a Spatial Storage with the dimensions of a large chest featuring 5 weaponry slots (10 slots on mastery). This storage is exclusively for weapons, with each item individually placed without stacking. This storehouse fuels the Gate of Babylon, providing a diverse and powerful arsenal.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Gates of Babylon - Gates of Babylon - Summon your arsenal deposited in your storehouse in the form of light (will be changed to weapon models in 1.21.1.). Wherever the user is looking, the Gates will fire at that location. There is a fixed cooldown of 5 seconds between uses.</li></ul>
<p>The number of portals scale with the mastery of the skill.
0 mastery - 1 portal.
250 mastery - 2 portals.
500 mastery - 3 portals.
750 mastery - 4 portals.
1000 mastery - 5 portals.
</p><p>Crouching and using the skill will cause the Gates to surround the targetted location. If you targetted an entity, the Gates will autonomously target and fire arrows of light. These arrows deal Spatial Damage.
The number of portals scale with the mastery of the skill.
0 mastery - 3 portals.
250 mastery - 4 portals.
500 mastery - 5 portals.
750 mastery - 6 portals.
Mastered - 7 portals.
</p>
