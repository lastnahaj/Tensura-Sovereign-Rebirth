<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Eye of Insight - When toggled on, gain the effects of Thought Acceleration and Magic Sense. Drains 50 Magicules every 5 seconds that this is toggled on.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Overuse:</li></ul>
<p>Using abilities from this skill will build up a counter. If the counter hits 10, it will strain the user’s eyes, putting the entire skill on cooldown for 180 seconds while the user recharges.
</p><p>Resting for 15 seconds after an ability usage will reset the counter to zero.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Intangibility:</li></ul>
<p>The next attack you take will be dodged with 100% chance. For 5 seconds, you are intangible and immune to all attacks, as they simply pass through you. This includes projectiles. However, this passive goes on a 15 second cooldown after.
</p><p>Will build up the counter if this is triggered.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Held/Press] Ejection:</li></ul>
<p>Crouch and Press the ability key to open the ejection GUI. Store up to three different projectile types in your dimension here, up to 128 per stack.
Holding the key will spawn portals. The user must charge for 3 seconds before they can fire, otherwise the portals will not be fully opened.
</p><p>Releasing the key forcefully ejects one projectile from EVERY SLOT in the GUI out at 3x speed than that of a bow would after a wind up, dealing increased damage. On mastery, you can now place 5 projectiles into your GUI.
</p><p>Will build up the counter if this is triggered. Additionally, the charge time is halved on mastery.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Held] Authority of the Gods:</li></ul>
<p>Crouch using will allow you to bring yourself into the Kamui dimension over a period of time, determined by your player’s current size. For reference, a 2 block tall default sized player will take 2 seconds to warp into the dimension. Releasing crouch will cancel this process.
</p><p>On mastery, double the speed that it would take to transfer you into the dimension. You can also now target other entities provided they are within your interaction range.
Will build up the counter if this is triggered.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active (Mastery), Press] Intangibility: The user becomes able to control their intangibility. On pressing the key, become intangible for 5 seconds, allowing the user to walk straight through projectiles, as well as dodge ALL attacks. Cooldown of 10 seconds after it wears off.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
