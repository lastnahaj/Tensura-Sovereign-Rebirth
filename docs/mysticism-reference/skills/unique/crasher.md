<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive - toggle] Thought Acceleration - Increase magic casting speed multiplier by 2, increase movement speed by 0.01 (0.02 with Mastery), increase attack speed by 0.2 (0.4 with Mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive - In Slot] Unstable Transmitter - Falling below 10% health gives you 75% of your maximum health as absorption. Furthermore, at the time of being damaged below 10%, all enemies nearby will be teleported away. Puts the skill on a cooldown of 120 seconds.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Void Infusion - Infuse your fists with the power of the void, dealing extra armor damage and passively severing the target for a small percentage of their health.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Press] Destroyer Haki - Emanate the aura of the Destroyer, heralding the end as you inflict nearby enemies with unavoidable, non-resistable Void damage at 25 per second (50 when mastered). Additionally, crush the souls of your enemies under the weight of their sins, applying Slowness for 10 seconds (12.5 seconds when mastered) — its level scaling with how far your power exceeds theirs. However, wielding such destructive power takes its toll: every 3 seconds there is a 20% chance to inflict Insanity upon yourself, stacking in level and lingering for 10 seconds per application. Costs 500 MP per second to use.
<ul><li>Bonus Info: Destroyer Haki is immune to cooldowns. Be careful when using in spawn areas or areas that limit ability use.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] Dimension Hopper - Switch the dimensions, allowing you to scroll to select the dimensions (Overworld, Nether, End, Hell,Spirit Dimension) and hop between them.  On release, your dimension will change. Nothing will happen if you release and you are still in that dimension. Cooldown of 600 seconds. 300 when mastered.</li></ul>
