<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Unbreakable Defense:</li></ul>
<p>This toggle requires at least 2,000 Loyalty points from all your subordinates.
Starting at 2,000 Loyalty, gain 2% Damage Reduction, up to a maximum of 50% at 50,000 Loyalty points.
Killing the user’s loyal subordinates is the only way to weaken this defense.
</p><p>Only the loyalty points of nearby subordinates (within the range of Undying Loyalty) is considered for your defense. While Unbreakable Defense is toggled, the user can no longer utilize their Skills, Magic and Battlewill. Only Physical attacks and Haki can be used.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Undying Loyalty:</li></ul>
<p>Loyalty will build two points (four on mastery) at a time on nearby subordinates (8 block range, doubled on mastery) every five seconds. For every 2,000 total loyalty points up to a maximum of 50,000, strengthen your Unbreakable Defense by 2%.
Use the (Return) mode to retrieve that subordinate’s loyalty points.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Under Orders:</li></ul>
<p>Your subjects are loyal and believe in your rule. Upon changing the targeting of a mob, grant them Inspiration I for 30 seconds. On mastery, this upgrades to Inspiration II for one minute.
</p>
<ul><li>[Mastery, Passive, True] Powerful Control:</li></ul>
<p>Any spells or skills that place targets under your control no longer requires you to stare at them for long periods of time. This includes the following:
Greed, Dominate, Demon Dominate, Demon Marionette
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Takeover:</li></ul>
<p>On activation, the target you are looking at instantly gets placed under your control with no wind up. The target’s EP must be lesser than your amount of magicules and cannot exceed 600k EP. On acquiring more Mental Magic, this cap increases by 100k per.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Thought Domination (Movement):</li></ul>
<p>Allows the user to control their subordinate's movements in a radius around them. There are four available options, "Follow", "Wander", "Stay" and "Meat Shield".
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Thought Domination (Targeting):</li></ul>
<p>Allows the user to control their subordinate's entity targeting in a radius around them. There are four available options, "Passive", "Aggressive", "Neutral" and "Protect".
</p>
