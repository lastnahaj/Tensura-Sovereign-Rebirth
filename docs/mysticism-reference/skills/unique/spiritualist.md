<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Chant Reduction - If a spell is not mastered, your cast speed is incredibly reduced through the spirits assistance.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Chant Annulment - You no longer require chanting to cast any magic if the spell is mastered.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Spiritfarer - The spirits protect you, reducing all magic and spiritual damage by 25%. Physical attacks are reduced by 10%.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Leyline Connection - All magicule consumption is halved.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Harvester - Passively gain Souls through kills, even if you are not a Majin.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Carve - Spin the Ritual Scythe, harvesting Souls in an area around you. The amount of souls gained is dependent on the damage dealt. Cooldown of 10 seconds. Earn 1 mastery point on usage.</li></ul>
<p>The Soul formula is as follows:
(Mainhand Weapon Damage + Base Atk Dmg) x Critical Multiplier (typically 1.5) / 5
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Scroll] Enhance - The souls of the dead help you. While holding down this mode, scroll to choose a percentage of your souls to use on your next Magic/Spiritual attack which will boost it's damage by that percentage, +10%.</li></ul>
<p>Example:
</p><p>Solar Rain deals 30 per bolt. Assume you have 8 bolts in one usage of Solar Rain. By default, this will deal 30 x 8 = 240 damage. Using 100% of your souls, you will deal +33 (110%) for each bolt resulting in 63 x 8 = 504 damage. If I set the level to 50%, it would instead be +60% damage. So on, so forth.
</p>
