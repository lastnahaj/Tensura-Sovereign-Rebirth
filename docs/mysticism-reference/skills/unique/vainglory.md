<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Soulscape - Killing any entity will allow their soul to linger for a short period of time. The user has 30 seconds to choose whether or not to keep this entity or it will disappear forever. See [Shadow Warrior].</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Essence of Eclipse - Your shadows grow restless. All Shadow Warriors gain increased damage and speed during the night and gain armor and armor toughness in the day.  Does not affect the player, unless a specific condition is met. See [Shadow Merge].</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Rewrite Fate - Any deaths that the user may take are rewritten at the cost of 25% of the user's MP (20% on mastery). The user will then teleport to a random location next to the target. Susano'o Fantasy Destruction overwrites this and outright kills you.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Shadow Warrior - Pressing the mode while looking at a lingering soul will create a Shadow Warrior version of the mob you killed, at the cost of the target's EP before you killed them. No longer consumes Maximum MP, but instead Current MP. Yes, includes Otherworlders but not bosses. Shadow Warriors are loyal to the player and act like a permanent subordinate.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Shadow Storage - Store any subordinate you are looking at into an item. Right click the ground to re-summon them.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Shadow Merge - While holding any of your shadow warrior subordinates in item form, pressing the skill key will allow you to harness their abilities, meaning their stats are added onto yours. Their max EP is added to your EP while Merge is happening. Pressing Shadow Merge again while merged will give you the original subordinate back in item form. Dying will make you permanently lose this subordinate.</li></ul>
<p>While Merged, Essence of Eclipse applies to you as well.
</p>
