<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Repeating Action:</li></ul>
<p>Any physical damage you perform will be repeated a second time.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Recursion Depth:</li></ul>
<p>When activated, millions and billions of the user from other realities come together for 5 seconds.
</p><p>Every successive hit in a row decreases the user’s next hit’s damage by 10%, but the target will accumulate damage over the next 5 seconds.
After the timer is up, all the damage you have dealt is added up and multiplied by 1.5x.
</p><p>Example:
Activate &gt; 50 &gt; 45 &gt; 40.5 &gt; 36.45 &gt; 32.805 &gt; Skill mode ends.
(50 + 45 + 40.5 + 36.45 + 32.805) x 1.5 = 204.755 x 1.5 = 307.1325.
Total Damage: 514.8875
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Flarefrost Storehouse:</li></ul>
<p>The essence of a painful, burning memory has been unlocked. An empty storage sits before you, awaiting power from a strange, two pronged blade.
The essence of a biting embraceful memory has been unlocked. An empty storage sits before you, awaiting power from a strange, long blade.
</p><p>Press to instantly move the held Memory Blades into your mainhand and offhand, Axiom-first. Crouch and press to manually manipulate the storage.
</p><p>(To unlock the mode memoires has the be defeated first)
</p><p><br/>
</p>
