<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Self Replication - Should the user be less than 20% HP, automatically create a clone of the user. Maximum of 5 clones at once. Your clones can also self-propagate.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Adaptive Replication - The user gains more learn points for resistances.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggled] Shared Design - Become able to master resistances. When a resistance is mastered, the nullifcation form is acquired automatically</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Organic Distortion - Warps and distorts the targets body, dealing 50 damage and giving them the Distorted effect for 10 seconds. Cooldown of 20 seconds.
<ul><li>Distorted: Grants enemy gravity increase, attack damage reduction, movement speed reduction and disrupts skill casting</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Anatomy Manipulation - Allows the user to change the size of their body with the scroll wheel while the mode is held down.</li></ul>
