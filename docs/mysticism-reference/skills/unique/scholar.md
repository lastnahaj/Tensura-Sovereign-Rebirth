<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Acquisition] Knowledge of the Ages:</li></ul>
<p>On acquisition of [Scholar], the user instantly learns all Schematics, provided that the skill is permanent and not temporary.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Quick Reader - All of the user's learning and mastery speeds is increased by +6.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Study - On being attacked, you have a 20% chance by default to gain a Study Point for that specific attacker entity type. Each Study Point you have will increase your innate dodge chance towards that specific entity type up to a maximum of 50% (75% on mastery). Each Study Point increases your dodge chance for that entity type by 2%.</li></ul>
<p>If the attacker is a player, you will instead gain dodge for THAT player, and not players in general. This can go up to 20% when unmastered, and 40% when mastered.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Library of Babel:</li></ul>
<p>Open a book storage GUI. In this menu, the user may only deposit books, such as book and quills, normal books, written books or enchanted books.
</p><p>By placing an enchanted book in a slot, the user may right click that slot to open an upgrading menu, allowing the user to choose which level they would like to upgrade or downgrade that book's level to.
</p><p>Be mindful that upgrading a book utilises MAXIMUM magicules and not CURRENT.
This can go up to five levels past the limit, with the cost increasing for each level of enchantment.
</p>
<ul><li>[Scholar]'s GUI now works when upgrading levels:</li></ul>
<p>Right clicking will now open a GUI that allows the user to select the level of the enchantment on the book, but it will take (10 * level of the incoming enchantment) experience levels from the user to upgrade.
</p><p>You will gain mastery points based on this formula: (Level of the incoming enchantment) / 2, rounded up.
</p><p>Example: I am upgrading Fortune 5 to Fortune 6. It will cost me 60 Experience Levels and give
me 6/2 = 3 mastery points.
</p>
