<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Isha’s Tears - All players, even the wielder, and mobs, regardless of EP, are slowed by the player’s baneful aura. Slowness 4 for 10 seconds while near the player. The wielder only gets Slowness 2. Player gains Resistance 2 passively.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Homing - All thrown objects home in on targets.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Veil - When dropping below 25% HP, knock all entities within a 12 block radius backwards by a large amount and grant them Slowness 10 and Burden 4 for 30 seconds. Cooldown of 5 minutes.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (100.0 MP)] Gust - Generate strong blasts of wind to throw and manipulate objects or deadly, sharpened wind. No cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Turbulent Force - Pick up and control the movements of an entity you pick up. Maximum height of 5 blocks. (10 on mastery).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press (1000.0 MP.)] Grief - Cry out, knocking back entities within 8 blocks with moderate knock back and inflicting them with blindness and weakness 2 for 10 seconds. Cooldown of 10 seconds</li></ul>
