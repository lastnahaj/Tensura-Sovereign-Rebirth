<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] Aortic Work of Art:</li></ul>
<p>When attacking, mark your enemies’ blood vessels. Every slash from a physical weapon grants the target +1 mark.
</p><p>If the target reaches 5 marks, the following happens:
If the target dies, they explode in a burst of burning blood, dealing splash damage and igniting other nearby enemies on fire.
</p><p>If the target reaches 10 marks, the following happens:
Rupture an increasing number of blood vessels every 10 seconds, dealing 5% of your target's maximum health until they perish.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Heighten:</li></ul>
<p>On activating this mode, gain an instant increase in blood flow for a short period of time, allowing you to react faster, move faster and attack faster.
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Rupture:</li></ul>
<p>If the target is marked with at least 15 marks, remotely <a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-rupturing/" title="Effects/Rupturing">Rupture</a> their blood vessels. It will slowly trickle down their health until they die, and this effect cannot be removed unless using a Predation skill to isolate the effects.
</p><p>If the target dies, they will explode in a violent burst of burning blood.
</p>
<ul><li>[Art, Press] 血淋林的爱:</li></ul>
<p>After killing an entity with over 10 marks,  gain a charge for (血淋淋的爱).
</p><p>Open a GUI, where the latest target you attack will be displayed. A model of the target’s anatomy is shown. You have up to three slashes. CLICK on their anatomy to choose where to butcher.
</p><p>Save and confirm your choice with the Decapitate button. On confirming, sever the blood flow to those parts, PERMANENTLY until they die. <br/>
If the target has any form of regeneration skill, this effect can be reduced. <br/>
No regeneration skills -&gt; Infinite duration. <br/>
Self Regeneration toggled on (prior to being affected) -&gt; 2 minutes. <br/>
Ultraspeed Regeneration/Survivor toggled on (prior to being affected) -&gt; 1 minutes. <br/>
Infinite Regeneration toggled on (prior to being affected) -&gt; 15 seconds. <br/>
</p><p><a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-severed-leg/" title="Effects/Severed Leg">Severed Leg</a>: Halves your ability to jump or move. <br/>
<a href="../../../core-mechanics/effects-severed-arm/" title="Effects/Severed Arm">Severed Arm</a>: Cooldowns multiplied by 1.5x for one. Multiplied by 2x for both. Additionally halves attack damage and attack power per arm. Makes the target unable to attack if both arms are failing. <br/>
Torso: Mutilate the target and consume their heart, dealing high damage and healing the user for the damage dealt. <br/>
</p><p><br/>
</p>
