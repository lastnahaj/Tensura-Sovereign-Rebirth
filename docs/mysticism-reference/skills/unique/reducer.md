<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Beloved by Energy:</li></ul>
<p>The user's magicule and aura regeneration rate is increased by 10x.
</p>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Purification:</li></ul>
<p>All of the user's attacks and abilities cost 50% less energy to utilize, be it Aura or Magicules.
On mastery, all abilities cost 66% less energy.
<br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] Emancipation:</li></ul>
<p>On toggling this ability on, you will deal additional Holy Damage (1.75x) on top of whatever damage you were dealing.
Press again to toggle the ability off.
Costs 2000 Magicules to toggle on, and 300 to maintain every second. This cost is affected by (Purification).
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Purity Edge:</li></ul>
<p>The ability to channel pure holy spiritrons into your blade. For the next 3 seconds (5 on mastery) after activation, the user's next physical strike with a weapon is greatly empowered! It will instantly deal 25% of the target's health in damage, multiplied by 2 if the target is a Majin.
</p><p>On mastery, this upgrades to 50%, which will one shot all majins without one-shot protection.
</p><p>However, beware the drawback. Failure to channel your energy in time will cause the Holy Energy to recoil and damage the user instead, dealing 75% HP (50% HP on mastery) in damage to the user. Cooldown of 120 seconds, reducing to 60 on mastery.
</p><p>Costs 10,000 Magicules to activate, which is affected by (Purification).
</p>
