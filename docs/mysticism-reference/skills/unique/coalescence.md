<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Hindrance Removal - When activating this passive, instantly be cleared of any movement-hindering effects. This includes Burden and Slowness.</li></ul>
<p>While toggled, those effects cannot be applied to you.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Fixation - Using this skill mode will aim to target an entity 5 blocks ahead of you (10 blocks on mastery).
<ul><li>When missing, this mode goes on a 5 second cooldown.</li>
<li>If you successfully target an entity, Fixate that entity in place, making it impossible for them to move or attack. Your target cannot utilize skills while fixated. They also cannot turn their mouse. This lasts for 15 seconds. When the effect wears off, it cannot be re-applied for one minute.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Solidification - Solidify the air molecules in front of you, creating an invisible shield three blocks in front. You cannot walk through this shield, but it blocks all damage and projectiles from passing through it.
<ul><li>Crouch and activate to remove it. Cooldown of 10 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>[Art, Press] Eternal Domain - At the user’s location, instantly create a Fixation field around you. When inside this field, gain the Stasis effect, setting all your magicule costs to ZERO while you are within the field. The field is 8 blocks big, doubling to 16 on mastery.</li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>Any targets caught WITHIN the radius of Eternal Domain are FIXATED while the domain exists.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li><ul><li>Cooldown of 180 seconds. On mastery, this reduces to 90 seconds.</li></ul></li></ul>
