<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, True] Heavens Favor - Changes the normal MP gain of 3% to 10% (15% on mastery) on killing a mob. Overrides any EP gamerules.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, In Slot] - Martial Prodigy - Battle wills can be learned instantly and gain a flat 50 (100 when mastered) to attack damage while holding no weapon in either hands.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Breakthrough - Every 1000 mob kills, you gain a Breakthrough level, allowing you to temporarily boost MP gain by 3x for 5 minutes. You also temporarily gain Strengthen 15 and Resistance 5 during these 5 minutes. After using Breakthrough, go on a 1 hour cooldown. Gain 100 mastery points every time you use Breakthrough.</li></ul>
