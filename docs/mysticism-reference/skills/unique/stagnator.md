<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Stagnate Aura - Costs 500 mp/s. While held, entities within a 25 block radius of the user cannot move or use physical/aura attacks. Mental/Magical/Skill based ones can still be used.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Inaction - Costs 100mp/s. While Held, the user cannot move or use physical/aura based attacks, but can also not have their current HP reduced (SHP can still be reduced. Effects like that of Cook’s chaotic Fate can bypass this.)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Toggle] Stasis Coat - When used, grants severance to the user’s melee attacks, as well as +10 damage.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Stasis Shot - Costs 1000 mp. User launches an Air Projectile that Bypasses Armor, doing 5 damage and severing the target’s HP. The Projectile travels at a speed of 25 m/s Cooldown of 20 Seconds {This mode must be learned}</li></ul>
