<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Turboblaze - Increases damage of all Fire-based attacks by 2x. Stacks with Flame Manipulation and Flame Domination.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Blue Flare - On activation, the user surrounds the area they are looking at in blue flames dealing 60 damage for as long as targets stay inside it. On impact, it creates a 20x20 zone. Cooldown of 10 seconds, 5 with mastery. Costs 400 MP to use.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Fusion Flare - Costs 400 MP to use. The user strikes where they are looking with a massive flame, scorching all those trapped, pulling them towards the center of it dealing 40 Flame attack damage per second, lasting 5 seconds.</li></ul>
<p>If the user has the Unique Skill [Zekrom] in slot, additionally summon lightning down to strike the user’s target, dealing 40 Electricity Damage, if this happens, the cost is increased to 700 MP. 20 Second CD
</p>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Draconic Meteor - Costs 400 Magicules to use. Using draconic power, the user forms a meteor and brings it down from the sky, striking down at the targeted zone dealing 70 Magic Damage, destroying surrounding blocks and knocking everything back. 20 second cooldown.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Flamethrower - Cost 70 Magicules per second while active. Works like Flame Breath, instead dealing 30 fire damage each second.</li></ul>
