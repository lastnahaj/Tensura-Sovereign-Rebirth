<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Damage Redirection - Any magic damage taken by the user is countered fully. (double the damage on mastery)</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, True, requires Mastery] Destiny Switch - When User takes damage below 25% swaps their place with the farthest mob in a 40 block radius.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Malice Exchange: Takes your or an entities debuffs and lets you put it onto other entities.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Redirect - Lets you swap the places of yourself with a different entity.</li></ul>
