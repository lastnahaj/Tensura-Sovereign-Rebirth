<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Excitation - Raise your body temperature to inflict Fire Damage to all enemies you touch. Additionally, while in slot, all Flame attacks deal 2x more damage.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active] Fire Breath - Spew superheated flames at your enemy which are 2x stronger than that of the intrinsic Fire Breath. Stacks with the passive.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active] Providence - Instantly melt and turn all nearby (16x16) blocks into Lava. Does not work if there is water nearby. Works so long as the skillgrief gamerule is not on.</li></ul>
