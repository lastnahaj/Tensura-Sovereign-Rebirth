<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Boost - increases damage dealt by fire and light attack x8 (does not stack with flame manipulation/domination and light manipulation/domination).</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active - Hold] Black Flame Breath: Breathe black flames that do 10 damage per second (20 when mastered). Cost 50 MP per second.</li></ul>
