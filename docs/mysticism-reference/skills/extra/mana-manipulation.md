<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive">Passive</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Increased Elemental damage by 20% (40% with mastery), and Magic damage by 100% (150% with mastery).</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Passive, Toggle] Decrease received Elemental and Magic damage by half.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[True Passive] Mana Optimization: Skill and magic spell's cost decreased by 33%.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Active">Active</span></h3>
<ul><li>[Active, Hold] Global Interference: Gives the Magic Interference effect to non-allied to the user. Cost 300MP per second.</li></ul>
<ul><li>[Active, Press] Local Interference: Gives the Magic Interference effect to a single target. Cost 300MP.</li></ul>
