<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Causes">Causes</span></h2>
<p>Imbalanced is an invincible effect that is caused by attacking someone that activated the ability "Counter" of <a href="../../skills/unique/restricted/" title="Restricted">Restricted</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span></h2>
<ul><li>Attack speed <b>decrease</b> by 100% (Disabled attack speed)</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<ul><li>Does not have a higher level than base level (1).</li></ul>
