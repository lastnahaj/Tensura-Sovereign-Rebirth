</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span></h2>
<ul><li>Healing is reduced by 80% and it takes 5 seconds for healing to activate.
<ul><li>Ex: If the target is missing 700 health and has a max health of 1000, instead of healing 200 HP, it heal 140 HP.</li></ul></li></ul>

<ul><li>Unlike <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_Flame%7C">Black Flame</a>, Brimstone Flame applies to Infinite Regen as well</li></ul>
