<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Causes">Causes</span></h2>
<p>Eternal Permafrost is applied by "Waltz" with its unique engraving, "Boreal Frost".
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span></h2>
<ul><li>For 10 seconds, movement speed is heavily reduced</li></ul>
<ul><li>Every 2 seconds, the effect of this movement speed reduction decreases.</li></ul>
<ul><li>While Eternal Permafrost is active, the target's Battlewill and Magic have a 50% chance to fail and enter a 5 second cooldown.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<ul><li>Skills are not affected</li></ul>
