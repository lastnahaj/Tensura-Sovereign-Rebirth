<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Causes">Causes</span></h2>
<p>Collective is caused by Activating the Skill Aleph: Null in <a href="../../skills/intrinsic/relapse/" title="Relapse">Relapse</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span></h2>
<p>While targeting your Aleph clone, instantly assimilate your Aleph clone into yourself, temporarily fusing your existences to enter an empowered state for a short period of time. Gain a 1.5x to the user's major stats for two minutes.
</p><p>including Health, SHP, magicules and aura.
</p><p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<p>Will first need to have your Aleph clone out. But also you will need to be in spirit form.
</p>
