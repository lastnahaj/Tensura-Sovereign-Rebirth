<p>Rupturing is an effect that...
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Causes">Causes</span></h2>
<p>The effect is caused by having 10 marks on a target with the Unique skill <a href="../../skills/unique/butcher/" title="Butcher">Butcher</a>.
</p><p>For level two of the effect, use the active ability "Rupture" when the target has 15 marks.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span></h2>
<p>Damages the player every second by 5% of their max health with "Ruptured Veins" damage type. The damage is scaled with the level of the effect.<br/>
The damage calculation is: (Amplifier + 1) * 0.05
</p>
<ul><li>Rupturing I : <code>(0 + 1) * 0.05</code> = 5% max hp / s</li>
<li>Rupturing II : <code>(0 + 1) * 0.05</code> = 10% max hp / s</li></ul>
<p><br/>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Info">Info</span></h2>
<p>Categorised as "Harmfull", similar to Poison and Wither.
</p>
