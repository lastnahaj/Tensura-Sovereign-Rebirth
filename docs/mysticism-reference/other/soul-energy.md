<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Functionality">Functionality</span></h2>
<p>On first reincarnation, the player is given a random Soul Energy amount ranging from 500,000 to 7,000,000. You will always have enough to gain your first Unique Skill.
</p><p><br/>
Soul Energy is consumed when obtaining more Unique Skills.
</p><p><br/>
Failure to meet the Soul Energy cost will prevent you from gaining these abilities.
Soul Energy is not rerolled by reset scrolls. It replenishes to your maximum value from character reset, and isn't affect by the other scrolls (though using a skill reset will typically cause you to gain a unique skill, which will cost soul energy).
</p><p>To gain more soul energy the following action will raise your soul energy permenantly per world:
</p>
<ul><li>Receiving a name. Self-naming only grants up to 250k. If you are named by another player, soul energy gain is based on how much more soul energy they have compared to you, with a max gain of 1 million. You can gain 0 soul energy if the namer has less soul energy than you. Only works once and cannot happen again even after a reset.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Awakening. Grants the player 500k to 1mil Soul Energy. Can trigger every reset.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Completing a Reset Counter. Grants the player 500k to 1mil Soul Energy.</li></ul>
<p><br/>
More ways to obtain more Soul Energy coming soon.
</p>
