<p>To enter the Spirit Realm one must fly to the roof at the praying platform in the labyrinth with <b>20K - 30K EP</b>, because else they will die to magic poisoning.
</p><p>To exit the Spirit Realm in 1.19 you will need to find a portal/big structure within any of the 7 biomes. (images provided within the biome links.)
</p><p>In 1.21.1 you just need to fall into the void.
</p><p>You can go into a singleplayer test world and use /locate while in the dimension to see what these structures look like!
