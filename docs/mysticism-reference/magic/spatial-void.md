<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<p><br/>
While charging the spell, all entities in a 30 block radius from the user (except subordinates) will gain intense gravity and movement speed reduction.
<br/>
</p><p>When fully charged, unleash a powerful Spatial Cube that rapidly deals 750 Spatial damage (2000 on mastery) per tick. AKA 20 times a second.
<br/>
</p><p>Enemies hit with the cube will be slowed down and given Movement Interference.
</p>
