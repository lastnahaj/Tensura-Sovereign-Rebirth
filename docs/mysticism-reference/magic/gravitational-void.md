<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect">Effect</span></h2>
<ul><li>On activation, summon a violent gravitational black hole. Nearby blocks and targets will be pulled into its center.</li></ul>
<p>Targets being pulled by the black hole gain Slowness 1.
</p>
<ul><li>Upon reaching the center, blocks will be deleted. The more blocks and entities that the black hole absorbs, the bigger the size.</li></ul>
<ul><li>The black hole bypasses resistances and barriers.</li></ul>
<p>The bigger the black hole, the more damage it deals. It initially starts at 50 Gravity/Earth damage, but can scale up to 250/250 at maximum.
</p>
<ul><li>The black hole lasts for one minute.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Crouching before using the skill will remove the black hole.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mastery_Bonus">Mastery Bonus</span></h2>
<ul><li>Cooldown reduced to 5 minutes</li></ul>
<ul><li>Decreases cost to 300,000MP</li></ul>
<ul><li>The black hole lasts for three minutes.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Decreases cast time to 120 ticks</li></ul>
