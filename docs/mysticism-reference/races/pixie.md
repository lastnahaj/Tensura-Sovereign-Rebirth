<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_Ability">Racial Ability</span></h2>
<ul><li>Spirits race are able to fly at all times. Upon receiving this race, members are randomly assigned one of seven elemental affinities: Darkness, Earth, Flame, Light, Space, Water, or wind.</li></ul>
<p>Each Element gives their respective elemental manipulation, resistance and magic.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Special_Traits">Special Traits</span></h2>

<ul><li>Earth Transform</li>
<li>Water Transform</li>
<li>Wind Transform</li></ul></li></ul>
