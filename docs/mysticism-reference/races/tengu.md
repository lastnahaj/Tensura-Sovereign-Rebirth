<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_Ability">Racial Ability</span></h2>
<p>The Tengu Race grants the ability to toggle flight when not crouching or sprinting. Activating or deactivating flight plays a level-up sound effect.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Special_Traits">Special Traits</span></h2>
<ul><li>Guarantee Elemental in Labrinth.</li>
<li>Light Spirit summoned will be Lesser or Higher.</li>
<li>Loses <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Possession">Possession</a> and <a class="external text" href="https://tensurareincarnated.wiki.gg/wiki/Light_Transform">Light Transform</a></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Evolution">Evolution</span></h2>
<p><a href="../tengu-saint/" title="Tengu Saint"> Tengu Saint</a> - 400K EP req &amp; slay 4 bosses as a Tengu.
</p>
