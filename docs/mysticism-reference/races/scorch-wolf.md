<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_Ability">Racial Ability</span></h2>
<ul><li>Mount: Make use of your speed and be a mount for other players! Press your Race key (default 'R') to put another player on your head. Pressing your Race key again will kick them off.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Evolution">Evolution</span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="../blazing-scorch-wolf/" title="Blazing Scorch Wolf">Blazing Scorch Wolf</a>: 200k EP and have Mastered <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Flame_Manipulation">Flame Manipulation</a>, <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Wind_Manipulation">Wind Manipulation</a> and <a class="external text" href="https://tensura.wiki.gg/wiki/Earth_Manipulation">Earth Manipulation</a>, while in the Nether.</li></ul>
