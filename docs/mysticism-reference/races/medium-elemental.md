<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_Ability">Racial Ability</span></h2>
<ul><li>Spirits race are able to fly at all times. Upon receiving this race, members are randomly assigned one of seven elemental affinities: Darkness, Earth, Flame, Light, Space, Water, or wind.</li>
<li>Each Element gives their respective elemental manipulation, resistance and magic.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Special_Traits">Special Traits</span></h2>
<ul><li>Spiritual being.</li>
<li>Gain Medium Magic.
<ul><li>Darkness - Darkness Transform</li>
<li>Earth - Earth Transform</li>
<li>Flame - Flame Transform</li>
<li>Light - Light Transform</li>
<li>Space - Space Transform</li>
<li>Water - Water Transform</li>
<li>Wind - Wind Transform</li></ul></li>
<li>Able to contract with a player.</li>
<li>Spawns in labyrinth.</li>
<li>Requires a body.</li></ul>
